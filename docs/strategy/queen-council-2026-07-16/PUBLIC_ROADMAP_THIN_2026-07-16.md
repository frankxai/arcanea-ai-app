# Arcanea Public Roadmap (thin slice)
**Status:** DRAFT for optional publish · **Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Full strategy remains private** in the product repository.

## Where the world is going
Arcanea is becoming a **World Creation OS** — tools for original worlds that stay consistent across stories, characters, and visuals — with the Arcanea franchise as living proof.

## Now
- First-session world creation and keepable proof artifacts  
- Honest product surfaces on arcanea.ai  
- Creator-first free tier with bring-your-own keys  
- Governed lore and visual quality for Arcanea Codex  

## Next
- Cloud continuity across devices  
- Studio compute packs for image and short video  
- Open tools for agents (MCP) so external builders can plug in  
- Community gallery of worlds (guided, not chaos)  

## Later
- Academy courses for creators and operators  
- Deeper encyclopedias and multiplayer world rooms  

## What we will not do
- Promise features we cannot ship  
- Train on or redistribute third-party franchise character databases  
- Force you to give up ownership of your worlds  

## Feedback
Founding Circle and waitlist on arcanea.ai. Product priorities follow real usage, not vanity.
