# Arcanea Build: setup-planning proposal

Recorded: 6 October 2026. Status: working name and product hypothesis; no canon, pricing, release or production change authorised by this document.
Source: Frank's visible Codex discussion on gaming, machines, creation and naming.

## Customer job

Help a gamer or creator decide what to keep, upgrade or buy for their actual games and creative workloads, budget and existing machine.

The result should be a justified compatible plan with sourced offers, transparent tradeoffs and a useful export. It should identify what is measured, inferred or unknown.

## Fit with Arcanea

Arcanea already has a broad creative-platform role. Setup planning could support people who play and create with the same machine. Its relationship to worldbuilding should be demonstrated through a useful customer workflow.

"Arcanea Build" is an assistant recommendation, not a selected brand. The proposed English pronunciation ar-KAY-nee-ah is also a preference for testing, not a new canonical truth.

Candidate copy:
"Plan a setup around the games you play and the things you want to create."

## Useful complete output

- Existing machine and goals, with permission for any imported profile.
- A keep/upgrade/build decision and explicit compatibility checks.
- Regional offer timestamps and total costs where available.
- Capability estimates labelled separately from real benchmarks.
- Clear reasons, alternatives, export and revision history.
- Recovery from stale data, interrupted tools or unavailable offers.
- Optional game recommendations based on capability and preference.
- Disclosed commercial incentives.

Do not invent FPS, current prices, stock, best-price coverage or endorsements.

## Architecture and overlap

Use grounded data and typed validation. Models can explain intent and tradeoffs; deterministic checks enforce compatibility and budget.

GenCreator has separate authorship and creator-tool-stack work. Reuse sound components where authorised without replacing that product or duplicating an accepted engine. Starlight has a technical role; RealityDiffusion's broader research role remains proposed.

The same product identity can appear on the web and through supported ChatGPT/Codex interfaces, with capabilities adapted to the surface:

## Comparison and evidence

Compare a real task against PCSpecialist and other existing configurators, plus the user's current workflow. Observe whether the plan is usable, how much repair it needs, completion time, total cost and trust in the explanations.

A first proof story should connect an existing machine and budget to a validated plan, then show what the person can play or create with the resulting setup. Each performance claim needs measurements or explicit inference.

## Preservation

Preserve the creative platform, current product work, locked lore and original IP. The save is documentation only. Partner, naming, commercial and implementation decisions remain open.
