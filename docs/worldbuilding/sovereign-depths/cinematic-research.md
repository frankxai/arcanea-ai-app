# Arcanea cinematic craft research

Research date: 2026-09-09. Reference analysis, not Arcanea canon.

Primary studio/developer/artist sources; facts are distinguished from our proposed transfers. Historical development articles are not treated as current feature specifications. Two additional contemporary games have explicit released-status evidence. No copyrighted reference images included.

## Coherent direction

Impossible life and ancient power made physically credible, culturally specific and dramatically consequential.

Adult means credible consequence, agency, restraint and emotional ambiguity. It does not require monochrome darkness, gore or universal cynicism. Luminous wonder uses the same physical discipline as horror.

No universal gold filigree, same-face sovereigns, obligatory crowns, ten recolored Gate arenas, compulsory tragic redemption, or cosmic inflation for every climax.

## Shared invariants

- Bodies have load, joints, breath, contact and an intelligible method of movement; intentional impossibility must be legible as an exception.
- Each image has one dramatic subject and a motivated viewpoint. Use occlusion, foreground evidence and atmospheric distance to establish depth.
- Material response remains consistent across daylight, firelight and magical light. Metal is not skin, wet membrane is not polished stone.
- Light has a source and a dramatic job. Emission is localized; darkness retains enough information to understand form.
- Age appears through use, repair, geological process and ecological succession rather than uniform dirt.
- Supernatural power changes a physical condition—pressure, heat, mass, vibration, motion or perception—rather than merely adding ornament.
- Identity persists through silhouette anchors, material family and movement signature across books, games, images and agent records.

## Benchmark triptych

- A close human-scale antagonist with an expressive adult face and restrained practical costume, dangerous through agency rather than size.
- A massive non-humanoid organism in a luminous ecosystem, with convincing locomotor anatomy and environmental displacement.
- An uncanny dungeon vista with visible traversable routes, historical strata and a single impossible condition.

## Review gates

- Original identity: recognisable without title and without recognizable borrowed franchise signatures.
- Adult proportions: no mascot head/body ratio, toy finish, stock cute gaze or decorative pet posture.
- Physical credibility: load-bearing contacts, limb attachment, clothing construction, scale and material response remain coherent.
- Composition: subject and encounter action readable at mobile thumbnail size; avoid a centered specimen floating in scenic fog.
- Narrative evidence: at least one readable consequence of the creature’s agency or the dungeon’s past.
- Range: the set contains human, animal, colossal, distributed and uncanny threats; daylight and warmth remain available.
- Canon: no new primary gods, false locked status, recycled retired names or Gate assignments unsupported by records.

Reject on any identity, adult-proportion or physical-credibility hard failure. Refine until the three benchmark images establish the standard before scaling. Scores document editorial judgment, not objective studio equivalence.

A cinematic illustration proves visual direction. It does not prove animation, readable gameplay, encounter balance or performance. Those require implemented scenes and playtests.

## Originality boundaries

- **Diablo IV:** Do not reuse Lilith, Blood Bishop anatomy, demon heraldry or Diablo class silhouettes.
- **Diablo IV:** Do not equate more sparks, bloom or colored fog with stronger magic.
- **God of War Ragnarök:** Do not borrow Kratos tattoos, axe, bracer arrangements, Norse runes or character relationships.
- **Dark Souls:** Do not reproduce gaping dragon body plans, gargoyle rooftop encounters, bonfires or immortal-scale experiments.
- **Bloodborne:** Do not substitute Victorian streets, plague churches or cosmic eyeballs for an original cultural history.
- **Skyrim / Elder Scrolls:** Do not clone Nordic crypt kits, Dwemer machinery, dragon walls or Skyrim’s specific dungeon circuits.
- **Ghost of Tsushima:** Do not reproduce samurai costume arrangements, Japanese shrine forms or historical people as generic fantasy decoration.
- **Hogwarts Legacy:** Do not copy Hogwarts towers, house iconography, common rooms or magical-school plot structure. The source’s projected release date is historical and was not used as a current release claim.
- **League of Legends / Runeterra:** Do not borrow champion weapons, costumes, facial identities or Runeterra faction symbols.
- **Dungeons & Dragons:** Do not import D&D proprietary monsters, settings or stat blocks. Use original systems and terminology for Arcanea mechanics.
- **Tolkien film craft:** Do not reproduce Elven filigree, Sauron silhouettes, Balrog anatomy, Gondorian symbols or identifiable Middle-earth architecture. The fabrication rule is our inference from the documented production work.
- **Marvel cosmic / Eternals:** Do not borrow Celestial faceplates, Kirby pattern arrangements, Eternals costumes or MCU cosmic hierarchy.
- **Avatar / Pandora:** Do not copy Na’vi anatomy, neural queues, banshees, tulkun silhouettes, floating mountain compositions or Pandora’s specific ecology.
- **Arcane — season two:** Do not reproduce Warwick, Hextech, Jinx or Arcane’s city/faction designs. Adult animation here is a craft reference, not permission to average its rendering style into every image.
- **Clair Obscur: Expedition 33:** Do not copy Paintress mythology, the age countdown, Nevrons or Belle Époque character designs.
- **Ghost of Yōtei:** Do not copy Atsu, Mount Yōtei compositions, regional architecture or the revenge structure.
