# Arcanea: The Sovereign Depths — World and Encounter Design

> STAGING / EXPERIMENTAL — 2026-09-09. Purpose: proposed adult cinematic creature, dungeon and transmedia direction. Touches locked canon: no. New records are local proposals, not approved canon.

The collection makes scale, danger and agency visible through bodies, places and decisions. Its 24 bosses occupy 12 designed spaces; each pair changes the other’s behaviour. Four proposed trilogies turn those encounters into causal stories with recurring people, competing interests and consequences that survive the final blow.

The pinned repository commit `fc1d1c00b2819c7514ce9061caae20c7a2d24fc1` identifies the canon baseline used for compatibility. It is not the source of the new text. `collection.json` labels every new record as a local proposal and preserves experimental boundaries.

## The creative correction

A credible creature has a body that must bear its own weight. A credible antagonist has something to gain while the protagonist is absent. A credible dungeon continues to function when nobody is fighting in it. The art should make all three claims visible before any lore caption is read.

The earlier pattern of decorative bosses and symbolic self-improvement is deliberately widened here. Animals pursue food, warmth and territory. Occupiers choose military advantage. Rivals defend work they have actually done. A lawful duelist can remain fair and formidable. A spatial mystery can produce reliable observations without surrendering its origin. The resulting stories can end in liberation, a negotiated compact, a profitable but constrained expedition, or a rescued convoy. None requires a universal confession, compulsory sacrifice or cosmic promotion.

The visual throughline is physical credibility under impossible conditions: abrasion at loaded joints, cloth with weight, moisture that follows gravity, mineral growth with structure, and light whose source can be located. It supports basalt heat, open-sea beauty, ceramic stillness, forest predation, high-altitude artillery, glacial daylight and cosmic unease without reducing every region to black stone and gold trim.

## Canon boundaries

- The Ten Gates and their bonded identities remain unchanged. No encounter title grants a new divine office.
- Nero remains fertile unknown and potential. The Lacuna’s danger does not make darkness morally evil.
- Fauna descriptions are ecological labels, not new Origin classes. Body size does not imply magical rank.
- Malachar remains sealed. The final ravine is an exterior road operation with local anchor lines; neither gameplay nor imagery approaches, depicts or opens the canonical prison.
- Chosen independent mortal wrongdoing in the Ashen Astronomer and the Lacuna ontology remain EXPERIMENTAL. The parent dungeons and affected book premises inherit that status so nested summaries cannot quietly promote those claims.
- The four new trilogies are independent proposals. They do not replace the Chronicles or merge themselves into the staged Living Arc.

## Encounter grammar

Each encounter has three authored phases triggered by a readable event rather than arbitrary health thresholds. The data includes the objective, physical arena, trigger, action, counterplay, failure and aftermath. Attack tells originate in the attacker or environment: a brace leg lifts, a furnace wheel moves, a fin folds, a cloth standard tightens, a mirror reflection narrows or a loaded anchor rises. Spectacle never excuses an attack that cannot be read.

Vorrak teaches load transfer and vibration. Ithraden teaches the manipulation of heat lanes. Serath makes anchoring and current-reading the central bodily verbs. Morvane combines shield positioning with releasing physical obstructions. The Regent turns acoustic propagation into traversal; Vaust weaponises the player’s predictable timing. Serevra and Kethrane support declared contests whose defeat states can cost access and priority. Rhaum depends on a supply cycle. Avarra’s fairness is a combat constraint she observes even when it disadvantages her. Mordren’s water reflection is a bounded feint, with the real blade remaining the source of injury.

The sequence deliberately changes scale: an artillery construct can be followed by a dangerous older human; a colossal migrating mollusc can precede a swordmaster whose entire advantage fits within one draw. Escalation comes from sharper constraints and more consequential choices as well as bodily size.

These are encounter designs, not playable implementations or verified balance claims. Animation, collision, camera distance, input latency, accessibility and difficulty tuning require prototype work before the mechanics can be called tested.

## Mythologies have speakers

Each dungeon contains a public story, a competing account, observable evidence and an unresolved question. Belief is attributed to people with reasons to hold it. A creature is not made divine merely because a frightened expedition calls it a king. A machine does not prove the founding legend of the institution that built it. Some material questions can be answered while authorship, motive and meaning remain contested.

### Furnace of the First Breach

Workers say the first furnace fire was borrowed from the mountain and must be returned at winter’s end. The occupation calls the same fire a right earned by conquest.

Furnace slag predates the military contracts; the cooling breach is measurable. Whether the borrowing tale predates the first foundry or arose after its accidents is not settled.

### The Pelagic Cathedral

Divers call the reef a cathedral built by the sea for people who never received burial. Old garrison songs insist the fort was given to the water by a victorious commander.

The inundation ledger records a refused evacuation, and the filter reef is younger than the stairs. The first singer of the drowned dedication is unknown; the reef’s biological history does not decide that authorship.

### Sepulchre of the Hollow Choir

Migrants said a name spoken into a burial bell would find a listener beyond the escarpment. The city’s former magistrates dismissed the avenue as a burial ground without legitimate witnesses.

Letters and household records survive alongside prestigious names; brace paths explain which sounds travel. A few names occur in several incompatible family accounts. Their relationships remain an archival question.

### The Thorn Meridian

The surveyors tell a founding tale about a line so straight the forest agreed to move aside. Canopy crews say the line survives only because generations of workers keep apologising with repairs.

Boundary nails occur inside later tree growth; the restored water channel, not an oath, makes the crossing quiet. No surviving map establishes the first agreement between the road authority and the canopy settlements.

### Bastion of Fallen Suns

Fortress veterans called the mirrors fallen suns that had chosen a military home. Valley households remembered them as borrowed daylight that could be taken back by weather.

Supply tablets and storm damage show the weapon’s dependence on food, clear sky and human calibration. The original dedication may have been propaganda, sincere ritual or both; the machinery does not answer belief.

### The Lacuna Stair

One road crew says the stair is a traveller’s debt: every step taken must be returned. Another insists the chamber simply refuses to accept a straight line drawn badly.

Marked landing connections repeat under documented conditions; neither account explains the surviving tool cache. Maker, intent and ultimate cause remain unresolved. These are experimental in-world interpretations, not cosmological facts.

### The Winter Reliquary

The monastery’s old harvest rite called a seed jar a small summer promised to someone unborn. A later sponsor’s charter treated each jar as the founder’s property held through winter.

Viability tests and warming records establish what can still grow; custody remains a human agreement. The founder’s intended ownership terms survive in conflicting copies and are not settled by the predator encounter.

### The Verdant Crucible

Early researchers described the crater as stone learning to become a forest. Miners tell the reverse tale: a forest slowly turning itself into a treasure house.

Root channels carry water; small parasite colonies and destructive outbreaks have different effects. The first stable living seam is not dated precisely enough to establish which introduced specimens influenced it.

### Court of the Unbroken Light

The two valleys say the court was founded when their champions finally agreed to see the same light. Petitioners also tell a less flattering story about exhausted armies that could no longer afford the pass.

The charter revisions and optical apparatus are recoverable physical records. The relative weight of conviction and military exhaustion in the founding settlement remains a historian’s dispute.

### The Blackwake Arsenal

Harbour crews call the first refuge berth a promise that no storm boat would be turned away. The occupiers recast that promise as a duty to provide ships to whoever can defend the coast.

Press rosters and launch orders show how the original refuge became coerced fleet production. Some early berth claims cannot be authenticated; liberation does not automatically resolve every later ownership dispute.

### The Meridian of Ash

Caravan families say the desert hides its rivers out of embarrassment at how slowly people learn to look. The Astronomer claims only a solitary witness can receive an undivided measurement.

Independent sighting stations can produce comparable water-route measurements; Veyrath follows buried brine. The earliest observational notebooks have missing leaves, leaving their original authorship uncertain.

### The Wound Beneath the Stars

Frontier travellers say a road exists wherever the next household agrees to receive you. The siege operators call isolation a proof that a settlement was never worth saving.

The anchor lines, supply records and surviving flood survey explain the local interdiction. No myth here discloses Malachar’s prison, changes his sealed condition or establishes a new cosmic truth.

## Four independent trilogies

### The Kiln and the Keel — STAGING

Engineer Tamsin Reed and shipwright Garrick Moss try to reopen a winter harbour, discover its material supply has been captured by a coordinated blockade campaign, and turn the occupiers’ production timetable against them.

Standalone proposed trilogy; separate from the Chronicles and Living Arc.

**Tamsin Reed** — Rebuild a harbour that can survive winter without accepting military ownership. Each fast material solution repeats a destructive practice she promised the workers would end.

**Garrick Moss** — Bring the pressed shipwrights home alive. He will trade away long-term independence for a rescue today, putting him at odds with Tamsin.

**The Heat We Inherit** (STAGING). A contract for winter anchors sends the pair into a still-burning foundry. They discover that the same alloy is supplying a mountain gun, and their choice of furnace output determines whether they can follow that shipment in time.

**Under a Broken Sun** (STAGING). The mountain road is blocked by obsolete artillery and a captain trying to claim it. Saving a stranded evacuation column costs the pair their best transport window but yields the blockade’s launch schedule.

**The Harbour That Refused** (STAGING). The shipwrights sabotage their own launch work while Tamsin turns recovered components into a controlled premature launch. Liberation succeeds only if the harbour predator is given a quiet exit and the workers keep command of the rebuilt arsenal.

### The Passage Compact — STAGING

Diver Brigid Vale and cargo assessor Isen Pike seek a winter passage for a struggling coast. Their journey moves from a drowned military obstruction to disputed seed custody and finally a public trial over the route’s governance.

Standalone proposed trilogy; no replacement for existing flagship book sequences.

**Brigid Vale** — Establish a reliable winter route that ordinary crews can navigate. She can survive hazards that the people following her cannot, so personal bravery is an insufficient plan.

**Isen Pike** — Keep promises measurable enough to survive changes of leadership. His precise contracts cannot substitute for trust when a living cargo begins to die.

**The Current Below** (STAGING). The divers reopen a starved reef inlet and recover evidence of a refused evacuation. Their new route reaches a mountain trade station whose seed stock may save the coastal harvest.

**A Winter Held in Glass** (STAGING). Two expeditions must agree on custody while the seed vault thaws around them. Brigid’s rivalry with Kethrane becomes a workable alliance only when both crews accept visible losses and shared transport obligations.

**The Measure of Passage** (STAGING). The mountain court can open the final route, but its old apparatus contradicts the terms of a fair trial. Brigid wins a passage and accepts a public maintenance duty, making the compact more durable than her own reputation.

### The Living Meridian — EXPERIMENTAL

Surveyor Corvin Holt and financier Fenwick Darr follow a promised mineral route through forest, crater and desert. Each discovery changes what their contract can honestly claim, until a water observation must become public knowledge rather than the venture’s private asset.

Independent proposed expedition trilogy; observed ecology is distinguished from disputed mythology.

**Corvin Holt** — Publish a map people can use without discovering its omissions through death. The expedition deadline rewards confident simplification of conditions that keep changing.

**Fenwick Darr** — Return enough value to keep his crew employed. A lower-yield truthful contract threatens his solvency, while an inflated claim would finance the next expedition immediately.

**The Line Through Leaves** (STAGING). A disputed crossing forces the surveyors to face an equally competent rival. Evidence of altered mineral growth beyond the frontier makes Serevra a necessary independent witness rather than a defeated obstacle.

**The Forest in the Stone** (STAGING). A living mineral seam promises the expedition’s fortune until extraction accelerates a parasite outbreak. Fenwick must sign a lower-yield contract before knowing whether it will save his company.

**The Water Under Stars** (EXPERIMENTAL). The desert observation can validate the new route, but a scholar seeks exclusive control of the result. Corvin and Fenwick establish independent measurements during a colossal migration and publish the water map, earning a different business through trustworthy passage services.

### The Unfinished Road — EXPERIMENTAL

Archivist Sabine Rook and surveyor Jolant Grey follow evidence of a campaign intended to isolate the frontier. They must preserve testimony, publish bounded uncertainty and use incomplete records fast enough to save a convoy.

Standalone proposed investigation trilogy; experimental Lacuna chapters remain explicitly opt-in.

**Sabine Rook** — Keep ordinary witnesses in the record of a war written by its commanders. She cannot physically rescue every object, and choosing what to carry has irreversible consequences.

**Jolant Grey** — Recover missing surveyors and prove a safe road exists. He must put his name to a useful account that leaves the chamber’s deepest cause unresolved.

**Names in the Quiet Bell** (STAGING). An occupied burial avenue hides a siege calibration method. Saving the quieter annex yields supply references to a vanished road survey, while destroying it leaves a faster but less provable trail.

**A Stair Without an Answer** (EXPERIMENTAL). The missing surveyors are trapped in a bounded impossible chamber. Their recovery provides an alternate exterior road plan; Jolant refuses to invent an origin story to make the report seem complete.

**The Road at the Edge** (STAGING). Records from the bells, survey and harbour campaign reveal a local bridge interdiction. Sabine and Jolant use that knowledge to protect a frontier convoy, ending at the exterior road with Malachar’s prison untouched.

## Adaptation rules

A novel must choose a sequence. A game can preserve alternate states. The two should share stable identities, materials, geography and historical evidence without pretending every branch happened in one continuity. For example, Kethrane’s testimony in the third Passage Compact book assumes a negotiated or honourably resolved bridge encounter; a game in which her crew was attacked follows a different state.

Books do not need every encounter to become a three-phase duel. The furnace can function as an infiltration, the hunter as a prolonged territorial danger, and the court as a full public contest. The authored mechanics provide causality and physical truth for prose; they should not appear as HUD language or a repeated chapter template.

Cross-series connections remain optional. Blackwake orders can reach Sabine by courier and give an evidence trail a material source. A later trade compact can carry a desert water chart. Those links enrich the shared world without turning twelve books into mandatory homework for one satisfactory ending.

## Agent knowledge and publication boundaries

An agent should retrieve each entity with its canon status, source-of-new-claims marker, baseline purpose and implementation state. It should distinguish the visual identity from an in-world epithet, the in-world belief from an observed rule, and the authored encounter from a tested game. Images do not approve lore. A merged proposal does not automatically make its claims canonical.

The collection keeps the existing repository as the authoritative production surface. It is compatible with a gallery, structured API or MCP projection without declaring those views an independent source of canon. Experimental content must require explicit inclusion in agent retrieval. Public gallery presentation may display proposals and experiments with visible labels; it must not quietly remove those labels in share cards or generated summaries.

## Review evidence and remaining gates

- Authoring consistency check: exactly 24 boss IDs and 12 dungeon IDs, each boss belonging to the declared pair, and 72 complete phase records.
- Completeness check: every boss has specific anatomy, materials, movement, entrance, ecology, history, rivalry and encounter aftermath; every dungeon has inhabitants, discoveries and traversal.
- Naming check: proposed boss names and recurring cast first names have no edit-distance-2 collision with the checked locked divine, Godbeast and retired-name list. This is a local collision check, not an exhaustive trademark clearance.
- Status propagation: b11, b12, b21, d06 and d11 are EXPERIMENTAL; affected book and trilogy summaries carry explicit status.
- Production images, visual critique and deployed UI verification belong to the production review record. This document does not self-certify image quality or claim a live deployment.

Review should challenge excessive human-like anatomy, mechanically unreadable beauty, repetitive grey-and-gold materials, implausible weight, false scale cues, unresolved status leakage and narratives where every ending becomes negotiated repair. The military branch already supports decisive liberation; the ecological branches retain lethal choices with intelligible costs; the rivalry branches preserve equal agency. Further revisions should protect that range.
