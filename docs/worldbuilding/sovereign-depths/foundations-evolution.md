# Arcanea foundations evolution — a wider creative contract

> STAGING PROPOSAL — 2026-09-09. Purpose: evolve creative direction and classification without changing locked identities or events. Touches locked canon: no. No proposed rule below is silently promoted to canon.

Arcanea needs a recognisable creative intelligence across many kinds of life, cultures and stories. A mandatory collection of visual motifs cannot provide that breadth. The proposed correction preserves identity through consistent decisions about bodies, materials, histories and agency while allowing different places to look and behave differently.

## What the audit exposed

The audited STAGING `VISUAL_DOCTRINE` combines a central emblem, beast, technology, celestial anchor and gold light with noble angularity, cosmic dusk and polish. Applied to every image, those requirements become a recipe: different creatures inherit the same decoration, lighting and posture. The earlier gallery's Gate/Guardian categories and ten-per-Gate allocation add a second pressure: classify a subject before discovering what makes it interesting.

The direct prior-art review found a separate but reinforcing problem. Glossy eyes, juvenile faces, spherical companion anatomy and catalogue presentation weakened physical danger and adult dramatic presence. Higher resolution would preserve those choices. The correction must occur before rendering.

These observations do not invalidate every earlier image or the Ten Gates. They identify where a useful brand vocabulary became a compulsory structure for unrelated subjects.

## Proposed replacements

| Audited constraint                                                   | Replacement rule                                                                                                                | Observable review test                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Every image carries emblem, beast, technology and celestial anchor.  | Include only forms and objects justified by the subject, place or event.                                                        | Removing a detail should change identity, function, history or composition; otherwise question its presence.                  |
| Gold light and cosmic dusk establish the universal look.             | Each scene has a deliberate light hierarchy appropriate to its location, weather and dramatic event.                            | A reviewer can locate the key light, separate the focal subject and describe the material response.                           |
| Noble angularity and polish govern every body.                       | Anatomy follows locomotion, sensing, feeding, defence and load. Materials show use and environmental exposure.                  | The body can support the depicted action; wear occurs where forces and contact predict it.                                    |
| Gate/Guardian categories and equal Gate quotas organise the gallery. | Classify by ecology, region, culture, faction, encounter role and narrative function. Gate association is optional and sourced. | A natural predator or mortal rival can be fully described without receiving an invented divine relationship.                  |
| Increasing importance means increasing cosmic rank.                  | Track physical scale, combat threat, political authority and magical attainment separately.                                     | A human captain can be strategically more dangerous than a giant animal without changing either identity.                     |
| Mythology supplies an omniscient answer.                             | Attribute beliefs to speakers; separate observation, interpretation and unresolved history.                                     | Conflicting local accounts can coexist without becoming contradictory approved assertions.                                    |
| Every boss resolves a symbolic wound.                                | Give each actor an independent objective and each encounter a consequence.                                                      | The actor has a plan or behaviour when the protagonist is absent; the aftermath changes a concrete relationship or condition. |

Together these rules establish four durable foundations: **physical causality, cultural identity, dramatic agency and light hierarchy**. They are demanding enough to produce coherence and broad enough to support sunlit wonder, inhabited cities, elegant courts, animal terror and hostile military power.

## Scope of creative expansion

Local ecology can generate predators, grazers, parasites, migrations and symbioses without a new Origin class. Mortal rivals can defend legitimate claims and remain equals after defeat. Unreliable myth can deepen a place while its observable mechanics remain precise. Bounded anomalies can support repeatable local rules while their ultimate origin remains unresolved; those ontology proposals stay EXPERIMENTAL until reviewed.

The current locked statement that Malachar is the only true antagonist remains in force. The Sovereign Depths military enemies are proposed local operations and remnants connected to existing Shadow corruption. Serevra, Kethrane and Avarra are rivals or trial adversaries. The Ashen Astronomer's independent coercion is explicitly EXPERIMENTAL; publishing an image or merging a proposal does not resolve that canon question.

Nero remains fertile unknown and potential, never evil. The Ten Gates and bonded identities remain unchanged. Malachar remains sealed, and the exterior finale neither depicts nor approaches an opening of his prison. No new creature title establishes a divine office.

## Adoption and evidence

Adopt the revised art and classification rules as the Sovereign Depths production contract. Preserve the earlier doctrine as a dated historical proposal and use existing work where its tone fits. Make any later universe-wide doctrine amendment explicit in a separate reviewed change.

The collection's structured records carry proposal status, baseline purpose and local authorship. Images, gallery pages, API responses and MCP retrieval must preserve those distinctions. Experimental markers propagate through nested stories and beliefs; agent retrieval requires explicit inclusion. Books choose one authored continuity, while games may preserve alternate outcomes without claiming that every branch occurred.

Success is demonstrated by individual images that remain recognisably Arcanean without the same emblem or palette, encounters with different bodily verbs and objectives, and stories that sustain independent motives. Production verification must separately establish correct assets, accessible mobile presentation, status-safe retrieval and a working release. None of those checks alone certifies artistic excellence.
