This license exists so the competition can function and winners can ship. It does not let Arcanea sell your workflow as a standalone product without a separate agreement.

Your entry receives a **limited, non-exclusive license to build within the canon for the purpose of this competition** and for personal, non-commercial use of your resulting world.

If your entry invents canon-adjacent lore (new locations, factions, characters consistent with canon), you grant Arcanea a non-exclusive option to adopt it into STAGING canon, **with attribution**. You keep the right to reuse your own inventions in your own non-Arcanea work, provided they are separated from Arcanea's canon identifiers.

You confirm that your entry is your own work (or properly licensed, declared in `entry.json`), that it contains no malicious code and no attempts to manipulate the judging pipeline, and that you have the right to grant the licenses above.

- Season 0 offers recognition and plugin distribution as its prize. No cash prize is offered or implied.

The program is **Arcanea Arena**; the Season 0 competition is **The Worldsmith Trials**. Entrants may say their entry "won / placed in Arcanea Arena Season 0" and may not present their entry as officially endorsed by Arcanea beyond its published placement.
