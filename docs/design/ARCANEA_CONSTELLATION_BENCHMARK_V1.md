# Arcanea Living Constellation — Starlight benchmark and evolution brief

> Status: design evidence, not release approval. Benchmarked on 2026-08-25 against the latest READY Starlight Constellation preview from branch `agent/hermes/constellation-wave0-i2v`, commit `d0f389584eda482d9c96f27eeb7c6563a9ca83d3`, plus its local page specification and implementation.

## Decision

Arcanea should inherit Starlight's rigor, not its skin. The reusable pattern is a public experience in which visual identity sits above inspectable contracts, proof obligations, stopping points, provenance, and a visible human authority boundary. The Arcanea expression must remain a Living Codex of characters, Godbeasts, worlds, and creative intelligences—not porcelain operators in a renamed command center.

## Where Arcanea is deliberately stronger

1. **Story consequence before character posing.** Every contracted image contains an action that changes a decision, exposes a material truth, resolves a relation, or reveals a system law. Mood alone is not a prompt.
2. **Five truth dimensions, kept separate.** Canon, identity, evidence, rights, and release states never collapse into one “approved” badge.
3. **Morphology is countable.** Godbeast limbs, Chords, terminals, sensory architecture, locomotion, and scale evidence have hard floors. Color and aura cannot rescue an incoherent body plan.
4. **World identity is causal.** A world plate must connect geography, labor, material economy, hydrology or energy, maintenance, ecology, and one consequential action. It cannot be an empty fantasy skyline.
5. **The prompt is a public teaching artifact.** All 100 engine-neutral contracts have inspectable routes and exact JSON copies. Provider prompts are derived adapters, not hidden creative truth.
6. **Style selection is an experiment.** Round 01 compares five hypotheses on one source-complete human and one countable non-human anchor. Later rounds use a deterministic 8/2 primary/probe allocation and blind two-critic review.
7. **Identity claims have an evidence ceiling.** Text-only generation cannot earn a perfect continuity score. A qualified identity requires eight distinct approved regression views and a job-specific smallest-sufficient assignment.
8. **Blocked is a designed state.** Nine jobs stay closed where canon, culture, anatomy, or ontology is unresolved. The gallery teaches why instead of silently filling the gaps.

## What Arcanea must not copy

- a uniform robot, porcelain shell, mascot, or collectible-card body system for all agents;
- generic starfields, node clouds, orbit diagrams, or an “AI brain” as the primary visual idea;
- generated labels, tablets, dashboards, glyphs, or capability claims baked into image pixels;
- motion that remixes a face, countable anatomy, material identity, or story action;
- a public team builder that implies deployment authority before the underlying runtime and product boundary require it;
- a hero image merely because it is cinematic; the image must be approved evidence for the exact page claim.

## Arcanea experience architecture

```text
real campaign state
        ↓
16 creative intelligences ── 10 Guardians ── 10 Godbeasts
        ↓                         ↓                 ↓
public dossiers            open identity       morphology truth
        └────────────── 20 book characters ──────────────┘
                              ↓
                    10 inhabitable world systems
                              ↓
                 100 exact prompt-contract lessons
                              ↓
              generated → reviewed → approved → published
                              ↓
                   human-held canon and release
```

The page should feel like an illuminated research institution: editorial scale, quiet geometry, material specificity, and precise status language. Mythic atmosphere is earned by the entities and their actions, not sprayed across the interface.

## Release-quality acceptance

Before proposing a Constellation preview:

- the current source, public prompt copies, schemas, and TypeScript route scope pass;
- the first approved visual is not treated as a universal style or identity master;
- every visible generated asset resolves to an output, evaluation, approval, and public-staging receipt;
- desktop, mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, and no-media fallbacks are inspected;
- no public copy claims a runtime, identity, image, review, or publication state that its receipts do not prove;
- the creator approves brand identity and the exact preview scope before push or deployment.

## Next visual activation

When the machine and authority gates open, execute Round 01 only. Do not prebuild a cinematic hero from an untested style. After blind review and the human style decision, use the selected primary system for eight jobs and the secondary mode for two deterministic probes. Promote a public hero only from later approved ensemble evidence; promote motion only after the corresponding still identity is qualified.
