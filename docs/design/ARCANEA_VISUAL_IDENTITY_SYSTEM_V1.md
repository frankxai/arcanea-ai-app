# Arcanea Visual Identity System v1

> Status: working foundation · human identity approval required
> Date: 2026-08-24
> Operational owner: Arcanea Visual Director, publicly represented by Prismatic

## Decision

Arcanea will operate as a **Living Codex**, not as a single rendering style.

The visual system has two layers:

1. **Identity grammar** — story, posture, silhouette, anatomy, countable structures, maintained objects, materials, localized phenomena, and relational behavior. This layer is stable across engines and media.
2. **Render mode** — editorial plate, cinematic scene, painterly intimacy, sculptural artifact, or dimensional feature form. This layer may change by surface while the identity grammar remains recognizable.

The leading hypothesis is **Living Codex as the primary system** and **Prism Realism as the secondary cinematic mode**. This is not yet a final style choice. Round 01 must test the same two anchors across five systems before a human locks the direction.

The implementation benchmark in `docs/design/ARCANEA_CONSTELLATION_BENCHMARK_V1.md` compares the latest inspected Starlight Constellation preview with this system. Arcanea adopts its proof hierarchy, public-safe dossiers, human authority, and still-first motion fallback while explicitly rejecting a porcelain reskin, generic node metaphor, or cinematic hero without approved evidence.

The existing 130-asset Resonant Kinforms collection is governed separately by `docs/design/ARCANEA_PRIOR_VISUAL_EVIDENCE_BRIDGE_V1.md`. It is a useful historical research corpus, not a sixth Round-01 style, a current campaign result, an identity lock, or a publication entitlement.

The controlled anchors are **Kael Thornfield**, a source-complete human identity with exact age, body, skin, hair, hands, scar, posture, object, and story action, and **Draconis**, a countable non-human morphology proposal with exact topology, six limbs, three Chords, material consequence, and story action. Draconia is intentionally excluded from the style benchmark because her physical identity remains open; using her would confound rendering style with invented casting. Round 01 begins without accepted image-reference hashes, so it may select a rendering hypothesis but cannot claim cross-style likeness continuity or lock either identity.

## The recognition order

An Arcanea visual should be identifiable in this order:

1. story action or choice;
2. posture and relational distance;
3. silhouette and countable anatomy;
4. maintained object, instrument, wardrobe construction, or ecology;
5. material behavior and wear;
6. one localized impossible phenomenon;
7. Gate or product-family color;
8. rendering polish.

If an image needs aura, rainbow light, or a caption to establish identity, the identity contract is not ready.

## Craft constitution

- **70% ecological/material neutral:** plausible skin, hair, cloth, stone, ceramic, metal, atmosphere, and physiology.
- **20% identity family:** garment construction, instrument, habitat, structural material, or domain rhythm.
- **8% active phenomenon:** one iris geometry, seam, ring, pressure shift, crack, refraction, or organ behavior.
- **2% relational accent:** the signal showing consent, bond, threshold, or consequence.
- Every identity remains readable in grayscale and at avatar size.
- Hair is physically plausible. Eyes use one stable base and one exact activation geometry.
- Most images begin Dormant or Resonant. Manifest and Transcendent states require a story reason.
- Generated typography is excluded from hero imagery. The site owns labels, captions, and accessibility text.

### Adaptive experiment policy

Round 01 holds Kael Thornfield and Draconis constant while testing all five hypotheses. After a human accepts a primary and a distinct secondary mode, each later full executable round uses an 80/20 allocation: eight primary-system jobs and two deterministic secondary-mode portability probes. If blockers reduce the executable set, the compiler reserves up to two probes while always preserving at least one primary job.

The probe assignment is content-addressed from the prior accepted reflection. The same evidence therefore yields the same subjects, styles, and packet hash; an operator cannot quietly move the secondary mode onto easier identities. Later probes are longitudinal evidence, not controlled A/B comparisons, because their subjects differ. A mode changes status only after repeated hard-floor evidence, independent review, and a human decision.

## Character identity contract

Every recurring character receives a versioned packet before a hero campaign:

- stable id, source and canon state, age, body, face, skin, hair, anatomy, posture, and movement;
- silhouette, wardrobe baseline, maintained object or instrument, material palette, and wear;
- emotional contradiction and one observable story action;
- dormant and resonant state;
- allowed variation and forbidden drift;
- front, profile, neutral three-quarter, full-body, hands/object, expression, and grayscale references;
- accepted reference hashes and supersession lineage;
- rights, cultural review, identity approval, and release states.

Book-bible facts outrank name-derived imagery. An ensemble is never used as the first identity lock.

### Character readiness portfolio

The generated knowledge base at `docs/design/ARCANEA_CHARACTER_IDENTITY_READINESS_V1.md` separates four states. In v1.10.0 the twenty book-character records comprise five source-complete identities, one source-complete identity with named Venezuelan review, twelve discovery-only identities with explicit open variables, and two blocked identities.

- **Source complete** may produce an internal identity-master candidate, never automatic approval.
- **Source complete, review required** may be explored, but specialist evidence is mandatory before approval.
- **Discovery only** may test provisional choices internally. The release tool denies approval and public staging until a new identity-locked contract supersedes it.
- **Blocked** may not generate. Korvash is blocked by a BIBLE/chapter pronoun conflict plus an explicit disability/neurodivergence review need; An is blocked by unreviewed physical identity and the book’s non-negotiable Vietnamese-reader protocol.

This state model preserves momentum without allowing a provider’s attractive invention to silently become character canon.

## Guardian and Godbeast rules

Locked names, Gates, domains, and bonds come from `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`. Morphology in the creator-supplied Tenfold Constitution remains proposal evidence until reconciled and locked.

- The generated `docs/design/ARCANEA_GUARDIAN_IDENTITY_READINESS_V1.md` atlas records all ten Guardians as discovery-only: behavioral myth is specific, but face, body, apparent age, garments, scale, and cultural embodiment remain open.
- The generated `docs/design/ARCANEA_GODBEAST_IDENTITY_READINESS_V1.md` atlas separates locked relation, local functional phenomena, locally grounded base form, creator-proposed detailed morphology, and open identity. Eight candidates are discovery-only; Sol and Source are blocked.
- The local lore currently grounds a Phoenix-Serpent base form for Veloura and a Worldtree Deer base form for Laeylinn. It explicitly leaves the other eight physical forms canon-pending; detailed forms in the creator packet therefore remain controlled candidates even when visually precise.
- Guardian-only and Guardian–Godbeast jobs may produce internal comparison plates, never identity masters; the release tool denies approval and public staging until a versioned human identity lock supersedes the open record.
- A Guardian and Godbeast are shown as sovereign counterparts, never owner and pet or rider and mount.
- Chord anatomy is exact and countable in silhouette when a proposed morphology is being studied. Holding the proposal steady makes comparison possible; it does not make the proposal canon.
- Godbeasts require a base clade, limb count, face architecture, locomotion, scale evidence, ecology, material system, and one functional phenomenon.
- Source / Ten-Chorded and Sol studies remain blocked where the locked canon and creator proposal conflict.

## Product-agent identity rules

The sixteen named agent identities are existing public product identities, not mythological proof and not autonomous runtime authority. Their current dossiers are public-safe operating proposals. Public surfaces avoid treating “Luminor” as an entity species because locked lore defines it as a rank.

- Each is recognized first by one working instrument, not a generic robot or superhero body.
- Each instrument has a unique silhouette, material system, working light, observable behavior, and spatial rhythm; shared Arcanea craft laws create family resemblance without uniforms.
- Identity differentiation is regression-tested across all 120 agent pairings. Exact instrument, silhouette, material, light, behavior, rhythm, and motif fields must remain unique, and meaningful visual-contract vocabulary must stay below a 0.18 pairwise Jaccard collision ceiling. At this closeout, the nearest pair is Prismatic/Analytica at 0.116. This is a drift alarm, not a substitute for human art direction.
- The four domains use spatial rhythm and working behavior, not uniform color-coded costumes.
- Prismatic fronts the Visual Director; this avoids inventing a seventeenth identity.
- Every public dossier teaches purpose, perspective, inputs, outputs, routes, stop conditions, and human gates.

## World-system rules

The generated `docs/design/ARCANEA_WORLD_IDENTITY_READINESS_V1.md` atlas defines ten bounded environmental plates: five source-complete, one source-complete with specialist review, two discovery-only, and two blocked.

- A source-complete plate is evidence for one action, viewpoint, and causal slice. It does not lock every region, culture, or institution in the world.
- Foreground work or maintained evidence, midground social organization, and distant ecological consequence must remain part of one navigable geography.
- Architecture follows water, energy, climate, food, labor, transport, waste, maintenance, and material supply. It is never a detached façade style.
- Exceptional phenomena have a localized physical behavior and limit. A portal, glow, or skyline cannot stand in for a world.
- Avilara requires named Venezuelan review evidence before approval. Forge Realm and Ultraworld remain internal discovery studies while their system variables are open.
- Van Linh remains blocked behind its two-reader Vietnamese protocol. Aurevalde remains blocked until the creator explicitly chooses whether direct depiction should spend its pure-absence canon resource.

## Gallery system

The first viewport shows a real campaign artifact: total contracted images, rounds, planned jobs, gated jobs, and generated count. Decorative mythology never replaces production truth.

Each identity dossier links to public-safe `AGENT.md`, `SKILL.md`, and `agent-card.json` files. These are explanatory blueprints, not credentials.

The public teaching pack also exposes all 100 exact engine-neutral prompt contracts, a generated structural quality audit, and strict draft-2020-12 JSON Schemas for the prompt contract, narrow human execution grant, and write-once output receipt. ## Provider routing

The dated first-party capability audit and lane rules live in `docs/design/ARCANEA_IMAGE_PROVIDER_ROUTING_V1.md`. The generic round compiler preserves evidence and governance; Provider Prompt Compiler v2.1.3 reads geometry from the machine contract, fails if it disagrees with prose composition, partitions visible instructions from operational constraints, hashes the withheld material, fails on governance leakage, and emits OpenAI, Gemini, FLUX.2, and Codex imagegen packets plus content-addressed immutable execution manifests. The live packets may report changing output status; every binary receipt instead binds the stable manifest and exact prompt hash. OpenAI, Gemini, and Codex receive identical creative semantics; only request controls differ.

One provider, exact model, quality tier, configuration policy, and reference policy is held constant within a ten-image round. Round 01 also holds geometry and intended surface constant for direct style comparison. Later portfolio rounds preserve each job's contracted dossier, workflow, world, or regression geometry rather than distorting unlike surfaces into one ratio. The receipt-bound 80/20 allocation is the only deliberate **render-system** variable after Round 01; subject and contracted surface are observed portfolio conditions, not a direct A/B. FLUX receives positive-language exclusion translation because its official interface has no negative-prompt channel. Exact provider and model selection remains runtime-bound and receipt-backed.

An approved image is still only one bounded view. Identity-master status requires eight distinct approved regression views, a versioned human identity-lock attestation, and a qualified-set receipt. A later job receives only one to five explicitly assigned smallest-sufficient views; the immutable execution manifest binds the exact assignment. Text-only, unverified, or single-view reference work is capped at 4/5 identity continuity. Only the complete qualified-set chain may support 5/5.

## Asset lifecycle

| State           | Binary location                                                  | Versioned evidence                                      | Publicly rendered |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Planned         | none                                                             | prompt contract and packet hash                         | no                |
| Discovery only  | ignored internal store                                           | open variables, receipt, scores, identity-lock blocker  | no                |
| Internal review | ignored `planning-with-files/arcanea-visual-assets/`             | output receipt, provider/preflight evidence, media hash | no                |
| Scored          | same internal store                                              | two scorecards and round reflection                     | no                |
| Approved        | exact-byte copy under `apps/web/public/images/arcanea-campaign/` | approval receipt plus five human gate records           | yes, as approved  |
| Published       | approved public copy plus canonical deployment                   | publication receipt and inspected external evidence     | yes, as published |
| Superseded      | retained internal lineage; replacement has a new revision        | rejection or supersession record                        | replacement only  |

The ignored internal store prevents Git and deployment bloat; it is not a backup. Do not clean a round's internal binaries until they are mirrored to an approved private artifact store or intentionally rejected under the retention policy. Selecting credentials, retention, and a private object store remains a human infrastructure decision.

## Generation and review loop

1. Validate machine, storage, spend, rights, and cultural-review gates.
2. Resolve canon, evidence, identity, rights, release eligibility, and release states independently.
3. Compile an engine-neutral prompt contract with explicit one-asset geometry, intended surface, delivery role, crop safety, and generated-text policy.
4. Discover current provider capabilities and add an execution adapter.
5. Generate one asset per call, fully decode it, copy it outside the web-public tree, fully decode the stored copy, prove exact byte and geometry equality, and only then record its receipt.
6. Stop after ten outputs.
7. Hard-link the ten internal assets behind opaque candidate ids, then give each critic only the style-blind source brief and their own scorecard.
8. Seal both scorecards before unblinding. Score ten dimensions out of five, with hard floors for canon, identity, and anatomy; text-conditioned or unverified-reference identity evidence is capped at 4/5.
9. Reconcile any two-point critic disagreement and record the human round decision. Round 01 must explicitly separate comparable source facts from between-style likeness noise and show that the decision transfers across both anchors.
10. Unlock the next compiler only with a hash-valid, complete reflection receipt; Round 01 style selection requires both controlled anchors to pass.
11. Record approval as an append-only, hash-linked decision tied to the exact output, passing reflection, and explicit canon, identity, rights, brand, and sensitivity gates; only then stage those exact bytes for web release.
12. Record publication only after approval and with canonical external evidence. The receipt records the event; it never performs it.

## Success standard

The system succeeds when a different qualified operator can reproduce a campaign, distinguish every truth state, keep a recurring identity stable across crops and engines, explain why each candidate passed or failed, and replace an image without losing provenance.
