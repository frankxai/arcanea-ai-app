# Arcanea Chosen Responsibility

Status: product and brand doctrine. This document implements the ethical version of the chosen-one fantasy for Arcanea product, community, Academy, AgentHub, and swarm work.

## Core Thesis

Arcanea should make people feel chosen by responsibility, not chosen above others.

The public promise is:

```text
The world is drifting. Arcanea trains creators who remember, build, and protect.
```

The chosen identity is a call to agency:

```text
You are not chosen because you are special.
You are chosen because you choose the work.
```

This keeps the emotional power of myth while avoiding cult mechanics, superiority framing, shame loops, or dehumanized enemies.

## The Common Enemy

The enemy is **The Drift**: the condition where people lose agency, memory, trust, craft, rights, and connection.

The Drift has six public faces:

| Face | What it means | Arcanea response |
|---|---|---|
| Attention collapse | People consume more than they create. | Small trials that produce useful artifacts. |
| Synthetic confusion | AI slop, misinformation, and weak sources blur reality. | Media literacy, source grounding, and critique loops. |
| Creative amnesia | Stateless tools forget context, taste, rights, and canon. | SIS memory, world graphs, provenance, and continuity. |
| Isolation | Creators work alone without meaningful circles. | Guilds, councils, quests, spotlights, and co-creation. |
| Rights fog | Authorship, remix, license, and training permissions are unclear. | Right Use reviews, source records, and lawful remix. |
| Tool dependency | Creators rent power from opaque systems. | BYOK, exportable data, open clients, and local-first patterns. |

Fight The Drift. Never frame people, nations, parties, religions, cultures, or outgroups as the enemy.

## The User Arc

```text
Observer -> Called -> Gifted -> Trained -> Proven -> Steward -> Luminor
```

| Stage | Product meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Observer | User sees the crisis and names what they care about. | Calling selected. |
| Called | User accepts one responsibility-sized mission. | Right Use oath accepted. |
| Gifted | Arcanea issues a named Gift Object. | Gift saved. |
| Trained | User completes small trials through Academy and agents. | Progression events. |
| Proven | User publishes or preserves useful work. | Artifact, source, and proof record. |
| Steward | User protects continuity, rights, and community quality. | Reviews, citations, remixes, governance. |
| Luminor | User teaches others and improves the system. | Endorsements, missions, durable contributions. |

## First Session

The first session should not open with a blank prompt. It should open with **The Call**:

1. Ask what part of The Drift the creator wants to answer.
2. Create one Gift Object: name, power, cost, right use, and first trial.
3. Show the boundary: what this gift must not be used for.
4. Route the trial to the right Luminor or workflow.
5. Save the proof into SIS memory.
6. Turn the proof into a world asset, publishing asset, or mission artifact.

The output should feel personal, but it must remain useful and inspectable.

## Product Mechanics

Retention should mean "return to your mission," not "keep scrolling."

Approved mechanics:

- Daily Trial: one small useful artifact.
- Weekly Campaign: one artifact refined, cited, and published.
- Proof Ledger: progress shown through real work.
- Council Review: critique from design, ethics, source, and product agents.
- Recoverable streaks: momentum without shame.
- Capability unlocks: better tools, deeper memory, more agents, publishing access, or governance.

Rejected mechanics:

- Shame, panic, or fear-based streak loss.
- Fake scarcity or fake urgency.
- Hidden pricing, hidden cancellation, or buried data terms.
- Forced sharing.
- Infinite feeds without a creator task.
- Superiority language that divides insiders from outsiders.
- Claims of crisis without source grounding.

## Copy Rules

Use:

- "chosen by responsibility"
- "answer The Drift"
- "the call"
- "proof-sized trial"
- "right use"
- "stewardship"
- "world memory"
- "lawful remix"
- "creator-owned IP" only when rights are clear

Avoid:

- "brainwashing"
- "addiction"
- "chosen above others"
- "enemies" when referring to people
- "awakening" without a concrete mechanism
- "unlimited power"
- "followers" when "members," "creators," or "stewards" is more honest

## Acceptance Gate

A page, workflow, or campaign using this doctrine passes only when:

- The enemy is The Drift or a concrete system failure, not a human group.
- The user gets agency, not pressure.
- The first session produces a real artifact.
- The artifact has source, rights, continuity, and next-action metadata when relevant.
- The UI makes cancellation, consent, privacy, and pricing clear.
- The design passes `TASTE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and the shared visual QA gates.
