# Arcanea Creator Forge Packages

Status: business and productization brief. This document turns Arcanea worlds, agents, and media workflows into creator-facing offers.

Updated: 2026-06-26

## Forge Thesis

Free users create. Paid creators accelerate. Marketplace creators sell. Partners scale. Proof rails protect.

Creator Forge is the paid depth layer of Arcanea: world assets, agent workflows, media packs, publishing packages, and licensing systems that help a creator turn a world into a business surface.

Emerging creator, author, game maker, or AI creator who needs a coherent world foundation.

```text
Leave with a named world, rules, visual DNA, first character pair, first proof artifact, and ownership map.
```

Test pricing hypothesis:

```text
$197-$497
```

Deliverables:

- Gift Object.
- World Seed.
- Three world laws.
- Two character diamonds.
- One visual direction brief.
- One proof artifact.
- Exportable markdown/JSON bundle.
- Plain-language rights summary.

Exclusions:

- Full manuscript.
- Custom app/game.

Fulfillment:

Self-serve flow first, assisted upsell second.

Assets needed:

Prepare a living world for publication, licensing, translation, or adaptation with canon, metadata, provenance, and rights boundaries.

## Fulfillment Workflow

1. Intake: buyer, world, goals, sources, rights, deadline.
2. Diagnostic: Drift face, mission lane, world maturity, monetization fit.
3. Package selection: First World Kit, Forge Sprint, Publishing Pack, or custom.
4. Production: agents generate drafts within constraints.
5. Review: Canon Director, Right Use Reviewer, QA Critic.
6. Delivery: files, rights sheet, next actions.
7. Follow-up: support path, upgrade, marketplace, or publishing route.

## Required Support Boundaries

- What Arcanea owns.
- What the creator owns.
- What AI contributed.
- What can be sold.
- What can be remixed.
- What cannot be used due to source, culture, trademark, or canon restrictions.
- What support is included.
- What requires a separate sprint.

## Launch Readiness Gaps

| Gap | Why it matters | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing source of truth | Current app/docs pricing can drift. | Create one offer/pricing map before public sales. |
| Checkout path | Simulated or incomplete checkout is not revenue proof. | Choose checkout/consultation path per package. |
| Sample deliverables | Buyers need to inspect the transformation. | Produce one sample First World Kit and one Forge Sprint demo. |
| Legal/support boundary | Rights-aware offers create trust only if boundaries are plain. | Add support articles and rights questionnaire. |
| Lead evidence | Waitlists and interest forms must show real demand. | Track offer clicks, consultations, proof artifacts, and paid conversion. |
| Founder bottleneck | High-touch packages can trap delivery on Frank. | Define agent-assisted fulfillment with human review gates. |

## Most Dangerous Assumptions

| Assumption | Why dangerous | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Creators understand repo ownership. | Many do not want Git complexity. | Managed export UX and user interview. |
| World seeds are enough to pay. | Delight may not convert without utility. | Offer CTA after proof artifact. |
| AI media quality is consistent. | Incoherence kills premium perception. | Visual QA gate and regeneration rules. |
| Rights-aware language creates trust. | It can also create anxiety. | Plain-language ownership screens. |
| Marketplace can wait forever. | Sellers need a path to money. | Start with curated drops and direct packages. |
| First Session proof will automatically create demand. | Delight is not the same as willingness to pay. | Place package CTA after proof and measure intent. |
| Agent workflows can fulfill premium creative packages alone. | Quality, rights, and taste still need review. | Keep Canon, Right Use, and QA gates in every paid package. |
