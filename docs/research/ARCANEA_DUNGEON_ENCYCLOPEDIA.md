## Leveling & Class System (Articulated from Research + Arcanea Canon)

**Core Philosophy**: Levels are not numbers — they are **Resonance Signatures** that change how the creator interacts with the world and other creators. Classes are social roles with visible consequences (titles, gate access, material crafting privileges, story weight).

**Rank Bands** (public): Apprentice → Mage → Master → Archmage → Luminor (hidden Crown layer).

**Progression Mechanics** (inspired by Solo Leveling hidden system + Primal Hunter/Defiance of the Fall + Wandering Inn social identity):

- **XP Sources**: Objective completion (25-120 XP per room based on estimatedMinutes), Boss victory (500+), Material crafting loops, Agentic mission completion, Community resonance (reader/creator engagement metrics).
- **Level Up Triggers**: Gate resonance events, Boss proof artifacts, Material breakthroughs, Title achievements.
- **Class Examples** (Arcanea-native, rights-clean):
  - **Resonance Hunter** (Solo Leveling homage): Specializes in rank mismatch detection, hidden depth scouting. Starts with "False Rank Veil" ability. Community loves the "I knew that D-Rank was wrong" moments.
  - **Luminor Scribe** (Wandering Inn + author toolkit): Turns vault materials into story seeds, prompt packs, book beats. High social status in creator circles.
  - **Vault Cartographer** (Jaquays nonlinear): Masters nonlinear maps, secret routes, ecology mapping. Rewards nonlinear navigation bonuses.
  - **Godbeast Binder** (Delicious ecology + D&D): Forms pacts with godbeast memory fragments. Materials become creature variants or mounts.
  - **Nero Shadow** (hidden progression): For those who embrace collapse risk. High risk, high material yield.
- **Why This Approach is Successful** (from research synthesis):
  - Visible power fantasy + dopamine (levels, titles, gate access).
  - Social identity (classes determine who you party with, what contracts you can take, how other creators perceive you).
  - Long-tail consequence (a level 12 Resonance Hunter has different story weight than a level 3).
  - Community debate fuel ( "Is the rank mismatch too predictable?" "Does the timer make it too stressful or perfect?").
  - Most loved in r/ProgressionFantasy, r/litrpg, Solo Leveling fandom: rank systems that reward pattern recognition and "I saw that coming" moments.

**Community Signals from Research**:
- Solo Leveling: Highest engagement on "double dungeon" / rank reveal episodes. Fans love the shock and the hidden system payoff.
- Dungeon Crawler Carl: Spectacle + achievements + public scoring turns survival into entertainment.
- The Wandering Inn: Classes as identity with real social cost/reward.
- Primal Hunter / Defiance of the Fall: Deep crafting and class synergy loops.
