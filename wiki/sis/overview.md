# Starlight Intelligence Systems (SIS)

Persistent, portable, open memory layer for AI tools. Enterprise AI consulting powered by Frank's Oracle AI CoE experience.

## Dual Identity

### 1. Open Source Product
- **SIS Core Engine** — v5.0.0 on npm (@frankx/starlight-intelligence-system)
- 5-layer cognitive architecture, 7 specialist agents, 6 memory vaults
- Platform adapters: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode
- MIT licensed, file-first, database-optional, cloud-never-required
- **Starlight Horizon Dataset** — CC-BY-SA 4.0, public AI alignment data (11 entries)

### 2. Consulting Practice
- Enterprise AI strategy & roadmap
- AI Center of Excellence (CoE) setup
- ACOS assessments
- Custom agent development
- Training & enablement
