# 09-Archive/

The graveyard. Searchable, not navigable. No MOCs point here.

## What goes here

- Completed projects (entire `06-Projects/{name}/` moved)
- Dead sparks (`04-Sparks/` items unclaimed after 7 days → `09-Archive/sparks/{year}/`)
- Superseded decisions
- Old Captured Prompts after Notion is the authoritative copy

## What doesn't

- Atoms — atoms never archive; they get `status: superseded` in frontmatter and link to the replacement
- MOCs — dead MOCs are deleted, not archived
- Briefs — they're historical records, stay in `02-Briefs/`

## Rule

If you're looking for something in 09-Archive/, use search. Don't browse. The folder exists so the active vault stays lean — not so you can nostalgia-scroll.
