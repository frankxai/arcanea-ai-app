# Ten-People Axis

You compound toward the median of the ten people you spend the most synchronous time with.

Not the ten smartest people you know. Not the ten richest. Not the ten most impressive. The ten people you *actually spend the most time with*, where time is synchronous (Zoom calls, dinners, working side-by-side), not async (following on Twitter, reading their posts).

The mechanism: behavior, taste, and risk tolerance are contagious. If your ten-people axis is risk-averse, you'll become risk-averse even if you've intellectually decided otherwise. If it's ambitious, you'll take bigger bets without noticing. If it's sloppy, you'll lower your quality bar. The compounding is pre-conscious; you don't choose it.

Frank's ten-people axis (as of Apr 21, 2026): co-founders (none yet, building Arcanea solo), strategic advisors (Ahmad Hashem + 2-3 others in Trinity discussions), immediate team (none yet; Arcanea is solo), family (brother, close friends), creators in his network (music producers, designers, AI researchers). The median: ambitious, technically rigorous, creative-first, financially sophisticated. Frank compounds toward that.

The lever: if you want to change your compounding trajectory, change your ten-people axis before you try to change your own behavior. Find ten people 2-3 levels above you on whatever axis matters to you, and spend synchronous time with them. Your behavior will follow.

The constraint: your ten-people axis is limited by geography, opportunity, and alignment. You can't force it. But you can optimize for *meeting* people with the right median and then *investing* in those relationships with presence (not just transactions).
