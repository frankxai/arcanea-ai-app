# Biotech Superintelligence Horizon

Biotech × superintelligence is the next 20-year compounding surface. Position now, cash out in 2040s.

Every 20-year wave has a flagship convergence. The 1990s were software × networking (web). The 2010s were AI × scale (deep learning). The 2020s-2040s will be AI × biology (synthetic life, longevity, pandemic prevention).

The thesis:
1. **The problem is data-bound.** Biology has 3.8B years of optimization (evolution) encoded in DNA. ML can decode it faster than labs can run experiments.
2. **The tooling is maturing now (2026).** AlphaFold, Copilot for biology, diffusion models for protein design. The API surface is becoming usable.
3. **The capital is flowing in.** $50B+ VC + $100B+ pharma + $200B+ defense budgets are all pointing at biotech-AI. The wave is starting.
4. **The timeline is long.** A biotech breakthrough takes 10-15 years to FDA, then 5-10 more to revenue. If you're thinking 2040 exit, you need to position by 2025-2026.

Frank's current positioning (Apr 2026):
- **Pile 3 (concentrated bets):** 1-2 early biotech companies with AI-first models (deferred to post-BV formation)
- **Knowledge:** Second Brain has emerging biotech atoms; will grow to 30+ atoms by EOY
- **Network:** Tracking biotech-AI founder communities (deferred)
- **Personal:** No lab work, but consulting optionality (e.g., Trinity might expand into biotech governance)

The exit scenario: if any of the 2-3 bets hit, the exit is 2038-2045. 15-year hold. IRR target 30-50% annually (extremely high, but biotech variance is extreme).

The insurance: most of these bets will fail. That's expected. You need only one to 100x to justify the portfolio.
