# Wealth Velocity

Wealth velocity is the product of three forces: time-to-liquidity × compounding rate × optionality. Maximize all three.

**Time-to-liquidity** is how fast capital can move from one bet to the next. A stock trade is 2 days (actually T+1). Real estate is 3-6 months. Crypto is 10 minutes. Everything else is somewhere in between. The faster you can unlock capital, the faster you can re-deploy it.

**Compounding rate** is the return per cycle. Index funds are ~10% per year (that's your floor). Concentrated bets in emerging tech or biotech can be 50-300% or 0% (that's the variance you pay for higher upside). Picking the compounding rate is picking your risk.

**Optionality** is the ability to say "no" to bad moves. With high optionality, you wait for the best move. With low optionality, you take whatever's available. Runway (your pile 1) is optionality. Diversification (your pile 2) is optionality. A network of other founders who might offer you a deal (your pile 3) is optionality.

The formula: Wealth velocity = (1 / time-to-liquidity) × compounding-rate × optionality-score

Arcanea's target: 3-month cycles (90 days from capital unlock to re-deploy), aiming for 30-60% annual on deployed capital, with optionality buffer of 18-24 months runway. That's faster velocity than real estate, slower than crypto, higher optionality than most tech founders.

The leverage: most people obsess over compounding-rate alone (get rich quick). Smart wealth-builders obsess over optionality first (don't blow up), then time-to-liquidity (stay nimble), then rate (compound what's left).
