# Taste as Moat

Taste — the ability to recognize what's good before the market prices it — is the only creator moat that compounds across mediums and decades.

Every other advantage decays. Technical skill gets commoditized. Followers leak. Access windows close. But taste is a pattern-recognition engine that strengthens with exposure and compounds across domains. A creator with taste in music will have taste in design. A designer with taste in typography will have taste in interaction. Taste + execution beats raw output on any timeline longer than 18 months.

The moat works because:

1. **Taste filters signal from noise.** You ship fewer pieces, each better. The market sees coherence, not prolific chaos. Coherence compounds trust faster than volume.

2. **Taste is repeatable across scales.** Once taste is proven at small scale, the same taste shipped at large scale doesn't dilute — it amplifies. A tasteful tweet becomes a tasteful tweet thread becomes a tasteful article becomes a tasteful course.

3. **Taste attracts taste.** Creators with taste seek collaborators with taste. Collaborators with taste find you because you've written the score they want to play in. Non-taste-havers flee or stay silent.

4. **Taste ages well.** Trends decay in 24 months. Taste ages into classics. Your 2016 designs are mocked if they're trend-dependent; they're referenced if they're taste-coherent.

The leverage: taste doesn't cost more to scale than non-taste. A song with taste costs the same to distribute as a song without taste. Taste is pure arbitrage — same input, different signal. That's why it's the moat.
