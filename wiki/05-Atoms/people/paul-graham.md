# Paul Graham

**Y Combinator co-founder. Essayist. Write-to-think proponent.**

## The idea I steal

**Writing is thinking.** *"The strongest proof that you know something is to be able to explain it to someone else."* Weekly Brief exists because of this — forced weekly public writing is forced weekly thinking.

## Where it shows up in the OS

- [[../frameworks/write-to-think]] — atom (to seed)
- `/weekly-brief` skill — Sunday long-form digest → OSS
- [[../../00-MOCs/MOC-Shipped]] — writing as compounding public evidence

## Canonical source

His essays at paulgraham.com. Start with "How to Think for Yourself" and "The Age of the Essay".

## Counter-read

Anne Lamott — *Bird by Bird* — argues for shitty first drafts + private practice before public writing. Tension: Graham publishes more freely; Lamott protects craft more. Frank's Weekly Brief sits between (publishes weekly, uses skinny-beats-skipped fallback for quality floors).
