# Naval Ravikant

**AngelList co-founder. Aphorist. Compounding evangelist.**

## The idea I steal

**Loyal opposition.** *"The best way to learn is to have a smart, loyal opposition."* Counter-Coach is this rule operationalized at prompt-OS layer.

Secondary: compounding. "All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest."

## Where it shows up in the OS

- [[../concepts/counter-coach-rule]] — direct operationalization
- [[../frameworks/compound-interest-of-thinking]] — atom in-flight
- Weekly Brief discipline (shipping as compounding)
- DAG provenance (replay = compound)

## Canonical source

*The Almanack of Naval Ravikant* (Eric Jorgenson, 2020).

## Counter-read

Ray Dalio — *Principles* — argues for full radical transparency from all directions, not just a loyal opposition. Could produce more signal but also more noise; Counter-Coach is calibrated closer to Naval's model.
