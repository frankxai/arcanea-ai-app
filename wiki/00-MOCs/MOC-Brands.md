## Cross-brand flows

- Prompts captured in any brand → [[MOC-Prompt-OS]] → potentially replayed across brands (Counter-Coach encourages)
- Atoms produced from Council promotion live in `05-Atoms/` — **brand-agnostic**
- Briefs reference multiple brands; Council's Connector role explicitly seeks cross-brand links

## Brand invariants

- FrankX voice: results-first, humble, excellence
- Arcanea voice: mythic-pragmatic (12 Chosen, Lumina Queen, but shipping)
- SIS voice: CTO-to-CTO, architectural, audit-grade
- GenCreator voice: creator-intimate, studio-tier
- Vibeclubs voice: format-not-platform, community-OSS

Anti-pattern: blending voices. Each brand owns its register.
