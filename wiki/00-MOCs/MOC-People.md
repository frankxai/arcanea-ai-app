## How atoms link people

Every person-atom follows:
```
---
title: {Name}
aliases: [...]
tags: [people, domain-tag]
---

# {Name}

**One-sentence who.**

## The idea I steal

Single paragraph on the load-bearing idea this person contributed to Frank's stack.

## Where it shows up in the OS

Links to 2–4 atoms/decisions/projects where this idea is active.

## Canonical source

One link — book, essay, talk. Not "a collection of their work".
```

Principle: **steal one thing per person.** Don't maintain biographies — maintain operational influence.
