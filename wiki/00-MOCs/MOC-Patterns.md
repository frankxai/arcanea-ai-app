# Pattern Clusters — Map of Content

Emerging themes the Observer role writes into `Pattern Cluster` on Captured Prompts. These are **not categories** — they're the shape of Frank's thinking over time.

Populated by Observer (daily 05:00) + surfaced weekly by Brief Composer.

## Currently tracked clusters (seed set — will evolve)

- `architecture` — system + OS design decisions
- `pricing-strategy` — monetization questions
- `creative-spark` — raw idea bursts, often music/visual
- `counter-move` — adversarial reframes (hunting for Counter-Coach material)
- `brand-voice` — register tuning across 5 brands
- `operator-instinct` — gut calls on product + people
- `compound-bet` — decisions with 90-day+ payoff
- `veto` — things Frank refuses to do (load-bearing; preserves integrity)
- `ritual` — daily/weekly practices being encoded as skills
- `leverage-arbitrage` — where low effort produces disproportionate output

## How clusters work

1. Observer writes one cluster tag per captured prompt.
2. Clusters with <3 entries/week get deprecated — not enough signal.
3. Clusters with >20 entries/week get subdivided — too coarse.
4. Frank reviews cluster taxonomy monthly — adds/removes based on usage.

Anti-pattern: using clusters as categories ("strategy", "tech", "health"). That's PARA territory (Areas). Clusters are **thematic**, not domain-based.

## High-energy × high-drift clusters = pivot signals

Per v1.4.0 architecture: when a cluster shows high `Energy Score` AND high `Drift Flag` for 3 days running, it's a pivot signal. The Strategist writes the drift alert; Frank decides whether to follow.
