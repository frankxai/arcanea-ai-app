Format: L99 universal prompt block. Global avoid block applies to every brief:
generic fantasy splash art, AI-maximalist ornament, random filigree, tangled glow trails,
fake tiny text, unreadable glyph noise, malformed hands, waxy skin, extra jewelry,
arbitrary straps, overexposed bloom, cyan-magenta neon overload, generic Atlantis,

one-hue teal/gold wallpaper, watermark.

Canon anchors for this wave: Seven Academy Houses are institutional, not geographic
(`CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 6 — Lumina, Nero, Pyros, Aqualis, Terra, Ventus, Synthesis); the
Academy sits inside the Kingdom of Light's Realm framework (`CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 5);
dragon-bond culture per `CHARACTER_CORE.md` Akamoto and `FACTION_COMPACT.md`'s Akamoto
Roost / Crew Akamoto entry. World-tower / kilometer-scale creature framing is new visual
staging for this campaign, not existing lore text — treated here as architecture and
atmosphere, not new named entities, per the brief.

## W2-01 Starlight Academy — full campus vista at dawn

Use case: environment establishing shot
Surface/aspect: 21:9 cinematic wide
Audience: readers and creators discovering the Academy's scale for the first time
First read: a civilization roughly a century more advanced than ours, expressed as
architecture that grew with its landscape instead of replacing it
One visual idea: seven Houses read as seven distinct silhouettes sharing one skyline
Shot: wide establishing plate, 24mm feel, elevated three-quarter view from a neighboring
ridge, soft dawn haze separating three depth planes, 15 percent negative space at the
horizon for sky
Subject: the Starlight Academy campus — seven House spires (Lumina, Nero, Pyros, Aqualis,
Terra, Ventus, Synthesis) arranged around a central Athenaeum dome, connected by raised
walkways and terraced training grounds, one visible dragon in flight near a roost tower
for scale
World/canon rules: `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 6 (Houses institutional not geographic — here
shown as one shared campus, not seven separate nations); no elemental-nation flags or
tribal costuming, this is one integrated institution
Material rules: pale quarried stone, aged Luminor-metal roof seams, living-crystal accent
lighting at entrances only, weathered wood walkways, no glass-tower sci-fi cliche
Lighting: low warm dawn key from the east, cool blue pre-dawn shadow still pooled in the
valleys, single practical glow from early-riser windows
Composition: three readable shapes max — House spire cluster, central Athenaeum dome,
ridge foreground silhouette; dragon is a small accent, not a fourth competing focal shape
Magic/bending mechanics: none active in-frame; this is architecture at rest
Palette: dawn gold and rose over Cosmic Blue shadow, small teal accent lighting at
building thresholds only
Must keep: sense of a lived, working institution — laundry lines, garden terraces, a
transport barge on a canal — not a museum or monument
Must avoid: identical-twin spires (each House must read distinctly), one-hue wallpaper
sky, generic "magic school" trope silhouettes, floating rocks with no structural logic
Inspection gate: each of the seven House shapes distinguishable at thumbnail, horizon
line clean, no more than one dragon accent, crop survives a 21:9 hero and a 16:9 crop
