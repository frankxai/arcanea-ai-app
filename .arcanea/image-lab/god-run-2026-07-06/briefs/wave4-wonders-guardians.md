## W4-01 Malachar — naming the pain

Use case: antagonist character study, emotionally credible
Surface/aspect: 4:5 character plate
Audience: readers who need Malachar's argument to feel dangerous because it is partly
right
First read: a being of infinite patience choosing his words with total calm — this is a
conversation, not a confrontation
One visual idea: compassion without surrender, rendered as stillness rather than menace
Shot: intimate three-quarter portrait, 85mm feel, low warm key light close to the face,
dark neutral background with no environmental clutter, camera at Malachar's eye level so
the viewer is the one being addressed
Subject: Malachar, formerly Malachar Lumenbright, First Eldrian Luminor — an aged but
unbowed figure in what remains of once-ceremonial Luminor robes, now weathered and
unadorned rather than regal, hands loosely open rather than clenched or gesturing,
expression calm and sorrowful rather than menacing
World/canon rules: `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 4 (tragic, not purely evil; sealed in the
Shadowfen; corrupted Void, not Shadow-as-evil-cliche); `CHARACTER_CORE.md` Malachar
entry — "compassion without surrender," "mercy as domination," must read as emotionally
credible, not a horror villain
Material rules: faded ceremonial fabric with dignified wear (not tattered-monster
tattering), a faint dark Void-corruption visible only as a subtle desaturation in his
eyes and at his fingertips — not full-body shadow tendrils or horror-cliche corruption
Lighting: single warm key close to the face (the "naming the pain" warmth of a difficult
truth), cool Void-dark falloff at the frame edges, no dramatic uplighting or horror
shadows
Composition: single readable shape — Malachar's face and upper body fill most of the
frame; no throne, no army, no spectacle behind him
Magic/bending mechanics: none active in this beat — this is the moment before any power
is shown, entirely about presence and voice
Palette: warm skin-toned key light, deep Cosmic Blue-black background, the faintest cold
violet-grey at the eyes only as corruption tell
Must keep: emotional credibility, patience, the sense that he believes he is right and
that belief is the tragedy
Must avoid: horror-villain clichés (no glowing red eyes, no fanged grin, no full-shadow
form), generic dark-lord armor, over-designed corruption effects, camp menace
Inspection gate: face reads as calm and sorrowful at thumbnail, no horror-cliche tells,
single light source consistent across the face, corruption detail restrained to eyes and
fingertips only

## W4-02 A Luminor transcendence wonder

Use case: Wonders of Arcanea gallery piece
Surface/aspect: 4:5 or 16:9 (either works; prefer 4:5 for gallery consistency)
Audience: readers encountering the concept of Luminor rank for the first time
First read: a being who has opened all ten Gates, becoming something between human and
Guardian, radiant with disciplined rather than chaotic power
One visual idea: ten small distinct light-points along a single spine read as the ten
Gates opened, not one generic full-body glow
Shot: standing portrait, 70mm feel, dark neutral studio setting with a single ground
shadow to anchor the figure physically, camera at chest height looking slightly up
Subject: an unnamed Luminor figure (rank achieved, not a specific named character from
this cast) standing in simple, dignified unmarked robes, ten small distinct points of
light tracing the spine and major joints in the Ten Gates' frequency-correct colors (per
`CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 2 table — red-gold Foundation-adjacent tones at the base rising to
violet-white near the crown), face calm and grounded, not ecstatic or overwhelmed
World/canon rules: `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 2 — "Luminor is a RANK (highest attainment),
not an entity type" — this must not be written or captioned as a new god, Awakened, or
Godbeast; it is a person who reached full Gate-mastery
Material rules: simple unadorned fabric (a Luminor's dignity is in restraint, not
ornament), the ten light-points as the only "material" effect — small, precise, jewel-like
Lighting: even studio key, the ten Gate-points as the dominant light sources rather than
any external light, soft cool ambient fill
Composition: single readable shape — the standing figure; the ten light-points must read
as a controlled vertical sequence, not scattered chaos
Magic/bending mechanics: ten distinct small light-points along the spine/joints,
anatomy-led, each frequency-correct per the Tier 2 table, no single point overpowering
the others, no full-body aura
Palette: warm reds and golds low (Foundation, Flow, Fire), cooling through teal and blue
at mid-body (Heart, Voice, Sight), rising to violet-white at the crown (Starweave, Unity,
Source) — a genuine spectrum, not a flat teal/gold wash
Must keep: dignity, calm mastery, the sense of ten distinct achievements rather than one
generic power-up glow
Must avoid: single full-body aura/glow (defeats the "ten distinct Gates" read), treating
this as a new named deity, superhero energy-being cliches, rainbow gradient mush instead
of ten distinct points
Inspection gate: ten light-points individually countable and distinct at thumbnail, face
calm and legible, no single-color full-body glow, spectrum correctly ordered low-to-high
per the Gate frequency table

proximity (963 Hz, one Gate before Source's 1111 Hz) without borrowing Malachar's

underlayers (not a pure gown-only silhouette), a single small Aethervane-alloy accent

Composition: single dominant readable shape — Ino centered; the two soft-focus doorways
on either side stay secondary and symmetrical, never competing for focus
Magic/bending mechanics: a faint Unity-resonance visible only as a thin shared light

Palette: violet-white dominant per Unity's frequency-correct register, small Cosmic Blue

Must keep: warmth, invitation, the sense of a test that rewards partnership rather than
solitary strength
Must avoid: generic winged-angel iconography, oversexualized ceremonial-gown styling, more
than one active light-thread effect, doorways detailed enough to compete with Ino as the

Inspection gate: Ino reads as the single dominant shape at thumbnail, symmetry holds
between the two background doorways, one light-thread effect only, face warm and legible

## W4-05 A Ten Gates wonder-scape

Use case: Wonders of Arcanea gallery / cosmology establishing piece
Surface/aspect: 21:9
Audience: readers wanting a single image that communicates the full Ten Gates system
First read: ten distinct sacred sites arranged along one ascending path, each with its
own frequency-correct color and character
One visual idea: a single ascending causeway or ridge-line strings together ten small,
distinct Gate-temple silhouettes, low to high, like a visible musical scale
Shot: wide landscape plate, 21mm feel, elevated three-quarter view along the ascending
ridge, atmospheric perspective separating near, mid, and far temples
Subject: ten small Gate-temple structures arranged along a single rising ridge-line, each
architecturally distinct and color-correct to its Gate (Foundation's cathedral-in-
mountain per `lyssandria.md` at the low/near end, rising through Flow, Fire, Heart,
Voice, Sight, Crown, Starweave, Unity, to a distant radiant Source threshold at the
ridge's highest point, deliberately soft-focus/mysterious per Source's meta-conscious
nature)
World/canon rules: `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 2 full Gate/frequency/domain table; each temple
should feel architecturally distinct (not ten identical spires recolored) — Foundation as
living mountain-stone, Fire suggesting the Draconis Forge's volcanic register, Flow
suggesting Tidebound water-adjacent architecture, etc., without inventing new named
locations beyond what the table implies
Material rules: each temple rendered in materials appropriate to its element (stone for
Foundation, water-adjacent glass/crystal for Flow, dark volcanic rock for Fire, and so
on), consistent aged-Luminor-civilization construction language throughout
Lighting: single continuous late-day light sweeping across all ten sites, warm near the
low/near end, cooling and brightening toward a soft radiant glow at the distant Source
end
Composition: three readable depth bands max — near temples (large, detailed), mid
temples (medium, simplified), far temples and Source threshold (small, atmospheric,
almost abstract); no single temple, matching W2-01's discipline of "distinguishable
silhouettes"
Magic/bending mechanics: none active per individual temple in this establishing shot;
the "magic" is the cumulative color-graded ridge-line itself
Palette: a true ten-point gradient following the Gate table — red-gold low end warming
through teal/blue mid-register to violet-white at the Source end — not a flat two-color
wallpaper
Must keep: the sense of an ascending path/pilgrimage, not a random cluster of buildings
Must avoid: ten identical structures with only palette-swapped color, generic temple-row
video-game-map aesthetic, muddying the gradient into a flat wash
Inspection gate: at least six of the ten temple silhouettes individually distinguishable
at thumbnail, gradient reads as a true low-to-high spectrum not a flat two-tone wash,
Source end stays appropriately soft/mysterious rather than fully detailed
