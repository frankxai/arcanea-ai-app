## Prompt Learnings

- "No text" is not enough. Use "no letters, no fake writing, no inscriptions, no symbols that resemble writing."
- For product objects, ask for "blank metalwork" and "smooth surfaces" to avoid decorative runes.
- For Arcanea characters, say "AI-lab myth-tech, sacred engineering, no fantasy gown, no medieval armor cosplay."
- For social hooks, ask for clean caption-safe zones and one first-read object.
