# Arcanea L99 Prompt Doctrine

Date: 2026-06-26

## Rule

Do not ask for "more detail." Ask for better decisions.

Every L99 Arcanea prompt must reduce ambiguity for the renderer: fewer motifs, clearer material rules, harder silhouette control, cleaner lighting, and stricter artifact rejection.

## Thal'Maris V2 Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Surface/aspect: 16:9 cinematic keyframe for Arcanea lore and site hero crop
Audience: readers and creators discovering Thal'Maris for the first time
First read: one underwater archive chamber where memory-water is being restored
One visual idea: truth held in water becomes architecture
Shot: wide but inspectable underwater film still, 40mm anamorphic feeling, f/5.6, clean foreground/midground/background separation, 20 percent negative space in the upper left, no busy skyline wallpaper
Subject: Mera in practical oceanic field gear stands on a bronze-coral archive bridge, one hand shaping a single liquid-glass memory ribbon into an open archive sphere
World/canon rules: Thal'Maris is Atlantis as a consent-aware memory-water civilization, not a mermaid kingdom; water remembers only what has been willingly offered
Material rules: aged Luminor bronze, translucent coral-glass, pearl stone, wet fabric, suspended archive lenses, physically plausible caustics and particulate depth
Lighting: controlled blue-green underwater caustics, one warm archive glow, volumetric falloff, no edge glow on every object
Composition: three large readable shapes max: Mera, archive sphere, chamber arch; distant city only as soft silhouette
Magic/bending mechanics: Mera's stance and wrist tension cause the ribbon; the water effect follows anatomy and pressure, not random decorative trails
Palette: deep void blue, star-teal, silver water, muted Arcane gold, small prism highlight only inside the archive sphere
Must keep: emotional wonder, grief, truth, restoration, body-led bending, crisp hands and face
Must avoid: generic Atlantis skyline, cartoon fish, mermaid aesthetics, glow spaghetti, random towers, filigree overload, fake text, unreadable micro-scenes
Inspection gate: subject reads at thumbnail, hands clean, one magic effect only, material surfaces are modelable, crop works for desktop hero and social 16:9
```
