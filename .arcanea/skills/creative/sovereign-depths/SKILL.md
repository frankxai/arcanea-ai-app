# Sovereign Depths

Create impossible life and ancient power that feel physically credible, culturally specific and consequential. Use this workflow for Arcanea boss, creature and dungeon collections; it does not prescribe the style of unrelated companions, product UI or other creators' worlds.

## Begin from the actual world

Read the current repository AGENTS.md, `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`, and the existing collection records before designing. Inspect relevant PRs and distinguish merged code, staged lore, locked assertions and artist proposals. Preserve known identities. Changes to foundational rules remain explicit proposals until ratified.

The Ten Gates are available context, not a requirement to allocate ten equal batches. Habitat, origin, disposition, threat, mythology, encounter function and story role are independent axes. A predator may simply hunt; a rival may be justified; a human antagonist may choose cruelty. Flag genuine conflicts with locked cosmology without erasing independent character motives. Nero is not a synonym for evil.

## Design from body, place and action

Let anatomy, a spatial discovery or a dramatic motive initiate different designs. Each boss needs a primary silhouette invariant, convincing load paths and material responses, characteristic movement, an entrance, and an encounter rule that can be seen in its body or equipment. Make the structure that causes an attack visible. Added horns, crowns, armour and filigree cannot repair an indistinct identity.

Every dungeon needs traversable connections, inhabitants with reasons to remain, evidence of at least two historical states, and discoveries that alter interpretation or action. Keep magnificent landmarks consistent with ordinary stairs, service routes, tools and building methods. A cinematic vista does not prove a playable level.

Use regional palettes, climates and fabrication traditions. Do not inject teal/gold, ceramic/metal, glowing runes or cosmic darkness into every image. Luminous wonder requires the same discipline as terror. Adult treatment means agency, restraint and consequence; it does not require universal grimness or gore.

Reference exceptional studios through specific craft principles. Keep original characters, silhouettes, symbols, histories and spatial arrangements. A franchise name alone is not an art direction. Store reference analysis separately from Arcanea lore.

## Prove the direction before the batch

For a new visual direction, generate three individual benchmark images: a physically dangerous creature, an adult humanoid or uncanny antagonist, and a luminous dungeon. Inspect the actual outputs. Two independent reviewers should assess identity, story, composition, anatomy/materials, lighting/finish and provenance. Reviewers receive the prompt, actual image and intended identity, but not the producer's preferred verdict.

Reject mascot proportions, toy surfaces, generic silhouettes, broken physical contact, implausible scale, lost identity features and illegible principal action. Record features as visible, occluded or absent. Never score a feature because it exists in the prompt. A polished render can still fail identity.

Repair specific faults, then re-review. Numeric scale requires shared-plane figures or credible architectural units. An imminent attack needs a physical tell, not only a dramatic pose. Preserve successful composition during local repairs. Contact sheets support internal comparison; final works remain individual images.

## Connect and publish

Use stable IDs, reciprocal boss/dungeon relationships, story-series boundaries, image dimensions, alt text, source hashes, prompts and review evidence. Keep proposed books and authored encounter designs distinct from published books and implemented games.

Reuse the production collection at `apps/web/lib/visual-encyclopedia/sovereign-depths/`, its Zod schema and `/gallery/sovereign-depths`. The read-only API is `/api/lore/sovereign-depths`; the existing MCP tool is `search_sovereign_depths`. Agent retrieval excludes proposals by default and requires `includeProposals=true` explicitly. Do not weaken the authenticated creator-world gateway.

Before publication verify every actual asset, link and relationship, public delivery, responsive presentation and repository build/typecheck/lint gates. Retain the collection STAGING status and each record’s STAGING or EXPERIMENTAL status in public data. Do not manufacture media-registry receipt IDs or claim production deployment from a local file or successful generation call. Report exact live deployment and commit evidence; preserve unfinished issues plainly.

Experimental records and stories require a second explicit `includeExperimental=true` opt-in. `includeProposals=true` alone returns only STAGING records whose nested story is also STAGING. Relationship links never promote the status of their targets.
