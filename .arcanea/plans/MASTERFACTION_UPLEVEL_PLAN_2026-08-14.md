# MasterFaction Up-Level Plan — 2026-08-14

> **Mission:** Raise Arcanea lore, story, and faction experience to the bar of elite human teams — Sanderson-grade systems married to Schwab/Le Guin human texture — without inventing a new cosmology and without merging unreviewed STAGING into LOCKED.

## 0. Naming and Scope

**MasterFaction** is not a single in-world cult or house. It is the **operating system for how Arcanea factions, orders, crews, origins, and conflicts generate human stories** that audiences feel.

## 1. Diagnosis (evidence-backed)

### What we already have (high ceiling)

- Locked cosmology with emotional spine: Lumina / Nero (Nero not evil), Malachar as tragic fall, Ten Gates + Godbeasts, Seven Wisdoms, Academy Houses.
- Faction architecture with **identity / visual / power / mission / internal tension** (FACTIONS design philosophy).
- Multi-arc story engine with moral questions, not only plot beats.
- Continuity audit trail and naming ledgers.
- Product spine: BYOK creative intelligence workspace — lore must serve both myth and product experience.

### Gaps vs highest human bar

| Gap | Symptom | Human-team standard |
|-----|---------|---------------------|
| **Dual cast drift** | Field Flagship (Kaelindra/Ryn…) vs Confluence core (Arion/Mera/Emilia/Akamoto) not reconciled on main | One public entry cast + clear migration ledger |
| **Volume without intimacy** | 10k–19k word faction sheets; thin sensory/body stakes | Iceberg world: 10% on page, 90% felt through character choice |
| **Institution over person** | Orgs speak as thesis statements | Every faction speaks as a person with a job, fear, and price |
| **Story sales language** | Meta OS voice (“universe operating system”) leaks into fiction surfaces | Franchise promise language is human, sensory, invitation-led |
| **Proof scarcity** | Few short scenes that would survive a New York / Tor first-reader | One scene can prove the world better than fifty tables |
| **Promotion discipline** | STAGING labeled but density tempts “treat as locked” | Explicit STAGING → Frank lock → LOCKED only |
| **Main incomplete** | Compact + Character Core missing from origin/main | Port as STAGING with bridge notes, not silent rewrite |

### The AI failure mode we are killing

AI often produces **confident encyclopedia** that *looks* complete and *feels* empty. Human elite teams produce **incomplete icebergs** that feel inevitable: limitation, cost, contradiction, food, shame, gossip, unfinished buildings, jokes that hurt.

MasterFaction’s job is to force **felt incompleteness with structural honesty**.

## 2. Competitor / best-world articulation (patterns only — no IP copy)

Abstracted from elite franchise practice (Cosmere / Mistborn–Stormlight craft laws, ASOIAF political pressure, Avatar elemental cultures, LotR linguistic depth-as-hint, Schwab “reveal on need,” HP brand invitation language, One Piece crew-as-family promise):

### How the best worlds *sell* the story

1. **Promise before encyclopedia** — One sentence a stranger would repeat after hearing it once.
2. **Character as lens** — World details arrive only when a person needs them (SFWA: story-as-worldbuilding).
3. **Limitation as drama** — Sanderson’s laws in spirit: reader understanding of magic correlates with problem-solving satisfaction; limitations > powers; deepen before expand.
4. **Faction = conflicting good** — Not evil vs good only; institutions that are right for the wrong reasons.
5. **Language of invitation** — Public surfaces use second-person wonder and first-person stakes (“You arrive…”, “She will not open the vial…”) rather than wiki tone.
6. **Sensory contracts** — Metal taste of burned power, cloth weight, weather of emotion, silence after a name.
7. **Moral residue** — After a chapter, the reader carries a question they cannot answer cleanly.

### How Arcanea should articulate (brand + story)

| Surface | Voice | Banned |
|---------|-------|--------|
| LOCKED mythos | Precise, sacred, short | Marketing superlatives |
| STAGING lore OS | Operator clarity + craft gates | Fiction first-person |
| Book / scene | Character POV, concrete sensory | “Universe OS”, CoE, swarm meta |
| Product / site hero | Human invitation + one mythic image | Feature laundry lists |
| Social / trailer | Single emotion + single conflict | Lore dump captions |

**Arcanea one-line franchise promises (candidates — STAGING)**

1. *A world where power opens without consent — and love is the only honest form of control.*
2. *Magic is frequency. Institutions are how people try not to drown in it.*
3. *The villain was the best of them — until he refused to be refused.*

## 3. North-star quality bar (human teams)

Work is not “done” when it is long. Work is done when it passes:

### MasterFaction Human Bar (ship gate)

1. **Emotional truth** — At least one relationship would still matter if all magic were deleted.
2. **Cost** — Power creates a concrete loss within the scene or sheet (body, bond, reputation, memory, time).
3. **Contradiction** — Institution or hero holds two incompatible goods without a neat speech resolving them.
4. **Sensory specificity** — Three non-visual senses or material details that are local, not generic cosmic.
5. **Dialogue pressure** — Someone wants something from someone else; silence or refusal is allowed.
6. **No AI solemnity spam** — Cut hollow words: *resonance* (unless frequency plot), *tapestry*, *delve*, *embark*, *journey of*, *in a world where*.
7. **Canon anchors** — ≥3 explicit ties to LOCKED tiers or approved STAGING names.
8. **Inversion** — At least one structural move that is *not* the default Western fantasy beat.
9. **Reader residue** — Ends on a question or unfinished action, not a thesis.
10. **Distinctive Bundle check** — No protected franchise names, maps, or mechanic bundles as Arcanean labels.

Score: **ship 9–10**, revise 7–8, restart ≤6. Independent verifier required before PR ready.
