Before I ask anything — a frame correction, because the question you asked has a trap inside it.

**"All I could and should aim for"** is the wrong optimization. The man who wants everything gets nothing clearly. The man who wants *specifically and enormously* gets it *inevitably*. ## PASS 1 — THE DEATHBED EDIT (collapses everything)

You are 94. You are dying in a specific bed, in a specific room, in a specific place. Before I ask anything about goals, answer these:

**Q1.** *Where is the bed?* Not generic "by the ocean" — which house, which view, which country, which room. One sentence. No list.

**Q2.** *Who is in the room with you at the end?* Name names. Including the children question — are there Riemer children who outlived you? How many? This one answer reshapes half your life architecture.

**Q3.** *What is the single sentence someone says at your funeral that, if not said, means the life didn't count?* Not three sentences. One.

**Q4.** *What did you build that still runs without you?* Arcanea is obvious. What else? Is there a school? A foundation? A physical place? A body of recorded work? A protocol that became infrastructure?

**Q5.** *What did you refuse?* The life you didn't live is as defining as the one you did. What path did 94-year-old Frank say no to, repeatedly, so that the yes could be total?

## PASS 2 — THE TEN YEAR COLLAPSE (builds resolution)

Now: **April 19, 2036.** You are 45. Tien is 45 (or the relationship is what it is — answer honestly). Alex is building something adjacent. Arcanea is ten years old as a company.

**Q6.** *What is your net worth, stated as a specific number?* Not "financially free." A number. Hill's autosuggestion law: the subconscious rejects vague. Pick the number that scares you by 20% and excites you by 80%. €25M? €100M? €500M? Pick.

**Q7.** *How many Arcaneans are active in the ecosystem?* 10K? 100K? 1M paid? The number determines the infrastructure, the team, the content cadence, the entire operating reality.

**Q9.** *What does your body look like and what can it do?* Bodyweight? Lift numbers? Can you free-dive to 20m? Can you do a standing backflip at 45? The specificity is the spell.

**Q10.** *What is your relationship architecture?* Monogamous with Tien, expanded, open, tribal? No moral framing — architectural. The truthful answer here unlocks or blocks 40% of your manifestation field because ambiguity here leaks energy into every other domain.

**Q11.** *What do you create on a normal Tuesday?* Not the peak day. The average Tuesday at 45. Music? Code? Writing? Teaching? Filming? Investing? Which one is the *main verb* of your days at 45?
