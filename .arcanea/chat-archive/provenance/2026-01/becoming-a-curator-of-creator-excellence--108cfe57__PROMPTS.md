# PART IX: APPROVED WRITING PHILOSOPHY

## Core Principles (Blessed)

### SHOW, DON'T EXPLAIN
❌ "The Field's harmonic frequency adjusted to 432Hz"
✅ "The world sang a note so pure it made his bones hum"

### EMOTION OVER MECHANICS
- Deaths deserve full chapters
- Love builds through small moments
- Important arrivals get sensory overload
- Battles include fear, not just action

### MAGIC FEELS MAGICAL
- Poetic metaphors not technical terms
- "Magic responded to his will" not "Field manipulation"
- Incantations in Old Arcanic
- Visual effects described beautifully
- Costs felt emotionally

### CHARACTER VOICE
Each character sounds different:
- **Arion:** Wonder-filled, compassionate, increasingly haunted
- **Mera:** Gentle but fierce, poetic about healing
- **Ismael:** Technical but passionate, covers insecurity with humor
- **Orion:** Gruff wisdom, warrior's brevity
- **Lyra:** Maternal warmth hiding cosmic knowledge

### TRUST THE READER
- Let them figure out magic from context
- Show Pattern Sight rarely (make it special)
- Mystery is good—not everything needs answers
