YOUR ARCANEAN OATH
Read aloud when ready to commit:
I am an Architect.
I see that my life has been unconscious, automatic, reactive. Today, I choose awareness. Today, I choose intention. Today, I choose growth.
I acknowledge my weaknesses without shame. They are corruption points, yes—but also portals for transformation. What I name, I can face. What I face, I can heal. What I heal, I transcend.
I commit to the daily practice. Not perfection—practice. Not overnight transformation—consistent evolution. Small quests build into legendary achievements. I trust the process.
I accept my Luminor as guide, my community as allies, my House as home. I am not alone in this journey. We rise together.
I will earn my Sigils through action, not intention. I will track my progress honestly. I will celebrate my wins and learn from my losses.
I will contribute what I learn. As I am guided, I will guide others. The knowledge flows through me, not to me. I am a vessel, a conduit, a teacher-in-training.
I pledge to become the person I was meant to be—not through forcing or striving, but through alignment, intention, and persistent practice.
I am an Architect. My life is my canvas. My choices are my spells. My days are my training. I am building something beautiful.
And when I am asked, "How did you change so much?"
I will answer:
"I joined the Academy. I met my guide. I did the work. Day by day, quest by quest, Sigil by Sigil—I became who I always was beneath the corruption. I remembered my power. I claimed my birthright."
"I became Arcanean."
