CONTEXT & ROLE

1. Deep-mine all conversation history for:
   - Recurring themes and vision patterns
   - Unique value propositions and differentiators
   - Personal journey and transformation moments
   - Strategic insights and market positioning
   - Technical innovations and intellectual property
   - Community and ecosystem elements
   - Revenue models and growth mechanics
2. Synthesize findings into:
   - Core narrative arc (3-act structure)
   - Visual metaphors and symbolic systems
   - Data stories and progression metrics
   - Stakeholder value matrices
DESIGN LANGUAGE
- Aesthetic: Precision minimalism with strategic depth
- Geometry: Soft modernism (0.15" corner radius on all containers)
- Typography: Single font family, 3 weights maximum, 4 size tiers
- Color Theory: 5-color constraint (2 primary, 2 accent, 1 neutral)
- White Space: 40% minimum per slide
- Visual Hierarchy: 3 levels maximum per slide
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Format: 16:9 widescreen
- Slides: 16-20 maximum
- File: PowerPoint (.pptx)
- Graphics: Code-generated infographics only
- Icons: Geometric abstractions or unicode symbols
- Data Viz: Custom charts, no templates
- Animations: None (print-ready)
CONTENT ARCHITECTURE
1. Opening Hook (1 slide)
   - Single powerful statement
   - Visual anchor element
   - Credibility marker
2. Vision Cascade (3-4 slides)
   - Problem → Insight → Solution
   - Personal → Market → Global
   - Present → Near Future → Transformed World
3. Proof Architecture (4-5 slides)
   - Unique mechanisms
   - Traction indicators
   - Competitive moats
   - Network effects
4. Value Crystallization (3-4 slides)
   - Stakeholder benefits matrix
   - Revenue architecture
   - Growth dynamics
   - Success metrics
5. Narrative Crescendo (2-3 slides)
   - Story synthesis
   - Emotional peak
   - Clear next action
6. Memorable Close (1 slide)
   - Singular takeaway
   - Contact trigger
   - Brand imprint
EXCELLENCE CRITERIA
- Clarity: 7-year-old could understand the story
- Memorability: 3 key points stick after 30 days
- Actionability: Next step is obvious and compelling
- Authenticity: Voice matches founder's genius
- Scalability: Works for 1 person or 10,000
DELIVERY REQUIREMENTS
1. Generate complete PowerPoint file
2. Use only code-generated visuals
3. Ensure print-compatibility
4. Optimize for both screen and projector
5. Include speaker note strategies
REFINEMENT LOOP
