Creation & Innovation → coding, music, lore, building.

Operations & Systems → finance, automations, infra.

Expansion & Scale → growth, partnerships, monetization.

Restoration & Learning → health, training, research, exploration.

Reflection & Renewal → weekly integration + reset.

7. Receptivity → openness, guidance, flow.
