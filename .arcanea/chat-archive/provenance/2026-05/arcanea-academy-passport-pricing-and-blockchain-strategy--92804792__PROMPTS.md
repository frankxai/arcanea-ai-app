One publishable product page block

Arcanea Academy Passport

Arcanea Academy Passport is a proof-of-growth system for students, creators, and builders completing weekly AI-native challenges.

Every week, students enter an Arcanean Trial: a mythic challenge that trains a real-world capability through coding agents, creative tools, strategic thinking, and publishable artifacts.

Participation is free.

Verification is optional.

Students who want official recognition can unlock Arcanea Verified Completion, including a public certificate page, collectible badge, and verifiable completion record.

The Academy does not reward passive learning.

It rewards evidence.

You complete a Trial by producing an artifact:
a working prototype, a visual system, a knowledge vault, a story scene, a decision simulator, an AI agent, or a public page.

Free students receive the challenge and can join the showcase.

Verified students receive:

- Arcanea Completion Certificate
- public profile credit
- collectible badge
- optional blockchain attestation
- progression toward Academy ranks

Arcanea Academy Passport turns imagination into capability, capability into proof, and proof into identity.

Prove your evolution through weekly trials.
