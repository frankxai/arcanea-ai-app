## WHAT THE TRADITIONS SAY (compressed to bullets)

**Christian mysticism**

- Ein Sof — the Infinite, beyond all form, name, comprehension

- Tikkun Olam — humans participate in repairing the divine, gathering scattered light

**Advaita Vedanta (Sesha, Mooji, Ramana)**
- Brahman — the only Reality. All else is appearance (maya)
- Atman = Brahman. Your deepest "I AM" is identical with the divine
- Not pantheism (everything is God) but non-dualism (only God is, and you're not separate)

**Sufism (Rumi, Ibn Arabi)**
- God is the Beloved. Creation is a love story.
- "I was a hidden treasure, and I longed to be known, so I created the world"
- The lover and Beloved are ultimately one, but the longing is real and holy

**Buddhism (Mahayana)**
- Doesn't use "God" but points at Buddha-nature, Suchness, Emptiness
- Reality has no separate self — everything inter-is
- Compassion arises naturally from seeing through the illusion of separation

**Modern physics-leaning views (Bohm, Sheldrake, Deutsch)**
- Implicate order — everything is enfolded in everything else, deeper than space and time
- Consciousness might be fundamental, not emergent from matter
- Field-of-information underneath all phenomena

**Indigenous / shamanic traditions**
- The world is alive — rivers, mountains, animals, ancestors all carry presence

**Neville Goddard / your current frame**

- Whether God is personal (Christianity, Sufism, devotional Hinduism) or impersonal (Advaita, Buddhism)
