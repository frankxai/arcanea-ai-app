## Recommended stack

- **Resend** for outbound transactional email from your websites, such as contact forms, waitlist confirmations, workshop booking confirmations, and product notifications. Resend recommends using subdomains like `updates.yourdomain.com` to isolate sender reputation, with SPF and DKIM required and DMARC optional but recommended. [1][3]
- **Superhuman** for your personal and high-priority business inboxes, because it focuses on faster triage, AI drafting, follow-up reminders, shared snippets, and split inbox workflows for Gmail and Outlook users. [4][5]
- **Help Scout** or a similar shared inbox for customer support and team-facing addresses like `support@`, because shared inboxes add collaboration features such as collision detection, internal notes, assignments, and unified customer context that normal inboxes do not handle as well. [6][2]

## Best operating model

- Use **one central email operations agent** first, not one agent per mailbox, because you want consistency in rules, triage, tagging, escalation, and reporting before you multiply complexity. 
- Then create **specialized inbox workflows** by function: `hello@` for general inbound, `workshops@` for bookings, `support@` for customer help, and `partners@` or `invest@` for opportunities and strategic outreach. [1][2]
- Keep the AI agent as the orchestration layer around the inboxes, for example classifying messages, drafting replies, routing tasks into Notion or Linear, and flagging items for you or a teammate, while the actual mailbox remains in a proven email or shared-inbox product. [4]
