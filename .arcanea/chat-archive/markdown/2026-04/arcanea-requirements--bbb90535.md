## Web3 / on-chain IP — does it replace this?

**Partially, but not fully.** Here's the real picture:

- **Smart contracts** can encode ownership and licensing rules — this works for digital assets
- **NFTs** can represent ownership of specific Arcanean creations
- **Traditional courts** (EU, US) still don't fully recognize on-chain IP as a substitute for trademark registration
- If a big company copies "Arcanea" name and logo in 2028, you need a **registered trademark** to legally enforce it fast — on-chain proof helps but courts want BOIP/USPTO records

So Web3 doesn't replace the trademark. But it can power your licensing/distribution system beautifully.
