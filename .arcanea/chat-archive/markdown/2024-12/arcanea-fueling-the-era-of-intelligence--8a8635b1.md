Based on my knowledge cutoff in April 2024, let me analyze the current state of AI art for webtoons:

- However, there are still challenges with:
  - Perfect consistency between panels
  - Dynamic action sequences
  - Complex emotional expressions
  - Hand and finger details
  - Maintaining exact character appearances

2. Current Webtoon Integration:
- Some creators use AI as a base/reference for their art
- Most successful implementations combine AI with traditional digital art
- Pure AI-generated webtoons often struggle with consistency

3. Best Current Approach:
- Use AI for:
  - Background generation
  - Initial character pose blocking
  - Scene composition reference
  - Color palette exploration
- Then refine with:
  - Manual digital art touchups
  - Consistent character details
  - Expression and emotion work
  - Action sequence adjustments

4. Tools to Consider:
- Midjourney for initial designs and backgrounds
- Stable Diffusion with custom training for character consistency
- Photoshop/Clip Studio Paint for editing and refinement
- Animation tools for smooth transitions
