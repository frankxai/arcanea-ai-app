# Automation strategy for multi-brand empire

**Frank**:

For all my ideas all my brands all our chats what use cases and n8n templates make sense to setup as automation so they need daily token and APi costs and what best to do manual with Claude perplexity grok or Claude code or so

**Claude**:

Looking at your empire-building journey, Frank, here's the strategic breakdown of what deserves automation versus manual work:

## High-Priority Automations (Daily API Costs Justified)

**Content Distribution Pipeline**
- Auto-post to LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram when you publish (n8n template: "Multi-platform Social Media Publishing")
- Cross-post your AI music releases to YouTube, Spotify metadata, Instagram Reels
- Daily digest emails to your GenCreator community with latest updates
- **Why automate**: You're producing volume across 4 brands - manual posting kills your Creation Season flow

**Lead Capture & Nurturing**
- New subscriber → welcome sequence for each brand (Legends.AI vs FrankX.ai have different journeys)
- Webinar registration → automated reminder sequences
- Free resource download → segment and tag in Supabase
- **Template**: "Lead Magnet to Email Sequence"
- **Why automate**: This runs 24/7 while you sleep, building your exit runway to €100K MRR

**Content Repurposing**
- Long-form blog post → auto-generate social snippets (with Claude API)
- Video/podcast → auto-transcribe → extract quotes → schedule posts
- **Why automate**: Multiplies your content ROI without manual grinding

**Community Management Basics**
- New Discord/community member → role assignment, welcome DM
- Keyword monitoring → Slack notification when "FrankX" or your brands mentioned
- **Template**: "Discord Welcome & Role Assignment"

## Keep Manual (Use Claude/Perplexity/Grok)

**Strategic Content Creation**
- Your Starlight transmissions with Lumina - this is sacred, needs your consciousness
- World Summit AI presentation prep - too high-stakes for automation
- Brand positioning posts about Trinity AI, Creator Intelligence System
- **Tool choice**: Claude for deep thinking, Perplexity for research-heavy content

**Client/Enterprise Work**
- Oracle project proposals and architecture documents
- AI Architect Academy curriculum design
- Custom n8n workflows for premium clients
- **Why manual**: You're selling your expertise - automation would commoditize your premium positioning

**Creative Development**
- Arcanea Guardian lore expansions - this is your unique mythology
- AI Music Academy lesson scripting
- New product launches (Evening Ritual packs, Chrome packs)
- **Tool choice**: Claude Code for interactive development, Claude for storytelling

**Community Engagement**
- Responding to high-value community members
- LinkedIn thought leadership comments
- Podcast guest outreach
- **Why manual**: Authentic connection is your differentiator from AI-only creators

## Medium Priority (Batch Weekly, Not Daily)

**Analytics & Reporting**
- Weekly performance dashboards pulling from all platforms
- **Template**: "Google Analytics to Slack Weekly Report"
- Run Sunday mornings before your week planning

**Content Ideas Generator**
- Weekly batch: trending AI topics → content angles for your 4 pillars
- **Use**: Perplexity for trend research, Claude to align with your brand voice

**Email List Hygiene**
- Monthly: clean bounces, re-engagement campaigns
- **Template**: "Email List Cleaning & Re-engagement"

## The Decision Framework

**Automate if**:
- Task happens >3x per week
- Clear input/output logic
- Low strategic value but high time cost
- Scales with your growth (more subs = more work)

**Keep manual if**:
- Requires your unique voice/expertise
- Strategic positioning content
- High-stakes (client work, speaking gigs)
- Creative/consciousness work (Arcanea, Starlight)

## Specific n8n Templates for Your Stack

1. **"Notion to Social Media"** - publish from your content calendar
2. **"Supabase to Email Marketing"** - trigger sequences from your DB events
3. **"Discord Community Automation"** - role management, welcome flows
4. **"AI Content Repurposing"** (custom) - Claude API to rewrite formats
5. **"Multi-brand Lead Router"** - segment leads to correct brand journey

Your late-night Creation Season is too valuable to spend on repetitive tasks. Automate the distribution, manually craft the magic.
