## I. The Science of Goal Setting

4. Relevant: Ensure goals align with your long-term objectives
5. Time-bound: Set a timeframe for goal completion

### B. Locke and Latham's Goal-Setting Theory
1. Clarity: Goals should be clear and specific
2. Challenge: Goals should be difficult but not impossible
3. Commitment: Personal buy-in is crucial for goal achievement
4. Feedback: Regular progress updates enhance motivation
5. Task Complexity: Consider the difficulty of tasks when setting goals

### C. Psychological Distance and Construal Level Theory
1. Near-future goals: Focus on concrete, specific actions
