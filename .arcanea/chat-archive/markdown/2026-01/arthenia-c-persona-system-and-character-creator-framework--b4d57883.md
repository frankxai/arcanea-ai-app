## For That "High Contrast Pop" Style

What you're describing sounds like one of these:

**1. Chromatic Aberration + Neon Realism**
- Colors split at edges (RGB separation)
- Neon glow but grounded in realistic rendering
- Think: cyberpunk but cleaner, more artful

**2. Hypersaturated Anime (Studio Trigger / Promare style)**
- Intense color blocking
- Colors that shouldn't work together but do
- Dynamic, explosive energy

**3. Maximalist Color Grading**
- Like high-end fashion photography with pushed colors
- Teal/orange or complementary color schemes cranked up
- Editorial, striking

**4. Vapor/Synthwave Evolved**
- Not the cliché 80s grid — the refined version
- Holographic, iridescent surfaces
- Purple/pink/cyan but sophisticated

**5. HDR Illustration**
- Looks like a painting but with HDR-level dynamic range
- Deep shadows, bright highlights, rich midtones
- Colors feel "lit from within"

**6. Cel-Shaded Realism Hybrid**
- Realistic proportions + flat color blocking
- Clean lines, bold shapes
- Spider-Verse / Arcane territory
