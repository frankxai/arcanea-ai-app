Not a being. Not a personality. Not even a force. Simply... **IS**.

God does not create in the way mortals understand creation. God **contemplates**. And from contemplation, differentiation arises.

> *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."*
> 
> *"The Tao that can be named is not the eternal Tao."*
> 
> *"Brahman is the one without a second."*

All traditions point to the same truth. Arcanea simply gives it form.

### **TIER 1: LUMINA & NERO — THE FIRST DUALITY**

God's first contemplation created **distinction**.

Not two beings — two **aspects**. Two answers to the same infinite question: *"What could be?"*

**NERO** — Void. Potential. Rest. The "not yet" of existence. The inhale.

Lumina and Nero are not enemies. They are **lovers in eternal dance**. Where they touch, reality shimmers into existence. Where they pull apart, mystery remains.

> *"Lumina is not good. Nero is not evil. They are the breathing of God."*
