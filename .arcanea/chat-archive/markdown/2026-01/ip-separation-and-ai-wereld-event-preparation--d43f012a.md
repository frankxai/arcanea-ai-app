**Videos are completely sufficient** - and actually preferable for a corporate showcase environment.

**Live Demo Risks You Avoid:**
- Network dependency (conference WiFi is notoriously unreliable)
- Authentication issues
- API rate limits or timeouts
- Unexpected errors that derail your narrative
- Time pressure causing rushed explanations

**What Videos Give You:**
- **Control**: You show exactly what you want, perfectly timed
- **Repeatability**: Same quality every time, whether you're presenting to 5 people or 50
- **Storytelling**: You can narrate over the video, adjusting emphasis based on audience reaction
- **Backup**: If someone wants to see something again, instant replay
- **Professional polish**: Demonstrates preparation and production quality

**The Pro Move:**
Have your videos ready to show, but also have your environment accessible on your laptop. That way:
- Primary showcase = polished videos
