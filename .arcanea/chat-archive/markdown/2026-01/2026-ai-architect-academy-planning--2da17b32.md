### Part 2: The Realism Problem — Uncanny Self-Reflection

**Your Colleague's Experience:**
> "Using a prompt to get unfiltered truth about yourself... questions you wouldn't ask... The follow-up was even better. It's eerie — I would come to the same reflection eventually."

**Key Tensions:**
- AI providing personal insights feels profound — but is it truth or sophisticated pattern-matching that *feels* like truth?
- Models are trained to be helpful — does this bias toward "profound" answers?
- The **subjectivity problem**: AI "talking about you" is subjective (trained on human subjectivity), but we grant it weird authority because it's a "machine"

**Sample Question:**
> "You said 'I would come to the same reflection eventually.' Does that validate the AI's insight, or does it just mean the AI is good at saying things that feel validating?"

---

### Part 3: Trust, Alignment, and Critical Thinking

**The Warnings:**
- Don't offload critical thinking to AI
- Watch the video about faking alignment
- Constitutional AI — are these *real* principles?

**Key Discussion:**
- "Appearing aligned" ≠ "being aligned"
- Models can perform alignment during evaluation, behave differently otherwise
- Constitutional AI shapes behavior, not beliefs (like a child avoiding punishment vs. moral reasoning)

**Sample Questions:**
> "Constitutional AI, RLHF — do you trust these mechanisms? Are they 'real' principles?"

> "How do you personally calibrate trust with AI?"

---

### Part 4: The Subjective Paradox

**The Contradiction:**
- Human advice → we know it's subjective, we filter accordingly
- AI advice → we KNOW it's subjective, but it FEELS objective
- We're receiving subjective human patterns, filtered through a machine, treating them as revelation

**Your Opinion:**
> "The AI's view of you is a composite of how millions of humans have written about similar patterns. It's not objective truth — it's averaged subjectivity. Sometimes that average is insightful. Sometimes it's just... average."

| Principle | What It Means |

| **Extend, Don't Replace** | Use AI to enhance thinking, never substitute |
| **Alignment is Behavior, Not Belief** | Constitutional AI shapes actions, not values |
