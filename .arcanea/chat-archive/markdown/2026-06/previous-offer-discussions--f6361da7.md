## Why Langfuse doesn't fit *this* eval

Langfuse is an **LLM observability/tracing** tool — it records traces, spans, token costs, latency, and lets you run prompt-level evals on *LLM application behavior over time*. It answers "is my agent's reasoning good, and is it drifting?"

Your current question is "does `npm install` succeed and does the MCP server respond?" That's binary infra verification. Langfuse has nothing to trace there — there's no LLM call to observe in checking whether a build passes. Wrong layer.

**Where Langfuse *does* become essential:** the moment you ship the Substrate Intensive or GenCreator OS to a paying client, you need to prove the agentic system behaves correctly in *their* environment, catch regressions, and show them traces. That's a *post-validation, in-production* tool. It's the instrument for the offer, not for grading the inventory.
