You already answered most of this in March — *build the agentic operation first, get SOPs and revenue in place before scaling humans, fund it from liquidated assets as a bet on your own vision.* That instinct was correct. This message is you pressure-testing it against the pull to hire. So two corrections before the allocation:

**One — the pull to hire production labor first is the trap.** Your bottleneck was never throughput. Agents own throughput. The bottleneck is taste-coherence across three brands and *your attention*. Spend there. Every human you add must either extend your taste or buy back your judgment — never add output. A human whose job the system will eat in two quarters is a liability you're putting on payroll.

**Two — do not cast the "genius woman" as the right hand if you mean Ana.** Ana's lane is the interior: the circle, the veil, the Guardian of the Inner Circle. Put her in an ops-driver seat and you break her — she shuts down under exactly the load a chief-of-staff carries. The right hand is a *different person*. Keep the lanes separate or one starves.

The filter for every hire, now and forever: **does this person improve the substrate, or merely operate inside it as a cost?** Substrate-improvers are investments. Operators are bridges you time-box until automated.

**MUST — the floor, start now (~€2.5–4k/mo + one-time):**

- **One ops VA, AI-fluent** — €1.2–1.8k/mo. Not "the muscle," a re-scoped attention layer: triage, scheduling, posting, research ops, getting captures into the system. Reclaims 10–15 hrs/week so the operational floor never drops. One, not a team — a pod before mature SOPs adds coordination overhead, not leverage.
- **Legal / IP / bookkeeping spine** — €500–1.2k/mo beyond Thijs's one-time BV work. Your entire thesis is *owning the IP*. The silent way a sovereign-IP founder dies is unprotected IP and three messy BVs. Unglamorous, non-negotiable.
- **Taste substrate** — €3–8k one-time (a 2–3 week sprint with one senior brand/design hand to *codify* the cross-brand system, not a hire), or €0 and your own time if you refuse to outsource taste. This is the keystone you're under-investing in relative to people. It's the multiplier that makes every later human *and* every agent render coherently across FrankX / Arcanea / Starlight. Build this before the editor, not after.

**THE ONE REAL HUMAN BET — within 1–2 months:**

- **Right hand / chief-of-staff-in-training** — €2.5–4k/mo (part-time → full-time) + a path to profit-share in the holding. This is the single highest-leverage human investment because it's ownership *and* a taste apprenticeship at once. Outcome: loop-closing, accountability, and the one human who can operate the system when you're in Marbella or Croatia — turning you from operator into architect. Worth waiting for the right person. Worth real teaching time. Title is hers to choose; the role is fixed.

**BRIDGE — time-boxed, designed to die into the system (1 quarter):**

- **Freelance editor/designer** — €1.5–3k/mo, defined backlog sprint across brands, scoped so their best output becomes the *style targets the agentic pipeline trains on*. This is how you honor "elevate the system": the human clears the backlog now *and* manufactures the exemplars that let the agents absorb production. Kill or convert at quarter-end. Never permanent.

**FUNNEL, not workforce:**

- **Student competition / academy module** — €2–5k prize pool; convert only the top 1–2 to a €500–1k/mo trial. Value is talent discovery, ecosystem proof, and Academy narrative content — not labor. Do not spray cash on mediocre student production; the system is cheaper and more consistent.

**DEFER until revenue justifies (the "could"):**

- Growth operator (your old Role 3) — wait until there's a funnel worth operating. VA + right hand + agents cover early Dream-100 motion.
- Magic / VA pod — wait for mature SOPs.
- One paid operator-advisor who's built a creator/IP business to 7–8 figures — €1–3k/mo or equity, for *judgment* on asymmetric-downside decisions. Explicitly not another coach.
