### 1. Core Visual DNA & Preferences (What We Love)
- **Epic god-like sovereign scale** — Towering, majestic, divine cosmic beings with massive presence.
- **Ultra-beautiful 3D sacred nature embodiment** — Smooth seamless integration of living leaves, roots, vines, bark, moss, petals, and crystals into characters and environments. Organic 3D connections that feel alive.
- **Profound 3D depth eyes** — Each eye has unique holographic living nature inside (root networks, flower galaxies, abyssal reefs, mycelium, cosmic nebulae, etc.).
- **Sacred thin-film iridescence** — Breathing, angle-dependent luxurious desaturated shifts: gold → rose → violet → cyan → emerald → silver (black opal soap bubbles on raven feathers, cathedral prisms, aurora compressed into surfaces).
- **Mature, sophisticated, premium aesthetic** — No childish/cute/anime. 28–45+ year old heroic maturity, alluring intelligence, grounded power.
- **Hybrid painterly-cinematic style** — 3D depth + visible expressive brush strokes + living painterly feel.
- **Warm golden dawn dominance** — Volumetric god rays, prismatic halos, floating bubbles/orbs, mystical triumphant wonder.
- **Full-body hero shots** — Wide angle, head-to-toe, dramatic cinematic lighting, perfect for web banners, game assets, character concepts, music video keyframes.
- **Stylize range** — 550–650 for maximum beauty and epic quality.
- **Aspect ratios** — 16:9 (cinematic), 9:16 (portrait), 1:1 (icons/logos).

## PART V — UI DESIGN SYSTEM (Liquid Glass Futurism)

### Core Principles
1. Everything has depth — cards float, buttons have thickness, nothing is flat
2. Glass is the material — backdrop blur 40–60px, multi-layer backgrounds, gradient borders suggesting refraction
3. Light comes from above-left — top/left edges lighter, bottom/right darker
4. Shadows are soft and layered — always 2–3 box-shadow layers: ambient, direct, contact
5. Colors are gradients — no flat fills. Gold is always a 3-stop gradient
6. Borders suggest refraction — top/left borders brighter = glass edge effect

**Forbidden:** Flat solid backgrounds · Single-value box-shadows · Sharp borders without gradient · Gray that isn't blue-tinted

### Bubble Iridescence System
Inspired by soap bubble thin-film interference. Arcanea interpretation: desaturated, luxurious — cathedral crystal, oil on dark water, aurora compressed into surface, shimmer on raven feather.

**Iridescence color palette:**
```
Shift 1: Gold → Rose → Violet
Shift 2: Cyan → Emerald → Gold
Shift 3: Violet → Blue → Silver
Tone: Desaturated, luxurious (black opal, not candy)
```

### Design Tokens
