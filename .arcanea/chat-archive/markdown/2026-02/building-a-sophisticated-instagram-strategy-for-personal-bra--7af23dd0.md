**Presence, Content & Narrative Tips:**

**The Narrative You're Building on @frank_riemer**

Your profile should tell the story of: a man who builds with intelligence, creates with soul, travels with purpose, and thinks deeply. Not a content creator. Not an influencer. A man with a rich inner and outer world who happens to share glimpses of it.

**Presence Rules**

Never post in real-time desperation. If you're at a beautiful spot and feel the urge to post immediately — take the photo, save it, post it tomorrow. The delay communicates "I was busy living, not performing."

Never explain too much. A photo of your desk at 2 AM needs zero caption. Maybe just "02:47" as the caption. People will fill in the story themselves and that story will always be more compelling than what you'd write.

Your face should appear rarely. When it does, it should be candid or cinematic — never selfie-mirror-pose energy. Shot from the side looking out at something. Walking away from camera. Silhouette against a sunset. These communicate "I'm not seeking your approval, you're just witnessing my life."

**Content Formats That Build Mystique**

The "Currently" story: just one word + image. "Currently." and a photo of a book, a view, a screen. Do this 2-3x per week. It becomes your signature.

The silent carousel: 4-6 beautiful images from one day or one trip. No caption or one-line caption. Let the visuals tell the story.

The midnight drop: posting at 1-2 AM occasionally. A thought, a song clip, a screen of code. Signals you're building while others sleep. People notice when posts come from late night.

The book ritual: this is becoming your thing already. Lean into it. Different books, same energy. Over time your followers start associating you with depth and wisdom. Some will DM you asking for recommendations — that's connection.

The cryptic one-liner: post a story with just text on black background. Something like "The architecture of silence is louder than noise." or "Building something that doesn't exist yet." No context. No explanation. The people who resonate will engage. The rest scroll past. Both outcomes are fine.

**What NOT To Do on @frank_riemer**

Don't post motivational quotes from Google images. If you share wisdom, it comes from YOUR reading, YOUR thinking, YOUR experience. The difference between a quote account and your account is that yours has a human behind it with a life that proves the philosophy.

Don't post "happy birthday" posts for friends or "grateful for this one" couple posts. Keep relationships private on the grid. Stories only for personal moments, and even then, sparingly.

Don't announce what you're doing. Don't post "excited to announce I'm working on..." — just show the work. "Announcing" is for LinkedIn. Instagram is for showing.

Don't respond to every comment. Reply to interesting ones. Like the rest. Selective engagement signals that your attention is valuable.

**The 30-Day Presence Challenge**

If you want a simple structure without it feeling like a content calendar: post one story per day for 30 days. Just one. Whatever caught your eye that day. Book, sky, food, screen, street, thought. No pressure to make it good. Just build the habit of sharing one moment daily. After 30 days your story views will have tripled and posting will feel natural instead of performative.

That's the whole game, Frank. Be interesting. Share glimpses. Stay mysterious. Let people come to you.
