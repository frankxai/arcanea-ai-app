I'd build this as a **live artifact dashboard** rather than static links—solves the structural problem you're facing: referral ecosystems are **distribution networks**, not lists.

Static approach breaks because:
- You can't track performance per channel (which audiences actually convert)
- No narrative cohesion across brands (FrankX, Arcanea, Starlight, Academy)
- Can't A/B messaging per audience segment (superfans vs gym influencers vs creators)
- Impossible to scale to 4 brands without it becoming noise

**Better move**: React component that:
1. **Segments links by brand + audience** (not just a dump)
2. **Embeds QR codes** for physical distribution (houseboat collateral, retreat swag, gym partnerships)
3. **Copy-optimized CTAs** that shift tone per context (superfans get mythology angle, creators get tools angle, gym crowd gets transformation angle)
4. **UTM tracking ready** so you can pull actual conversion data
5. **Shareable as single URL** you can link from bio/sites—then people pick their entry point

This becomes your **distribution controller**—one artifact, infinite positioning.

- **Both** (locked version for partners, public version for general audience)?
