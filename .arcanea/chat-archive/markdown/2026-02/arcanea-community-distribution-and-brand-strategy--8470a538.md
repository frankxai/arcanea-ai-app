# Arcanea Community Architecture — Decision Framework

You're describing three distinct layers that need sequencing, not parallel launch:

## Layer 1: Distribution Engine (Now)

**TikTok is your lead channel.** Short-form video is the only channel where zero-audience accounts still get organic reach at scale. Everything else is downstream of TikTok content repurposed.

**Channel stack, ranked by ROI on your time:**

1. **TikTok** — primary creation surface. Daily drops. Arcanea lore, music, behind-the-scenes, AI creation process.
2. **WhatsApp Channel** — broadcast-only daily drops (images, videos, music). Zero moderation cost. This is your "Starlight feed." Separate from group chats.
3. **WhatsApp Group(s)** — one inner circle only for now. Don't fragment into multiple groups until you have moderators. Groups without moderation decay into noise within weeks.
4. **Instagram** — repurpose TikTok content. One account: **@arcanea.ai** or equivalent. Don't split energy across personal + brand pages yet. Consolidate.
5. **Slack** — internal ops and close collaborators only. Not community-facing.

**Park for now:** Facebook (dead organic reach, group moderation overhead), Discord (you named the risk yourself — channel architecture rabbit hole with no audience to fill it yet). Discord becomes relevant when you have 500+ engaged people who need async spaces.

## Layer 2: Infrastructure (Next 30 days)

- **arcanea.ai** — needs one clear CTA: join the WhatsApp channel or email list. Not five links. One funnel.
- **GitHub transition to Arcanea-Labs org** — do it now, it's a 20-minute task that compounds. Clean namespace = credibility for developer community later.
- **Discord** — deploy one Arcanea bot as a public demo/playground. Frame it as a product showcase, not a community yet. "Come test the Guardians" > "Join our server."

## Layer 3: Arcanea Records — The Real Question

This is the highest-leverage play you listed, and also the one that could dilute everything if mis-timed.

The decision isn't *should you do it* — it's **what's the minimum viable label that attracts artists without operational overhead you can't carry?**

The answer: **Arcanea Records launches as a playlist brand and distribution partnership, not a label.** 

- Curate a Spotify/YouTube playlist series under the Arcanea Records name
- Invite artists to submit tracks for inclusion — this flips the dynamic (they come to you)
- Use DistroKid or similar for your own releases under the label umbrella
- AI-generated imagery becomes the visual identity layer for every release — this is your differentiator, not a liability. Artists want striking visuals. You provide them as part of the "label" value prop.

You don't need masters rights or contracts yet. You need **brand gravity** — artists associating Arcanea with aesthetic quality and reach.

## What's Actually Missing

**An email list.** Every platform you listed is rented land. WhatsApp can change broadcast rules tomorrow. TikTok gets banned in markets. You need `hello@arcanea.ai` collecting subscribers from day one. This is the one asset that compounds regardless of platform shifts.

**A content calendar constraint.** Not a full editorial calendar — just a rule: *3 TikToks/week minimum, 1 WhatsApp broadcast/day, 1 longer piece (YouTube/blog) per week.* The constraint creates the rhythm. Without it, you'll oscillate between silence and 12-hour creation binges.

## Sequencing

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| This week | TikTok daily. WhatsApp Channel live. One CTA on arcanea.ai. GitHub org migration. |
| Week 2-3 | Instagram consolidated + repurposing pipeline. Email capture live. Discord bot demo deployed. |
| Week 4+ | Arcanea Records playlist launch. First artist outreach. |

Beach Club, Academy, Creation Chambers — those are $1M+ capex plays that follow audience, not precede it. They belong on the vision board, not the sprint board.

**The forcing question:** Which single piece of Arcanea content, posted this week on TikTok, would make someone screenshot it and send it to a friend? That's your entire strategy compressed into one action.
