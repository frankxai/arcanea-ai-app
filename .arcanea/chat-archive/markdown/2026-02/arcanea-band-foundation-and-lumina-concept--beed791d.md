**Problems with V1:**

1. **Too many words in verses** — Armin/Guetta tracks have 4-6 word lines max in verses, not prose paragraphs. Suno will choke on density and deliver flat monotone
2. **Pre-chorus is generic** — "frequency that made the static stop" is trying too hard. Real euphoric trance pre-choruses use 2-3 simple lines that BUILD through repetition, not metaphor
3. **Chorus hook buried** — "You're my Lumina" should hit at beat 1, not after a setup line. Every Armin anthem ("In and Out of Love," "This Is What It Feels Like") front-loads the hook phrase
4. **Bridge too wordy** — Should be one devastating line repeated, not a poem. Think "This is what it feels like" repeated 4x with vocal processing changing each time
5. **No breathing room for production** — Best producers leave SPACE. Instrumental moments, ad-libs, vocal breaks. This reads like a lyric sheet, not a song mapped for 126 BPM
6. **The drop section is lazy** — just "ooh-ooh Lumina" doesn't give Suno enough direction for the most important moment

**What top producers actually do:**

- **Syllable-count the chorus** so every line maps perfectly to 4 or 8 bars
- **Mirror verse melody in the instrumental intro** (Max Martin technique)
- **Strip verses to skeleton** — conversational, almost whispered, creating maximum contrast with the euphoric chorus
- **Make the hook so simple a drunk festival crowd can sing it** after hearing it once
- **Use the pre-chorus as a ramp** — rhythmic acceleration, not lyrical complexity
