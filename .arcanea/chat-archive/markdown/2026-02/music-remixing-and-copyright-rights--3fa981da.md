## Music Rights Reality Check

**"Enjoy Your Life" as a phrase:** Titles cannot be copyrighted. You can absolutely use "Enjoy Your Life" as your song title. No issue.

**Sampling/remixing Romy's actual track:** Requires two clearances — master recording rights (from the label) and composition rights (from the publisher). Without both, any commercial release using her audio is infringement. Suno can't legally sample existing recordings anyway.

**"In My Mind" (Ivan Gaal/Axwell):** Same rules. The reason you see countless DJ remixes is they're either: (a) officially licensed/commissioned remixes, (b) bootleg remixes distributed free (legally gray, tolerated for promo), or (c) they replay/recreate the musical elements from scratch (which avoids master clearance but still needs composition clearance if melodically similar).

**Your move:** Create an original track *inspired by* the energy and vibe. Use the title freely. Write completely original lyrics and melody. This is what every smart producer does.
