Three forks that decide the shape of everything downstream:

**Fork 1 — Is the rank ladder public or private?** Public ranks make it a status game (Cialdini surface, social proof, leaderboard gravity) but invite rank-farming and dilute SOV honesty. Private ranks keep the status window an honest instrument between you and the System but forfeit the network effect of visible ascension. Solo Leveling's whole tension is *public* rank vs *hidden* true power — you may want exactly that: a public rank, a private SOV.

**Fork 2 — Does Second Awakening gate a product tier or a capability?** If it's a paywall (buy your way into the World Creation Engine), you monetize but break the "cannot be purchased" invariant that makes the threshold real. If it's strictly earned (SOV + irreversible act), the mechanic keeps its integrity but you need a *different* revenue surface for the Sovereign band. These are mutually exclusive; pick which one Second Awakening is for.

**Fork 3 — Ship order: System-first or Forge-first?** System-first (§13.1) reframes your existing 85-repo output as diegetic in days, near-zero new capability — fastest to a playable loop, but it's a skin until the Forge lands. Forge-first (§13.2) makes the shadow-army mechanic *materially* real from day one — slower, but the first thing anyone touches is owned power, not a status window. Which do superfans meet first?
