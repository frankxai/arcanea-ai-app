## Entity Structure: Dutch Holding + Operating BV

**Two-BV stack.** This is non-negotiable for your situation.

**Holding BV** (you as DGA - directeur-grootaandeelhouder) sits on top. It holds shares in your **Operating BV** (where FrankX.ai, Arcanea, AI Music Academy, AI Architect Academy revenue flows). **Why not eenmanszaak?** You're past it. Once you're spending €30K+/year on infra, talent, and ads, the BV's 19% corporate tax rate (on first €200K profit) beats box 1 income tax rates that climb to 49.5%. Plus liability protection matters when you're running paid campaigns and hiring models.

## What Becomes Deductible Immediately

Everything you listed is a legitimate business expense through the Operating BV:

**EV** — Through the BV, you get significant tax advantages. Bijtelling (private use addition) on full EVs is only 16% over the first €30,000 of list price. Lease through the BV.

## VAT (BTW) Play

Your BV is BTW-registered. You charge 21% BTW on Dutch/EU B2C sales, but you **reclaim BTW** on all business purchases. That MacBook Pro? You get 21% back. Every subscription, every ad spend, every piece of equipment — BTW recovery. This alone is a ~17.4% effective discount on everything you buy for the business.

For digital products sold to consumers in other EU countries, you'll use the OSS (One Stop Shop) scheme to handle VAT across member states through a single Dutch filing.

## Credit & Cash Flow Strategy

**Business credit card** — Get one through the BV immediately. ABN AMRO, ING, or Bunq Business. All expenses flow through it, creating clean audit trails and building business credit history.

**Btw-lening or credit facility** — Once you have 6-12 months of BV revenue, you qualify for a business credit line. Dutch banks and fintechs (Qredits, New10, Funding Circle NL) offer these.

**Invoice financing** — If you land larger coaching contracts (€5K-€15K Trinity tier), you can use factoring to get immediate cash against outstanding invoices.

**R&D tax credit (WBSO)** — This is your sleeper weapon. Building AI systems, multi-agent architectures, custom intelligence frameworks — this qualifies as R&D. WBSO gives you a reduction on wage tax (loonbelasting) for hours spent on qualifying R&D. If you're paying yourself a DGA salary (minimum ~€56K in 2026), the savings are meaningful.
