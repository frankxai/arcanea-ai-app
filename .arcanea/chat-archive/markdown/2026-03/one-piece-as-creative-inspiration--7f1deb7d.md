## THE POWER SYSTEM: DEVIL FRUITS (悪魔の実, Akuma no Mi)

**The tradeoff:** Eat one, gain supernatural power, permanently lose the ability to swim. In a world of ocean — that's existential risk as currency. Only one fruit per person (one exception: Blackbeard). When a user dies, the power reincarnates in a nearby fruit.

### Three Categories

**PARAMECIA** — the broadest class. Body alteration, substance generation, or environmental manipulation. Not elemental, not animal. The weird ones.

**ZOAN** — animal transformation. Three forms: human, beast, hybrid. Sub-types: Ancient Zoan (dinosaurs, prehistoric creatures — massively durable), Mythical Zoan (rarest tier — legendary creatures with extra supernatural abilities), Artificial Zoan (lab-created, usually flawed).

**LOGIA** — elemental transformation. User becomes the element itself — intangible, can generate and control it. Considered the most naturally overpowered class. Only Haki can bypass the intangibility.

### The Key Devil Fruits (Japanese name / English / User / Type)

**Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika** (Human-Human Fruit, Model: Sun God Nika) — Monkey D. Luffy — Mythical Zoan. Originally believed to be the Gomu Gomu no Mi (rubber Paramecia), the World Government hid its true name for 800 years. When awakened, it becomes Gear 5 — the "Drums of Liberation" — granting the user cartoonish freedom limited only by imagination. Uniquely exhibits both Paramecia-type awakening (extending rubber properties to environment and living things) and Zoan-type awakening (unique transformation) simultaneously.

**Gura Gura no Mi** (Tremor-Tremor Fruit) — Whitebeard → Blackbeard — Paramecia. Considered the strongest Paramecia, capable of generating world-destroying earthquakes and tsunamis.

**Yami Yami no Mi** (Dark-Dark Fruit) — Marshall D. Teach (Blackbeard) — Logia. Creates black holes, absorbs attacks, and nullifies other Devil Fruit abilities on contact. Blackbeard holds BOTH this and the Gura Gura — the only dual-fruit user in history.

**Ope Ope no Mi** (Op-Op Fruit) — Trafalgar Law — Paramecia. Creates a spherical "Room" where the user can surgically manipulate everything — teleport objects, swap personalities, cut without killing. Can grant eternal youth to another at the cost of the user's life.

**Mera Mera no Mi** (Flame-Flame Fruit) — Portgas D. Ace → Sabo — Logia. Full fire creation, control, and transformation. Named from "meramera," Japanese onomatopoeia for fire blazing.

**Soru Soru no Mi** (Soul-Soul Fruit) — Big Mom (Charlotte Linlin) — Paramecia. Extracts lifespan/souls from people, implants them into objects to create "Homies" — sentient servants. Her signature Homies: Zeus (thundercloud), Prometheus (sun), Napoleon (bicorn hat).

**Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu** (Fish-Fish Fruit, Model: Azure Dragon) — Kaido — Mythical Zoan. Transforms into a massive eastern dragon. Fire breath, wind scythes, lightning. The "World's Strongest Creature."

**Hie Hie no Mi** (Ice-Ice Fruit) — Aokiji (Kuzan) — Logia. Complete ice control. Froze entire oceans.

**Magu Magu no Mi** (Magma-Magma Fruit) — Akainu (Sakazuki) — Logia. Magma generation. Overpowers fire — this is how Ace died.

**Pika Pika no Mi** (Glint-Glint Fruit) — Kizaru (Borsalino) — Logia. Light-speed attacks, laser beams, teleportation at light speed.

**Goro Goro no Mi** (Rumble-Rumble Fruit) — Enel — Logia. Lightning control. Two hundred million volts. Enel called himself God.

**Ito Ito no Mi** (String-String Fruit) — Doflamingo — Paramecia. Creates razor strings that can slice, puppet-control people, create clones, and when awakened, turns surroundings into strings.

**Nikyu Nikyu no Mi** (Paw-Paw Fruit) — Bartholomew Kuma — Paramecia. Repels anything — including abstract concepts like pain, memories, fatigue. Can teleport people across the world with a slap.

**Hana Hana no Mi** (Flower-Flower Fruit) — Nico Robin — Paramecia. Sprouts body parts anywhere. Her awakened form "Demonio Fleur" creates a massive demonic avatar.

**Yomi Yomi no Mi** (Revive-Revive Fruit) — Brook — Paramecia. Resurrects the user once after death. Brook also channels the "Chill of the Underworld" — freezing soul attacks.

## THE POWER SYSTEM: HAKI (覇気)

Haki is willpower made manifest. Unlike Devil Fruits, anyone can potentially awaken it. According to Kaido, Haki is the true determining factor in conquering the sea — it transcends all Devil Fruit powers.

**Kenbunshoku Haki (Observation Haki)** — Sixth sense. Presence detection, emotion reading, intent prediction. Advanced form: **Future Sight** — seeing seconds into the future (Katakuri, Luffy). Rarest sub-form: **Observation Killing** — only Shanks can do this. Shuts down opponents' Future Sight entirely.

**Busoshoku Haki (Armament Haki)** — Invisible spiritual armor. Hardens body/weapons (visible as black coating post-timeskip). Bypasses Logia intangibility. Advanced form: **Ryuo** (流桜, "Flowing Cherry Blossom") — emits Haki as shockwaves. Further advanced: **Internal Destruction** — sends Haki inside a target to destroy from within.

**Haoshoku Haki (Conqueror's Haki / Supreme King Haki)** — Born into one in a million. Projects pure willpower. Knocks out weaker foes en masse. Advanced form: **Conqueror's Infusion** — coats attacks with Conqueror's Haki, creating black lightning. When two top-tier users clash with this, the sky literally splits. Sub-form: **Conqueror's Knot** — Joy Boy stored Haki in knots that lasted centuries.

## THE DEEP LORE

### The Void Century
A 100-year period (900-800 years before present) completely erased from historical record by the World Government. A "Great Kingdom" with technology far surpassing modern civilization was destroyed by the Twenty Kingdoms (now the World Government). The mere knowledge of this kingdom's existence threatens the Government's legitimacy.

### The Poneglyphs
Indestructible stone blocks created by the Kozuki Clan of Wano during the Void Century to preserve forbidden history. Written in an ancient script almost no one can read — Nico Robin is the only living person who can. **Road Poneglyphs** (four exist) — their combined information reveals the location of Laugh Tale. **Historical Poneglyphs** — contain the true history. **Instructional Poneglyphs** — reveal the Ancient Weapons.

### The Ancient Weapons
Three weapons of world-ending power, named after Greco-Roman gods:

**Pluton** — An ancient battleship built on Water 7 during the Void Century, now buried beneath Mount Fuji in old Wano. Its release requires literally "opening Wano's borders."

**Poseidon** — Not an object but a person. A mermaid princess born once every several centuries with the power to command Sea Kings. Currently: Princess Shirahoshi of Fish-Man Island.

**Uranus** — Still mysterious. Recent events suggest Imu may already possess or wield it — a sky-based weapon that obliterated the entire Lulusia Kingdom.

### Joy Boy
A figure from the Great Kingdom who lived 900 years ago, the first pirate in history. He left a treasure on Laugh Tale that became known as the One Piece. He made a promise to Fish-Man Island involving Noah (a colossal ship) and Poseidon, but failed to keep it. Left an apology on a Poneglyph. When Luffy awakened his Devil Fruit, the ancient elephant Zunesha proclaimed that Joy Boy had returned.

### The Will of D.
Characters bearing "D." in their middle name — Monkey D. Luffy, Gol D. Roger, Portgas D. Ace, Trafalgar D. Water Law, Marshall D. Teach, Rocks D. Xebec, Monkey D. Dragon, Monkey D. Garp. The D. carriers are considered "sworn enemies of gods" (the Celestial Dragons), stemming from Queen Nefertari D. Lili who scattered the Poneglyphs as an act against the World Government's founders.

### Laugh Tale & The One Piece
The final island of the Grand Line, reachable only by following all four Road Poneglyphs. Gol D. Roger and his crew are the only ones to have reached it. When they arrived, they laughed — hence "Laugh Tale." Roger left behind everything there, calling it "One Piece." Nobody alive knows what it is, though Roger, Rayleigh, and the crew know the full truth of the world.

### The Sea Level Rise
During the Void Century, global sea levels rose 200 meters, submerging the ancient continents. Vegapunk concluded this was impossible through natural means — the Ancient Weapons caused this man-made catastrophe. The current islands are merely ancient mountaintops.

### Rocks D. Xebec & God Valley
The most dangerous pirate before Roger. Captain of the Rocks Pirates — a crew that included young Whitebeard, Big Mom, Kaido, and Shiki. Defeated at God Valley 38 years ago by the combined forces of Gol D. Roger and Monkey D. Garp. The island was erased from maps.

### The Eleven Supernovas / Worst Generation
Eleven rookie pirates who arrived at Sabaody Archipelago with bounties over 100 million Berries. Plus Blackbeard as the "twelfth." They're the generation that will reshape the world — and they're doing exactly that.
