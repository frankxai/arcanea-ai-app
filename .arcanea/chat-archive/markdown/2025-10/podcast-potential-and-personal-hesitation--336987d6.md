**Claude**:

Alright, let's get practical and strategic then.

**First, validate the opportunity cost:**
You have a genuine asset (voice + expertise + perspective people want). NOT leveraging this means leaving money, influence, and career optionality on the table. That's a real cost, especially if you're in a competitive field or building toward something bigger.

**Address the blockers systematically:**

**1. The "what to talk about" problem:**
- Spend 2 weeks documenting: What do people already ask you about? What questions come up repeatedly in your work? What misconceptions do you see constantly?
- That's your content map. You don't need original ideas – you need to articulate what you already know in a way that helps people.
- Format: Start with a simple framework like "3 common mistakes in [your field]" or "How I think about [topic]" – structure creates constraint, constraint creates clarity.

**2. The confidence/comfort issue:**
- This improves primarily through repetition, not mindset work. Record 10 practice episodes that no one hears. You'll hate the first 5, tolerate 6-8, and by 9-10 you'll find your rhythm.
- Reframe: You're not performing, you're having a conversation with one person who needs your help. Lower the stakes.
- Consider starting with written content (LinkedIn posts, newsletter) first – lets you develop your voice with editing capability before going live audio.

**3. The fame/peace tension:**
- You can absolutely build a meaningful platform while maintaining boundaries. Strategies:
  - Don't show your face (voice-only podcast)
  - Niche down hard (be known in your industry, not to randos on the street)
  - Don't do social media engagement (publish and ghost)
  - Batch content quarterly so it's not consuming your daily life
  - Build for "respected expert" not "influencer"

**Tactical starting point:**

Start with the **absolute minimum viable version**:
- Monthly 15-minute solo podcast on one specific topic in your expertise
- Or: Write one LinkedIn post per week for 12 weeks, see what resonates
- Or: Do 3 guest appearances on others' podcasts (less work, tests the waters)

Pick ONE of those, commit to 3 months, then evaluate: Is this energizing or draining? Is the response worth the effort?

**The real question:** Are you afraid of failure (starting badly) or afraid of success (it taking over your life)? Because those need different solutions.
