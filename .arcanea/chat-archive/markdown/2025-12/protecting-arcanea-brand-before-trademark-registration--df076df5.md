## The Arcane vs Arcanea Question

**The good news:** "Arcanea" is sufficiently distinct from "Arcane" for several reasons:

1. **Different spelling and pronunciation** - Arcanea (ar-kah-NAY-ah) vs Arcane (ar-KAYN)
2. **Different category usage** - Riot's "Arcane" is specifically their Netflix series/entertainment property. Your Arcanea is a mythology/world-building universe with different characters, lore, and purpose
3. **Generic root** - "Arcane" itself means "mysterious/secret" - it's a common English word. No one owns the concept. Your Arcanea is a coined proper noun for a specific fictional world

**The realistic risk:** Low. Riot would need to prove consumer confusion AND that you're competing in their space. You're building original mythology, not League of Legends content. AI image generators pulling Arcane aesthetics is an AI training data issue, not a trademark issue on your end.

## Smart Moves Before Formal Trademark

You can establish priority rights **right now** through documented use:

1. **Date-stamp everything** - Keep records of your first social posts, content, domain registrations
2. **Use the ™ symbol** - You can use Arcanea™ immediately (no registration needed). This signals intent to claim rights
3. **Document your original creation** - Your Guardian lore, Vel'Tara beasts, harmony frequencies, chakra alignments - all unique to you
4. **Grab the domains** - arcanea.com, arcanea.ai, etc. if you haven't already
5. **Register socials** - Lock in @arcanea handles across platforms

## Traffic First or Trademark First?

**My take: Traffic first, trademark soon after.**

Here's why:
- Common-law trademark rights begin with **first use in commerce** - your social posts count
- Formal registration takes 8-12 months anyway, so start the process while building
- The real protection comes from being the *obvious* original creator with documented history
- Someone "fcking you over" would need to prove *they* had prior use - which they can't if you're documenting now

**The play:**
1. **Now:** Document everything, use ™, secure handles/domains
2. **Next 1-2 months:** Build traffic, establish presence, maybe early NFT drops
3. **When revenue hits:** File trademark registration (locks in your priority date retroactively to first use)

## One Caution on NFTs

If you're considering NFT sales, do those *after* at least filing your trademark application. NFT space has more IP disputes, and having "application pending" status gives you legal standing if someone copies you.
