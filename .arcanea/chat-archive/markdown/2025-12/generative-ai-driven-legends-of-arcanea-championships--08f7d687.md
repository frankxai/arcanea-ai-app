## The Three New Screens

**1. Vision/Battle Split (`arcanea-vision-battle.jsx`)**
- Left side: VISION - contemplative, write your intention, provocations like "What if AI art became the primary way humans process grief?"
- Right side: BATTLE - join sprints, see live challenges, your rank
- Center connection point - they're ONE experience

**2. The Sprint (`arcanea-sprint.jsx`)**
- MASSIVE timer (180px, fills the screen)
- Your intention visible throughout
- Turns orange at 5 min, red at 1 min
- Contextual encouragement changes with time
- Pure focus mode

**3. Results (`arcanea-results.jsx`)**
- Your placement with trophy animation
- Community feedback on your creation
- Reflection prompt: "Did your creation capture this intention?"
- XP, streak, stats
- Loop back to next sprint
