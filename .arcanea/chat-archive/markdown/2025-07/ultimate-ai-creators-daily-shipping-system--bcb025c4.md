📅 2. Weekly Rhythm: The 5–1–1 Model

Day	Focus Area	Deliverable Output

---

⚡️ 3. Daily “Ship Ritual” (30–90 min)

🕗 Morning (Post-Meditation, Pre-Meetings) or 📱Evening (Phone-Based Flow)

1. Spark (10 min)

“What truth or insight moved me today?”

“What’s working in my mind right now?”

Quick brain dump or voice note.


2. Build or Publish (20–60 min)
Choose one of the following:

🔧 Ship: new product feature, code, post, video, landing page

📤 Share: X post, LinkedIn, newsletter excerpt

🧪 Test: user feedback, product demo, poll, image gen


3. Archive + Tag (5 min)

Add it to Notion / GitHub / Vault

Tag it to connect to your personal knowledge graph
e.g. #arcanea #frankx #visiondrop #mvp
