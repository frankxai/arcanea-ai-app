### **2. Multi-Agent Architecture Best Practices**
Based on the research, here are the essential patterns for your setup:

**Hierarchical Agent Structure:** Use a supervisor agent to coordinate specialized agents rather than having them all communicate directly. This prevents chaos and maintains clear control flow. The most effective approach is breaking your system into specialized teams managed by individual supervisors.

**State Management:** Most successful developers use a shared message list where agents only add their final messages, not intermediate tool calls. This keeps the context clean while maintaining collaboration.

**Error Handling & Monitoring:** Focus on end-state evaluation rather than turn-by-turn analysis. Evaluate whether agents achieved the correct final state, not whether they followed a specific process, as agents may find alternative valid paths to the same goal.
