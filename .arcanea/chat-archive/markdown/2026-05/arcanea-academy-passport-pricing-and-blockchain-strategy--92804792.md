Don't hold inference cost at all. Ship the reviewer *as a prompt the student runs on their own agent*. You write the rubric-as-prompt once; their Claude Pro / Cursor / API pays for every grading pass forever. The free layer then contains only zero-marginal-cost goods (brief, build prompts, self-review prompt, showcase, template repo). Your marginal cost on a free participant goes to literally zero.

This also reframes what you're selling. Feedback is the product, proof is the flywheel, access is the ceiling. The certificate costs you nothing and is worth nothing externally until Arcanea has reputation — so **give the certificate away and sell the profile**.
