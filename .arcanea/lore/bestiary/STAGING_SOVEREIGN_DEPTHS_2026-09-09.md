# The Sovereign Depths — staged collection index

Status: STAGING collection, with explicitly EXPERIMENTAL records and narrative links. No locked canon changes. New claims originate in this collection, not in the referenced canon baseline.

This direction treats impossible life as physically credible, culturally specific and consequential. Habitat, anatomy, motives, faction, encounter function and story role vary independently. The Ten Gates remain available context; equal Gate allocations, a universal region palette and universal moral lessons are not requirements for this collection. Independent wrongdoing that conflicts with a locked foundation remains EXPERIMENTAL until deliberately ratified.
