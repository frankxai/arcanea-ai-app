# MasterFaction OS — How Arcanea Factions Generate Human Story

> **Status:** STAGING — not LOCKED. Does not replace CANON_LOCKED.
> **Purpose:** One governing system so every faction, crew, order, and academy feels inevitable, costly, and alive.

## 1. Definition

**MasterFaction** is the meta-architecture that turns Arcanea’s locked cosmology into *lived politics and intimate drama*.

It is:

- a **layer model** (who you are, where you train, who you serve, who you travel with);
- a **pressure model** (what every group wants, fears, and will not do);
- a **story interface** (how those pressures reach a reader’s body).

It is not:

- a twelfth Origin Class;
- a single ruling cabal;
- permission to invent new primordials or rewrite Malachar.

## 3. The seven layers of belonging

A character may sit in multiple layers. Story starts when layers pull opposite directions.

| # | Layer | Question it answers | Examples (existing) |
|---|--------|---------------------|---------------------|
| L1 | **Origin** | What made your power extraordinary? | Arcan, Gate-Touched, Awakened, Synth, Bonded, Celestial, Voidtouched, Architect |
| L2 | **Realm / culture** | Where does your body think home is? | Veldoria, Aurevalde, Mar Arcano; bending spheres when Compact is ported |
| L3 | **House** | How were you trained to name yourself? | Lumina, Nero, Pyros, Aqualis, Terra, Ventus, Synthesis |
| L4 | **Institution** | Who claims your duty on paper? | Academies, Starlight Corps, Athenaeum, Forge, Underground |
| L5 | **Order / Guild** | What craft or vow specializes you? | Refracted Light, dragon-rider ethics, maker commons |
| L6 | **Crew** | Who would bleed with you tonight? | Starbound crews, Flagship field team, independent crews |
| L7 | **Compact / League** | When crisis arrives, who must share a table? | Leagues of Arcanea; Confluence Compact (STAGING, port pending) |

**Master rule:** Never introduce a new faction without declaring its **primary layer** and the layers with which it conflicts.

## 4. Faction soulprint (required fields)

Every faction sheet — short or long — must fill this soulprint before visual or power expansion.

1. **Public face** — what a stranger sees in a street parade
2. **Private fear** — the failure that would end them as themselves
3. **Offer** — what they give a protagonist (training, shelter, legitimacy, weapons, silence)
4. **Price** — what they take (registry, obedience, memory, time, bond, pride)
5. **Refuse line** — the act they will not do even to win (if none, they are a cult or a machine — mark it)
6. **Internal fault** — the argument inside the room when doors close
7. **Sensory signature** — smell, sound, cloth, food, light behavior (tie VISUAL_DOCTRINE)
8. **Story pressure** — one recurring scene type they force on heroes

## 5. Pressure physics (story generation)

### 5.1 Conflicting goods matrix

Elite worlds do not run on cartoon evil alone. MasterFaction prefers **goods that cannot all be kept**:

| Good A | Good B | Typical Arcanean collision |
|--------|--------|----------------------------|
| Protection | Freedom | Registry vs Underground |
| Truth | Stability | Athenaeum unsealing vs seal belief |
| Speed | Consent | Forge readiness vs dragon/Bonded ethics |
| Beauty | Surveillance | Prisma craft vs iris monitoring |
| Unity | Personhood | Awakened usefulness vs rights |
| Reform | Faith-in-seal | Fixing institutions may expose their failures |

Malachar is the attractor that turns every unfinished collision into hunger. He is not required on-page every chapter; his *logic* is.

### 5.2 The seven Compact-compatible protocols (story verbs)

When a crisis candidate appears (Avatar, Ascendant, meteor generation, Architect threshold), institutions tend toward these verbs. Use them as scene engines:

1. **Locate** — quiet finding; leak risk
2. **Shelter** — who is protected, who is caged
3. **Train** — who owns the curriculum
4. **Witness** — who records without propaganda
5. **Challenge** — mandatory dissent seat
6. **Anchor** — who stands near high-risk power
7. **Refuse** — consent that can stop a ritual

If a faction has no path to **Refuse**, mark them as authoritarian risk and write the cost.

### 5.3 Scale engines (from FACTIONS, kept)

- Institution → Crew → Sector/Division → Regional squad
- Infinite expansion only through **named crews with fault lines**, not clone armies

## 7. Humanization laws (non-negotiable craft)

1. **Jobs before titles** — a Sentinel has laundry, a debt, a sibling, a favorite bad soup.
2. **Gossip is lore** — what students whisper is more true than banners.
3. **Bodies keep score** — Gate stress is breath, tremor, migraine, appetite, sleep — not only light shows.
4. **Institutions are slow** — paperwork and waiting rooms are weapons.
5. **Love is tactical and tender** — bonds change deployment maps.
6. **Humor is allowed** — without it, cosmic luxury becomes plastic solemnity.
7. **Children and elders exist** — not only chosen teens and demigods.

## 8. Expansion gate (before any new faction)

Before generating a new order, house splinter, or secret society:

1. Retrieve comparative Pattern Cards if available (knowledge-tree / atlas) — abstract patterns only
2. State human appeal retained
3. List protected expression rejected
4. Ground in ≥3 Arcanea canon anchors
5. Add ≥1 structural inversion
6. Fill Faction soulprint
7. Write one 80–150 word pressure scene seed
8. Run Distinctive Bundle + naming collision check
9. Label STAGING; never self-promote to LOCKED
