## The Integration Rule

New magic enters canon only by answering five questions:

1. Which locked or staging source does it extend?
2. What does the practitioner do in the body?
3. What does the world, community, or material pay?
4. Who regulates, fears, teaches, or exploits it?
5. What story pressure does it create that would not exist otherwise?

If a system cannot answer those questions, it remains a visual motif or metaphor, not canon mechanics.

## Arcanean Bending As The Practical Interface

Bending is the practical interface that lets Arcanea's systems operate together on the page, in games, in codex surfaces, and in creator education.

Use this stack:

1. **Bending**: what the practitioner does.
2. **Element/Gate/Color/Material/Bond/Code/Story**: what medium answers.
3. **Cost**: what shifts, scars, remembers, or must be restored.
4. **Teacher**: who trains, regulates, exploits, fears, or protects the practice.
5. **Story pressure**: what conflict becomes possible only because this bending exists.

Core sentence for agents:

> Arcanean magic is bending: embodied resonance shaping through Five Elements, Ten Gates, Prisma, Synthesis, bonds, materials, code, story, and reality architecture.

## Prisma And Color Resonance

Use **Prisma** as the common-language name for color-resonance practice in community/product-facing surfaces. Use **Prism State** for the formal in-world phenomenon. Use **prismwork** or **Prism bending** for trained practice.

Public rules:

- Color resonance is an expression of Gate frequency through perception, light, and body.
- Color does not replace the Five Elements; it reveals fine-grained frequencies inside them.
- Iris corona records cost and overuse.
- Prisma practitioners are watched by House Synthesis and the Order of Refracted Light.
- Any direct external terminology in older staging docs must be rewritten before public release.

## Bending Realms And Institutions

Arcanea can support nations, but they should be Bending Realms and sovereignties inside a layered world rather than the only political map.

Use the staging set from `BENDING_CANON.md`:

- Cinder Courts: Fire, forge, oath, dragon heat-memory.
- Tidebound Cities: Water, memory, healing, archive currents.
- Stoneward Holds: Earth, growth, craft, protection.
- Skyward Freeholds: Wind, voice, travel, weather, song.
- Veilward Sanctuaries: Void/Spirit, witness, death, dream, mystery.
- Prisma Commons: color, light, design, perception.
- Synthesis Cantons: material, metal, biotech, AI, tools.

These realms give readers a strong map while preserving Houses, Academies, Orders, Crews, Leagues, Origin Classes, and Mirror worlds.

## Public/Private Boundary

Public canon may say:

- Arcanea studies real-world myth, religion, philosophy, and ancient texts as Mirror Realm echoes and wisdom lenses.
- Arcanea's creator philosophy teaches imagination, learning, love, agency, and responsibility.
- The Kingdom of Light is within and between worlds as a story frame.

Public canon must not claim:

- that a real religion is literally proven by Arcanea;
- that Arcanea owns or supersedes living traditions;
- that suffering is simply the fault of the sufferer;
- that creators can bypass ethics because reality is imaginative.

Arcanea's philosophical claim is practical: stories train attention, attention shapes action, action shapes reality.
