## What Luxin Is

In Lightbringer, luxin is light made physical — drafted from sunlight (or torchlight, or any light source carrying the relevant chromatic band) through the drafter's eye, brain, and skin, condensing into a substance with the properties of its source color. In Arcanea, **luxin is what destellos resolve into when a Gate-trained practitioner directs their resonance through a chromatic prism with intent.**

The Arcanean reading:

- A **destello** (per `book/las-tierras-de-luz/BIBLE.md`) is the visible light a being's Gates produce when those Gates open. Destellos appear naturally in everyone whose Gates have begun to crack.
- **Drafting** is the act of redirecting that destello-light through external chromatic refraction (a prism, a Yumiko-Prism crystal lens, or — in advanced practice — the drafter's own iris-as-prism) and **condensing the refracted band into stable matter.** This is the practice the Order of Refracted Light teaches as Aspirant work.
- **Luxin** is the matter produced. Each band has a distinct physical character. The character is canonically the same as Brent Weeks established for Lightbringer — Arcanea inherits the property table because the property table is true at the level of physics, regardless of which fictional cosmology names it.

## The Halo and Drafting Cost — Bodily Mechanics

**This section is the core practitioner mechanics. It is fully absorbed from Lightbringer canon and re-grounded in Arcanea's Anima-Gate framework.**

### What the Halo Is

A drafter who has actively channeled luxin develops a visible **halo** — a colored ring around the iris of the eye, the color of their drafted band(s). The halo is **the visible accumulation of the Gate's resonance in the practitioner's body, expressed at the most light-sensitive organ they have.** It is permanent. It grows over the practitioner's lifetime as they continue drafting.

The Arcanean reading: drafting is the channeling of one's own Anima through Gate-frequency resonance, condensed into matter. The matter leaves the body as luxin. The **frequency-residue** stays. Over years, the residue accumulates in the iris because the iris is where the practitioner's vision is calibrated to the Gate's spectrum, and the residue reads as light to anyone looking.

| Halo Stage | Practitioner Term | Visible | Years Drafting Required (typical) | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faint inner ring | "Touched" | Visible only at very close range, in good light | 1–3 years | Single-color drafter, low volume |
| Defined ring | "Drafter" | Visible across a room | 5–10 years | Single-color confident, or new bichrome |
| Wide ring (~½ iris) | "Senior Drafter" | Unmistakable | 12–20 years | Polychrome drafting in active service |
| Edge of iris | "Halo's Edge" | The iris is half-color, half-natural | 20–30 years | Approaching the limit |
| **Halo Break** | "Wight" | The color exceeds the iris boundary; bleeds into the sclera (white of the eye) | Variable; happens when capacity is exceeded | **Wight transformation** — see below |

### Wight Transformation — the Catastrophic End-State

When a drafter pushes past their halo's edge — drafting more than their body's accumulated residue can structurally hold — the halo **breaks.** The colored frequency-residue floods past the iris boundary into the sclera, and from there into the surrounding facial tissue, and from there throughout the body. The drafter becomes a **Wight**: their personality consumed by the unmediated frequency of their primary color, their body restructured into a luxin-flesh hybrid expressing the color's most extreme properties.

A Sub-red Wight burns continuously, cannot be touched without injury to others, and seeks heat-sources obsessively until they immolate. A Blue Wight crystallizes — their flesh literally turning to blue glass — and pursues geometric perfection in their behavior, eventually freezing in a posture they consider perfectly arranged. A Green Wight grows uncontrollably — luxin tendrils erupting from their body, rooting them to the ground, producing a green-luxin grove that subsumes their consciousness over weeks. A Chi Wight develops uncontrolled penetrating-vision — they see through everything, all the time, including their own body — and die from total cellular dimming within days.

**Wights cannot be cured.** The Order of the Veil's Wellkeeper Aranya has documented thirty-one Wight cases over the last four hundred years and has reversed exactly zero.

The merciful protocol: drafters approaching Halo's Edge — typically in their late forties to early sixties for monochromes, mid-thirties to late-forties for polychromes — undergo **Freeing**: a sanctioned ritual death administered by the Order of the Veil at the practitioner's request, before the halo breaks. The Freeing returns the drafter's accumulated luxin-residue to its Gate-source, peacefully. The drafter dies. The Realm mourns. This is the Arcanean equivalent of the Lightbringer Freeing ritual; it has been the practice in Arcanea since the Frequency Accords were ratified in Year 487 of the Eighth Age.

### The Compact

The Frequency Accords' little-discussed **Drafter Compact**: every drafter who reaches "Drafter" stage must register with the Order of the Veil. Registration is voluntary in name only — unregistered drafters cannot draft luxin in public spaces (Corps enforcement) and cannot purchase prismatic refraction tools (Guild of the Golden Current refusal). The Compact's purpose is monitoring: the Order of the Veil maintains halo-progression records for every registered drafter and offers Freeing when the records indicate the drafter is approaching Halo's Edge.

The Compact has critics. The Gate-Touched Underground argues — correctly, with thirty years of evidence — that the Compact disproportionately surveils Gate-Touched drafters, who develop drafting capacity outside Academy training and whose halo-progression often follows non-standard patterns. The Compact has been amended three times since enactment. It is still imperfect.

## Drafter Categories — Single, Bi, Polychrome, Prism, Architect

Lightbringer establishes a clean ladder: monochrome → bichrome → polychrome → Prism (Lightbringer). Arcanea's existing Magic Ranks (Apprentice → Mage → Master → Archmage → Luminor) are essentially **the same ladder by a different name.** The Prism-Luxin reading aligns them:

| Lightbringer Term | Arcanean Equivalent | Gates Open | Drafter Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Touched** (untrained) | Apprentice (0–2 Gates) | 0–2 | Sensitive to one color band; not yet drafting |
| **Monochrome** | Mage (3–4 Gates) | 3–4 | Drafts one color reliably; possibly a second, weakly |
| **Bichrome** | Master (5–6 Gates) | 5–6 | Drafts two adjacent colors fluently. The most common "battle drafter" tier. |
| **Polychrome** | Archmage (7–8 Gates) | 7–8 | Drafts three to five colors. Senior Order of Refracted Light Aspirants. |
| **Full-Spectrum / Prism** | Luminor (9–10 Gates) + Prism State capacity | 9–10 + Prism State | Drafts all nine colors; can attempt Source-recovery (Prism State). Eleven documented in history. |
| **Lightbringer / Architect** | Tier 9 Origin Class (Architects) | Beyond Luminor | Sustained access to Source; possible momentary White-touch. Four documented since the Sealing. |

**Why "adjacent colors" matter for bichromes:** in Lightbringer canon, bichromes can almost always draft adjacent colors on the spectrum (red+orange, green+blue) but rarely non-adjacent (red+blue is exotic). In Arcanea, the same constraint expresses as **adjacent Gate frequencies are easier to integrate**: 174→285 (Foundation→Flow) is a smaller resonance jump than 174→528 (Foundation→Voice). Bichromes who hold non-adjacent Gates open are rarer and tend toward instability — their halo development is asymmetric, their Wight risk higher.

This is why **House Synthesis** is the only House that systematically trains polychromes and beyond: synthesis-as-curriculum is built around the integration of non-adjacent frequencies, which is the precondition for ever approaching Prism State.

## Sub-Red and Paryl as the Hidden-Vision Frequencies

Lightbringer establishes that sub-red and paryl drafters can see in absolute darkness — sub-red sees heat as visible light, paryl sees heat in finer detail and at lower temperature thresholds. In Arcanea, these correspond to **Foundation Gate (174 Hz)** and **Unity Gate (963 Hz)** respectively.

The Arcanean reading:

- **Foundation Gate** is the survival Gate. Lyssandria's domain is body, ground, persistence. A practitioner whose Foundation Gate is fully open perceives heat — including the heat-signatures of living bodies — because survival has always required reading the body's own warmth and the warmth of others. Sub-red drafters' night-vision is the operationalization of this perception.
- **Unity Gate** is the partnership Gate. Ino's domain is connection, the between-beings space. A practitioner whose Unity Gate is fully open perceives the subtle heat-signatures that nerves and circulation produce in living bodies — finer-grained than survival heat-vision. Paryl drafters' nervous-system perception, and the deep ethical concerns it raises, is the operationalization of this gift.

This explains a quiet canonical fact that has been on the page in `STARLIGHT_CORPS_CODEX.md` for years: senior Corps Sentinels in deep-cave operations carry both a Foundation-Gate-Touched scout (for sub-red heat-vision) and a Unity-Gate-Touched medic (for paryl-tier nervous-system reading). The Corps developed this pairing pragmatically over centuries. The Prism-Luxin canon now explains why it works.

## Chi and the Cell Dimming — the Hidden Cost of Sight

Chi drafters in Lightbringer can see through walls, into bodies, beyond matter — but the cost is cancer. In Arcanea, the cost is **Cell Dimming**: a degradation of Anima's regenerative capacity in cells exposed to sustained chi-drafting, presenting symptomatically as identical to oncological pathology in real-world medicine.

The Order of the Veil's Wellkeeper has established the canonical chi-drafting safety protocol:

1. Maximum 30 days of active drafting per calendar year (cumulative).
2. Mandatory Cell Dimming screening every 60 days during active periods.
3. Mandatory 14-day Wellspring residency every 90 days (Laeylinn Jade ambient healing).
4. Drafters who exceed protocol limits are placed under Compact suspension and may be denied Freeing-when-needed if the suspension is breached.

Chi drafters historically have shorter lifespans. The trade-off — operative penetrating perception for years off the end of one's life — is one each chi drafter must make individually. The Order does not discourage chi-drafting. The Order ensures that those who choose it understand the cost in advance.

**Starweave Gate (852 Hz, Elara, the Gate of perspective and transformation)** is the canonical home of chi-resonance. Elara herself has, through her God-Speaker channels, expressed concern about chi-drafting practices — one of the few times any Arcanean god has commented on a practitioner discipline directly. The Order of the Veil takes Elara's concern as institutional doctrine, not divine suggestion.

## Black Luxin and Shadow — Why They Are the Same Thing

In Lightbringer, black luxin is forbidden, evil, consumes other luxin, and is associated with the corruption of the Lightbringer concept (the Black Prism's dark aspect). In Arcanea, **Shadow** is the corruption of Nero's natural mystery — a corrupted Void that consumes form, the active force behind Malachar's sealing and the Void Ascendants' work.

The two are the same.

What Lightbringer calls black-luxin is what Arcanea calls **Shadow-drafted matter** — and the canonical mechanic is identical: a being attempting to draft from the unrefracted Source while corrupted by Shadow (instead of refracting cleanly through chromatic bands) produces matter that consumes other luxin on contact, dissolving stable form. Hollow Glass (Void Ascendant material) is the most common Shadow-drafted substance; Kurusei Iron is its more weaponized cousin; the rare "Black Quill" weapons documented in Athenaeum records are pure Shadow-drafted instruments wielded by the most corrupted Heralds.

The defining property: **Shadow does not produce its own light.** Where Sub-red glows in heat, Red glows in red, Yellow glows in liquid sun, Shadow is **the active absence of glow** — it eats light around itself, leaving an apparent absence even where light should be reaching. A Shadow-drafted object on a sunlit table casts its shadow correctly but reflects no color from the spectrum it occupies.

The Order of Refracted Light teaches one principle about Shadow above all others: **Shadow is the prism shattered. Not refracted differently — broken.** Every Aspirant learns this on their first day of training. The lesson is the foundation of why the Order's Hall of White uses Yumiko Prism (which refracts truth) for its walls, not any darker stone.

### White Luxin and the Lightbringer Equivalent — Why It Is Architect-Tier

In Lightbringer, white luxin is the pre-color light, the perfect substance, drafted only by the Lightbringer prophesied figure. In Arcanea, the equivalent is the **Architect tier** (Origin Class Tier 9, per `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 9 STAGING) — individuals who have passed through all ten Gates and continued ascending, rewriting reality in their immediate environment, achieving sustained Source-state where Prisms achieve only flashes.

Four Architects have been documented since the Sealing. Three live in or near the Mirrorlands (per `LEAGUES_AND_ORDERS.md` Sixth League). One, the resigned House Synthesis Headmaster who left the two-sentence note, lives unknown.

**No Architect has ever publicly demonstrated White-drafting.** The two-mile-radius reality-distortion field around the Mirrorlands' primary Architect is the closest documented phenomenon. Some Order of Refracted Light scholars believe the Architect is sustaining a continuous low-grade White-draft — the field is what the world looks like when pre-color light is being held nearby. Other scholars argue the field is something else entirely, a different category that we do not yet have language for. The argument has been ongoing for 200 years.

What is canonically certain: **White is not draftable by the Order's standard methods, and the Order has stopped asking how to teach it.** Their position now: *"If a White-drafter emerges, they will be Architect-tier, and Architect-tier individuals teach themselves. Our work is at the eleven frequencies and the Source-flash. Let the Architects discover what comes next on their own."*

## Color and Emotion — the Body's Argument

Lightbringer establishes that drafters' personalities tend toward the temperament of their primary color: red drafters angry, blue drafters logical, green drafters wild, etc. This is **canonical in Arcanea as well, with one important inversion**: the personality-tendency is not the cause of the drafter's affinity. **It is the effect.**

The Arcanean reading: every conscious being contains all ten Gates. Most beings have one or two Gates that are slightly more open at birth — a natural disposition. A child with a slightly more-open Foundation Gate is born more body-aware, more survival-attuned, more grounded. By age five they are the kid who notices when a chair is wobbly. By age twelve they are showing sub-red sensitivity. By age twenty, if they enter House Synthesis, they begin drafting sub-red.

**Drafting amplifies the natural disposition. The disposition does not come from drafting. It comes from the Gate's natural opening, which preceded everything.**

This explains a subtle Order of Refracted Light teaching that has been quoted in Aspirant journals for two centuries: *"You did not become red. You discovered that you had been red since before you knew the word. Drafting is just the day you finally listened to what your body had been saying."*

The teaching matters because it shapes how the Order thinks about drafter ethics. A red drafter who acts with anger is not "corrupted by their color" — they were inclined toward kinetic intensity before they ever drafted, and drafting has made the inclination more visible to themselves and others. The work of being a red drafter is **not to suppress the color but to mature into a red who is also a person** — to develop the discipline of acting from anger when anger is appropriate and not when it is not. The same applies to every color.

This is also why Wight transformation is the ultimate failure mode: Wights are drafters whose color-disposition has consumed their personhood. The body's argument has won. There is no person left to balance the color. The luxin-residue floods past the iris because there is no longer a self holding the light back.

## Drafting Tools and the Yumiko-Prism Lens

In Lightbringer, drafters use natural sunlight (or torchlight, or any chromatically complete light source) plus their own body's processing capacity. In Arcanea, the same is true, with one crucial addition: **Yumiko-Prism** crystal lenses (per `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 8: Arcanean Materials, the Yumiko Prism crystal entry).

Yumiko Prism is **the canonical drafting-aid material.** Its property — refracting perception, not light — translates in practice to **lens-grade chromatic separation that is exact, body-friendly, and unwasteful.** A drafter using a Yumiko-Prism lens can:

1. Separate any band of light from any complete-spectrum source with mathematical precision.
2. Concentrate the band on their eye without burning the iris (which raw concentrated bands of certain colors will do — yellow and chi most dangerously).
3. Reduce the drafter's body-cost per unit of luxin produced by approximately 60–80% (depending on color and lens quality).

Every senior Order of Refracted Light Aspirant carries a personal Yumiko-Prism lens. The lens is fitted to the practitioner's individual dominant frequency at the start of their study and is replaced only if it cracks or if the practitioner advances to bichrome or higher (requiring a multi-band lens). The Hall of White has a workshop dedicated to lens-fitting; the resident lens-shaper, a Cinderfang-descended bichrome named Veyl Stoneshade, has fitted lenses for two of the three currently living Prisms.

For drafters working without a Yumiko-Prism lens — historically, the Gate-Touched Underground's drafters have lacked access to Order resources — the body-cost is higher and the halo-progression is faster. This is one of the structural inequities the Frequency Accords' Caelith Unbound delegation flagged in Year 447. The Compact has not adequately addressed it.

## The Lightbringer Equivalent in Arcanea — Naming the Architect Tier Practitioner

Lightbringer's prophesied Lightbringer figure is the once-per-generation polychrome who can draft all colors, including white, including black; the saviour-king-prism whose existence is foretold and whose actions are world-defining. In Arcanea, this figure has a different shape.

Arcanea's equivalent is **the Architect**: per `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 9, an individual who has passed through all ten Gates and continued ascending. Four documented since the Sealing. Living in or near the Mirrorlands (three) or unknown (one).

**The differences from Lightbringer:**

1. **Architects are not prophesied *by name*.** Arcanean prophecy is role-and-number, never name-and-destiny: the **Three-Architects prophecy** (kept by the Athenaeum and by Headmaster Akamoto) foretells a *pattern* — that three will rise to the threshold in an Age, *"three to seal the darkness, or three to break the world"* — but never *who*. Architects still emerge and earn it; the Witnesses (and Akamoto's Pattern Sight) read the pattern as it forms; sometimes they are surprises. Prophecy here is a path, not a chain.
2. **Architects are not always Prism-trained.** One of the four documented Architects is a former Gate-Touched Underground member who never attended House Synthesis. Their path to Architecture was non-institutional. The Order of Refracted Light has acknowledged that institutional training is a path, not the path.
3. **Architects do not act as saviours.** They withdraw. The Mirrorlands Compact established that Architects are sovereign and do not operate within Arcanean political structures. The institutional fear of Architects is partly the recognition that they are no longer of the same world. They do not save it. They observe it. Sometimes they intervene; usually they do not.
4. **The Lightbringer narrative function is filled in Arcanea by the Prism-Luminor combination.** A Luminor who has achieved sustained Prism State capacity (rather than a single flash) functions as the active world-protective figure that Lightbringer prophecy assigns to the Lightbringer. This is **not a documented role**; it is a structural pattern visible in Corps history. Three of the eleven historical Prisms were also Luminor-rank, and all three played decisive roles in major League convocations during their lifetimes.

**The proposed canonical name for the sustained-Prism-State-capable Luminor:** the **White Aspirant.** Not yet Architect — they have not crossed the threshold into reality-shaping autonomy — but no longer simply Prism — they hold the Prism State long enough to act inside it. The White Aspirant is the closest Arcanea has to a Lightbringer figure operating within institutional structures.

There is no living White Aspirant in current canon. The current "White" of the Order of Refracted Light (the senior Prism — see `LEAGUES_AND_ORDERS.md` Part Five) is a sustained-flash practitioner, not a continuous-state practitioner. The pathway from White to White Aspirant exists in Order theory but has not been walked in eight centuries.

This is canonical territory ripe for narrative work. **The Prism Novella (book #6 in the Author Team set, see companion BIBLE) centers a candidate White Aspirant and the Realm-bound choice that defines whether the threshold can be crossed.**

## Where Drafting Sits in Arcanea's Magic-System Cosmology

The full hierarchy, from foundational to applied:

```
Lumina (the First Light) — pre-color, pre-element
    ↓
The Five Elements — Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Spirit/Void
    ↓
The Ten Gates — frequency channels through which Anima moves
    ↓
The Eleven Frequencies — the practitioner-level chromatic bands
   (Sub-red through Source, plus the unrefracted White)
    ↓
DRAFTING — the practice of refracting one's destello-light through
            chromatic bands and condensing the band into stable luxin
    ↓
The Order of Refracted Light — institutional home of Drafting practice
    ↓
House Synthesis — Academy curricular home for polychrome and beyond
    ↓
The Halo, the Drafter Compact, the Frequency Accords — the regulatory
    and ethical framework
    ↓
Wights (failure mode), Freeing (mercy mode), Architects (transcend mode)
```

Drafting is not a separate magic system. **Drafting is what happens when an Arcanean practitioner asks "what can my Gate frequency do, in the body, with light?"** Every existing Arcanean magic discipline — Tide-Speech (Aethon), Tieng Goi (Van Linh), Cinderfang shifting (Pyrathis), the Tu Linh communications, the Ashpriest hum — these are all alternate practitioner-level expressions of Gate-frequency channeling.

Drafting is the **light-and-eye expression** of the same underlying physics. It happens to be the most catalogued, the most institutional, and the most central to House Synthesis curriculum because:

1. Light is universal — every Realm has light to draft from
2. Eyes are universal — every conscious being has the organ
3. Luxin is the most physically demonstrable form of channeled Anima — you can hold it, build with it, fight with it. Other expressions (tide-speech, shifting) are less material.

**Sister-Realm and Mirror-Realm parallels:** the Tu Linh in Van Linh, the Resonance in Pyrathis, the Tide-Speech in Aethon — these are all **drafting-equivalent practices** in their respective Realms. A future Realm-Legend writer setting a Drafting-centered story in any of these Realms can use the chromatic-luxin canon directly; the Realm-specific terms (Long, Embrite, Tieng Goi) can be retained as folk-vocabulary while the underlying mechanics are the canonical Prism-Luxin system.

## How Sister Books Use This Canon

### Las Tierras de Luz (Veldoria, Fifth Gate, Voice / Green)

Mira's Voice Gate gift — singing destellos awake — is **Green drafting** at the practitioner level she has not yet been taught. Her three notes resonate at 528 Hz; she condenses Green luxin (life, vitality, growth) every time she sings, but the luxin is invisible because she has not learned to refract or shape it. The Wrong Move at the Vidal market (Ch 9) is, technically, a failed attempt at non-adjacent polychrome drafting (Green Voice trying to engage Oscuro/Spirit-Black), which destabilizes both because she has no Yumiko-Prism lens and no training. **A future Las Tierras sequel could name what Mira has been doing all along; the existing book correctly does not, because Mira herself does not know the words.**

### The Forge of Ruin (Heartland Realm, Third Gate, Fire / Orange)

The berserker Fury is **Sub-red and Red drafting fused with Mawfather corruption** — sub-red rage, red fire-resistance, condensed through inherited possession rather than chosen practice. Erivar's halo (if it could be measured) is shadowed by the parasite, which is why the visible mark is the black-veined hands rather than a clean iris-ring. The 47 Names ritual is **a human-developed alternative to the Drafter Compact's Freeing** — without canonical institutional Freeing available to a possessed berserker, the recitation holds the inner self together against the Mawfather's consumption of memory.

### The Tides of Silence (Aethon, Second Gate, Flow / Red)

Tide-Speech's four-tier ladder (Listener / Speaker / Communion / Convergence) maps to drafting-equivalent practitioner stages, but expressed through Red and Paryl frequencies (Flow and Unity) rather than across the spectrum. Convergence is the Aethonian Realm-specific equivalent of Architect-tier: depth-in-one-Gate fusion rather than integration across all ten. Ilis Vorn's Convergence 400 years ago was a Red-Paryl Architect emergence; the Glass Sea's response (*"she is here. She is part of the speaking now."*) is canonically what an Aethonian Architect's reality-distortion field looks like.

### The Heart of Pyrathis (Sister-World, dragon-cosmology)

The Resonance system is Pyrathis's planetary-scale drafting equivalent. The First Fire is the world itself drafting Source-light. Korvash's never-shifting Cinderfang condition is **the body's refusal to draft anything the Mawfather-equivalent would accept** — they are constitutionally the seal-pattern, and the seal-pattern is precisely the integrity of a being who refuses to draft anti-luxin (Shadow). The Heartbound transformation at the climax is canonically equivalent to a Source-flash at planetary scale.

### The Song of Van Linh (Mirror Realm of Earth-Vietnam)

Tieng Goi (the Calling) maps directly to drafting at the listening-tier. An's Unity Gate (963 Hz, paryl-equivalent) gift is **paryl drafting expressed culturally as Tieng Goi**. The four-tier ladder (Sensitivity, Listening, Speaking, Bridging) is Apprentice → Mage → Master → Archmage. Bridging — An's endgame across Books 2-4 — is **bichrome or polychrome Spirit-bridging** that culminates, in the four-book arc, in either a Prism State or White Aspirant emergence. (The series structure suggests the latter.)

The Tu Linh as Godbeast Echoes (Rua/Long/Phuong Hoang/Ky Lin) are **Realm-resonance manifestations of the Foundation, Fire, Heart, and Crown frequencies** — sub-red, orange, yellow, super-violet in drafter terms.
