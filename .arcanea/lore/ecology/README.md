# Arcanea Ecology Registry

> **Status:** PROPOSAL REGISTRY (with staging-source inventory)  
> **Canon authority:** `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`  
> **Product surface:** `/lore/ecology`  
> **Working doctrine:** Covenant Ecology — wonder is a visible relationship with a measurable cost.

This directory is the source-facing registry for Arcanean flora, fauna, fungi,
colonial organisms, and ecosystem forms. It does not promote anything into
locked canon. At the time of this Wave 01 registry, no botanical entity is
listed in `CANON_LOCKED.md`. Every combined Wave 01 organism record remains
`proposal` until the Creator explicitly promotes it; individually cited source
claims retain their staging or reference posture without promoting the organism.

## Provenance rule

Every record keeps two kinds of statement separate:

- `sourceClaims[]` contains only the smallest claim directly attested by an
  existing Arcanea source, plus its repository path.
- `proposalMechanics[]` contains every new anatomical, metabolic, magical,
  cultural, ecological, or visual extension.

A generated image can illustrate a proposal. It cannot turn that proposal into
a source claim or canon fact.

## Classification guardrails

- Raíz del Velo is mycelial, not a botanical root.
- Coral and Builder Polyps are colonial animals.
- Geological formations do not become forests taxonomically because lore uses
  the visual metaphor.
- Structural colour, fluorescence, reflection, phosphorescence, and
  bioluminescence are distinct mechanisms.
- No glow is accepted without trigger, energy source, signal, cost, ecological
  effect, and failure mode.
- No famous specimen ships without population, juvenile, pollinator,
  decomposer, season, and exploitation consequences somewhere in its graph.

## Promotion gate

Promotion requires: source review, canon decision, biological and cultural
review, rights receipt for public media, accessibility review, and an explicit
update to the locked canon authority. Product polish is never canon evidence.
