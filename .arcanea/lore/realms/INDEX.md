## What is a Realm

A **Realm** is a sovereign land within the Kingdom of Light — bounded by geography, distinguished by its dominant Gate-resonance, and self-governing in the ordinary affairs of its people. Realms are the structural layer of the Kingdom of Light: large enough to carry their own dialect of Veldarín or Aurevaldan, small enough that a single sustained cosmology shapes daily life.

Realms are not nations as a Saga reader from outside Arcanea would recognize. There are no standing armies between them. There are no border treaties. There is, instead, **resonance** — each Realm hums at the dominant frequency of one Gate, and that hum tunes the temperament of the land and the disposition of those born to it.

The Kingdom of Light contains an unknown number of Realms. New ones are still found. Old ones are sometimes lost.

## Settlement Eras (taxonomy)

Every Realm carries its era of settlement in its stone. Era determines how the Arcane flows there, how its people remember (or have forgotten) the First War, and how transparent the land is to those who would read it.

| Era | Definition | Arcane signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Heartland** | Settled before the First War. Carries deep-time wound-memory of Malachar's rising and the Sealing. Arcane flows are dense, layered, sometimes turbulent. | Thick, knotted, reactive |
| **First Settling** | Founded in the immediate aftermath of the Sealing of the Dark Lord. Populated by survivors and the children of survivors. The land remembers the loss. | Solemn, watchful, slow to bloom |
| **Second Settling** | Populated gradually in the four centuries after the Sealing. Carries no wound-memory in its stone. Arcane flows transparently. | Calm, clear, unusually legible |
| **Frontier** | Reached only in the last two or three generations. Resonance still being established. Few formal institutions. | Thin, unstable, fast to change |
| **Fallen** | Once-Realm whose corridor has closed and whose population has dispersed or vanished. Some are remembered; some are not. | Dormant, occasionally still warm |

A Realm's era does not determine its worth. A Heartland is dense the way an ancient forest is dense; a Second Settling is clear the way a high meadow is clear. Each carries what the Kingdom needs.

## Corridor mechanics

Movement between Realms is not by ordinary geography. The Realms are connected by **corridors** — channels of sympathetic Arcane that run beneath ordinary terrain, sometimes through mountains, sometimes under seas, sometimes (it is said) through the dreams of certain trees. A traveler who knows a corridor can walk between two Realms in a fortnight that would take a year by surface.

Corridors are **not fixed.** They move. A corridor that opened in one century can shift course or close entirely in the next. The pattern of these shifts is the subject of long study at certain Houses; no one yet has reduced it to law. What is known:

- **Corridors prefer continuity of frequency.** A corridor between two Realms of the same Gate-resonance is more stable than one between Realms of differing resonance.
- **Corridors close.** A traveler who arrives by corridor may find, years later, that there is no longer a path home. This is the most common form of involuntary diaspora in the Kingdom of Light.
- **Aquifer-corridors do not move.** The deep underground waterways that feed inland seas like Mar Arcano are stable across geological time. They carry water, not travelers — but they carry the resonance of every Realm they pass beneath, which is why a Realm's deep-water signature can be tasted in seas it has never touched on the surface.
- **The Academy Houses (Tier 6) train corridor-readers** as one of their oldest disciplines. A skilled reader can sometimes feel a corridor's drift before it closes. Most do not.

## How sister books extend this tier

When a future book introduces a new Realm:

1. **Author the book's local BIBLE** with the Realm's geography, culture, dialect, fauna, and dominant Gate-resonance.
2. **Promote the Realm to canon** by creating a new file at `.arcanea/lore/realms/<realm-slug>.md`, mirroring the structure of `veldoria.md` and `aurevalde.md`.
3. **Add an entry to the catalogue table above.** Status starts as STAGING; Frank approves to LOCK.
4. **Cross-link from CANON_LOCKED.md Tier 5** in the next canon-update commit.
5. **Verify resonance compatibility.** A Realm under Fifth Gate influence may share corridors with another Fifth Gate Realm but not (without further canon decision) with a Tenth Gate Realm.

The pattern: book-local BIBLE writes the Realm in the voice of its own narrator; canon-tier file writes the Realm in the voice of the world's chronicler. Both are necessary.

## What is NOT a Realm

A Realm is not:

- A **Region.** Realms contain regions (Veldoria contains El Valle de las Piedras Vivas, La Costa de los Mil Reflejos, Las Tierras Altas — these are regions of one Realm, not separate Realms).
- A **House.** The Seven Academy Houses (Tier 6) are institutional, not geographic. House Lumina is a pedagogy, not a place.
- A **Faction.** The Eight Origin Classes (Tier 9) and the Starlight Corps (Tier 9 organizations) cross Realm boundaries.
- A **Plane.** The Ultraworld (Tier 7 staging) is meta-realm, not Realm. A traveler does not corridor-walk to the Ultraworld; they enter through other means.

A Realm has dirt. A Realm has weather. A Realm has people who can be born, raised, and buried within it without ever crossing into another. This is the distinction.

## Cross-references

- **Tier 1 (Cosmic Foundation):** Realms are inside the larger frame of Lumina/Nero. Each Realm hums at one of the Ten Gate frequencies, but every Realm contains all five elements and both primordial aspects.
- **Tier 2 (The Ten Gates):** A Realm's dominant Gate determines its texture. Veldoria's Fifth Gate dominance makes its Anima transparent and its truth-telling unusually direct. Aurevalde's Third Gate dominance makes it kinetic and bright.
- **Tier 4 (The Dark Lord):** Malachar is sealed. No Realm currently catalogued was directly touched by his rising — wound-memory belongs to the Heartlands. Future Realm-Legends in Heartland settings will need to handle this carefully.
- **Tier 6 (Seven Academy Houses):** Houses are institutional, not geographic. A House may have its primary campus in one Realm, but its members come from many Realms. House Lumina's ancestral campus is unconfirmed; canon authors should propose.
