# Aurevalde

> **Realm of the First Settling (estimated) · Third Gate (Fire / Solar / 396 Hz)**
> **Status:** STAGING ⏳
> **Source text:** *Las Tierras de Luz* (off-page only — Aurevalde appears in canon entirely through the memories, gestures, and song of Señor Bela).
> **Canon authority:** `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 5 (Realms). Pure-absence Realm — see Author Guidance below.

---

## Identity

Aurevalde is a sovereign Realm of the Kingdom of Light under the dominant influence of the **Third Gate** (Fire, Solar, 396 Hz — Draconia and Draconis). Faster than Veldoria. Louder than Veldoria. More kinetic. More bright.

Its architecture is **piedra roja** — volcanic rust-red stone that stores the day's solar heat and releases it slowly through the cool of the night. Its markets are louder than Veldorian markets. Its people speak faster, eat hotter, sing higher.

In the Kingdom of Light's catalog of resonances, Aurevalde is the Realm where **the Fire of being is the ordinary register.** A Veldorian visitor would find an Aurevaldan morning exhausting; an Aurevaldan visitor finds a Veldorian morning slow enough to feel the silence between heartbeats.

## What canon attests directly

The whole of Aurevalde's current canon comes from one character's living memory across sixty years of exile in Veldoria. The following is **all** that *Las Tierras de Luz* establishes as fact about Aurevalde. Future Aurevaldan-set work must extend from these seeds without contradicting them.

### The architecture

**Piedra roja** — volcanic rust-red stone. Stores solar heat through the day; releases warmth into the cool of night. Aurevaldan walls are warm to the back at midnight in a way Veldorian piedra viva walls are not. The stone's color is constant across regions of Aurevalde; the variation in mortar (where mortar is used, which is more often than in Veldoria) signals locality.

### The voice

**Faster speech than Veldarín.** Bela sixty years on still occasionally falls into Aurevaldan vowels when she is moved — she lengthens certain stressed syllables and shortens unstressed ones in a pattern that Veldorian speakers find both beautiful and slightly disorienting.

**Aurevaldan speakers understand approximately 70% of Veldarín** without study, suggesting Aurevaldan and Veldarín are in the same Romance family of Arcanea, separated by enough centuries to drift but not enough to lose mutual intelligibility entirely.

### The food

**Bollos de maíz solar** — round flatbreads made from an Aurevaldan grain (canonically named *maíz solar*; species details unconfirmed). The grain does not grow in Veldoria. Bela approximates the recipe with Veldorian grain. The result is **almost right, never quite.** That gap — the failure of the approximation across sixty years — is one of the book's quiet recurring textures.

### The gesture

**Touching her own chest before reaching toward someone.** Aurevaldan body-language for *I bring only what I have.* Bela does this unconsciously, especially in moments where sixty years of Veldorian acclimation cannot suppress the deeper habit. In Chapter 11 of *Las Tierras de Luz* the gesture appears at a load-bearing moment of recognition; Bela does not know she has done it.

### The song

**A harvest song of Aurevalde.** The song contains the three notes that Mira sings throughout the novel. The genealogy:

> Bela's Aurevaldan harvest song (carried in memory across the corridor) → Don Emilio caught three notes through an open window of Bela's house in his youth → he hummed them over dough every morning for forty years → the floración azul outside his bakery responded → a small girl named Mira walked past the bakery and absorbed them into her body.

Mira thinks the three notes are hers. They are — but they came from Aurevalde, through Bela, through Don Emilio, through forty years of bread.

The full Aurevaldan harvest song is canon-existent but not canon-rendered. A future Aurevalde-set Realm-Legend may transcribe it.

### The cosmology

Aurevaldans are familiar with the destellos. Bela saw one fully at twenty in Aurevalde — a neighbor's infant named **Serena**. Her family called her tired and imaginative. She left.

What this attests: **first-witnesses occur in Aurevalde** (the Realm produces seers natively, not as imports). What it leaves open: **whether Aurevaldan culture has names for the destellos**, whether there are practitioners who understand them, whether there is any Aurevaldan equivalent to the valley grandmothers' oral cosmology of the chispas. Bela's family rejecting her seeing suggests the cosmology, if it exists, is not common knowledge.

### The corridor

**A transit corridor connected Aurevalde to Veldoria.** Bela arrived via this corridor sixty years ago. The corridor has since shifted course; she cannot return. She does not know whether the corridor still exists at all, only that she cannot find it from her end.

**Corridors prefer continuity of frequency** (per `INDEX.md`). The Aurevaldan Third Gate (Fire) and Veldorian Fifth Gate (Voice) are not adjacent in Gate sequence — the Aurevalde-Veldoria corridor was less stable than a same-Gate corridor would have been. This is a possible explanation for its drift.

## What canon implies (cautious extrapolation)

The following is **soft canon** — extrapolated from the seeds above for author guidance, but unwritten. A novel that contradicts the items below should propose changes through staging.

- **Aurevaldan markets are louder than Veldorian markets.** The Realm's Fire-resonance favors action over stillness. A bargaining call in an Aurevaldan plaza is direct, percussive, often sung.
- **Aurevaldan ceremonies cluster at solar noon** rather than dusk. Where Veldoria's *hora de los destellos* is the cultural anchor, Aurevalde's *hora de Lumina* (midday — full form, full presence) is. Communal meals are eaten at noon under shaded plazas.
- **Aurevaldan Anima signature is bright, fast, demanding.** Objects acquire destellos quickly through use, but require frequent attention to retain them. A Veldorian piedra viva pendant worn across a lifetime gathers Anima slowly and steadily; an Aurevaldan piedra roja pendant gathers it faster but loses it if set aside for a season.
- **Aurevalde may carry First-Settling wound-memory** in its Heartland equivalents. The Realm's Fire-resonance could either insulate it from the Sealing's solemnity (Fire transforms grief into action) or amplify it (Fire remembers heat). Canon has not decided. A Realm-Legend writer choosing Aurevalde must make this choice deliberately.

## Author guidance

Aurevalde is **pure-absence canon** within the *Las Tierras de Luz* novel. This is a deliberate craft decision. The Realm gains weight through what Bela cannot return to, what she cannot reproduce, what she carries in her body without being able to fully share it. To write Aurevalde directly is to risk dispelling that weight.

When extending Aurevalde:

- **Do not retroactively explain the corridor's drift in Las Tierras chapters.** The drift is part of Bela's wound; explaining it lifts the wound. Future books may reveal mechanism in their own contexts.
- **Honor the food, the gesture, the song.** Anything you write about Aurevaldan culture must be consistent with: warm flatbreads from a grain that does not grow in Veldoria, the chest-touch gesture meaning *I bring only what I have*, the harvest song carrying three load-bearing notes.
- **Aurevaldan dialogue convention is not yet locked.** Veldorian uses Spanish raya. Aurevalde could use raya, could use a different convention entirely (a sigil, a direct quoted form, an unmarked free-indirect render). Choice is open and will be locked by the first Aurevalde-set work.
- **Bela is canonical Aurevaldan diaspora.** Any new Aurevaldan-born character must not contradict her: her speech patterns, her recognition of destellos as a Realm-known phenomenon, her family's rejection of her seeing.
- **The harvest song.** If a writer chooses to render the full Aurevaldan harvest song in canon, the three notes Mira sings throughout *Las Tierras de Luz* must occur within it. They are the song's most load-bearing motif — likely the closing phrase of each stanza, given the way Don Emilio's morning version closed his hum.
- **First-witnesses occur natively in Aurevalde.** Bela was one. There must have been others before her and after her. Their fate (encountered with hostility, or quietly, or with reverence in some sub-region) is open canon.

When introducing an Aurevaldan region, settlement, or institution:

- **Propose a name in Aurevaldan.** Romance family, faster cadence than Veldarín, more sibilants and more rolled R's. Hard not-yet-canonized. Consult the Lore Master.
- **Place it relative to existing Aurevaldan canon.** Where is Bela's home village? Where did her family live? Where is the harvest song native? Where was Serena (the infant Bela saw at twenty) born? Each of these is open and the first answer becomes canon.
- **Choose its Settlement Era explicitly.** The Realm is currently dated *First Settling (estimated)*. A regional novel may refine — perhaps a particular Aurevaldan city is itself a Heartland enclave, or a frontier outpost.

## Open canon questions

These are spaces for future works to extend Aurevalde:

1. **What is the name of Bela's home village?** Open.
2. **What happened to Serena?** Bela saw her destello at twenty. Did Serena's life confirm Bela's seeing? Did Serena become a known seer in Aurevalde, or did she live an ordinary life with her Gate quietly open?
3. **Are there Aurevaldan first-witnesses living today?** Statistically there must be. Whether they are recognized in their society is unknown.
4. **What is the Aurevaldan equivalent of Veldoria's grandmothers?** Who carries the cosmology of the chispas and the loving dark? Is there an Aurevaldan oral myth, or has the Realm's Fire-temperament moved its cosmology into temple, ceremony, or written text?
5. **What Realms border Aurevalde via stable corridors?** At least one corridor existed (to Veldoria) and is now lost. Others must exist or have existed. A future Aurevalde-set work may catalogue.
6. **What did the Aurevaldan corridor's shift signify?** Was it a normal drift, or was it a response to a larger event? Bela's exile is the only canon datum; broader Realm-history is open.
7. **Where is Aurevalde geographically in relation to the Heartlands?** Is Aurevalde itself a Heartland Realm by virtue of First Settling, or is it a peripheral First-Settling Realm at the Kingdom of Light's solar margin?
