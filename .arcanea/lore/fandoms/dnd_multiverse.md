## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Creator**: Gary Gygax & Dave Arneson (Extended by Ed Greenwood, Jeff Grubb, Zeb Cook)
*   **Core Medium**: Tabletop Role-Playing Game (TTRPG - *Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual*)
*   **Scale**: The multiverse, comprising the Material Plane (worlds like Toril, Oerth, Krynn) and numerous Outer/Inner planes connected by the Astral and Ethereal seas.
*   **North Star**: Collaborative storytelling and progression-by-achievement. D&D's structure of class levels, armor class, and spell slots is the mechanical foundation of modern fantasy gaming.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
D&D's standard cosmology is known as the **Great Wheel**:
*   **The Material Plane**: The physical worlds where mortals live (Toril, Greyhawk).
*   **The Inner Planes**: The elemental foundations (Elemental Planes of Fire, Water, Earth, Air) surrounding the Material Plane.
*   **The Transitive Planes**: The Astral Plane (sea of thought and travel, connecting Outer Planes) and the Ethereal Plane (a ghostly overlay of the Material Plane).
*   **The Outer Planes**: Seventeen realms arranged in a ring based on moral alignments:
    *   *Mount Celestia* (Lawful Good - peak of virtue and light).
    *   *The Abyss* (Chaotic Evil - infinite layers of raw demonic fury).
    *   *The Nine Hells (Baator)* (Lawful Evil - structured, devilish bureaucracy).
    *   *Limbo* (Chaotic Neutral - raw, changing elemental chaos).
*   **Sigil (The City of Doors)**: A ring-shaped city sitting atop the Infinite Spire at the center of the multiverse, containing portals to every plane.

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
Magic is divided into Divine (granted by gods/nature) and Arcane (manipulated through study or bloodlines):
*   **Vancian Spellcasting**: Magic users memorize or prepare spells in advance. Spells are catalogued by levels (0-9) and cast using finite **Spell Slots** per day.
*   **The Weave**: An interface between raw magic energy and mages, maintained by Mystra (the Goddess of Magic). Bending the Weave allows spells to form; tearing it causes dead magic zones.
*   **Spell Schools**: Evocation (damage/elements), Abjuration (defense), Conjuration (summoning), Divination (sight), Enchantment (charm), Transmutation (alteration), Illusion (deception), Necromancy (life/death).
*   **Class/Level Progression**: Characters start at Level 1 (Apprentice) and gain experience points (XP) to reach Level 20 (Demi-god status), unlocking subclass paths, feats, and hit points.

## 4. Linguistic Atlas & Sound Palettes

### Draconic Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Guttural, harsh consonants, containing double vowels.
*   *Key Roots / Examples*: Bahamut, Tiamat, Toril, Karsus.

### Abyssal/Infernal Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Guttural, heavy use of hard 'x', 'z', and 'k' sounds.
*   *Examples*: Demogorgon, Orcus, Asmodeus, Zariel.

### Sylvan Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Gentle, nature-focused, flowing syllables.
*   *Examples*: Corellon Larethian, Titania, Silvanus.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Concepts & Artifacts
*   **The Weave**: The magical conduit that wraps around Toril. Created by Mystra, it allows spellcasters to safely access raw magic. When it collapses, magic fails entirely.
*   **The Lady of Pain**: The enigmatic ruler of Sigil. She maintains the city's portals, prevents gods from entering, and flays or traps her enemies in dimensional Mazes.
*   **Spelljammer**: A magical ship capable of sailing through wildspace (the void between star systems) and entering the phlogiston.
*   **Bag of Holding**: A common magical pouch connected to a pocket dimension, allowing users to carry massive weight easily.
*   **Deck of Many Things**: A legendary card deck. Drawing a card can grant immense wealth and power, or immediately trap the drawer's soul in a void.
*   **Eye and Hand of Vecna**: Corrupted artifacts of the lich god Vecna. Grafting them onto one's body grants supreme dark magic at the cost of alignment and soul.
*   **Spell Slots**: The mechanical representation of a wizard's mental stamina to hold and release specific spell formulas.

## 6. Core Factions & Organizations
*   **The Harpers**: A semi-secret organization dedicated to preserving historical lore, balancing power, and aiding the weak (similar to the Starlight Corps).
*   **The Zhentarim (The Black Network)**: A shadow cartel focused on wealth, mercenary work, and political control.
*   **The Red Wizards of Thay**: An evil magocracy specializing in necromancy and magic item commerce (corrupted Arcans).
*   **The Lords' Alliance**: A coalition of rulers from major cities to defend against existential threats (similar to the Leagues of Arcanea).
