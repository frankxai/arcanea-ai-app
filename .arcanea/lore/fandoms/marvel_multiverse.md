## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Creator**: Stan Lee, Jack Kirby, Steve Ditko (Extended by Jonathan Hickman, Kevin Feige)
*   **Core Medium**: Comics, Film, and Television (*Marvel Comics, Marvel Cinematic Universe*)
*   **Scale**: The Multiverse, comprised of infinite alternate dimensions and timelines (Earth-616, Earth-1610), oversaw by cosmic entities and organizations.
*   **North Star**: Collaborative science-fantasy. The Marvel Multiverse blends street-level heroics (X-Men, Spider-Man) with cosmic reality-bending (Infinity Stones, TVA, Celestials) to create a giant, interconnected sandbox of teams, conflicts, and events.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
The Marvel Multiverse operates on multiple spatial and temporal dimensions:
*   **The Multiverse**: Infinite parallel Earths representing different timelines (branching paths).
*   **The Mirror Dimension**: An exact spatial overlay of the physical world where magic can be cast without affecting physical reality.
*   **The Astral Plane**: A realm of mind and spirit, independent of physical bodies.
*   **The Quantum Realm (Sub-Atomica)**: A subatomic dimension where space and time are irrelevant, serving as a corridor between timelines.
*   **The Void at the End of Time**: A dump realm where pruned timelines are sent.
*   **The Dark Dimension**: A corrupted, timeless void ruled by Dormammu (corrupted Void/Shadow parallel).

---

## 3. Magic & Cosmic Power Mechanics
Magic and power are cosmic, elemental forces that can be tapped:
*   **Eldritch Magic**: Channeled by Sorcerers (Kamar-Taj). Uses hand signs and runes to draw energy from other dimensions (e.g., the Vishanti) to conjure shields, portals, and energy whips (structured Arcane/Gates).
*   **Chaos Magic**: Reality-warping magic wielded natively by the Scarlet Witch, which does not require runes or channeling (Gate-Touched/Architect level).
*   **The Infinity Stones**: Six singularities that represent core cosmic elements, controlling different vectors of reality:
    *   *Space Stone* (Blue - Teleportation/dimensional corridors)
    *   *Time Stone* (Green - Time manipulation/Chronology)
    *   *Reality Stone* (Red - Matter alteration/illusion)
    *   *Power Stone* (Purple - Raw kinetic/elemental energy)
    *   *Mind Stone* (Yellow - Telepathy/consciousness)
    *   *Soul Stone* (Orange - Life/soul manipulation)

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Concepts & Artifacts
*   **Infinity Gauntlet**: A gold gauntlet created by Eitri the Dwarf, capable of channeling the combined power of all six Infinity Stones, giving the wearer total control over reality.
*   **The Sacred Timeline**: A stabilized timeline monitored by the TVA to prevent the birth of branching realities that could lead to a multiversal war.
*   **Pruning**: The act of erasing a branching timeline or individual using a Reset Charge or TemPad, sending the erased contents to the Void at the End of Time.
*   **Nexus Event**: A moment in time where an individual deviates from their scheduled path, creating a branch in the timeline (Veil fracture).
*   **Kamar-Taj**: A secret temple in Kathmandu, Nepal, serving as the headquarters and training ground for the Masters of the Mystic Arts.
*   **The Vishanti**: A trinity of cosmic magic gods (Agamotto, Hoggoth, Oshtur) who act as patrons to the Sorcerer Supreme, granting them magical spells.
*   **Sling Ring**: A small two-finger ring used by sorcerers to open portals between spatial locations and dimensions.

---

## 6. Core Factions & Organizations
*   **The Avengers / Defenders**: Operational squads of heroes gathered to protect the earth (flagship Starbound Crews).
*   **Masters of the Mystic Arts**: The order of sorcerers operating from Kamar-Taj and three Sanctums (New York, London, Hong Kong) protecting Earth's dimensional boundaries.
*   **The Time Variance Authority (TVA)**: A bureaucratic organization tracking timeline integrity, pruning branches that stray from the Sacred Timeline (temporal Starlight Corps).
*   **The Brotherhood of Mutants / X-Men**: Identity-based groups of mutants born with the X-Gene, representing spontaneous, untuned magic (Gate-Touched).
