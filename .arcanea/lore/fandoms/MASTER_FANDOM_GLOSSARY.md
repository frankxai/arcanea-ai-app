## 3. Linguistic Atlas & Sound Palettes
How the masters created phonetic consistency. Creators can use these sound rules in Arcanea's naming engine to generate original, non-infringing names:

### High Fantasy Flowing (Tolkien Elvish / Sylvan)
*   **Phonotactics**: Voiced fricatives (v, z), liquid consonants (l, m, n, r), long open vowels (ae, ea, ie). Consonant clusters are restricted to soft transitions.
*   **Rules**: Avoid hard plosives (g, k, p, t) at the end of syllables. Syllables must glide.
*   *Genius Name Generator Target*: **Thal'Maris, Elariel, Lúmendell, Vaelith**.

### Whimsical Scholastic (Rowling / British Folklore)
*   **Phonotactics**: Alliterative consonants (S.S., M.M.), double consonants (tt, ll, gg), and old English compound descriptors.
*   **Rules**: Combine an old Germanic or Latin root with an ordinary object.
*   *Genius Name Generator Target*: **Hogsmeade, Silvermere, Cinderwall, Otome**.

### Rugged Epic (Skyrim Nordic / Khuzdul)
*   **Phonotactics**: Unvoiced plosives (k, p, t), harsh fricatives (kh, th), consonant clusters at endings (ld, rth, nd).
*   **Rules**: Short, heavy vowels (o, u). Compound nouns that indicate action or geographic feature.
*   *Genius Name Generator Target*: **Ironhold, Stonefather, Kaelith, Dragonsreach**.

### Cosmic Technical (Cosmere / Sci-Fi Magic)
*   **Phonotactics**: Short, mathematically clean prefixes, single-syllable root concepts, and hard endings.
*   **Rules**: High occurrence of clean vowel-consonant alternation.
*   *Genius Name Generator Target*: **AonDor, Sazed, Kyuro, Sol**.

To build a massive worldbuilding ecosystem that remains **100% legal, transparent, and non-infringing**, we follow the **Arcanea Moat Protocol**:

### What IS Protected (Avoid Completely):
*   **Expression**: Specific lines of text, copyright-protected maps, artwork, and unique trademarks.
*   **Specific Proprietary Names**: Proper nouns like *Harry Potter, Frodo, Hogwarts, Allomancy, Stormlight, Westeros, Sigil*.
*   **Explicit Character Biographies**: Directly copying the history of Peter Parker or Gandalf.

### What is NOT Protected (Free to Use & Index):
*   **Ideas and Concepts**: Magic schools, elemental magic, value-based sorting hats, magical focus tools, parallel planes, soul-capturing gems, cosmic entities, and timeline branching.
