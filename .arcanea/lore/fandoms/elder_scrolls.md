## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Creator**: Bethesda Game Studios (Key designers: Ted Peterson, Julian Lefay, Michael Kirkbride, Todd Howard)
*   **Core Medium**: Video Games (*Arena, Daggerfall, Morrowind, Oblivion, Skyrim, ESO*)
*   **Scale**: The planet Nirn, focusing on the continent of Tamriel, spanning five major eras of history.
*   **North Star**: Lore as living infrastructure. The universe embraces deep, conflicting accounts, mythologies, and meta-physical concepts (CHIM, Dragonbreaks) that make the world feel ancient and debated by its own inhabitants.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
The Elder Scrolls cosmology is highly structured:
*   **The Aurbis**: The totality of existence, created from the interaction of **Anu (Light/Order)** and **Padomay (Darkness/Chaos)**.
*   **Aetherius**: The magic realm of pure light, containing the stars (which are holes torn in the barrier by fleeing spirits).
*   **Oblivion**: The dark void surrounding Mundus, divided into planes ruled by the Daedric Princes (similar to Void / Nero aspects).
*   **Mundus**: The physical, mortal plane (Nirn). It is held stable by towers (e.g., White-Gold Tower, Red Mountain, Snow Throat) that act as cosmic anchors.

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
Magic is the flow of energy from Aetherius (magicka) into Mundus:
*   **The Practical Schools**: Organized by functional verbs rather than elements:
    *   *Destruction*: Harming targets with elemental force (Fire, Frost, Shock).
    *   *Restoration*: Healing wounds, warding off damage, curing poison.
    *   *Conjuration*: Summoning weapons, armor, or Daedra from Oblivion; raising the dead.
    *   *Illusion*: Manipulating minds (fear, calm, frenzy, invisibility).
    *   *Alteration*: Bending natural laws (levitation, water breathing, shielding, telekinesis).
*   **The Thu'um (The Voice)**: Shouting in the tongue of dragons to rewrite reality. Each shout is composed of three words of power (direct Voice Gate resonance).
*   **Soul Gem Economy**: Capturing the spiritual essence (soul) of creatures in crystals to power enchantments or recharge items (Vael Crystal parallel).

## 4. Linguistic Atlas & Sound Palettes

### Nordic Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Germanic/Scandinavian, short, harsh consonants, compound descriptions.
*   **Phonology**: Uses heavy ending clusters (*-helm, -run, -reach*).
*   *Key Roots / Examples*: Windhelm, Whiterun, High Hrothgar, Sovngarde.

### Dunmer (Dark Elven) Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Guttural, soft but harsh transitions, apostrophe-divided.
*   **Phonology**: Uses sounds like *hn, l'r, m'r*.
*   *Examples*: Balmora, Ald'ruhn, Sadrith Mora, Vivec.

### Dwemer (Dwarf) Sound Palette
*   **Aesthetics**: Guttural, mechanical, long consonant clusters.
*   *Examples*: Arkngthand, Mzinchaleft, Bthanual.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Concepts & Artifacts
*   **The Elder Scrolls**: Artifacts of unknown origin that exist outside of time. They contain all possible pasts and futures. Reading them grants prophetic insight but slowly blinds and drives the reader mad (Source Gate / 1111 Hz).
*   **Soul Gems**: Translucent crystals used to trap souls. Divided into White Gems (for beast souls) and Black Gems (for sentient humanoid souls). Used to enchant weapons and armor.
*   **The Amulet of Kings**: A legendary amulet containing the Chim-el Adabal (a large red soul gem). It holds the souls of the Septim Emperors and keeps the barrier between Mundus and Oblivion sealed.
*   **Mazzahun (The Dragonbreak)**: A temporal paradox where linear time breaks down, causing multiple contradictory timelines to occur simultaneously.
*   **The Heart of Lorkhan**: The physical heart of the god Lorkhan, buried beneath Red Mountain. It was used by the Tribunal (Vivec, Almalexia, Sotha Sil) to drain divinity, before being freed by the Nerevarine.
*   **CHIM**: A state of enlightenment where a being realizes the universe is a dream, but maintains their individual ego, gaining reality-warping power.

## 6. Core Factions & Organizations
*   **The Mages Guild / College of Winterhold**: Institutional organizations dedicated to the academic study of magicka.
*   **The Imperial Legion**: The militaristic force enforcing law and order across Tamriel.
*   **The Dark Brotherhood & Thieves Guild**: Underground networks that operate in the shadows (similar to the Gate-Touched Underground).
*   **The Daedric Princes**: Eighteen powerful beings of Oblivion, representing specific concepts:
    *   *Azura* (Twilight, Prophecy)
    *   *Mehrunes Dagon* (Destruction, Revolution)
    *   *Hermaeus Mora* (Knowledge, Fate)
    *   *Sheogorath* (Madness)
