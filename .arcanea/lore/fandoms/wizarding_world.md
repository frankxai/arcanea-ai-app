## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Creator**: J.K. Rowling
*   **Core Medium**: Literature & Film (*Harry Potter, Fantastic Beasts*)
*   **Scale**: Secret society overlaid on modern contemporary Earth (Hidden locations: Hogwarts, Diagon Alley, Ministry of Magic).
*   **North Star**: Value-based community and academic progression. The Sorting Hat sorting children based on who they *choose* to be is the most viral identity mechanic in modern fiction.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
The Wizarding World operates as a hidden mirror layer on Earth:
*   **The Veil**: The division between the wizarding and Muggle (non-magical) worlds. Maintained by the *Statute of Secrecy* (and magically reinforced by Muggle-repelling charms).
*   **Dimensional Pockets**: Spaces that are larger on the inside (un-detectable by Muggle physics), like Platform 9¾, Diagon Alley, and the Ministry.
*   **The afterlife/death border**: Symbolized physically by the stone archway Veil in the Department of Mysteries (Void Gate corridor).

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
Magic is a genetic trait that must be channeled and refined:
*   **Spell Crafting (Incantations)**: Structured verbal spells derived from corrupt Latin roots. Spells are classified into:
    *   *Charms*: Changing what an object does without changing what it is (e.g., *Wingardium Leviosa*).
    *   *Transfiguration*: Changing what an object is on a molecular level (e.g., turning a teacup into a gerbil).
    *   *Curses/Hexes/Jinxes*: Offensive magic designed to harm or impede. Includes the three *Unforgivable Curses* (Cruciatus, Imperius, Avada Kedavra).
*   **Academic Progression**: Standardized testing levels:
    *   *O.W.L.s (Ordinary Wizarding Levels)* taken in Year 5 (Apprentice/Mage transition).
    *   *N.E.W.T.s (Nastily Exhausting Wizarding Tests)* taken in Year 7 (Master level).

## 4. Focus Tools: Wand Woods & Cores
Magic exists in wizards but is chaotic and weak without a wand to focus it:

### Wand Cores (Elemental Resonance)
*   **Dragon Heartstring** (Fire / Draconis energy): Produces wands with the most power, capable of flamboyant spells. They bond quickly but are the easiest to turn to the Dark Arts.
*   **Phoenix Feather** (Crown / Sol energy): The rarest core type. Capable of the greatest range of magic, but slow to select an owner and highly independent.
*   **Unicorn Hair** (Heart / Maylinn energy): Produces the most consistent magic, least subject to fluctuations or blockages. Hardest to turn to the Dark Arts and extremely faithful.

### Wand Woods (Sensory Affinities)
*   **Elder**: The most volatile wood. Selects only wizards who are superior in power. Fits the Crown/Source gate.
*   **Holly**: Attuned to protective magic. Works best for mages with emotional intensity (Heart Gate).
*   **Yew**: Retains a reputation for dark magic and dueling, but excels at longevity and transformation (Void/Fire Gate).

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Concepts & Artifacts
*   **Sorting Hat**: A sentient wizard's hat containing the minds of the four founders. It looks into a student's thoughts to sort them into one of the four houses based on their traits and choices (Unity/963 Hz).
*   **Horcruxes**: Objects in which a Dark wizard has hidden a fragment of their soul to achieve immortality. Creating one requires a supreme act of evil (murder) which tears the soul (Dissonant / Nero Shard parallel).
*   **Deathly Hallows**: Three legendary artifacts created by Death: the Elder Wand (unbeatable wand), the Resurrection Stone (calls spirits from the dead), and the Cloak of Invisibility (perfect concealment).
*   **Marauder's Map**: A magical map of Hogwarts showing every classroom, hallway, secret passage, and the exact location and movement of every person in the castle.
*   **Pensieve**: A stone basin used to store and review memories. Wizards draw thoughts from their minds as silver threads and pool them in the basin to view them contextually (Tide/Water element).
*   **Time-Turner**: An hour-glass device used for short-term time travel (up to 5 hours). Highly regulated.
*   **Floo Network**: A fireplace transportation network using Floo Powder to travel between grates.

## 6. Core Factions & Organizations
*   **Hogwarts School**: The premier Academy of magic, divided into four houses:
    *   *Gryffindor*: Courage, bravery, daring (Pyros / Fire affinity).
    *   *Slytherin*: Ambition, cunning, resourcefulness (Nero / Void / Water affinity).
    *   *Ravenclaw*: Intelligence, wisdom, curiosity (Lumina / Sight / Wind affinity).
    *   *Hufflepuff*: Loyalty, patience, hard work (Terra / Earth affinity).
*   **The Ministry of Magic**: The bureaucratic governing body that regulates magical law (similar to Sixth Age institutions).
*   **Death Eaters**: Voldemort's faction of pure-blood supremacists who corrupt magic (Voidtouched/Shadow users).
*   **The Order of the Phoenix**: The emergency resistance faction (similar to a Starbound Crew).

## 8. World Engine Template Directives
Creators using the **Wizarding World Template** on `arcanea.ai` adopt:
1.  **Scholastic Progression**: Magic is unlocked through academic years, research tasks, and examinations.
2.  **Focus Tool Dependency**: Characters cannot cast spells effectively without a bonded artifact (wand, ring, staff).
3.  **Hidden World Overlay**: The game/story maps a hidden magical society directly on top of real-world coordinates (Mirror Realms).
