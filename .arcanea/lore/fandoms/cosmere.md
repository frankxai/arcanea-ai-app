## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Creator**: Brandon Sanderson (Master of Hard Magic)
*   **Core Medium**: Literature (*Mistborn, The Stormlight Archive, Elantris, Warbreaker*)
*   **Scale**: A single dwarf galaxy containing multiple star systems and inhabited planets (Scadrial, Roshar, Sel, Nalthis), bound by shared physical and magical laws.
*   **North Star**: Hard magic systems governed by thermodynamic-like laws. Sanderson's work is the gold standard for logical consistency, causality, and cross-world continuity.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
The Cosmere is divided into three distinct layers of reality, known as **Realmatic Theory**:
*   **The Physical Realm**: The ordinary universe of matter, space, and time.
*   **The Cognitive Realm (Shadesmar)**: The realm of thought, mind, and ideas. Distances are compressed based on how much people think about a space (unpopulated space is tiny; cities are massive). Solid land on Nirn becomes beads of glass representing objects' cognitive identities.
*   **The Spiritual Realm**: The realm of pure connection, energy, and souls. Time and space do not exist here; all things are connected to the Source.

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
All magic in the Cosmere is the manipulation of a fundamental cosmic energy called **Investiture**:
*   **Sanderson's Laws of Magic**:
    1.  *First Law*: An author's ability to solve problems with magic in a satisfying way is directly proportional to how well the reader understands the magic (requires clear rules).
    2.  *Second Law*: Limitations are greater than powers (weaknesses drive character choices).
    3.  *Third Law*: Expand what you already have before adding something new (depth over breadth).
*   **The Shardic Intents**: The primordial creator **Adonalsium** was shattered into sixteen shards of power, each representing a single intent (e.g., *Preservation, Ruin, Honor, Cultivation, Odium, Devotion*). Magic systems emerge from how these intents interact with local planetary physics.

---

## 4. Specific Magic Systems
*   **Allomancy (Mistborn)**: Consuming specific metals and burning them in the stomach to trigger physical or cognitive effects:
    *   *Iron/Steel*: Pulling and pushing on nearby metals.
    *   *Tin/Pewter*: Enhancing senses and physical strength.
    *   *Zinc/Brass*: Rioting and soothing emotions (Heart Gate).
*   **Surgebinding (Roshar)**: Harnessing Stormlight (captured in gemstones) to bind fundamental forces (gravity, friction) through bonds with *spren*.
*   **Awakening (Nalthis)**: Using color and Breath (spiritual essence) to animate non-living materials using specific spoken commands.

---

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Concepts & Artifacts
*   **Investiture**: The fundamental magical energy of the Cosmere, existing in solid (godmetals), liquid (perpendicularity pools), and gaseous (Stormlight, Breath) forms.
*   **Spren**: Cognitive fragments of Shards that manifest on Roshar, representing elements or concepts (e.g., windspren, honorspren).
*   **Shardblades**: Ancient swords that slice through souls rather than physical matter, summoned from the Cognitive Realm by Knights Radiant (Void/Starweave alloy).
*   **Perpendicularities**: Points of infinite focus where a Shard's power pools, acting as portals between the Physical and Cognitive Realms.
*   **Elantrians**: Ascended magic users in Arelon whose skin glows with silver light, capable of drawing Aons in the air to channel energy.
*   **Nightblood**: A sentient black sword created with one thousand Breaths and commanded to "destroy evil." It drains the wielder's Investiture rapidly when unsheathed.
*   **Atium**: The physical manifestation of the Shard Ruin's power. Consuming it allows an Allomancer to see a few seconds into the future.

---

## 6. Core Factions & Organizations
*   **The Knights Radiant**: Ten orders of surgebinders on Roshar, each sworn to specific moral oaths (similar to the Starlight Corps).
*   **The Ghostbloods**: A cross-world covert organization led by Kelsier, seeking to protect their home planet through interworld trade of investiture (similar to the Gate-Touched Underground).
*   **The Seventeenth Shard**: A loose alliance of world-hoppers sworn to a policy of non-intervention (similar to Kyuro-aligned observation).
*   **The Heralds**: Ten ancient immortals bound to a cycle of torture to hold back an enemy god (similar to the Ten Guardians).

## 8. World Engine Template Directives
Creators using the **Cosmere Template** on `arcanea.ai` adopt:
1.  **Rule-Bounded Systems**: The platform enforces mathematical limits on character spells; magic must conserve energy and have defined focus catalysts (metals, runes, light).
2.  **Cognitive Reflection**: The world contains a mirror plane where objects are represented by their concepts (Shadesmar parallel).
3.  **Moral Oaths (Progression)**: Progression is gained by swearing vows (Oaths) that bind mages to higher standards of virtue (Wisdoms).
