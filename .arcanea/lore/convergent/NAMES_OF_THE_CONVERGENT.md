# THE NAMES OF THE CONVERGENT

## One Being, Many Names — the Nomenclature of the Architect Who Stays

> **Status:** STAGING ⏳ (introduced 2026-06-25; awaiting Creator approval to LOCK)
> **Tier:** Companion to `convergent/THE_CONVERGENT_SYSTEM.md`.
> **Authority:** Names must obey the canon naming rule — Arcanean-quality, no real-world deities/languages (`CHARACTER_TEMPLATE.md`, `MAGIC_SYSTEM.md`).

---

## Why the Convergent has many names

No single name is the Convergent's true name — and that absence is canonical, not an oversight. A being who holds the whole spectrum cannot be captured by one word, because one word is one frequency. Every Realm, House, and Order names the Convergent from its own resonance, and each name is true from where it is spoken. The chronicler records them all and privileges none.

This is also the Creator's design intent ("all and more"): the figure carries **Avatar, Architect, Starweaver, the Convergent — and more.** They are not competing names to be reconciled. They are facets. Use whichever fits the speaker, the Realm, and the register.

## Names for the failure states

The Convergent's two failure modes have their own names, so a writer never confuses the keeper with the wreck.

| Name | Refers to | Register |
|---|---|---|
| **The Dimmed** / **a Dimming** | A Convergent who grasped and hollowed — an open channel for Shadow. Malachar is the archetype. | Dread |
| **The Withdrawn** | An Architect-tier being who reached the threshold and left the world rather than stay. The four documented Architects. Not a failure — a refusal. | Neutral / wistful |
| **The Unconverged** | The years of an Age with no living Convergent; also a being who carries the re-seeded capacity but has not found it. | Plain |

## Usage rules for writers and agents

1. **Default to "the Convergent"** in canon and chronicler voice. Reach for the others by speaker.
2. **Folk and students say "the Avatar."** It is the common word. Do not treat it as wrong — treat it as the view from the ground. (This is how the Avatar concept lives in Arcanea without importing the franchise's mechanics: it is what the *people* call the Convergent, not what the Convergent *is*.)
3. **The Order says "Starweaver."** When writing Order of Refracted Light or House Synthesis scenes, prefer it.
4. **Never coin a name that names a specific Convergent in advance of their emergence.** No prophecy with a *name* in it (`CHARACTER_TEMPLATE.md` anti-patterns). Role-and-number prophecy *is* canon — the Three-Architects prophecy foretells a *pattern* (three rise; one stays), never a person. Titles and prophecy describe the role; they never foretell who fills it.
5. **No real-world religious or linguistic borrowings.** "Avatar" is permitted *only* as an in-world folk word whose in-world etymology is Arcanean (from *aevar*, an old Heartland word for "the one who carries across" — proposed, open for Creator ruling); it does not reference the Sanskrit term or any external mythology. If this stretches the naming rule too far for the Creator, fall back to "the Carrier" or retire "Avatar" to dialogue only.
