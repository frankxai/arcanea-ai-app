## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: The Hebrew Bible (*Tanakh*), the New Testament, Pseudepigrapha (*Book of Enoch*, *Testament of Solomon*), Dead Sea Scrolls, and Kabbalistic literature (*The Zohar*, *Sefer Yetzirah*).
*   **Scale**: The Seven Heavens, the material Earth, Sheol (the underworld), and Gehenna (the abyss of fire).
*   **North Star**: Absolute hierarchy, divine authority, and the power of the Word. The cosmos is governed by a singular, supreme Creator, whose plans are carried out by terrifying, multi-eyed, burning hosts of angels who command the laws of physics.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
Reality is structured vertically into Seven Heavens and subterranean abysses:
*   **The Seven Heavens**:
    1.  *Shamayim*: The firmament containing the stars and clouds.
    2.  *Raqia*: The heaven where the sun and moon are fixed, and where fallen angels are bound in darkness.
    3.  *Shehaqim*: The heaven containing the mills that grind manna for the righteous.
    4.  *Zebul*: The heavenly Jerusalem, featuring the temple served by Archangel Michael.
    5.  *Ma'on*: The dwelling of choirs of angels who sing praises during the night.
    6.  *Makhon*: The treasury of snow, hail, rain, and judgment.
    7.  *Araboth*: The highest heaven, containing the Throne of Glory (Kisse ha-Kabod) and the supreme light of the Creator.
*   **Sheol / Gehenna**: Sheol is the silent, dusty underworld of the dead; Gehenna is the purging abyss of fire where transgressors are cleansed or bound.
*   **The Garden of Eden**: The terrestrial paradise, guarded by a Cherub wielding a revolving sword of fire.

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
*   **Logos (The Word of Creation)**: The cosmos was spoken into existence. Words and letters are not arbitrary representations; they are the literal molecules of reality. Writing or speaking these letters in specific, mathematical combinations alters physical law.
*   **Names of Power**: The supreme deity possesses multiple names (e.g., the Tetragrammaton *YHVH*, *Adonai*, *El Shaddai*). Invoking or inscribing the 72-letter name (*Shemhamphorash*) releases blinding, unstoppable creation energy.
*   **The Seal of Solomon**: A legendary brass and iron ring engraved with a secret divine name, allowing the wearer to command, bind, and seal angels and demons (Djinns) inside vessels.
*   **Enochian Script (Celestial Alphabet)**: The mathematical, angelic script revealed to Enoch. Carving or projecting Enochian symbols creates absolute, unbreakable barriers (Wards) and gateways.

---

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Celestial Hierarchy & Texts
*   **The Choirs of Angels**:
    *   *Seraphim (The Burning Ones)*: Six-winged spirits of pure fire and love who surround the Throne, chanting the sanctus (Voice Gate).
    *   *Ophanim (The Wheels)*: Terrifying, golden, nested wheels covered in hundreds of eyes that spin alongside the Throne, steering the cosmos (Sight/Starweave Gate).
    *   *Cherubim*: Four-faced guardians of sacred boundaries, wielding elemental force (Earth/Wind).
*   **The Archangels (The Seven Lords)**:
    *   *Michael*: Chief commander of the heavenly hosts, wielder of the solar sword (Forge Gate).
    *   *Gabriel*: Messenger of heaven, wielder of the silver trumpet (Voice Gate).
    *   *Raphael*: Lord of healing, wind, and restoration (Heart Gate).
    *   *Uriel*: Lord of light, wisdom, and the fire of prophecy.
    *   *Metatron*: The Scribe of Heaven, chancellor of Araboth, who translates divine thought into cosmic geometry.
*   **The Book of Enoch**: An apocryphal text detailing the fall of the Watchers (Grigori) who descended to Earth, took human wives, and taught humanity forbidden arts (metallurgy, astrology, cosmetics, magic).

---

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Beasts
*   **Leviathan**: The colossal dragon/serpent of the deep sea. It represents the untamable watery abyss. At the end of time, its flesh will be served as a feast to the righteous (Tide Gate).
*   **Behemoth**: The giant, unshakeable land beast of the dry desert. Its bones are like bronze tubes and its limbs like iron rods (Foundation Gate).
*   **Ziz**: The monstrous bird of the air, whose wingspan can blot out the sun, protecting the Earth from fierce ocean winds (Wind Gate).
*   **Lilith**: The legendary first wife of Adam who refused subjection, fled Eden, and became a demon queen of the night (succubus/shadow wielder).
*   **Nephilim**: The giant offspring of the fallen Watchers and human women. They were titans of physical strength who corrupted the Earth, triggering the Great Deluge.

## 7. World Engine Template Directives
Creators using the **Abrahamic Angelology Template** on `arcanea.ai` adopt:
1.  **The Enochian Ward System**: Magic is highly defensive. Players construct physical "circles of protection" using copper, iron, and Enochian seals. Being inside the circle grants immunity to demonic attacks, but leaving it exposes them to instant curse effects.
2.  **The Solomon Binding Loop**: Demons and elementals cannot be easily killed. They must be weakened, named, and sealed into physical vessels (rings, jars, books) to be summoned later.
3.  **The Divine Commandment (Decrees)**: The world features a "Commandment" system. Taking a holy vow (e.g., Vow of Silence, Vow of Poverty) multiplies magical efficacy by orders of magnitude, but breaking the vow triggers immediate, lightning-strike chastisement.
