## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: The *Poetic Edda* (anonymous collection of older poems), the *Prose Edda* (compiled by Snorri Sturluson, c. 1220), and runestone inscriptions.
*   **Scale**: Nine interconnected realms held within the branches and roots of the World Tree, Yggdrasil.
*   **North Star**: Absolute fate (Wyrd) and honorable struggle. The Norse cosmos is fragile and doomed to end in Ragnarok. Glory is achieved not by avoiding doom, but by facing it with unyielding courage.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
Reality is anchored by **Yggdrasil (The World Tree)** and divided into Nine Realms:
*   **Asgard**: The high fortress of the Æsir gods, connected to Midgard by the Bifrost (a rainbow bridge of light).
*   **Midgard**: The realm of humans, surrounded by the great ocean and the coils of the Midgard Serpent.
*   **Jotunheim**: The wild, rocky home of the Jotnar (giants).
*   **Niflheim**: The primordial realm of ice, mist, and darkness.
*   **Muspelheim**: The primordial realm of fire and soot, ruled by the fire giant Surtr.
*   **Helheim**: The cold, subterranean afterlife for those who die of sickness or old age, ruled by the goddess Hel.
*   **Alfheim**: The luminous home of the Light Elves (Ljosalfar).
*   **Svartalfheim (Niðavellir)**: The dark underground labyrinth of the Dwarves (Dvergar), masters of forge and craft.
*   **Vanaheim**: The ancient forest realm of the Vanir gods (fertility and nature deities).
*   **Ginnungagap**: The vast, empty void that existed before creation, between Niflheim and Muspelheim.

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
*   **Runes**: The cosmic letters of the Futhark alphabet. They are not merely writing, but the structural code of reality. Odin hung himself from Yggdrasil for nine days to win their secrets. Carving runes into wood or stone, then coloring them with blood, activates their power.
*   **Seiðr**: A form of magic associated with weaving fate, seeing the future, and causing illusion or sickness. Primarily practiced by Freya and later Odin, it requires a trance state on a raised platform (*seiðhjallr*).
*   **Galdr**: Vocal, high-pitched chants and verbal incantations used to focus runic magic or cast wards.
*   **Ørlög & Wyrd**: The threads of fate spun by the Norns (Urd, Verdandi, Skuld) at the Well of Urd. Even the gods have their doom written in these threads.
*   **The Mead of Poetry**: A magical draft brewed from the blood of the wise being Kvasir mixed with honey. Anyone who drinks it becomes a skald (poet) or scholar (Voice Gate).

---

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Gods, Texts, and Concepts
*   **Major Deities**:
    *   *Odin (Woden)*: The Allfather. God of wisdom, war, poetry, and runes. He sacrificed his eye at Mimir's Well to drink of wisdom (Void Gate).
    *   *Thor*: God of thunder, strength, and humanity. Wields the hammer Mjöllnir (Forge Gate / Earth).
    *   *Loki*: The trickster giant who was blood-brother to Odin. God of fire, deceit, and change.
    *   *Freya*: Goddess of love, beauty, fertility, and Seiðr magic. Wears the necklace Brísingamen.
    *   *Baldr*: The beautiful god of light and peace, whose death by a mistletoe dart initiates the countdown to Ragnarok.
    *   *Tyr*: The one-handed god of law, justice, and heroic combat.
    *   *Heimdall*: The watchman of Asgard, who guards the Bifrost and can hear the grass grow.
*   **Valhalla & Fólkvangr**: The two halls of the heroic dead. Odin takes half of those slain in battle to Valhalla (the Hall of the Slain) to prepare for Ragnarok; Freya takes the other half to her field, Fólkvangr.
*   **Ragnarok**: The twilight of the gods—a series of apocalyptic battles and natural disasters (including the Fimbulwinter) where Odin, Thor, Loki, and others die, and the world is swallowed by water and fire before regenerating.

---

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Monsters
*   **Fenrir**: The monstrous wolf, son of Loki, bound by the gods with the magical ribbon Gleipnir. He will break free at Ragnarok and swallow Odin.
*   **Jörmungandr (The Midgard Serpent)**: The colossal serpent that encircles Midgard, biting its own tail. At Ragnarok, it will emerge and spit poison into the skies, dying by Thor's hand, who will then walk nine steps and die of its venom.
*   **Sleipnir**: Odin's eight-legged grey horse, born of Loki, capable of running over land, sea, and air.
*   **Valkyries**: Angelic, armored warrior maidens who fly over battlefields to choose who will die and guide them to Valhalla.
*   **Níðhöggr**: The great dragon that gnaws at the roots of Yggdrasil from Niflheim, chewing on the corpses of the wicked.
*   **Jötnar (Giants)**: Primordial beings of chaos and nature, ranging from mountain frost giants to fire lords.

## 7. World Engine Template Directives
Creators using the **Norse Template** on `arcanea.ai` adopt:
1.  **The Rune Scripting System**: Magic is not cast silently; it must be inscribed (carved/bloodied) into physical tools or weapons to take effect.
2.  **Ragnarok Doom Metagame**: The campaign or world has an absolute countdown. The environment becomes colder and more hostile over time, forcing players to prioritize legacy and preparation for the final stand.
3.  **Fatalist Progression**: Characters do not gain health indefinitely; they trade physical integrity for runic wisdom, mimicking Odin's sacrifice.
