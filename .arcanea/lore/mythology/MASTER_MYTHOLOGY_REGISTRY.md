## 2. Comparative Linguistic Palettes
Linguistic guidelines for generating original names that echo these mythologies in Arcanea's name generator:

### Aegean / Classical (Greek Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: High frequency of unvoiced plosives (p, t, k) combined with liquid sounds (l, r), diphthongs (eu, oi, ae), and grammatical suffixes like *-eos*, *-as*, *-on*, *-is*.
*   *Generator Formula*: [Soft Root] + [Vocalic Slide] + [Suffix] (e.g., *Thalassos, Kaeloreas, Zephyron, Aethelis*).

### Boreal / Runic (Norse Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: Heavy, consonant-packed syllables, harsh endings, dental consonants (d, t), and alliterative pairings. High frequency of *g, r, v, th, ld, rn*.
*   *Generator Formula*: [Heavy Root] + [Consonant Cluster] + [Suffix] (e.g., *Grimnir, Vaelgard, Thorgar, Skaldrun*).

### Solar / Nilotic (Egyptian Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: Nasals (m, n), open vowels (a, e, u), clean alveolar stops, and prefixes of divinity (*Ra-*, *An-*, *Os-*). Suffixes ending in clear vowels or *et/ot*.
*   *Generator Formula*: [Divine Prefix] + [Vowel Core] + [Plosive Suffix] (e.g., *Anubet, Rameses, Nekhbet, Atum-Re*).

### Yamato / Kami (Shinto Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: Perfect vowel-consonant alternation (CV), short open syllables, no consonant clusters (except *ts, sh, ny*), and liquid nasal *n*.
*   *Generator Formula*: [Alternating CV Syllables] (e.g., *Amatsu, Inari, Tsukuyomi, Susanoo*).

### Sanskrit / Devic (Hindu Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: Aspirated consonants (bh, dh, gh, th), palatals (j, sh, ch), long resonant vowels (aa, ii, uu), and endings like *-ra*, *-da*, *-ya*.
*   *Generator Formula*: [Aspirated Root] + [Resonant Vowel] + [Soft Ending] (e.g., *Bhadra, Dhyana, Shivatma, Rudraya*).

### Celestial / Enochian (Abrahamic Resonance)
*   **Phonotactics**: Sibilants (s, z, sh), clear glottal stops, double vowels, and the divine suffixes *-el* (of God) or *-iah* (of Yah).
*   *Generator Formula*: [Glottal/Sibilant Root] + [el / iah Suffix] (e.g., *Metatron, Uriel, Samael, Zephariah*).
