## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: The *Rigveda*, *Samaveda*, *Yajurveda*, *Atharvaveda* (c. 1500–1000 BCE), the *Upanishads*, the *Puranas*, the *Bhagavad Gita*, and the epics *Ramayana* and *Mahabharata*.
*   **Scale**: An infinite, multidimensional multiverse containing millions of universes, each cyclical in nature and governed by a local Brahma creator.
*   **North Star**: Cyclical time, Karma (causal moral law), and Dharma (cosmic duty). Reality is *Maya*—a divine play or illusion. The ultimate goal is *Moksha*—liberation from the cycle of reincarnation (Samsara) by realizing the oneness of the individual soul (Atman) with the ultimate cosmic reality (Brahman).

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
Reality is cyclical and divided into nested dimensions:
*   **Brahman**: The formless, infinite, genderless, supreme cosmic reality that underlies all things.
*   **The Lokas (Fourteen Realms)**:
    *   *Satyaloka*: The highest spiritual realm where Brahma resides.
    *   *Svarga (Heaven)*: The celestial realm of the Devas, ruled by Indra, filled with light, music, and divine gardens.
    *   *Bhu Loka (Earth)*: The physical human plane.
    *   *Patala Loka*: The subterranean realms inhabited by the Nagas and Danavas.
    *   *Naraka*: The cleansing purgatories where souls exhaust negative karma.
*   **The Yugas (Cyclical Time)**: Time is divided into four repeating ages:
    *   *Satya Yuga (Golden Age)*: Truth and dharma are absolute (lasts 1.728M years).
    *   *Treta Yuga (Silver Age)*: Virtue declines by one quarter (lasts 1.296M years).
    *   *Dvapara Yuga (Bronze Age)*: Virtue declines by half (lasts 864k years).
    *   *Kali Yuga (Iron/Dark Age)*: The current age of discord, ignorance, and spiritual decay (lasts 432k years).

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
Power in the Vedic cosmos is earned through sound, focus, and self-denial:
*   **Tapas (Ascetic Heat)**: Intense spiritual energy accumulated through meditation, penance, and self-discipline. A mortal or sage with high Tapas can terrify the gods, force them to grant boons, or incinerate mountains with a single thought (Spirit/Forge Gate).
*   **Mantras**: Sacred sonic vibrations that reflect the underlying code of the cosmos. Chanting specific mantras with exact pitch and rhythm alters the physical world, summons gods, or invokes weapons. The cosmic syllable **OM (AUM)** is the primordial vibration of creation.
*   **Astras (Astral Weapons)**: Supernatural weapons summoned via mantras. Each Astra is powered by a specific deity:
    *   *Brahmastra*: The ultimate weapon of absolute destruction. It never misses and incinerates entire worlds, curing the land of rain and fertility for generations.
    *   *Agneyastra*: The weapon of cosmic fire.
    *   *Vayavyastra*: The weapon of gale winds.
*   **Kundalini & Chakras**: The energy architecture of the human body, consisting of seven primary chakras (energy hubs) through which spiritual energy rises to achieve ultimate realization (Crown Gate).

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Gods, Texts, and Concepts
*   **The Trimurti (The Divine Triad)**:
    *   *Brahma*: The Creator, who designs the physical forms of each universe.
    *   *Vishnu*: The Preserver, who descends to Earth in various Avatars (such as Rama and Krishna) to restore Dharma.
    *   *Shiva (Mahadeva)*: The Destroyer and Transformer, the Lord of asceticism, dance, and dissolution (Void Gate).
*   **Other Major Deities**:
    *   *Ganesha*: The elephant-headed god of wisdom, success, and the remover of obstacles.
    *   *Durga / Kali*: Terrifying warrior goddesses who destroy demons that threaten cosmic balance.
    *   *Hanuman*: The monkey deity of devotion, wind-power, and absolute physical strength.
    *   *Indra*: King of the Devas, lord of storms, rain, and wielder of the thunderbolt Vajra.
*   **The Bhagavad Gita**: A 700-verse dialogue between Prince Arjuna and Lord Krishna on the battlefield, explaining duty, yoga, and the nature of the cosmos.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Monsters
*   **Garuda**: A divine, golden giant eagle-deity, king of birds and the mount (vahana) of Lord Vishnu. He represents solar fire and speed (Spirit/Wind).
*   **Airavata**: A colossal, white, multi-tusked elephant that carries Indra, lord of heaven. He can reach his trunk into the underworld to spray ocean water into the clouds.
*   **Makara**: A legendary sea creature with the trunk of an elephant, the jaws of a crocodile, and the tail of a peacock, representing water's fertility and danger.
*   **Nagas**: Sentient serpent-beings that can shapeshift into humans, living in subterranean palaces (Patala). They guard treasures and control waters.
*   **Asuras**: Powerful, cosmic beings who contend with the Devas for control of heaven, representing material ambition.
