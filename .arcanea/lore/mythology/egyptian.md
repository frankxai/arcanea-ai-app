## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: The *Pyramid Texts* (Old Kingdom), the *Coffin Texts* (Middle Kingdom), and the *Book of the Dead* (New Kingdom, specifically the *Papyrus of Ani*).
*   **Scale**: The Nile River valley, the heavens where the sun barque sails, and the subterranean chambers of the Duat.
*   **North Star**: Balance, ritual resurrection, and preservation. The central theme is the eternal struggle to maintain Ma'at (cosmic order, justice, and truth) against the creeping chaos of Isfet (represented by the serpent Apep).

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
Reality is a clean cycle of light and water divided into three domains:
*   **The Nun**: The infinite, chaotic primordial waters from which all creation emerged and which still surround the universe.
*   **The Terrestrial World (Egypt/Kemet)**: Divided into the East Bank (land of the living, associated with sunrise and birth) and the West Bank (land of the dead, associated with sunset and tombs).
*   **The Duat (The Underworld)**: A vast, dangerous subterranean region containing twelve gates (guarded by knife-wielding demons) corresponding to the twelve hours of the night.
*   **Aaru (The Fields of Reeds)**: The heavenly paradise where virtuous souls live eternally in a perfect mirror of fertile Egypt, free from labor or sorrow.

---

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
Magic in Egypt is an essential cosmic force co-equal with the gods:
*   **Heka**: The personification of magic. Heka is the energy that activated creation. It is wielded through spoken words (*words of power*), amulets (like the Ankh and Eye of Horus), and ritual actions. Heka requires absolute precision; a single misspoken syllable in a spell cancels its effect.
*   **Ren (True Name)**: A person's true name is a vital part of their soul. Knowing a being's Ren gives a mage absolute command over them. To erase a name from a tomb is to condemn the soul to second death (permanent deletion).
*   **The Soul Parts**: Soul architecture is modular:
    *   *Ka*: The double or life force, requiring food and drink offerings in the tomb.
    *   *Ba*: The bird-like personality/soul that can leave the tomb to visit the living world.
    *   *Ib*: The heart, the seat of emotion, thought, and memory.
    *   *Sheut*: The shadow.
    *   *Akh*: The intellect/glorified spirit formed when the Ka and Ba unite successfully in the afterlife.
*   **Weighing of the Heart**: The ultimate judgment in the Hall of Ma'at. Anubis weighs the deceased's heart against the feather of Ma'at. If the heart is heavy with sin, it is devoured, and the soul ceases to exist.

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Gods, Texts, and Concepts
*   **The Great Gods (The Ennead & Others)**:
    *   *Ra*: The Sun God, who sails the sun barque across the sky by day and through the Duat by night, fighting Apep (Forge Gate).
    *   *Osiris*: Lord of the dead and resurrection. He was murdered and dismembered by Seth, then assembled and revived by Isis.
    *   *Isis*: The supreme sorceress, who discovered Ra's true name and holds ultimate magical wisdom (Sight Gate).
    *   *Horus*: The falcon-headed god of kingship and sky, who defeated Seth to avenge his father.
    *   *Seth*: The god of storms, chaos, deserts, and foreign lands.
    *   *Anubis*: The jackal-headed god of mummification, who guides souls and weighs hearts (Void/Tide Gate).
    *   *Thoth*: The ibis-headed god of wisdom, writing, magic, and time. He invented hieroglyphs (the *Medu Neter* / Words of the Gods) (Voice Gate).
    *   *Ma'at*: The goddess of truth, balance, and order, wearing an ostrich feather on her head.
*   **The Book of the Dead**: A collection of magical spells, hymns, and instructions buried with the deceased to help them navigate the dangers of the Duat and pass the judgment of Osiris.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Monsters
*   **Ammit (The Devourer of Souls)**: A hybrid beast with the head of a crocodile, the forequarters of a lion, and the hindquarters of a hippopotamus. She sits beneath the scales of Ma'at to eat heavy hearts.
*   **Apep (Apophis)**: The colossal serpent of pure chaos and void that lives in the depths of the Duat, attempting to swallow Ra's sun barque every night and plunge the world into darkness.
*   **The Sphinx**: A creature with the body of a lion and the head of a human, acting as a guardian of sacred temples and portals.
*   **The Bennu (Phoenix)**: A self-created golden heron associated with Ra and resurrection. It represents the first land rising from the primordial waters of Nun.
*   **Serpopard**: A mythical predator with the body of a leopard and an elongated serpent-like neck, representing untamed wilderness.
