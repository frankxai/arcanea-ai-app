## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: The *Kojiki* ("Records of Ancient Matters", 712 AD), the *Nihon Shoki* ("The Chronicles of Japan", 720 AD), and ancient *Norito* (ritual prayers).
*   **Scale**: The islands of Japan, the celestial Plain of High Heaven (Takamagahara), and the subterranean land of pollution (Yomi).
*   **North Star**: Animism, purity, and connection. There are *Yaoyorozu-no-Kami* (Eight Million Kami)—an infinite number of spirits inhabiting all natural objects, forces, and ancestors. The core ethical principle is maintaining purity and avoiding ritual pollution (Kegare).

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
The cosmos is layered horizontally and vertically:
*   **Takamagahara (Plain of High Heaven)**: The celestial realm where the heavenly Kami reside, connected to Earth by the Ama-no-ukihashi (Floating Bridge of Heaven).
*   **Ashihara no Nakatsukuni (Central Land of Reed Plains)**: The physical terrestrial world (Japan/Earth).
*   **Yomi-no-kuni (Land of Yellow Springs)**: The dark, decaying underworld where spirits go after death, ruled by Izanami. It is a place of absolute pollution (Kegare) and rot.
*   **Ryūgū-jō**: The underwater palace of the Dragon King of the Sea (Ryūjin), where time flows differently.
*   **Tokoyo-no-kuni (Land of the Eternal)**: A mystical realm across the sea associated with youth, immortality, and spirits.

## 3. Magic & Power Mechanics
*   **Kami Resonance**: Magic is not an independent energy bar. It is the result of cultivating harmony with the Kami of the environment. Offending the Kami brings misfortune and natural disasters; appeasing them brings blessings (Shinretsu).
*   **Kegare (Impurity) & Harae (Purification)**: 
    *   *Kegare*: Impurity accumulated through death, blood, disease, and selfish acts. It weakens a person's life force and blocks magic.
    *   *Harae*: Ritual purification using water (Misogi), salt, or the waving of a paper wand (Onusa) to wash away Kegare and restore magical capacity.
*   **Ofuda & Omamori**: Paper or wooden talismans inscribed with the name of a Kami. Worshippers hang them to ward off evil spirits, create barriers, or channel elemental protection.
*   **Kagura**: Ritual music and theatrical dance performed to entertain, pacify, and summon the Kami.

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Gods, Texts, and Concepts
*   **Major Kami (Spirits)**:
    *   *Izanagi & Izanami*: The primordial creator couple who churned the ocean with a jeweled spear to form the islands of Japan. Izanami died and became queen of Yomi.
    *   *Amaterasu Omikami*: Goddess of the Sun and ruler of heaven, born from Izanagi's left eye. The imperial lineage claims descent from her (Lumina Gate).
    *   *Susanoo-no-Mikoto*: God of storms, seas, and chaos, born from Izanagi's nose. He defeated the eight-headed serpent Yamata-no-Orochi (Tide/Forge Gate).
    *   *Tsukuyomi-no-Mikoto*: God of the Moon, born from Izanagi's right eye.
    *   *Inari Okami*: The Kami of agriculture, rice, foxes, and worldly success (Heart Gate).
    *   *Hachiman*: The god of war, archery, and protector of the state.
*   **Kojiki**: The oldest surviving historical and mythological record of Japan, documenting the creation of the world and the deeds of the Kami.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Monsters (Yokai & Others)
*   **Kitsune**: Celestial or wild fox spirits, messengers of Inari. They can grow up to nine tails as they age, gaining infinite wisdom and the power to shapeshift into beautiful humans, manipulate fire (Kitsunebi), and create illusions (Mind Gate).
*   **Ryū**: Water dragons that inhabit lakes, rivers, and oceans. They lack wings but fly through atmospheric magic, controlling rain, storms, and tides.
*   **Tengu**: Bird-like mountain protectors and martial arts masters. They carry magical feather fans (Uchiwa) that can summon gale-force winds (Wind Gate).
*   **Oni**: Giant, muscular demons with horns, red or blue skin, and iron clubs (Kanabō). They represent physical greed, violence, and pestilence (Earth/Forge).
*   **Kappa**: Water sprites with a bowl of water on their heads that holds their strength. They are mischievous but bound by absolute politeness.
*   **Yatagarasu**: The three-legged giant crow of sun-fire, sent by the gods as a guide of destiny.

## 7. World Engine Template Directives
Creators using the **Shinto Template** on `arcanea.ai` adopt:
1.  **The Kegare Meter**: Characters accumulate impurity when they kill or enter corrupted zones. High Kegare reduces magical power and causes friendly NPCs to avoid them. Purity must be restored via Misogi (ritual washing at shrines).
2.  **Kami Attunement**: Rather than leveling up spellbooks, mages build reputation with local Kami (Kami of the Forest, Kami of the River). Attuning to a Kami grants localized passive element manipulation.
3.  **Yokai Contracts**: Monsters (Yokai) are not meant to be systematically exterminated. They can be bargained with, pacified, or bound as familiars using correct offerings and riddles.
