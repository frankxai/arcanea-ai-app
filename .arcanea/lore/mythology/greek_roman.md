## 1. Overview & Meta-Context
*   **Primary Sources**: Hesiod (*Theogony*, *Works and Days*), Homer (*The Iliad*, *The Odyssey*), Ovid (*Metamorphoses*), Virgil (*The Aeneid*), and Orphic gold tablets.
*   **Scale**: The terrestrial Mediterranean, Mount Olympus, the Underworld, and the primordial Void.
*   **North Star**: Divine archetype intervention and human tragedy. The gods are highly anthropomorphic, acting as larger-than-life representations of human passions, natural forces, and social contracts.

---

## 2. Cosmological Architecture
Reality is structured in descending layers of age and density:
*   **Chaos (The Void)**: The primordial, formless state of nothingness from which the first gods emerged.
*   **Mount Olympus**: The shining summit where the Twelve Olympians reside, untouched by storm or wind, fueled by light.
*   **The Terrestrial Realm**: Earth (Gaia), surrounded by the freshwaters of Oceanus.
*   **Hades (The Underworld)**: The subterranean realm of the dead, ruled by Hades and Persephone. It contains:
    *   *Elysium / Elysian Fields*: The sun-lit paradise reserved for heroes and the virtuous.
    *   *Asphodel Meadows*: The grey, misty plain where ordinary souls wander, forgetting their mortal lives.
    *   *Tartarus*: The deepest abyss beneath Hades, where the Titans are imprisoned and the wicked are tortured.
*   **The River Styx**: The river of hatred that bounds the Underworld. Oaths sworn upon it are absolute, binding even the gods.

---

## 3. Magic & Divine Power Mechanics
*   **Ichor**: The golden, toxic blood of the gods. It grants immortality and is highly volatile when handled by mortals.
*   **Ambrosia & Nectar**: The food and drink of the gods that preserves their youth and strength. Eating it can incinerate or deify a mortal.
*   **The Moirai (The Fates)**: Clotho (spins the thread of life), Lachesis (measures it), and Atropos (cuts it). Even Zeus cannot alter their decisions.
*   **Hubris & Nemesis**: The central moral mechanic. Mortals who display excessive pride or claim equality with the gods (Hubris) are inevitably hunted and broken by the goddess of retribution (Nemesis).
*   **Manteia (Prophecy & Oracles)**: Divine will channeled through mortal mediums. The Oracle of Delphi (Pythia) breathes volcanic vapours to deliver ambiguous, fate-locked prophecies.
*   **Goetia & Theurgy**: Low magic (necromancy and curse tablets) vs. High magic (invoking the gods through precise, sung rituals).

## 4. Extensive Glossary of Gods, Texts, and Concepts
*   **The Twelve Olympians**: 
    *   *Zeus (Jupiter)*: King of gods, ruler of the sky, wielder of the thunderbolt (Voice Gate / Lightning).
    *   *Hera (Juno)*: Queen of gods, protector of marriage and family.
    *   *Poseidon (Neptune)*: Lord of the seas, earthquakes, and horses (Tide Gate).
    *   *Athena (Minerva)*: Goddess of wisdom, tactical warfare, and weaving (Sight Gate).
    *   *Apollo*: God of light, prophecy, music, and plague (Voice Gate).
    *   *Artemis (Diana)*: Goddess of the wilderness, hunt, and childbirth.
    *   *Ares (Mars)*: God of chaotic bloodlust and physical battle (Forge Gate).
    *   *Aphrodite (Venus)*: Goddess of love, beauty, and desire (Heart Gate).
    *   *Hephaestus (Vulcan)*: God of blacksmithing, fire, and technology (Forge Gate).
    *   *Hermes (Mercury)*: Messenger god, guide of souls, lord of thieves and travelers.
    *   *Demeter (Ceres)*: Goddess of agriculture and the seasons.
    *   *Dionysus (Bacchus)*: God of wine, ritual madness, and theatre.
*   **Theogony**: Hesiod's epic poem detailing the genealogy of the gods, from Chaos through the Titans to the triumph of the Olympians.
*   **Orphic Hymns**: A collection of 87 ritual poems used in the mystery cult of Orpheus, invoking deities through musical recitation and specific incense.
*   **Sibylline Books**: A collection of oracle utterances purchased by the last king of Rome, consulted during state emergencies to appease the gods.

## 5. Extensive Glossary of Creatures & Monsters
*   **Typhon**: The most lethal monster in Greek myth—a colossal giant with a hundred snake heads, defeated by Zeus and buried under Mount Etna.
*   **The Hydra**: A multi-headed swamp serpent of Lerna. For every head cut off, two grow back, unless the stump is cauterized with fire (Earth/Fire).
*   **Pegasus**: A divine winged stallion born from the blood of the decapitated Gorgon Medusa (Wind/Spirit).
*   **The Minotaur**: A bull-headed man-eating beast kept at the center of the Labyrinth in Crete (Earth/Forge).
*   **Cerberus**: The three-headed hound that guards the gates of Hades, preventing the dead from leaving and the living from entering.
*   **The Chimera**: A fire-breathing hybrid monster with the body of a lion, a snake tail, and a goat head rising from its back.
*   **Gorgons**: Three sisters with snakes for hair; their gaze turns any living onlooker to stone (Medusa was the only mortal one).
