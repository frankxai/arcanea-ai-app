# STAGING — The Ten Godbeast Forms

> Status: ⏳ STAGING. Nothing here is canon. Every line is a proposal awaiting Frank's ruling.
> Prepared 2026-09-08. Does not modify `CANON_LOCKED.md`.

## Why this exists

The ten Godbeast **names** have been LOCKED since 2026-01-16. Only two **forms** were ever
ratified (2026-02-21: Laeylinn = Worldtree Deer, Veloura = Phoenix-Serpent). In the absence of a
ruling, four separate form-sets were independently authored across the repo. They disagree.

| Source | Authority | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Frank's 2025-10 roster + recovered CANON v6** | Frank's own words | The original naming session. Chat archive. |
| **B. `book/legends-of-arcanea/VII_THE_GODBEAST_CODEX.md`** | Authored prose | Legend VII. Full entries, partnership notes consistent with the god profiles. Best-written source. |
| **C. Vault heraldry §3 + prompt lexicon Part V** | Unsourced table | Silhouettes for crest rendering. No chat, no bible, no ruling behind it. |
| **D. `las-tierras-de-luz` support files** | Incidental | Invented a third Otome in passing. Novel chapters never name her. |

`godbeasts/*.md` still say *"Canon details pending on specific physical form"* — so the profiles
themselves record that no ruling was ever made.

## PROPOSED RULINGS — approve, strike, or amend each line

### R1. Otome = Whale. *The Leviathan of the Unheard Song.*
Provenance priority: Frank's own roster, twice, plus recovered CANON v6 ("Whale Dragon"), plus the
2025-11 **Otomari — "Children of Otome"**, lesser memory-whales already built on top of it.
The songbird and the wind-eagle have no ruling behind them.

Argument on merits, independent of provenance: **a songbird is nature's liar.** Mimicry is the
defining songbird trait — lyrebird, mockingbird, starling. Bird song is display. The Voice Gate's
grammar is *"a voice cannot be borrowed."* A whale's song cannot be borrowed; it identifies the
singer to every whale in the ocean. That is Otome Resonite as an animal. Whale calls cross ocean
basins below human hearing — the Godbeast of the Voice Gate is literally **the voice unheard**,
which is the Book 5 title. And a whale must surface to breathe: truth surfaces or the speaker
suffocates.

**Carry forward from the Codex's Storm Moth** (this is the one genuine synthesis, not a
compromise): the reversal is what makes that entry good — *fragile, silent, seemingly harmless,
until it speaks.* It transfers onto the whale nearly intact, because a whale is also silent to
human ears until you can hear beneath them. Keep the Codex's **"When Otome Screams: storms of
revelation, mass exposure of secrets"** verbatim. Keep the partnership note verbatim — *"Alera
once feared her own voice. Otome taught her that silence is not safety, it is a different kind of
violence."*

Body: deep blue, silver patterns that pulse with each note (Frank's original).
Element: **Wind, by breath** — voice is breath given shape (*ruach, pneuma, prana, spiritus*).
The Codex lists Void here; that contradicts the profile and should be corrected either way.
Medium: whatever carries a voice, so she is seen rising through cloud as often as through water.
Heraldry: whale in the diving posture, fluke up, inside three sonic rings.

**What is lost:** the intimate dawn-lit human-scale encounter, and the trial-chamber Otome-chick.
Both survive as *echoes* (see R11). A whale is hard to put in a courtyard scene. The Voice Gate
should be hard to put in a courtyard.

### R2. Kaelith = Tortoise. *The Obsidian Tortoise, World-Bearer.*
Against the Codex's Primordial Serpent on two grounds. First, **it duplicates**: Veloura is
already a serpent (locked), and a second serpent in ten costs the set a silhouette. Second, the
tortoise already has a canon foothold — **Tier 11 stages Rua, the Hoan Kiem tortoise, as
Kaelith's Vietnamese echo.** Cross-mythology: Kurma, Akupara, Ao's legs as the sky's pillars,
Xuanwu the Black Tortoise of the north and winter, Turtle Island. Frank's own roster had a
world-turtle before it settled on a wyrm.

Keep from the Codex: *"the oldest Godbeast — older, some whisper, than the Gates themselves"*
and Lyssandria's hundred years of waiting. Keep the obsidian-and-basalt body; make the carapace,
not the coil, the thing it is wrapped in.

**Only Kaelith does this:** it bears. Every other Godbeast approaches, hunts, sings, or phases.
Kaelith does not come to you — you walk to it and stand on it. Which is Lyssandria's whole
teaching in one image.

### R3. Draconis = Dragon. *The Solar Wyrm.*
Takes the dragon slot cleanly once Sol is a lion and Kaelith a tortoise. The Codex's "World-Forge"
is a title, not a form, and should be kept as the epithet's second half.
**Only Draconis does this:** every fire in Arcanea burns; his is the one that *makes.*

### R4. Sol = Lion. *The Sun Lion.*
The roster's lion-dragon was Sol and Draconis sharing one body; the split improves both. Sekhmet,
Durga's mount, Narasimha at the threshold, Leo as the sun's house.
**Only Sol does this:** it annihilates. Sol Quartz overcharges into pure white light — every other
Godbeast at full strength is still its gift; enlightenment at full strength is an erasure. That is
why the Vault of Unmade Things sits under the Luminary.

### R5. Vaelith = Panther. *The Void Panther.*
Dionysus's panther (the dissolved self), Tezcatlipoca's jaguar and smoking mirror. The medieval
*Physiologus* panther sleeps three days, rises, and breathes a sweetness that draws every animal
from every direction — perspective from all angles, written eight centuries early. A black panther
is the night sky with stars in the fur: the Void element with nothing invented.
Keep the Codex's **Fractal Eye** as an aspect, not the form.
**Only Vaelith does this:** it is never fully here. You cannot corner it.

### R6. Kyuro = one Godbeast in two wolf bodies. *The Silent Twin.*
Canon says each God has ONE Godbeast, so this is not a loophole — it is the Unity Gate made flesh.
Huginn and Muninn, Geri and Freki, the she-wolf's twins.
**Amendment to the vault heraldry:** it shows them *howling.* Kyuro is "perfectly silent, absorbs
all frequency, watches without participating." Howling is broadcast; Kyuro is reception. Show the
pair mirrored with heads lowered toward the central spark, ears up. Listening, not calling.
Keep the Codex's **Bridge-Beast** as epithet.

### R7. Source = formless. Pulsing circle in an open borderless diamond. No creature, ever.
Ten is not a count; it is nine plus the octave. 1111 is "the frequency that contains all
frequencies," beyond the Extended Solfeggio scale. Give it a body and it becomes a tenth note
instead of the resolution of the chord, and Luminarch ("all nine") stops making sense as the
material ceiling.

**The structural payoff: formlessness is why Shinkami could refuse Malachar.** He could only seize
what had a shape. The nine have bodies and can be approached, ridden, echoed, alloyed. Source has
none, so the only available relation to it is being *given* it. He arrived at the tenth Gate having
taken nine by mastery and found nothing to master. The recoil corrupted the temples, not the beasts.

Precedent: the Four Symbols have a fifth position at the centre that is not a direction. Ezekiel's
four living creatures stand around a throne that is only glory. The ensō is a circle because the
thing it names has no edges.

**Operational rule:** any rendered creature at the Source Gate is the beholder's highest open
Gate, and must be captioned that way. Image pipelines will try to give it a face; this is the
standing answer.

### R8. Yumiko — **OPEN. Frank's call. Do not treat this as decided.**
Three candidates, and unlike the others, the weakest-provenance option has the best prose:

- **Nine-tailed fox** (vault) — *recommend refusing.* It is the single most recognisable silhouette
  in the property Arcanea is deliberately not copying. Ten beasts with a nine-tailed fox among them
  reads as Naruto in one glance regardless of execution.
- **Three-tailed Veil-Walker fox** (Codex) — materially different from the vault's, and *much*
  better: three tails each extending into a different dimension, "sees from angles physical beings
  cannot access." The three-tail count breaks the Kurama collision. And `deep-lore.md:343` has real
  authored prose on it: *"Yumiko is a fox, and then she is not, and then she is again — never in a
  way that feels like trickery, but in a way that feels like honest incompleteness."* That line
  answers the fox-means-illusion objection directly and on purpose.
- **Crane, the Mirror Crane** — the seer-animal of still water. The heraldic crane holds a stone so
  it cannot fall asleep on watch; the tsuru; Chinese immortals riding cranes; Egypt's Bennu heron on
  the first mound. Total stillness, then one precise movement. No franchise owns it.
- **Owl-serpent** (Frank's roster) — the third option, on provenance.

**Assessment:** the fox-as-illusion objection is real, but the Codex answers it deliberately, and
the Codex is authored prose rather than a table. The crane is the cleaner silhouette and the safer
distinctiveness play. The three-tailed Veil-Walker is the better *character.* This one genuinely
needs Frank, not an agent.

### R9. Reaffirm Laeylinn = Worldtree Deer and Veloura = Phoenix-Serpent.
Already LOCKED 2026-02-21; restated so the ratified table is complete in one place.
Note: Laeylinn's threshold packet uses "she", the profile uses "it". Leave gender unlocked.

### R10. Retire counted tails on the Ten.
The 2025-10 roster's "Tails: 1–9" scheme and v6's "Ten-Tailed Wolf-Dragon" Shinkami are superseded.
Counted tails are the one visual that belongs to Kishimoto rather than to humanity.

### R11. Bond doctrine, for Tier 2.
> *Each God and Godbeast are two beings of one frequency. The Godbeast is the Gate's nature and its
> threshold; the God is that frequency as it can be taught. The bond is recognition, not ownership,
> and cannot be transferred, sealed, or fragmented.*

This is the **vahana** pattern, and it carries a rule worth adopting outright: *the vehicle is the
raw form of what the deity has mastered.* Ganesha's mouse is desire made small; Durga's lion is
pride made obedient; Saraswati's hamsa separates milk from water. Read the pairs that way and none
is redundant — **Kaelith is weight, Lyssandria is chosen endurance. Otome is truth as compulsion,
Alera is truth freely spoken. Sol is annihilating light, Aiyami is enlightenment that knows what
must stay dark.** The same frequency before and after a person has learned it.

### R12. Godbeasts cannot be corrupted, sealed, carried, or fragmented.
Only the temples were corrupted at the Fall. Canon never describes a corrupted beast.

## FURTHER STAGING — needs shaping before it can be ruled on

### S1. The Echo system — the answer to "should Arcanea have jinchūriki"
**No host mechanic.** A Godbeast is a Gate's frequency *as a body*; a mortal is not a Gate, a mortal
is someone who opens Gates. You cannot seal a frequency into a person any more than you can seal a
note into a singer.

What a mortal carries is an **Echo**, and both halves already exist in canon unnamed:
- **World-echoes** — Tier 11's staged Tu Linh; Taura's Vaerith ("related distantly to the Godbeast
  lineage but not of the Ten"); the Otomari.
- **Borne echoes** — Tier 9's "Bonded: Godbeast-linked". Veshenka Dawnfallen, bonded to Vaelith and
  severed, is the extant precedent and already has a cost in the text.

It does **not** sit between Guardian and Godbeast — there is no slot there, because Guardian is the
God's role, not a rank. It sits at the top of the Bonded origin class.

**Placeholder term: "Echo-bearer." Needs an Arcanean-quality name from Frank.**

### S2. The cost — one lost freedom per Gate, derived from the nine Vael crystals

| Echo | The freedom lost, permanently | Derived from |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | You cannot flee. Your body will not leave what it has chosen to hold. | Kaelith Stone absorbs kinetic force |
| Flow | You cannot forget. Every feeling retained at full pressure. | Veloura Glass stores emotional memory |
| Fire | You cannot act without will. In the moment you are a coward, your hands fail. | Draconis Ember: cowards cannot lift it |
| Heart | You cannot un-bond. What you join to, you stay joined to. | Laeylinn Jade: Resonance Bloom, irreversible |
| Voice | You cannot lie. Your own falsehood fractures you as it fractures Resonite. | Otome Resonite shatters at lies |
| Sight | You cannot un-see — including your own intentions. | Yumiko Prism reveals things-as-they-are |
| Crown | You overcharge. Take in too much and you burn out in white light. | Sol Quartz detonates on overcharge |
| Starweave | You are never fully here. The world loses grip on you by degrees. | Vaelith Obsidian phases through matter |
| Unity | You cannot be alone. Separation is physical pain. | Kyuro Void Crystal absorbs all frequency |
| Source | No echo can be borne. The attempt is annihilation. | Luminarch: theoretical, never achieved |

The cost is the loss of the *opposite* freedom: each Gate's gift becomes an obligation the body
enforces. Intimate, permanent, and different at every Gate — ten kinds of bearer, ten kinds of story.

**Severance:** a bond cut does not return the freedom. It leaves the Gate silent in you. A Voice-
bearer severed is mute. That is Malachar's actual instrument, and it is why the corrupted temples
are dungeons of inversion rather than monster pits.

### S3. Luminor Fallout, refined
Tier 8 says the Vael crystals condensed from the fallen Eldrians. So **the crystals carry Godbeast
names because they are the residue of fallen Echo-bearers.** Kaelith Stone is what a
Foundation-bearer's obligation becomes when the bearer is gone. Amends Tier 8's "the Guardians in
mineral form" to "echoes in mineral form." Materials, mutations, the Bonded class and the Luminor
rank were always one system.

### S4. Lumina, Nero, and the two faces
Lumina and Nero have no Godbeasts **because they are what Godbeasts are made of.** Lumina is the
Form-Giver; Nero is fertile potential; every Godbeast is a form drawn out of the unformed. So each
of the ten has two faces:
- **Lumina-face** — the form. What heraldry shows and art renders.
- **Nero-face** — the presence before the form resolves. All five threshold packets already write
  this: *"nothing about the Godbeast's outline stayed long enough to become a fact."*

Source is all Nero-face and no Lumina-face. That is the third independent reason it has no body.

Source is **not** Lumina's. It is Shinkami's, and it is where Lumina and Nero *meet* — which the
Void/Spirit colours (black/gold, purple/white) already draw.

### S5. Hollows — the answer to "is Nero's beast an absence"
Not Nero's. Nero's absence is *fertile* (the dark between Laeylinn's branches, the silence in which
Kyuro hears); Shadow's absence is *hungry* (Hollow Glass consumes frequency from the living).
Conflating them is the drift the continuity audit already flagged.

A **Hollow** is Shadow's counterfeit: a silhouette without a frequency inside, wearing one of the
ten shapes and eating the resonance its original emits. Replaces any "corrupted Godbeast" (the
roster's "Kraven the Broken"). Heroes will meet what looks like Otome and feel their own voice
draining. A Hollow is defeated by the thing it lacks: the real frequency, spoken, seen, stood on.

### S6. Luminor's relation to the Ten
**Recognised by all nine, bonded to none.** Someone carrying all nine obligations at once — and
therefore free, because a person bound to every Gate's law has no freedom left to lose. That is what
a saint is in every tradition, and it is why Luminor is a rank and not a species. Luminarch is the
Luminor in metal. The tenth cannot be alloyed or opened, only received; a Luminor who has received
it is not a higher rank, they are the same rank with the door open. **Architects** stand on the
other side of that door, which is why they sit outside the rank table.
Stellaris is the extant proof case: "resonates at all 10 frequencies, bonded to Arcanea itself."

### S7. The Source mirror
A seeker perceives Source as the Godbeast of the highest Gate they have opened. A Master at six sees
Yumiko; an Archmage at eight sees Vaelith. A Luminor with nine open sees the circle — and seeing
nothing is the sign of readiness. **Malachar, Lumina's Champion, saw Lumina's champion.** Explains
why no one who entered has returned: they went in to meet what they saw.

### S8. The Gods were the first Echo-bearers — **Frank-only, touches Tier 4 and Book 5**
The Godbeasts are older than the Gods; a frequency has no origin story, and canon already says "the
Gods were not always Gods." The Codex says it independently: *"Kaelith is the oldest Godbeast —
older, some whisper, than the Gates themselves."* Two sources, written apart, arrived at the same
thing.

If the seats were earned by recognition, they are attainable — and that is the truth the Gods tried
to erase, and the reason Malachar's heresy is not that he wanted to ascend but that he wanted to
ascend by seizure.

### S9. What Arcanea must not take from Naruto — and why it already refuted it
1. **Beasts as fragments of one beast.** In Naruto the nine are pieces of the Ten-Tails and the
   villain's project is reassembly; power is a sum. In Arcanea the nine are whole and Source is not
   their sum — it is what they are *in*. You cannot reach it by collecting. **Arcanea does not need
   to avoid this idea; it has already made it the Dark Lord's theology.** Malachar opened nine
   Gates, read completeness as entitlement, and went to the tenth to add it to the pile.
2. **Sealing.** No infant vessels, no state-owned hosts, no beast as a village's weapon. The State
   can breed Bonded scouts; it cannot manufacture an Echo-bearer.
3. **Counted tails.** See R10.
4. **Takeover.** No rage-mode, no beast wearing the host. The danger of an Echo is not that the
   beast takes you over; it is that its law does.
5. **The befriending arc.** Naruto's beasts are hated demons redeemed by a boy. Arcanea's are
   revered from page one; the arc is earning the threshold and then paying the obligation.

**Keep** (universal, not his): the beast has personhood; the bond costs; the pair are stronger as
one intent than as rider and mount — Taura and Vaerith's Bond-Strike already does this well.
