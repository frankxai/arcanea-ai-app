# Story Articulation Standard — Arcanea

> **Status:** STAGING
> **Job:** Make our language as rigorous as our cosmology. Prevent “AI-impressive, human-empty” output.

## 1. The problem this solves

We can generate infinite faction tables and still fail the only test that matters:

> Would a tired, intelligent human stay for the next page because they *feel* someone?

Best worlds do not win by word count. They win by **articulation**: how the story is offered, how the world is revealed, and how language treats the reader as a guest with a body.

## 2. Pattern matrix — how elite worlds articulate (abstract)

Use patterns. Never copy protected names, maps, spells, or distinctive mechanic bundles into Arcanea labels.

| Pattern | What audiences receive | How they *sell* the story | Language habits | Arcanea translation |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Hard-magic satisfaction** | Problems solvable once rules are known | “Watch them think” | Precise verbs, cost-first | Gates with limits; no free deus |
| **Soft-myth awe** | Wonder + dread beyond full explanation | “You will not know everything” | Withhold, hint, ritual silence | Gods speak rarely; Nero remains mystery |
| **Political realism** | Institutions as characters | “No clean side” | Dry specificity, titles as weapons | Compact conflicts; refuse protocol |
| **Elemental culture** | Power shapes food, sport, dress, humor | “Belonging has a texture” | Local idiom, material culture | Realms + Houses lived, not logoed |
| **Crew-as-family** | Found family under pressure | “Who sits at your table?” | Nicknames, in-jokes, debt | Starbound crews + Confluence core |
| **Invitation brand** | Instant emotional entry for non-readers | One image + one feeling | Second person light, concrete nouns | Site/trailer: one Gate, one face, one cost |
| **Reveal-on-need** | Trust that depth exists off-page | Iceberg confidence | No tour-guide prose | STORY_ENGINE arcs; dossiers not dumps |
| **Limitation drama** | Weakness creates plot | “What can’t they do?” | Scars, bans, cooldowns | Gate emotional demand; bond separation pain |

Sources informing this matrix (craft discourse, not canon): Sanderson’s laws of magic (power/limit/depth), SFWA story-as-worldbuilding guidance, organic worldbuilding craft pillars (language, culture, sensory immersion), and franchise invitation practice across major fantasy/media properties.

## 3. Three channels of articulation

### A. Myth channel (in-world truth)

- Short. Sacred. Exact.
- No superlatives (“most powerful,” “unimaginable”).
- Prefer names, frequencies, consequences.

### B. Story channel (scenes, books, dialogues)

- Character POV.
- Desire + obstacle + cost.
- Sensory first, cosmology second.
- Dialogue does work; monologue is earned.

### C. Offer channel (product, social, sales, onboarding)

- Human invitation.
- One promise, one image, one next action.
- Never leak agent meta, “OS,” swarm, or internal doc names into customer-facing fiction voice.

## 4. Arcanea voice spectrum

| Register | Use for | Sound like | Example seed |
|----------|---------|------------|--------------|
| **Hearth** | Intimate scenes, Mera, home loss | Quiet, tactile, unfinished sentences OK | “The vial is warm. She does not open it.” |
| **Hall** | Academy, Compact meetings | Formal but human; dry wit | “We can shelter him. We cannot own him.” |
| **Field** | Corps, crews, dragons | Short clauses, breath, dirt under cosmic | “Hold the left flank. Do not look up yet.” |
| **Void edge** | Malachar logic, Shadow temptation | Plausible compassion, dangerous clarity | “If the system loved you, it would have arrived sooner.” |
| **Invitation** | arcanea.ai, trailers, social | Warm precision, no hype fog | “Your Gate opened. No one asked. Begin anyway.” |

## 5. Selling the story without cheapening it

### Promise stack (write in this order)

1. **Feeling** — fear, wonder, grief, belonging
2. **Conflict** — two goods, not only good vs evil
3. **Person** — a face the audience can hold
4. **World spark** — one rule or image (Gate, frequency, seal, bond)
5. **Cost** — what power takes
6. **Next step** — read / enter academy / create / watch

### Franchise promise candidates (STAGING — Frank chooses)

- *A world where power opens without consent — and love is the only honest form of control.*
- *Magic is frequency. Institutions are how people try not to drown in it.*
- *The villain was the best of them — until he refused to be refused.*

### Offer-channel do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Name a person and a pressure | “In a world where…” openers |
| Show a cost in the first sentence | Feature lists as mythology |
| Use concrete materials (jade, iron, rain, paper) | Neon-cyber fantasy clichés |
| Let antagonists make sense | Pure darkness cosplay |
| Invite participation (“open the next Gate”) | Fake urgency / growth-hack tone |
| Preserve founder-approved wording | Generic “X, not Y” contrast spam |

## 6. Humanization checklist (every scene / dossier / hero copy)

- [ ] Body: breath, hands, hunger, injury, sleep, tremor
- [ ] Place: one local smell or sound
- [ ] Social: status difference in the room
- [ ] Want: someone wants a concrete thing *now*
- [ ] Price: success would cost something named
- [ ] Contradiction: no TED-talk resolution mid-scene
- [ ] Aftertaste: reader leaves with a question
- [ ] Canon: ≥1 locked anchor true if magic details blur

## 7. Ban list (craft, not theology)

### Words / patterns under suspicion

Use only when earned by plot physics:

- resonance (prefer frequency / hum / gate-tone when technical)
- tapestry, delve, embark on a journey
- “in a world where”
- masterpiece / best quality (visual prompts too)
- destiny called / chosen one (unless subverted on-page)
- empty “light and dark” binaries that erase Nero’s non-evil nature

### Structural bans

- Infodump before desire
- Faction as pure aesthetic without refuse line
- Power without limitation scene
- Villain without understandable wound
- Meta (Starlight Swarm, CoE, agent names) inside fiction sheets

## 8. Team craft method (how professionals actually work)

Elite human rooms iterate:

1. **Story break** — beats as wants/costs, not locations
2. **Table read** — dialogue aloud; cut anything no mouth would say
3. **Bible vs page** — bible can be huge; page stays iceberg
4. **Sensitivity / continuity** — separate from drafting ego
5. **First-reader cold pass** — someone who did not write it scores the Human Bar
6. **Lock late** — volume early is fine; canon lock is expensive

Arcanea multi-agent work must simulate this with **role separation**, not more tokens in one mouth.

## 9. Evaluation rubric (pair with MasterFaction Human Bar)

Score 0–2 each (max 20):

1. Desire clarity
2. Cost clarity
3. Sensory life
4. Dialogue pressure
5. Institutional texture
6. Moral residue
7. Canon fidelity
8. Originality / inversion
9. Language cleanliness (ban list)
10. Re-read desire

**Ship ≥18. Revise 14–17. Restart ≤13.**

## 10. Worked micro-examples

### Weak (AI-impressive)

> In the luminous realm of Arcanea, the Gate-Touched resonate with cosmic frequencies as factions clash for the fate of all existence.

### Strong (human bar)

> Registration smells like wet ink and boiled barley. Arion keeps his hands on the table so no one sees them shake. Across from him, the clerk does not look up. “Name,” she says, as if the word cannot start a war.

### Offer-channel strong

> Your Gate opened without asking. Arcanea is the academy, the crew, and the fight over what happens next.

## 11. Operator summary

**Articulate feeling first, cost second, cosmology third. Sell with a person and a pressure. Write like a guest is in the room — because they are.**
