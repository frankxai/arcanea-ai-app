# Mamoru — Vanguard Shield-Diver (STAGING)

> **STAGING — not canon.** This profile is proposed forward-staging material for the
> Dungeon Diver arc, written in the format of `CHARACTER_CORE.md`. It requires explicit
> approval from Frank before any promotion toward locked or public canon. Nothing here
> overrides `CANON_LOCKED.md`. Mamoru does not appear in `CHARACTER_CORE.md`'s Cast
> Center and must not be treated as one of the four public leads (Arion, Mera, Emilia,
> Akamoto) until approved.

## Forward role

Vanguard and shield-diver of the Dungeon Diver party — the fourth student. Mamoru is the
one who goes through the gate threshold first and the one who is last to leave a
collapsing chamber. Where Arion carries the Confluence burden, Mera carries memory, and
Emilia carries invention, Mamoru carries the party's *margin for error*. He is the reason
the other three can afford to take risks.

## Origin

Foundation/Earth-aligned, in the Gate-Touched lineage rather than formal Arcan
lineage — closer to Kael/Arion's spontaneous-opening pattern than to Academy-bred
mastery, but stabilized years earlier and far more disciplined about it. Raised in a
Stoneward Holds-adjacent craft-and-repair community (per `FACTION_COMPACT.md`'s
Stoneward Holds: "mountain holds, farming commons, artifact guilds... demand repair for
every world-saving disruption"). That upbringing is the emotional key to his character:
he was raised by people who fix things, and he has come to believe protecting a person
is the same discipline as repairing a wall — you find the load-bearing point and you
hold it.

His Foundation Gate opened early and without incident — no Ironhold-style disaster, no
death, no headline. This is unusual enough among Gate-Touched origin stories that some
in the Academy quietly doubt he is Gate-Touched at all, and wonder if he is Arcan with an
unusually plain manifestation. Mamoru has never corrected them either way. He finds the
question less interesting than the work.

## Core trait

Load-bearing steadiness.

## Supporting strength

Structural reading. Like Arion, Mamoru "notices what is load-bearing" — but where Arion
reads it in objects and rooms and people's hidden weight, Mamoru reads it specifically in
*formations*: where a party's line will break, which teammate is one hit from collapse,
which wall in a dungeon chamber is about to give. He calls it "reading the wall," whether
the wall is stone or a person.

## Internal contradiction

He wants to be the one who is never the story — the shield in the background of
everyone else's victory — but the Dungeon Diver arc keeps putting him at the literal
front of every frame, first through every gate, and he does not know what to do with
being seen.

## Shadow/mask

Cheerful invulnerability. Mamoru jokes his way through injuries, exhaustion, and fear so
consistently that the party has learned to distrust his smile as a diagnostic tool — the
wider it gets, the worse things actually are. He would rather be teased for being "fine"
than have anyone slow down to worry about him.

## Visible desire

To make it so no one in the party has to learn how heavy a dungeon gate truly is by
being the one who gets crushed under it.

## Hidden need

To let someone hold the shield for him, once, without treating it as a failure of
duty.

## Wound

Before the Academy, a support beam Mamoru had repaired failed during a Stoneward Holds
mine collapse — not from bad craft, but because the load above it had grown beyond what
anyone had accounted for. He was the one who held the gap open long enough for the
others to get out, and he has never stopped quietly recalculating what a "safe load"
actually means for a person instead of a wall.

## Vow

"I go first so the door stays open behind me."

## Fear

That the day will come when holding the door open costs more than he has left to give,
and that he will do it anyway without telling anyone the math didn't work.

## Power path

Foundation/Earth ward-bending as primary path — barriers, braces, and standing ground
raised through stance and planted feet, per the anatomy-led bending doctrine in
`MAGIC_SYSTEMS_INDEX.md` ("Fire joins when action stops being domination... Earth joins
when guilt becomes responsibility instead of self-punishment" applies to Mamoru as
directly as it does to Arion). Secondary affinity toward Unity/Bond-adjacent bending
through the party itself — Mamoru is not a Confluence candidate and should never be
written as one; his ceiling is a very strong Triadic Bearer (per the Development Ladder
in `CONFLUENCE_CANON.md`), specializing as an **Anchor** and **Shield** Bearer function
for Arion specifically, should the Compact ever formalize Bearer roles around him.

## Limit

Mamoru's wards hold weight, force, and collapse — they do not hold intention. A ward
raised for someone who does not want to be protected will not take, and Mamoru feels
this refusal as physical resistance in his own stance. He cannot ward against grief,
memory, or persuasion; that is Mera's and Emilia's terrain, not his.

## Signature object

A tower shield built from salvaged Luminor alloy plate, its surface a visible record of
every field repair he has made himself rather than had made for him — the same instinct
as Arion's repaired tool from home, but worn on his arm instead of kept at his belt.

## First scene pressure test

At the gate threshold before the party's first real descent, Mamoru is asked whether he
wants to switch positions — someone else can go first this time. He says no before the
question finishes, and for half a second his shield-arm shakes before he steadies it.
No one comments. Everyone notices.

## Visual identity

- **Signature object:** Tower shield of layered Luminor alloy, visibly field-repaired —
  weld seams, patch plates, and re-riveted edges tell the story of every dive that
  almost went wrong, not factory-perfect craftsmanship.
- **Ward-light discipline:** Teal ward-light appears **only along the shield rim** — a
  single controlled line, never a full-body glow, never a shield-face pattern. This is
  the anatomy-led rule: the ward answers his stance and planted feet, not decoration.
- **Presence:** Warm, reliable, physically grounded. Broad-built but not exaggerated;
  reads as someone built by repeated real weight-bearing, not a gym-sculpted hero.
  Practical vanguard kit — matte alloy plate over flexible dark weave, guard-blade
  sheathed and secondary to the shield, helmet more often carried than worn so his face
  stays legible.
- **Expression default:** The "honest tired grin" — warmth that is a little frayed at
  the edges, never performative confidence.

## Relationship to the Cast Center

Mamoru complements rather than duplicates the existing four:

| Character | Primary domain | Mamoru's relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Arion | Confluence burden, five elements | Mamoru is the reason Arion can afford to be uncertain mid-fight — he holds the line while Arion decides. |
| Mera | Memory, truth-witness | Mamoru trusts what Mera says without needing to see the memory himself — practical faith, not evidence. |
| Emilia | Invention, Prisma tools | Emilia field-repairs his shield between dives; he is her most consistent proof that a tool built with consent gets *used* correctly. |
| Akamoto | Mentor, protective severity | Akamoto sees his own younger refusal-to-abandon instinct in Mamoru and worries about it for the same reason he worries about himself. |
