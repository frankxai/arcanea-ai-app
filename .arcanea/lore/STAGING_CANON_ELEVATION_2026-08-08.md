## What this is

A canon-elevation audit of Arcanea against premium published fantasy and the best-regarded game worlds, run on 2026-08-08. It audited 406 proper nouns, read 108,000 words of drafted fiction, benchmarked six novelists and seven game worlds, produced four design proposals, and then subjected those proposals to three adversarial reviews (genre reader, production lead, authorial soul).

**The full decision document is `publishing-house/runs/2026-08-08-arcanea-canon-elevation/CANON-ELEVATION-PROPOSAL.md`.** Read that to approve or reject. This file exists so that a future agent working in `.arcanea/lore/` knows the audit happened, knows what it concluded, and — critically — **knows what it rejected**, so that rejected proposals are not revived.

## Headline

**Fifty-one decisions. Zero renames of anything Frank named.** The Tier-1 recommendation set is eleven free reframes plus five cheap mechanical fixes, invalidates no drafted prose, and touches no canonical list in CI.

The audit's own most damaging finding has nothing to do with naming: **16 files in `book/` and `docs/` ship unresolved `<<<<<<< HEAD` merge-conflict markers**, four of them containing two complete, canon-contradictory drafts of themselves concatenated.

## Hard constraints any future run must respect

**1. Three proposals in this run would have broken CI.** `.github/workflows/canon-lint.yml` hardcodes `CANONICAL_GODS`, `CANONICAL_GODBEASTS`, `CANONICAL_GATES`, `CANONICAL_ELEMENTS` and `CANONICAL_HOUSES`, and hard-fails (`exit 1`) on three specific string patterns — **CHECK 3** (the Primordial Darkness paired with a moral condemnation), **CHECK 5** (`Starweave` split or hyphenated), and **CHECK 6** (any assertion that the element count is fewer than five). It also warns on bare frequency values in `book/`, `.arcanea/lore/` and `apps/web/content/`. Read the workflow for the exact patterns; they are deliberately not quoted here, because **this file is itself under `.arcanea/lore/` and quoting them would fail the build**. That is not a joke — the first draft of this document did exactly that, in all three places. **Any rename of a God, Godbeast, Gate, Element or House must change that workflow in the same commit**, or the guard goes stale silently and every later PR passes for the wrong reason.

**2. Migration costs in the rename docket are 1.5–2× low.** Verified 2026-08-08: `Malachar` is 395 files (docket: 239); `Luminor` is 2,657 files; the slug `draconia` appears 578 times in `apps/` and `packages/` (docket: 233). Re-measure before acting on any cost figure.

**3. Frank's approval log is not re-litigable.** `Veloura`, `Yumiko`, `Sol`, `Kyuro` (2026-01-16) and the ten Gate Frequencies, each Gate unique (2026-01-16 **and again** 2026-02-21) are LOCKED by his name. Three proposals in this run contradicted one or both.

**4. Six things are the thesis, not positions.** Not available for dispute, contest, or unresolved ambiguity: *the Primordial Darkness is not a moral wrong — Shadow is its corruption, per `CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 1* · *Shinkami refused Malachar; the Source cannot be forced* · *Malachar is the only true antagonist* · *the Gates are ten* · *the frequencies are cosmological fact* · *each God has one **bonded** Godbeast, and a bond is a partnership.*

**5. The reframe budget is three, and it is spent** (D5, D6, D13). Applied once, attribution is depth. Applied to the pantheon, the Wisdoms, the Houses, the Gate count and the founding refusal at once, it stops making the world older and starts making the author absent.

**6. Slugs are not names.** `/gods/draconia` stays whatever a display name says. No bulk find-replace on `book/`.
