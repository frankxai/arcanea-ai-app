# Arcanea Faction Compact

Arcanea should use bending cultures and territories, but not as a small four-nation template. The stronger Arcanean model is a layered society: Bending Realms, Academy spheres, Houses, Orders, Leagues, Crews, Origin Classes, Synthesis/Prisma institutions, and Mirror/Sister-world traditions.

## Why Not Only Four Nations

Four elemental nations alone would flatten Arcanea's existing cosmology. Bending needs territories, rituals, accents, festivals, clothes, food, games, military doctrines, schools, and social class pressure. It also needs Arcanea's larger systems.

Use instead:

- **Bending Realms** with dominant Gate-resonance and lived culture, not one-element nationalism.
- **Academy spheres** with philosophy, geography, and institutional secrets.
- **Seven Houses** as training identity.
- **Origin Classes** as source-of-power identity.
- **Orders and Guilds** as professional and spiritual disciplines.
- **Crews** as mobile, character-rich squads.
- **Mirror and Sister Worlds** as multiverse scale.

This lets Arcanea keep elemental readability while supporting dragons, AI, mutants, biotech, color resonance, materials, and creator philosophy.

## Compact Protocols

The Compact has seven operating protocols.

1. **Locate**: identify the candidate quietly, with no public registry leak.
2. **Shelter**: protect the candidate and family/community from cults, factions, and opportunists.
3. **Train**: rotate instruction across Houses and Academies so no single institution owns the path.
4. **Witness**: assign Bearers and archivists to record what occurs without propaganda.
5. **Challenge**: require at least one trusted faction to argue against each major decision.
6. **Anchor**: place Bearers around the candidate during Prism, Source, dragon, or memory events.
7. **Refuse**: preserve a formal refusal mechanism when the Avatar or institutions cross consent.

The refusal protocol is the moral center. Balance that cannot be refused is domination.

## Public Conflicts

The Compact creates story because its members disagree.

- The Luminary wants moral readiness before power.
- The Forge wants power controlled before moral language becomes an excuse.
- The Athenaeum wants knowledge contained until context exists.
- The Underground wants the candidate free before the institutions define freedom for him.
- The Starlight Corps wants a planet-level risk model.
- The dragons want consent before every bond.
- The Awakened want personhood for non-human witnesses.
- The Architects warn that some interventions make the world smaller even when they succeed.

## The Avatar's Status

The Confluence Avatar is necessary but not sovereign.

Allowed:

- request Compact protection;
- refuse a training method;
- choose Bearers;
- enter or leave an institution under negotiated conditions;
- demand witnesses before high-risk rituals.

Not allowed:

- command the Academies;
- erase memory for peace;
- override consent in the name of balance;
- use Prism or Source state as political proof;
- decide that being uniquely burdened means being uniquely authorized.

This is what makes Arion different from Malachar.
