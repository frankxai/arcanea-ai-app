## 4. Sanderson's Three Laws of Magic Applied to Arcanea

1. **Law of Comprehension:** The audience's investment is directly proportional to how well they understand the rules, limits, and tangible costs of the magic.
2. **Law of Limitations (The Friction Engine):** Limitations are infinitely more dramatic than powers:
   - **Bodily Toll:** Channeling causes physical consequences (e.g. retinal flash blindness, capillary frost, tooth tremors).
   - **Failure Boundary:** The precise counter-harmonic breaking point where spells backfire or invert.
   - **Counter-Remedy:** Tangible physical remedies (e.g. cold brine soak, willow bark, grounding rods).
3. **Law of Deeper Foundations:** Deepen existing magic systems before inventing new ones.

## 5. Tolkien-Grade Philological Families

Language shapes worldbuilding. The Arcanea linguistic engine enforces six distinct dialect trees:

1. **High Eldrian:** Musical, flowing, vocalic; long vowels (ā, ē, ī, ō, ū), liquid consonants (l, r, v, th). No harsh stops.
2. **Veldarín:** Soft, sibilant, nature-infused; whispered fricatives (sh, th, f, s), front vowels (i, e, y).
3. **Aurevaldan:** Strong, rhythmic, percussive; plosives (k, t, p, d, g), back vowels (a, o, u), trilled r.
4. **Sunder-tongue:** Guttural, clipped, harsh; glottal stops, back consonants (kh, q, gh, z), abrupt cadence.
5. **Solar Common:** Pragmatic, balanced, trade-standard; wide phoneme distribution, simple syllables.
6. **Deep Runic:** Resonant, heavy, subterranean; voiced stops (b, d, g), heavy nasal clusters (mb, nd, ng).

## 7. The Humanizer Standard (Five Sensory Anchor Laws)

AI slop and sterile prose destroy immersion. Every agent, engine, and narrative surface must obey:

1. **The Tactile Pivot:** Never cite power without its physical cost (ozone, heat, friction, copper).
2. **Asymmetrical Cadence:** Mix blunt, compact statements with rich architectural descriptions.
3. **The Vernacular Crack:** Street-level reality grounds high-concept cosmic lore.
4. **Relatable Stakes:** Anchor complex cosmic abstractions in tangible human and creator consequences.
5. **Specificity as Magic:** Exact numbers, concrete files, verified commands. Zero hand-waving.

### Banned AI Slop Patterns
Never use: "unleash", "elevate", "unlock", "harness", "delve", "rich tapestry", "testament to", "resonates deeply", "in this digital age", "beacon of hope", "dynamic symphony".
