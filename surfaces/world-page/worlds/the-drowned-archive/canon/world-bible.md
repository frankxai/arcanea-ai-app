# The Drowned Archive

> A sunken city where memory is the only currency that survived the Flood.

**Genesis:** a drowned city where memory is currency

## Premise
Centuries after the Flood swallowed Vael, its survivors learned that gold dissolves but memory does not. Down in the pressure-dark, divers trade remembered moments — a first kiss, a mother's voice, the exact blue of a sky no one alive has seen. The richest are those who have lived the most and spent the least.

## The Laws
1. A memory spent is a memory lost — forever. No copy survives the telling.
2. The deeper you dive, the older the memories you can reach.
3. No one may sell a memory that is not their own.

## Visual DNA
- **Palette:** deep-water navy, cosmic blue, atlantean teal, phosphor green, archive gold
- **Style:** bioluminescent ruin · refracted light · weathered brass and coral
- **Motifs:** drowned libraries, memory-coins, lantern-fish, tide-glyphs
