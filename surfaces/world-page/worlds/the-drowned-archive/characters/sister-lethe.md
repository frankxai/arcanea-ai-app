# Sister Lethe

A diver-nun of the Order of the Clean Slate, who believes the only way to be free is to spend everything. She gives her memories away to strangers for nothing, growing lighter and emptier with every dive — and no one can decide if she is the holiest soul in the deep or simply disappearing.

## Backstory
Lethe entered the Order carrying a grief too heavy to surface with. She has spent it, piece by piece, into the hands of the grieving — and now remembers almost nothing of who she was. What remains is a startling gentleness and a smile that arrives a half-second late, as if she has to relearn it each time.
