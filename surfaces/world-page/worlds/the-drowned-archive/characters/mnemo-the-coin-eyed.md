# Mnemo the Coin-Eyed

The Archive's most feared memory-broker, with one eye replaced by a minted tide-glass coin that lets him appraise a memory's worth at a glance. He never spends his own — he only moves other people's, taking a sliver of each as commission until the sliver is the whole.

## Backstory
No one knows what Mnemo gave up to earn the coin-eye; he claims he traded the memory of how he got it. He runs the floating exchange above the Sunken Stacks, where the desperate come to sell their last warm afternoon. He is unfailingly polite, which is what makes him terrifying.
