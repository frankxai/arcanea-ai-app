# The Sunken Stacks

The drowned heart of the old city library, now the deepest licensed dive in the Archive. Shelf-canyons of swollen books trail ink like smoke; lantern-fish nest in the card catalogues. The oldest, richest memories settle here at the bottom — and the pressure is such that to reach them, a diver must leave most of who they are at the surface.
