# Code of conduct

This code applies to participation in this repository's issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions. Creators and developers should be able to ask questions,
share permitted work and disagree about an implementation without being targeted.

## Participation

- Discuss the work and explain criticism with specific examples. Respect the
  creator's stated goals and boundaries.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds. Ask for clarification
  before attributing intent.
- Credit contributors and source material. Respect privacy, consent and the
  applicable terms for manuscripts, images, code and other assets.
- Keep discussion relevant. Explain commercial affiliations when they affect
  a recommendation or contribution.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, sharing private
information without consent, impersonation, repeated unwanted contact and spam
are unacceptable. Criticism of a feature or decision is allowed; personal attacks
and retaliation against reporters are not.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to [frank@arcanea.ai](mailto:frank@arcanea.ai),
the maintainer contact in the repository's package metadata. Include links,
relevant context and the outcome you are seeking. Share only the personal
information needed to understand the report. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for
software vulnerabilities.

Maintainers may request an edit, remove harmful content, restrict a discussion,
or limit participation based on the behavior and its impact. Affected people may
request reconsideration through the same contact and provide additional context.
When the complaint concerns the maintainer, GitHub's
[abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is an additional route for violations of GitHub's policies.

This file establishes repository participation rules. It does not promise a
moderation team, scheduled community events, a response deadline or a license
for contributed material.
