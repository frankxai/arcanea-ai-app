# Code Of Conduct

Arcanea expects direct, respectful, evidence-based collaboration.

## Expected

- Be precise about problems and risks.
- Critique work, not people.
- Respect maintainer decisions and project scope.
- Keep discussions useful for future readers.
- Credit sources, prior art, and contributors.

## Not Accepted

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information.
- Spam, low-effort promotion, or unrelated agenda pushing.
- Submitting work you do not have the right to contribute.

Maintainers may edit, close, remove, or block participation that violates this
code or creates operational risk for the project.
