## Composition (back to front)
1. **Sky:** Hanoi skyline silhouette — temple spires, neon, motorbike amber streaks, paper lanterns
2. **Far Water:** Lake reflecting city in broken amber and teal, concentric ripples from center
3. **The Turtle:** Enormous shape beneath luminous water, shell inscriptions glowing Arcanea Gold, one ancient eye visible
4. **An:** Three-quarter back view, lower-left third, small against scene. Olive jacket, rolled trousers, loosening bun. City amber light catching her face.
5. **Linh Chi + Details:** Crystalline creature on stone balustrade glowing emerald. Tiny forest spirits in banyan shadows. Fireflies.

## Style
- Studio Ghibli background art meets contemporary literary fantasy cover
- Painterly gouache/watercolor quality with digital precision
- References: Kazuo Oga, Victo Ngai, James Jean
- NOT anime, NOT photorealistic, NOT generic fantasy

## AI Image Gen Prompt
A young Vietnamese woman seen from three-quarter back view, standing at the stone edge of Hoan Kiem Lake in Hanoi at night. She is small against the vast scene, wearing a practical olive jacket and rolled trousers, dark hair in a loosening bun. In the luminous teal water before her, an enormous ancient turtle is surfacing — its massive shell covered in glowing golden inscriptions resembling sacred geometry. The golden light illuminates the water from below. On the stone balustrade near her, a small cat-sized crystalline creature glows emerald green. Background: Hanoi skyline with temple spires, motorbike amber light trails, paper lanterns. Style: Studio Ghibli background art quality meets literary fantasy book cover. Painterly, atmospheric, luminous. Deep midnight teal, warm Hanoi amber, glowing gold, emerald bioluminescence, cosmic blue depths. 2:3 vertical.
