# Supporting Cast

---

## HOANG VAN PHUC — The Antagonist

**Age:** 52. Director of regional development for agricultural/infrastructure interests in the Mekong Delta.

**He is right about:**
- Rural Vietnamese communities have been systematically dispossessed by foreign-funded conservation
- Infrastructure in flood zones has saved real lives
- Economic development for poor Delta communities is a genuine moral priority

**He is wrong about:**
- The Gate sites are simply undeveloped land (they are the structural substrate of regional ecological coherence)
- The choice is binary (development vs. preservation)
- His methods are proportionate

**Diamond:** Grew up poor in a flood zone. Watched his father lose everything to flooding that better infrastructure would have prevented. Watched NGOs protect landscapes that poor families needed. Concluded environmentalism is a story rich people tell about land poor people need.

Not performing being reasonable. Genuinely reasonable within his framework. This is what makes him frightening.

**Voice:** "Your research is good. The methodology is solid. I just think you're asking the wrong question. You're asking what the ecosystem needs. I'm asking what the people who live in it need. Those are not the same question."

## BA NOI NGUYEN — The Mentor (Grandmother)

**Age:** 74. The last living practitioner of "noi chuyen voi dat" — speaking with the land.

Small, fierce woman with a back like a question mark. Makes the best banh cuon in the family and considers this her actual legacy. She is not wrong.

Knew about An's gift when An was four. Named it — mot nguoi nghe, a listening person — and said nothing until An was ready. An has been ready for fifteen years and Ba Noi has waited without apology.

Not Gate-Touched in the formal Arcanean sense. She has been in relationship with the Unity Gate frequency her entire life without calling it that. Last practitioner of the tho than compact — an ancient agreement between certain human families and the land-spirits.

**Voice:**
> "The universe doesn't need you to carry it. It just needs you to show up. There's a difference."
> "You've been calling a river a problem for fifteen years. The river doesn't care what you call it. It goes where it goes."

## PHAM THUY LINH — Best Friend / Found Family

**Age:** 25. Graphic designer, An's roommate. Vietnamese-Chinese (Hoa family from Hai Phong).

Mainlines reality television with rigorous analytical engagement. Code-switches between Vietnamese, Cantonese, and English within single sentences when excited. Loud in public, tender in private. Noticed An's loneliness before An did.

**Function:** The person who sees the everyday An — crackers for dinner, pangolin monologues at 10 PM — and loves that person specifically.

**Voice:**
> "You're aware that you talk about him the way you talk about a research problem that's been bothering you for months, right? Which is to say: constantly, with this specific face."
> "That's not a choice you get to make for me. I know I seem like a comic relief character sometimes but I've actually been your friend the whole time."

## DANG THU HA — Rival to Ally

**Age:** 29. Research ecologist. An's professional rival in Book 1, complicated ally in Book 2, chosen family by Book 3.

Brilliant in a way she knows about and carries without apology. Raised in a Da Nang academic family where emotional demonstration was considered imprecision. Has her own affinity for the Foundation Gate — reads landscape intuitively. Calls this good empirical modeling. It is more.

**Voice:**
> "Your methodology is fine. Your sample size is too small to support your conclusions. I'd reframe the claim."
> "I've been thinking about what you said last month. About the cost of the armor. I don't know what to do with it yet but I wanted you to know I'm thinking about it."

## NGO BAO — The Trickster with Hidden Depth

**Age:** 31. Street photographer, urban folklore archivist. Quan's oldest friend. Born into a Hoa family with a Kinh grandmother who was a len dong medium.

Irreverent in public, careful in private. Jokes about everything and works hard to make sure no one notices how much he knows. Custodian of a household spirit (tao quan aspect) that has been in his family for generations. He did not choose this. Does not want it. Is very good at it.

**Function:** Provides levity at exactly the right moments and information at exactly the right costs. His hidden depth arrives in a single scene in Book 2 where everything funny falls away — four pages of what he has actually been carrying. Then he makes a joke. The joke lands differently now.

**Voice:**
> "Brother. I say this with love. The woman is already in things. The question is whether you're going to be useful or just decorative about it."
> "Okay. No more jokes. Tell me what you need and be specific." [Three seconds.] "Sorry, one more joke. [Delivers joke.] Okay now I'm serious."
