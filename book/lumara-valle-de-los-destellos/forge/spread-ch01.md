# Spread Ch 1 — Lila crosses into Lumara

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-destellos-spread-ch01.png`
**Book beat:** Lila steps through the moonlight doorway and Lumara opens
beneath her for the first time.

## What this image must do

Show the **first sight of Lumara.** A child seeing a true thing she did
not know was possible. The image is the threshold itself: behind her, a
sliver of the moonlight doorway just closing; before her, the whole
valley as a single quiet first impression.

## Composition

Wide landscape framing. The viewer's vantage is just over Lila's right
shoulder, slightly behind her — we see her in three-quarter back view at
the left third of the frame, having just taken one bare step forward
onto the soft plum-violet grass of Lumara.

Behind her, at the upper-left corner of the frame: the moonlight doorway,
a faint vertical pale-cream rectangle in the air, already beginning to
close — barely there, the kind of thing you'd miss if you weren't
looking.

Before her, the **valley reveals itself for the first time**: a wide
soft-rolling plum-violet meadow stretches across the centre of the
frame, dotted with the faint gold of scattered florchispas. A riachuelo
catches a single cream moon-strip in the middle distance. The horizon is
high, two-thirds up the frame, and beyond it the dusty plum sky scattered
with tiny pencil-point stars.

Conejito hangs from Lila's left hand by one ear.

## Negative space

The right two-thirds of the frame is the valley — and most of it is paper
showing through, with only the scattered constellation of florchispa
golds and the single moon-strip on the riachuelo to mark it. Most of
Lumara at this moment is **breath, not detail.**

## Colour choreography

- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown and on the moon-strip across the
  riachuelo.
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for the scattered florchispas across the
  meadow — kept soft and distant, no single one brighter than another.
- **Cream rectangle** ghost for the closing moonlight doorway behind her.
- Grey-violet paper everywhere else.

## Light

The waxing moon is overhead and slightly behind the viewer's vantage. It
catches the riachuelo strip and the back of Lila's nightgown and hair.
The florchispas glow faintly at meadow-level. The closing doorway has a
last faint cream wash.

## Lila's face

Mostly hidden — three-quarter back view, perhaps a quarter-profile sliver
of her cheek and the corner of one calm closed eye visible. Hair tousled
from sleep. The emotion lives in her **posture**: one bare foot just set
down on Lumara's grass, the other foot still in the air mid-step, her
small shoulders very still. She has just stopped breathing for a moment.

## Make-or-break detail

The moonlight doorway behind her must be **almost gone** — the kind of
ghost-rectangle that the viewer reads as "something was just here, and
isn't anymore." If it's bright or hard-edged, the image fails. The
implication is that Lumara has always been here; the door just lets you
in.
