# Spread Ch 4 — The Snail with a Galaxy Inside

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-los-destellos-spread-ch04.png`
**Book beat:** Lila lies on the warm stone, slows down to snail-time,
and sees the galaxy turning inside the shell.

## What this image must do

Show **stillness as a doorway.** The image must feel patient. Nothing
in it is moving except, somewhere very slowly, a tiny spiral of
starlight inside a shell the size of a fingernail.

## Composition

Extreme intimate framing — closer than a child reads a book. The image
is essentially a small still life with one human face at its edge.

The left third of the frame is **Lila's face** in three-quarter view,
chin propped on the back of her two folded hands, mouth closed, eyes
focused gently on the small thing at the centre of the image. She is
lying belly-down on warm pale-rose limestone. Only her face, her hands,
and the edge of her shoulder are visible.

The centre and right of the frame is the **warm rose-pink limestone
slab** itself. Visible texture, visible fissures, a few patches of
silvery moss in the cracks. The texture is the painting.

At the right-third intersection: **a small snail** emerging from a
shallow fissure. Its shell is no larger than a child's fingernail. The
shell visibly contains a tiny **spiral galaxy**: faint mauve-pink
spiral arms, a small bright nucleus at the centre, and a few tiny
metallic-ink stars scattered within the curl. The galaxy fills the
shell entirely.

The snail's two small antennae are raised toward Lila's face.

Conejito rests near Lila's elbow on the rock, head tucked sideways as
if he too has decided to wait.

## Negative space

The upper third of the frame is the surface of the limestone receding
slightly out of focus — almost monochrome warm rose, visible texture,
no other marks. The right edge has breathing space for caption type.

## Colour choreography

- **Warm rose-pink** dominates the limestone (this is a cream-register
  warm wash, still within palette).
- **Cream linen** on Lila's face, hands, sleeve edge.
- **Tiny metallic-ink gold-mauve glow** in the snail's shell-galaxy —
  the brightest single point in the image, but it is also the smallest.
- **Silvery moss** suggested with cool pencil-grey strokes in the rock
  cracks.
- Grey-violet paper edges only.

## Light

A cool cream moon-wash from upper-left rakes across the limestone,
casting subtle shadows from the rock texture. The galaxy in the shell
provides its own tiny faint inner glow — almost imperceptible, just
enough to suggest "the shell is alive with something."

## Lila's face

Calm. Patient. The kind of face a child wears in the fifth or sixth
minute of waiting for a small thing to do something small. Her chin is
on her crossed hands; her hair has fallen slightly over one eye and she
has not bothered to move it. She is in **snail-time.**

## Make-or-break detail

The galaxy inside the shell must be **clearly a real galaxy, not a
swirl** — distinct spiral arms, a brighter nucleus, scattered tiny
metallic-ink points as individual stars. The viewer should be able to
look at the shell and recognise the same shape they've seen in a
photo of the Milky Way. If it just reads as "a glowing swirl," the
magic falls flat. The galaxy is what justifies the patience.

The patience of Lila's posture should be **felt before it's noticed.**
Her stillness is the rhyme of the snail's stillness. They are doing
the same thing.
