# Spread Ch 5 — The Silver Fox Accepts the Gift

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-destellos-spread-ch05.png`
**Book beat:** Lila offers a florchispa on her open palm; the silver
fox, half-hidden behind a slender tree, decides to come closer.

## What this image must do

Show **the pause before trust.** The image lives in the silence between
"I offer this" and "I accept this." Two beings holding very still while
something small and important decides.

## Composition

Wide framing, low angle. A grove of slender silver-leafed trees occupies
the middle ground, their trunks like vertical pencil-strokes against the
deep plum.

Lila kneels at the lower-left third, on one knee, three-quarter view.
Her right palm is extended flat in front of her — and on that flat palm
rests **a single freshly-picked florchispa**: five petals visible,
silver-edged, the small gold heart of the flower glowing softly. The
flower's tiny silvery stem and one tiny silvery leaf still attached.

Her left arm holds Conejito loosely against her side.

At the right-third intersection, **the silver fox** — small, silver-blue,
slender — stands partly behind a slender silver-leafed tree trunk. Only
the front half of its body is visible: one paw forward and slightly
lifted, mid-step in suspended curiosity; nose extended; ears forward;
its luminous fluffy tail visible curling around the trunk behind, the
tip catching the faintest gold glow.

Lila and the fox are at the same eye-line. They are looking directly
at each other across the small distance, the offered florchispa exactly
between them on the lower-third axis.

## Negative space

The upper third of the frame is the canopy of soft silver pencil-stroke
leaves with a few pencil-point stars showing through.

The lower-right quarter — between Lila's outstretched hand and the
fox's foreliegen position — is the **emotional centre** of the image
but is mostly empty: just paper, just air, just the small offered flower
on her palm. The negative space holds the meaning.

## Colour choreography

- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown and skin.
- **Silvery cool-grey** on the fox's coat and the silver-leafed trees
  (this is a cool variant of the cream register).
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for the offered florchispa's gold heart
  and a single touch on the fox's tail-tip.
- Grey-violet paper for sky, ground, deeper grove.

## Light

A cool moon-wash from upper-right dapples through the silver leaves,
catching the silver tones of the fox and the leaves and rim-lighting
Lila's hair. The offered florchispa adds a single small warm-gold
counter-anchor on her palm.

## Lila's & Fox's faces

Lila: calm, three-quarter view, eyes on the fox — completely steady. No
encouragement, no smile, no coaxing. She is simply offering and
waiting. The face of a six-year-old who has been told that one does
not chase friends.

Fox: small, intelligent, slightly turned toward her. The eyes are
focused, alert, not afraid. The slight lift of one paw is the entire
emotional state of the picture — the visible verb of considering.

## Make-or-break detail

The single lifted paw of the fox is **the picture.** That suspended
foot must read as "deciding." Not "frozen in fear," not "about to flee" —
deciding. If the fox is shown with both feet planted, the tension dies.
If the fox is shown clearly committed forward, the gift becomes a
foregone conclusion. The picture wants the **moment of choosing,** held.

The offered florchispa on Lila's palm should be drawn slightly more
carefully than anything else in the image — every petal edge, the small
gold heart, the silvery stem and leaf. The viewer should be able to feel
the weight of it.
