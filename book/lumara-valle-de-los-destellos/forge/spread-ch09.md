# Spread Ch 9 — Lila and Aurelia, Two Florchispas as One

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-destellos-spread-ch09.png`
**Book beat:** Lila and Aurelia kneel knee-to-knee, both florchispas
glowing in synchrony — two children meeting as equals.

## What this image must do

Show **mutual recognition** between two children. The image must feel
balanced like a small ceremony. Two small bodies forming a closed
circle of attention with two glowing flowers as the shared anchor.

## Composition

A small round clearing in a young silver-leaf grove at night. At the
centre, under a single graceful tree with a milk-cream trunk and soft
drooping branches.

The two girls sit cross-legged facing each other, **knees almost
touching**, holding each other's small hands. They are at the centre
of the frame on the rule-of-thirds horizontal, slightly left of the
absolute centre to break perfect symmetry. The milk-cream tree trunk
rises directly behind and slightly between them.

**Left:** Lila in cream linen nightgown, light brown wavy hair, Conejito
sitting just beside her on the grass.
**Right:** Aurelia in plain white-silver shift dress, single long dark
braid falling forward over her shoulder, **one florchispa woven into
the braid clearly glowing.**

On the ground between their nearly-touching knees, exactly centred:
**two florchispas**, one closer to Lila's knee, one closer to
Aurelia's — both pulsing gold-silver in synchrony. They are clearly
the brightest paired light source in the image.

A soft ring of slightly-less-bright florchispas grows in a circle
around the girls at the perimeter of the clearing, framing them.

## Negative space

The upper third of the frame is the canopy of soft silver pencil-stroke
leaves with a few pencil-point stars showing through. The lower edges
left and right are mostly empty meadow grass with a few faint dotted
florchispas.

## Colour choreography

- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown.
- **Pale silver-cream** on Aurelia's shift dress.
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for the two paired florchispas between
  them (the brightest points, equal in intensity), the florchispa in
  Aurelia's braid, and the softer ring around the clearing.
- **Silver-grey** on the slender silver-leafed trees of the ring.
- **Milk-cream warm wash** on the central tree trunk behind them.
- Grey-violet paper for sky and ambient ground.

## Light

The two paired florchispas between their knees are the **dominant
light**, casting a soft gold wash equally onto both girls' faces — a
visible balance. Aurelia's braid-florchispa adds a third smaller
anchor. A faint cool moon-wash from upper-right rakes across the
silver leaves overhead.

## Lila's & Aurelia's faces

Both in three-quarter view, facing each other, eyes meeting. **Same
expression in both faces** — calm, serious, the gravity of meeting
another child like yourself. Mouths closed. Neither smiling.

The faces should be recognisably **two different children**, not
mirror-images. Lila slightly rounder of cheek, brown-haired, the
sleep-tousled look. Aurelia slightly more angular, darker, more
settled — she has lived here always.

## Make-or-break detail

The two paired florchispas between their knees must **glow at exactly
the same intensity.** Visible equality. If one is brighter, the
meeting becomes hierarchical and the chapter's meaning collapses.

The held hands at the lower centre should be drawn carefully — four
small fingers each, no stylisation. This is one of the most important
small images in the book.

The faces are equally lit from below by the paired flowers. This
under-light is gentle, not dramatic — like the light cast upward from
a small fire shared between two friends.
