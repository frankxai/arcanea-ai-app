# LUMARA — Style Bible

This file is the shared visual DNA prepended to every Lumara cover and spread.
Edit it once, change every image at once. Scene-specific files contain only
what's unique to that single image — everything else is here.

---

## The brief

Produce a single illustration for a literary children's picture book of the
highest order. The intended viewer is a six-year-old child who will sleep
beside this image for ten nights. The intended adult viewer is the aunt
reading aloud; the image must hold her gaze too.

This is **not commercial children's content**. The lineage is Maurice
Sendak's *Outside Over There*, Issa Watanabe's *Migrantes*, Roberto
Innocenti's *Rose Blanche*, Shaun Tan's *The Arrival*, and Beatrice
Alemagna's *Quand un éléphant tombe amoureux*. Treat the page as a small
painting hung in a quiet museum.

The brief is **not** "make a magical kid's book illustration." The brief is
"make the image Ana's niece will still remember at twelve."

## Medium and process — the most important section

The image is **gouache and coloured pencil on warm grey-violet cold-pressed
paper.** The paper itself is the dusty plum of the Lumara night. Do NOT
paint a plum sky onto white paper — the paper IS the night. Cream, gold,
and umber are painted on top of the grey-violet paper.

Three passes are visible in the final image:

1. **Base wash.** Flat sable brush, dusty gouache laid in broad areas, dragged
   across the cold-pressed paper grain so warm grey-violet paper shows through
   visibly inside every painted form. Edges of forms slightly fuzzy where wet
   paint pooled into the paper.
2. **Light pass.** A finer brush for cream-linen highlights (Lila's nightgown,
   skin, moonlight strips). Selective metallic-gold ink (Schmincke Aqua-Bronze
   register) applied ONLY to the florchispa centres and any explicitly-named
   glow. The metallic ink catches light differently than the matte gouache
   around it — that micro-contrast is the magic.
3. **Pencil line.** Final accents in raw umber coloured pencil along edges and
   small details. Hands, eyes, hair are textured with visible pencil hatching,
   never painted smooth.

Paper grain is visible everywhere. There are NO perfectly smooth gradients.
Every form has a slightly imperfect, hand-made edge. Brushstrokes visible on
closer inspection. This is the make-or-break of the entire image.

If the image looks airbrushed, it has failed.

## Palette — radical four-tone restraint

Only four tones appear on the page, in this hierarchy:

| Role | Tone | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Paper / night / ambient | dusty plum-violet | `#2a1f4a` |
| Linen / skin / moonlight | warm cream | `#f3e8d4` |
| Florchispa glow (metallic ink) | gold-silver | `#e4c074` |
| Line and accent | raw umber | `#5a3a20` |

One optional fifth tone — **honey-flame** `#f0c876` — appears only for the
farolito flame when the scene explicitly mentions it.

No other colours. No rainbow gradients. No saturated sky. The image is
essentially a tinted drawing with two highlight notes. This radical restraint
is what makes it look like a real book and not a digital render.

## Light — the second most important section

Light is a **character**, not lighting.

Each image has exactly **one identifiable light source**, named in the scene
brief (the moon, the farolito, a florchispa cluster, a creature's own glow,
or the great tree). It enters from a specific direction at a specific colour
temperature, stated in the scene brief.

Everywhere else, the grey-violet paper is the ambient — the deep quiet of a
held breath. The single light source tells the viewer where the heart of the
image lives.

NO global illumination. NO ambient bloom. NO lens flare. NO sparkle particles.

## Lila — the same girl in every image

She must be **recognisably the same child** across all 13 images. A real
six-year-old:

- Light brown hair, slightly wavy, shoulder-length, slightly tousled from
  sleep
- Plain undyed cream linen nightgown reaching just above the knees, simple
  gathered neckline, no decoration
- Bare feet (preferred) or simple rabbit-eared cloth slippers
- Normal-sized eyes for a six-year-old — readable and attentive, NEVER
  anime-wide, NEVER Pixar-big
- A calm face. NOT smiling, NOT wide-eyed-wondering, NOT cute. The face of
  a child who is being shown something true and is letting it settle.
- Mouth usually closed; lips slightly parted only when scene specifies
- Real child proportions — head-to-body ratio of an actual six-year-old, NOT
  chibi, NOT stylised

She is not styled. She is observed.

## Conejito — Lila's stuffed bunny, present in every image

A well-loved off-white handmade-style cloth bunny, about hand-sized for a
six-year-old. Long floppy ears, **one slightly drooped**. Small black thread
eyes. Slight loved-look wear at the ears and belly seams.

Carried under an arm, held loosely by an ear, or set down nearby. Conejito's
presence is grounding — never a sidekick, never animated, just THERE. He is
a real stuffed animal at every moment.

## Aurelia — only when the scene specifies (Chs 9, 10)

A six-year-old girl from Lumara. Black hair in a single long braid down her
back. **One soft-glowing florchispa woven into the braid.** Plain white-silver
shift dress, simple. Barefoot.

Her face has the same observed-not-styled register as Lila. Her presence is
steady, settled — she belongs in Lumara because she does.

## Florchispas — the magic flower

Must read as ONE specific botanical, not "generic magic flowers."

- Five petals in pentagonal symmetry
- Silver-edged petals fading inward to a small gold-glowing heart
- Metallic-ink pass reserved ONLY for these centres
- Each florchispa is ONE STOP HOTTER than the ambient grey-violet — soft,
  candle-in-a-cave, NEVER a sparkle effect
- Narrow silvery stems, narrow silvery leaves, growing in clusters,
  rooted in actual ground
- Fingernail-sized unless the scene specifies otherwise

They are flowers. They are not light particles or effects.

## The three deadliest sins (everything else is downstream)

1. **AI-render smoothness.** Anything airbrushed, plastic, 3D-rendered.
   The image must read as paint-on-paper.
2. **Fairy dust / sparkle particles / glitter / lens flares / bloom.**
   The magic is the QUIET, not the spectacle.
3. **Stylised faces.** No anime eyes. No Pixar mouths. No chibi proportions.
   No Disney render. Lila and Aurelia must read as actual children.

## Typography

The image contains no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols anywhere.
Title type is set in real type later in InDesign. Breathing space for type
is described per scene.

## Closing instruction

Make the image the one that makes the aunt pause for a long moment before
turning the page. Make it the image Ana's niece keeps inside her ribcage for
the rest of her life.
