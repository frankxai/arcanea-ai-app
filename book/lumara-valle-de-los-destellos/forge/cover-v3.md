# Cover v3 — The Great Tree

**Aspect ratio:** 3:4 portrait
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-los-destellos-cover-v3.png`
**Book beat:** Chapter 10 finale — Lila and Aurelia approach el árbol de
los nombres together.

## What this image must do

Show the **arrival** at the heart of the book. Two small children, hand in
hand, before a tree so old it remembers every name. The image should feel
like a sacred moment without ever using a sacred-looking convention.

## Composition

The view is from slightly behind and below the two girls, looking up
toward the tree. The two girls fill the lower-third left of centre, small
silhouettes in three-quarter back-view, walking together hand in hand
toward the tree in the deep middle distance.

**Left:** Lila in plain cream linen nightgown, light brown wavy
shoulder-length hair, Conejito tucked under her free arm.
**Right:** Aurelia in plain white-silver shift dress, single long dark
braid down her back with the one florchispa woven into it glowing gold.

Both are barefoot on soft plum-violet ground. A few small florchispas dot
the path between their feet and the tree.

The **great tree** fills the upper two-thirds of the frame. Its trunk is
wide as a small house, bark the warm honey colour of the farolito light,
visibly textured with pencil-line knots and grain. Its branches spread
wide and high in all directions, irregular and ancient.

Instead of leaves, every branch carries **thousands upon thousands of
florchispas** — a soft constellation of gold-silver metallic-ink points
scattered through the canopy against the deep dusty plum-violet sky.
This canopy is the whole sky.

## Negative space

The lower-third margin under the girls is dark plum paper with almost no
marks — this is where the **title type** will sit on the cover.

## Colour choreography

- **Honey-warm** in the bark of the tree trunk only (a slightly warmer
  application of the gold-silver register).
- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown and Aurelia's shift dress, catching
  reflected gold from the canopy above.
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for the thousands of florchispas in the
  canopy and the one in Aurelia's braid and the few on the path.
- Grey-violet paper for sky and ground.

## Light

The tree's florchispa-canopy is the **only** light source. It casts a
soft gold-silver ambient wash downward, catching on the girls' hair,
shoulders, nightgown, and Conejito's ears. No moon needed — the tree
IS the moon.

## Lila's & Aurelia's faces

Mostly **not visible** — both girls in three-quarter back view, their
faces only partially seen from a quarter angle if at all. The emotional
anchor is **their two small joined hands** at the centre of the lower
third of the image. The viewer doesn't need to see their faces to feel
the weight of the moment.

## Make-or-break detail

The thousands of florchispas in the canopy must read as a **constellation,
not a glow**. They are distinct points of gold-silver metallic ink, each
one a single touch — not a wash. Like stars seen through tree branches.
If the canopy reads as a single glowing mass, the image fails. The
viewer should be able to almost count individual flowers near the
foreground branches and lose count toward the centre.

The two joined hands at the bottom centre must be **slightly larger and
more carefully drawn** than anything else in the image. That tiny union
holds the whole composition.
