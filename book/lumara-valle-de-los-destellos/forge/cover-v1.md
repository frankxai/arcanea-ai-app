# Cover v1 — The Threshold

**Aspect ratio:** 3:4 portrait
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-los-destellos-cover-v1.png`
**Book beat:** Chapter 1, the moment the farolito flame splits and the
moonlight doorway opens.

## What this image must do

Show the **invitation.** The single instant before Lila accepts. The viewer
should feel that something has just decided to open for her, and that she has
just decided to walk through.

## Composition

Interior of Lila's small old-fashioned bedroom at night, framed from inside
the room looking toward an open window. The wooden window frame divides the
image: room interior in cool grey-violet shadow on the left two-thirds,
night beyond the window on the right.

In the lower-centre, on the wooden windowsill: a small worn-bronze oil lamp
(the farolito). Its honey-coloured flame is in the act of softly dividing —
a slow cloud of tiny gold-silver destellos (destellos the size of apple seeds)
drifting upward from the wick like a thinking thought.

Lila stands at the sill in three-quarter profile facing the window, one
bare foot still on the wooden floor, the other half-lifted as if she has
just decided to climb up. Conejito hangs loosely from her left hand by one
ear. Her right hand rests on the windowsill near the lamp. Her face is
calm, turned toward the rising destello-cloud, mouth softly closed. She is
not surprised — she is recognising.

Through the window, two stars hold the upper-right corner of the frame.
Between them, a faint vertical lightening of the night sky — the moonlight
doorway just barely beginning to form. It is not a hard-edged door. It is a
suggestion. The viewer should have to look twice to see it.

## Negative space

The upper-left quarter of the image — the dark interior of the bedroom — is
almost entirely the grey-violet paper itself, with only the suggestion of
a bedpost in pencil-line. This is where the title type will sit.

## Colour choreography

- **Honey-flame** `#f0c876` glowing softly on the lamp wick, casting a small
  warm pool on the windowsill, Lila's hand, and her cheek.
- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown catching that pool plus a touch of
  cooler moon-wash from outside.
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for each tiny destello as it lifts from the
  flame — these should read as small gold pencil-dots with a single
  metallic gleam.
- Everything else: paper-grey.

## Light

Two sources, both gentle:

1. The farolito flame — warm honey, casting a small pool on a 30cm radius
   around the lamp
2. Cool cream moon-wash from outside the window, entering at upper-right,
   barely brushing the inside of the windowsill

## Lila's face

Three-quarter profile, hair falling forward over her cheek. Her expression
is **recognition** — not surprise, not delight. She has known, somehow,
that something like this could happen, and now it is happening. Her mouth
is closed. Her eyes are on the rising destellos, not on the moonlight
doorway behind them.

## Make-or-break detail

The moonlight doorway between the two stars must be **barely there** — a
suggestion, not a structure. If the model renders it as a clear glowing
rectangle, the image fails. It should feel like the kind of thing only
Lila can see, that the viewer is allowed to see because they are
looking very carefully.
