# Spread Ch 8 — The Deer with Candles in Its Antlers

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-destellos-spread-ch08.png`
**Book beat:** The great deer lowers its head and breathes a small flame
loose from one antler-candle — a gift, drifting toward Lila's cupped
hands.

## What this image must do

Show **what gravity looks like when it is gentle.** The deer is enormous
and full of dignity; Lila is small and full of attention. Both are
making the other smaller and bigger at the same time. The image must
feel like a bow.

## Composition

A high meadow plateau under a vast plum-violet sky. Hand-tall florchispas
glow in scattered constellation across the ground. A thin silver mist
curls around hooves and knees.

**Lila** kneels at the lower-left third, three-quarter view, both small
hands cupped open in front of her chest, palms upward, fingers loose.
Conejito sits on the meadow grass beside her bare knee, settled.

**The deer** occupies the upper-right two-thirds of the frame — large,
stately, coat warm hazel-cream, head lowered toward Lila's cupped
hands. On each branch tip of its wide ramified antler-crown, **a small
honey-coloured candle burns calmly** — about ten candles in total. The
flames are tiny, individual, each one no bigger than a fingernail. The
antlers themselves are drawn with visible pencil-line knots and
delicate branching.

From one antler-tip candle, **a single small flame has just detached.**
It floats slowly downward through the air toward Lila's open palms —
exactly between the deer's lowered nose and Lila's cupped hands. This
descending flame is at the rule-of-thirds centre, the gravitational
heart of the image.

## Negative space

The upper-left quarter is the plum-violet sky receding above the
deer's antlers — paper with a scatter of stars. The lower-right
corner is empty meadow with a few faint florchispas. The right
edge has breathing room for caption type.

## Colour choreography

- **Warm hazel-cream** on the deer's coat (this is a warmer wash within
  the cream register).
- **Honey-flame** on each of the antler-candle flames and especially
  on the single descending flame between them.
- **Cream linen** on Lila and on the silver mist around their
  knees/hooves.
- **Gold-silver metallic ink** for the scattered meadow florchispas,
  kept dim so the candle-flames dominate.
- Grey-violet paper for sky and ambient meadow.

## Light

The antler-candles are the **dominant warm light** in the image. They
cast a soft honey wash onto the deer's lowered face, the underside of
its jaw, Lila's upturned cupped hands, and her face. Cool plum-violet
ambient holds the rest of the meadow in restraint. The descending
single flame casts its own small additional glow on Lila's wrists.

## Lila's & Deer's faces

Lila: upturned three-quarter view, calm, eyes on the descending flame.
Lips closed, breath held, attention complete. Her face is lit warm
by the candles above.

Deer: huge head lowered toward her. Eyes calm and deep — the eyes of
something very old. Nostrils not flared. The bow is **slow and
serious.**

## Make-or-break detail

The **scale relationship** is the whole image. The deer must be
clearly enormous compared to Lila — its lowered head alone larger
than her whole torso. But the deer's posture must read as
**reverent, not looming.** The lowered head is a bow, not a
threat.

The single descending flame between them is **the gift.** It must
clearly be in mid-air, in mid-descent, with the very faintest hint
of a downward direction in its small flame-flicker. The viewer
should feel they could lift the page and catch it.

Count the antler-candles — about ten, with one slightly empty
where the descending flame just left. That tiny "one missing" is
what makes the image story-true.
