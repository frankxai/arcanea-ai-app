# The visual promise

The art must sell the same book the prose delivers: intimate human choices inside beautiful systems that can protect, measure, and imprison. It is luxury cosmic myth-tech seen at working distance—worn brass, repaired stone, witness glass, mineral water, ordinary paper, cold air, and people whose bodies carry the price of magic.

The image rail is photoreal cinematic fantasy with tactile production design, believable skin and fabric, controlled celestial light, and precise material behavior. The interface rail remains restrained and editorial. Neither rail may become a generic magic-school poster, a glowing-power lineup, a purple fantasy gradient, or a display of lore the story has not authenticated.

## Non-negotiable visual grammar

- Warm celestial gold against cool mineral blue, with controlled teal and charcoal. Violet appears only when a scene requires prism behavior; it is never the edition's blanket grade.
- Living stone looks quarried, jointed, worn, repaired, and load-bearing. Starlight metal is noble but used. Memory glass holds pressure and condensation, not ghost portraits.
- Magic appears through material response: dust changing direction, water holding an edge, brass carrying a color split, load moving through a joint. No superhero beams, floating spell circles, generic holographic interfaces, or luminous runes used as decoration.
- Cost stays visible. Wrapped hands, wet hems, chalk in skin creases, a bent key, an imperfect repair, and a coat used as support matter more than ornamental armor.
- Academy beauty must be plausible enough to inhabit and specific enough to distrust. Doors have latches. Instruments have maker marks. Records have owners. Exit paths are visible.
- No dragon, monster, Malachar body, face in water or stone, clean portal, prophecy text, chosen-one coronation, or authenticated sentient lower structure.
- Characters read as adults in their twenties, not schoolchildren. Their silhouettes come from work, practice, and personal habits—not costumes.

# Character identity sheets

These are cover-casting decisions for visual continuity, not locked Arcanea canon. Human approval is required before they become public identity assets.

## Arion

**Visual age:** 24. **Read:** neighborhood mason and repairer before magical subject.

- Warm olive-brown skin; dark brown, slightly wavy hair that keeps dust even after washing; deep-set brown eyes; a strong, imperfect nose; compact working strength rather than heroic bulk.
- His attention lands on joints, hands, and exits before faces. When overwhelmed, he becomes still rather than theatrical.
- Opening continuity: charcoal work shirt, weathered umber coat, waxed satchel, ordinary work boots, both palms wrapped in grey cloth. His hammer is a plain repair tool, never a fantasy weapon.
- Later continuity: right forearm splinted, three ribs bound beneath clothing, ring and small fingers contracted. Injury is serious but never aestheticized as corruption.
- Foundation response is nearly invisible: grit aligns, mortar dust changes direction, a bearing face settles. Grey branching lines may be visible only on freshly injured palms where the prose establishes them.
- Recognition objects: folded repair-house sketch, three pencils, wrapped ordinary hammer, exposed repaired seams.

## Mera Tidecrest

**Visual age:** 25. **Read:** exacting field archivist and Water-memory practitioner, never mystical oracle.

- Deep brown skin with cool undertones; black coiled hair worn in a practical low arrangement that loosens in rain; dark, observant eyes; long planes through cheek and jaw; a pale healed scar crossing one palm.
- Her stillness is investigative, not passive. She looks toward the source label or boundary another person has missed.
- Ink-blue weather coat with sea-glass lining, practical dark trousers and wet leather boots; minimal silver fastenings; no mermaid ornament, crown, flowing gown, or excessive aquatic jewelry.
- Water behavior stays physical: condensation, surface pressure, unequal drying, a held edge, mineral reflection. No faces, memories, or spectral people appear in water.
- Recognition objects: sealed blue vial with visible owner seal, plain lamp, pencil, separate folded pages, black neutral-silver stopper, red thread used as evidence separation rather than costume motif.

## Emilia

**Visual age:** 23. **Read:** brilliant maker who can see the flaw in a perfect system and the person after the flaw.

- Warm bronze skin; straight black hair cut to the jaw and usually tucked behind one ear; grey-green eyes; compact build; precise hands with small workshop nicks, later ground with white chalk.
- Her face moves quickly through calculation. Her most human frames catch the instant she stops solving and asks.
- Soot-black Forge work coat with one sleeve rolled and one buttoned, faded cream work shirt, brass fasteners, practical boots. The asymmetry is functional, not quirky styling.
- Prism behavior is optical and material: a thin spectral edge on glass, a color shift across polished brass, brief violet beneath skin only when the specific scene calls for it. Avoid rainbow aura and generic holograms.
- Recognition objects: rough brass manual release, fine driver, three-window witness, chalk-whitened yellow cuff, bent lamp key.

## Headmaster Akamoto

**Visual age:** late forties. **Read:** field mentor whose authority comes from watching the load, not occupying the center.

- Lean, weathered, wind-cut face; medium brown skin; dark hair with silver at the temples; scar disappearing below the left ear; red cord at one wrist.
- High-cold field coat in blackened brown leather and dense woven charcoal, practical harness points, no throne, ceremonial crown, ornate staff, or visible dragon bond.
- He enters a frame by checking joists, wind, perimeter, or another person's breath. He does not pose as a demigod.

# Cover system

## Primary concept — The held interval

Portrait artwork without typography, designed for a 1.6:1 book cover and recoverable at thumbnail size.

Arion occupies the lower center-left, not facing the viewer, his bandaged left hand hovering above an old stone threshold rather than touching it. A narrow dry gap runs through the threshold into a monumental Academy interior. On the far side of upright mineral water, Mera holds a plain lamp and three folded pages against her coat. Emilia kneels beside a dismantled brass interface, yellow cuff over an invasive line, one hand open and waiting rather than reaching for Arion. Their three positions form an incomplete triangle around a visible exit path. Above them, crystal ribs, black Forge braces, and shallow water channels meet without blending into one architectural style. Morning gold enters through a new mortar crack; cool blue remains below. The brightest object is the path they made, not a magical aura.

The frame must feel intimate before epic: a choice under pressure, not three heroes advertising power. No one looks at the viewer. No centered academy crest. No title, lettering, symbols, fake author name, or pseudo-writing inside generated art.

## Alternate concept — Three records

An editorial still life from the final chapter: three non-aligning signed pages on the same unrepaired table, held against the wind by a pencil, bent lamp key, and chipped tea cup. The table crack remains visible beneath all three. Beyond the shallow depth of field, the trio sits in distinct positions without becoming a lineup. This is the literary lane for title A/B testing and deluxe interior/title-page use.

## Typography and lockup

Typography is added after generation, never generated inside the artwork. The title must survive at 120 pixels wide. Use a high-contrast literary serif with one custom structural intervention: a measured break or offset in the word *Path*, never faux-runes. Series and edition labels use a quiet sentence-case sans or mono. No all-caps blocks. The provisional title remains *The Last Free Path* until human title/legal approval.

# Web and artbook behavior

- The first viewport may pair cover art with real prose, but prose and primary action remain legible without the image.
- Mobile uses intentional portrait crops, never automatic center-crops that remove the waiting hand, exit path, or evidence boundary.
- Chapter plates are optional atmosphere between sections, not backgrounds behind body copy. Alt text states material action and never interprets unproven lore.
- Reduced-motion mode receives static images. Any parallax is shallow, decorative, and disabled without changing comprehension.
- The downloadable artbook contains final plates, scene captions, crop studies, and public-safe provenance. It does not contain failed generations with confusing canon, private paths, system prompts, or hidden reasoning.
