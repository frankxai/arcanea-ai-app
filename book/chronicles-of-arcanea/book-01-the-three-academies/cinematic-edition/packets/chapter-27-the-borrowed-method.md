# Chapter packet — 27. The borrowed method

## Story contract

**POV:** Emilia
**Movement:** IV — The refusal
**Target length:** 3,300–4,100 words
**Story time / continuity position:** Immediate continuation from Chapter 26; all three are still beside the basin vestibule, Mera's dry personal page is folded inside her coat, the interior cord has just lost slack and rung once, and Arion's Chapter 25 medical/no-unentered-method terms remain active
**Location:** Level return gallery, a pre-existing buried operator bay, and the memory-water passage beyond it
**Visible want by word 500:** Explain the cord's physical tension change and preserve one observable route without turning the buried interface into a person-reader or measuring Arion by proxy
**Opposing want:** The route is changing faster than manual chalk states can follow; the pre-existing interface's next-joint feelers produce a lead state before motion reaches the bay and record more than the trio consented to expose; a thin route line correlates with the right-wall seam nearest Arion when his injured side catches; he answers a narrower question than Emilia asks and withholds the movement that entered him; Mera must cross a memory-water passage to inspect the retained return from the far ledge; and Arion's body cannot climb, carry right-arm load, use Foundation, or use the undisclosed method
**Dramatic question:** Can Emilia keep the trio on one route when the most useful map is also an involuntary measurement and Arion refuses the fact that would explain it?
**Irreversible turn:** With Mera stopped on the far ledge beyond a level memory-water channel, the interface's upper row displays a projected closure state at the central standing slab while the lower row still shows it held. Emilia covers the thin right-wall line and asks whether another method entered or remains in Arion. He answers the narrower truth—*I have used no method below*—and she says, *That wasn't what I asked.* He then uses the borrowed method through his splinted forearm. The transferred counter-motion happens to hold the central slab for one interval, then travels through the connected seam to the retained-return bar and opens aligned floor/ceiling slots. Pressure drives mineral-grey water between them; when the counter-motion exhausts, the slab retracts and the passage is severed
**Persistent cost:** Mera is isolated beyond a closing current with memories she refused to open. Arion has breached his medical and admission terms, loaded the injured right side, and exposed that he concealed a method. An unresolved branch returns through the contact before he is thrown clear, leaving the carried movement lodged more deeply through his forearm and ribs for Chapter 28. Emilia and Arion are thrown into the operator bay; the manual cord is pinched and parts at the first chalk contact. The interface retains physical lead-state route lines that may expose crossings near protected boundaries but contain no protected content themselves
**Exit image:** Through the slotted mineral-grey water curtain, Mera's lamp remains visible. Beside Emilia, the upper shutter row already holds two narrow branches shut; only then does Arion try and fail to open his ring and small fingers

## Interface, movement, and rupture mechanics

- **Cord diagnosis:** The weighted bell-board remains at the first landing. The coiled free end remains near the basin gallery. Neither endpoint changes position. Between Emilia's two chalked contact marks, a floor joint lengthens by less than a finger, consuming visible slack through friction and briefly ringing the board. The cord is a witness to changed distance only; it neither pulls the floor nor locates the cause.
- **Level movement:** The gallery and operator bay remain level. Arion moves slowly with Mera and Emilia within reach, reports breath and right-hand sensation, and stops on any grade change. He uses no stair, climb, carried load, Foundation brace, or right arm before the breach.
- **Operator bay:** A shallow wall recess contains mineral glass over two rows of narrow brass shutters, counterweighted feelers in the bay joint and one joint ahead, and three mechanically separate channels aligned with the unauthenticated receipt-form impressions and visible return groove. Local water and stone pressure drive the assembly. One lower shutter is shimmed with hand-cut kitchen brass. When the paired lead feelers disagree, an upper shutter rests between notches instead of forcing a clean state. No carried power, credential, remote link, or Academy operator is present.
- **Function boundary:** The upper row is physically coupled to feelers one joint ahead; the lower row records movement only after it reaches the bay joint. One ordinary, necessary floor movement makes the upper row move, then the lower. Emilia may then call the upper row *predictive* as a functional shorthand, but it does not foresee the future. She cannot authenticate original purpose, maker, channel meaning, legality, accuracy outside the observed sequence, or whether the interface reads people. Her only compact explanation is: *Top row feels the next joint. Bottom row records this one.*
- **Imperfect workmanship and tool hunger:** Emilia admires the hand-cut shim and the unresolved between-notch state because both preserve visible uncertainty. Her thumbnail fails when she instinctively tries to test a fine retaining screw; the proper driver is with her sealed tools above. She does not improvise a substitute or alter the interface.
- **Unwanted correlation:** During necessary level travel, a thin lead-state line advances along the right-wall seam nearest Arion when his injured side catches. It does not form an anatomical diagram before the breach. Emilia does not stage a test or ask him to repeat movement. She covers the relevant shutters with her yellow cuff and stops further inference.
- **Arion's narrowed answer:** Emilia asks, *Did another method enter your body?* Arion answers, *I have used no method below.* The sentence is literally true before the breach and materially deceptive because it avoids the movement that entered and remains from Chapters 19 and 22. Emilia replies, *That wasn't what I asked.* He remains free to dispute what the interface line represents; he is not free to erase the fact he withheld.
- **Crossing geography:** The operator bay ends at a dry sill. Beyond it, one central standing slab crosses a narrow horizontal memory-water channel to a far ledge. Matching floor and ceiling slots stand behind the central slab. The retained-return bar can be seen only from the far ledge. Mera chooses to cross the slab under her own power with one lamp and her dry page, names her own stop, and reaches the far ledge. The crossing is not assigned to her as a Water instrument. Arion remains at the bay sill.
- **Pre-rupture stop:** The upper row displays a projected return/closure state at the central slab while Mera stands on the far ledge; the lower row still records the slab held. Emilia calls stop. Mera stops and does not touch the water. The upper row is a one-joint-ahead physical lead, not a present fact or guaranteed outcome.
- **Borrowed-method breach:** Arion makes a bounded, unauthenticated inference that transferring the carried movement into the bay seam may provide a temporary load path and delay the projected slab return. Rather than disclose the method and let Emilia/Mera decide with him, he presses the padded outside of his splinted right forearm into the visible seam, exhales, and sends the trapped movement forward. This violates no-right-arm-load and no-unentered-method terms. He does not use Foundation.
- **Unified hold and rupture:** Mera is already standing safely on the far ledge when Arion acts. His released counter-motion holds the central slab—and therefore her possible return route—for one observed interval, then travels through the connected seam to the retained-return bar. That pulse opens the aligned floor and ceiling slots; pressure drives mineral-grey water vertically between the ledge and bay. When the counter-motion exhausts, the slab retracts, the bay joint shifts sideways, and the cord pinches. Before Arion loses contact, one unresolved branch returns through the seam and lodges deeper along the existing forearm/rib path. Emilia and Arion are thrown into the recess without discharging the carried movement.
- **Cord failure:** The changing joint traps the cord at the first chalk contact. Tension rises until the plain fiber parts there. The bell-board-side segment remains toward the basin vestibule; the free segment falls into the operator bay. No magical snapping, message, or rescue function is implied.
- **Lead-state retention:** During the rupture, lower-row current shutters lose alignment. The upper row continues registering its one-joint-ahead feelers for several intervals and provides only a provisional search cue correlated with Arion's thrown position. Emilia physically verifies him by lamp and touch before acting. The retained lines settle into two narrow closed branches resembling his contracted-hand geometry only after his movement enters the mechanism. The physical route lines may expose crossings near protected boundaries but contain no protected content. Nothing is copied, absorbed, or transferred into Emilia, her lamp, chalk, or cord.

## Prose constraints

- Close third, past tense, entirely inside Emilia's senses, inference, desire, and admitted uncertainty.
- Open on the changed cord as mechanics, not a horror sting. Establish the stationary endpoints and lengthened joint before introducing the interface.
- Preserve Chapter 25 medical terms during ordinary movement: level only, assisted/observed, breath and hand reports, no right-arm load, no Foundation, no unentered method, stop on grade change. The later breach must be unmistakable.
- Do not introduce a carried aid, powered tool, credential, remote control, magic HUD, or automatic rescue device. Lamps, chalk, cord, and the pre-existing interface are all Emilia has.
- Make the interface compelling through visible states, latency, counterweights, and workmanship. No lore inscription explains it.
- Separate current state from the one-joint lead state on every crucial beat. The upper row's physically coupled warning can motivate a stop but cannot be narrated as certainty, prophecy, or proof of cause.
- Do not let Emilia deliberately test Arion. The unwanted correlation appears during necessary movement. She covers it before asking one direct question.
- Emilia asks whether another method entered or remains in Arion. He answers only, *I have used no method below.* Make Emilia notice that he answered a different question. The narrower truth makes the concealment credible without weakening its deception.
- Mera chooses and executes the far-side crossing. She is not sent because she is a Water practitioner. Emilia may ask what view is needed; Mera decides whether and how to obtain it.
- Keep Mera's page dry and her memory boundary closed. The rising water may carry pressure but no voice, face, memory montage, or forced content enters before Chapter 28/29.
- Arion's method use requires splinted-forearm contact, exhalation, and released trapped movement. It violates both medical load and the no-unentered-method condition. Before he loses contact, an unresolved branch must return and lodge more deeply along the same forearm/rib path so Chapter 28 inherits a worsened carried movement rather than an emptied one. Do not call it Foundation.
- The propagation is a mechanical/magical recurrence through connected seams, not a living Root chasing Arion.
- The useful outcome does not absolve Arion. Emilia's immediate rescue action and anger coexist.
- The interface retains its own physical route lines. Emilia neither absorbs nor copies them. She uses the upper row only as a provisional cue, verifies Arion by lamp and touch, and then covers the deeper routes.
- End on the difference between the two closed lead-state branches and Arion's physically unresponsive ring and small fingers, with Mera's lamp still visible beyond the water. No Malachar dialogue, confession, forgiveness, or route reunion.
