# Chapter packet — 06. What the instrument saw

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** I — The fracture
**Target length:** 3,600–4,100 words
**Access:** Paid
**Visible want:** End the expanded test without feeding the institutions a reason for permanent custody.
**Opposing want:** The mirror and hall translate Foundation, Water, Prism, and Void-adjacent responses into one cross-domain pattern; every observer fears a different failure if the field closes too early.
**Dramatic question:** Who has authority to stop a safety test after safety is redefined by what the test discovers?

## Voice and life

- Arion's decisive act is release, not power accumulation.
- Akamoto must not be omniscient; his request is defensible because people are still clearing the adjoining hall.
- Mera and Emilia act independently. Neither waits for Arion's instruction or exists to amplify him.
- Avoid chosen-one language, triumphant multi-element display, prophecy, “Confluence” coronation, or Malachar contact.
