## Access, model, and signature mechanics

- The Chapter 20 offer contains three distinct positions that must remain distinct in prose:
  1. **Corrective-operator responsibility:** Emilia accepts responsibility for every change she makes during the bounded session and preserves her Chapter 9 responsibility statement. Signing this opens a temporary, model-specific credential.
  2. **Technical-reality affirmation:** Emilia affirms that the observed cross-domain pattern occurred in the retained instrument record. She does not claim the pattern was false.
  3. **Consent-validity and present-suitability attestation:** To retain authority after the session, Emilia must attest that the observed pattern is consent-valid and presently suitable as a base state for custody decisions. She refuses this claim.
- Renn presents the three claims on one page with three separate brass signature tabs. Emilia physically separates the tabs before signing. In dialogue they call them *the repair is mine*, *the pattern happened*, and *the pattern may decide custody* rather than reciting abstract taxonomy.
- The temporary credential grants real edit authority over the derived adaptive custody model for one bounded session. It grants no access to the sealed original instrument, raw subject record, Mera's materials, Arion's private interview, the hammer/chamber, the exit witness, or live Roost fields.
- The model maps declared instrument states to possible protection, placement, and supervised-practice conditions. It is an advisory custody surface, not an autonomous legal authority and not a simulation of Arion's mind.
- The current model preserves its last valid source state when local participation ends. The status appears safe because remote retention continues to supply a stable pattern. This is the same hold-last-state rule Emilia identified in Chapter 9 and matched at protocol level in Chapter 18; neither Renn nor the model is thereby proved to own the unlisted course line or the Hollow Root infrastructure.
- Emilia shows Renn the Chapter 18 former-sponsor protocol attribution. Renn acknowledges that the notation comes from his training system and that he taught the retention-return rule. He does not confess to installing, ordering, or knowingly operating the unlisted line. His defensible position is that continuity prevents dangerous data loss when a local instrument fails.
- Emilia's first revision wrongly treats any local signal loss as withdrawal. Renn countertests it by cutting a simulated scaffold signal. The change suppresses a rescue recommendation that still needs the last known safe state, proving her first design unsafe. Emilia corrects the transition: accidental loss preserves the last incident state, raises an unknown/rescue flag, and cannot create a fresh placement recommendation; only authenticated participant withdrawal or a current witnessed refusal record changes participation to *withdrawn* and ends future placement use.
- Emilia's corrected manual exit is deliberately small and legible. When an authenticated withdrawal enters, it:
  - preserves the prior observed event and instrument self-state for incident review;
  - changes the current participant state to *withdrawn*;
  - prevents the retained pattern from generating placement or practice recommendations;
  - requires a new witnessed participation record before the person can re-enter the model.
- The exit cannot delete evidence, erase risk, stop a live structural emergency, or overrule separately authorized immediate-harm action. It prevents a past observation from impersonating present participation.
- Emilia cannot activate withdrawal for Arion herself. She makes the model consume the independently witnessed Chapter 16 statement *No further measurement* through its authorized council-record reference. Her revision makes his existing choice actionable and does not create a new refusal, open his Chapter 20 interview, or give her access to his current state.
- Emilia authenticates the revision under her maker mark while the temporary credential is valid. Once authenticated, the Forge record cannot remove it without leaving a superseding attributed revision.
- Renn administers the bench session and removes the credential after Emilia refuses the consent-validity and present-suitability attestation. This is professionally costly and emotionally real, but not arbitrary retaliation: keeping her active beyond the council offer's terms would let the council void the revision as former-sponsor interference and could invalidate the evidence trail.

## Prose constraints

- Close third, past tense, entirely inside Emilia's senses, knowledge, and inference.
- Open on her maker mark being recognized while the bench refuses her ordinary credential, echoing Chapter 9 without copying its opening syntax.
- The Forge must feel desirable: communal craft, visible seams, good tools, food or wit, and the bodily pleasure of a system responding cleanly.
- Do not convert Renn into a conspirator, coward, or mouthpiece. His continuity argument must identify a real safety problem the exit cannot solve.
- Do not retell Chapter 9. The new choice concerns present recommendation authority, not whether the original result occurred or whether Emilia signed the intake procedure.
- Keep *corrective responsibility*, *technical reality*, *consent-validity*, *present suitability for custody decisions*, *historical evidence*, and *present participation* distinct.
- Do not imply the model reads minds, predicts destiny, owns Arion, controls a live field, or possesses current medical data.
- The manual exit must preserve evidence while stopping recommendation use. It is not deletion, sabotage, a perfect consent solution, or a retroactive cure.
- Emilia may be tempted by access. Let her enjoy authority before she relinquishes it.
- Renn's removal of credentials must protect the revision's chain of authorship as well as enforce the offer; avoid a melodramatic firing scene.
- Mera's Chapter 20 breach remains neither excuse nor direct plot device. Emilia can recall Mera's exact refusal to claim Emilia's choice, not access the cup or adjudicate the friendship.
- Arion's current right-hand condition and Malachar contact remain unknown to Emilia.
- No edit in this chapter activates a live field, reaches the Roost, opens the Root, or causes Chapter 22's emergency.
- End on material persistence after personal lockout, not an aphorism about control, ownership, or freedom.
