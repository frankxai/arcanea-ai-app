# Chapter packet — 24. A manual door

## Story contract

**POV:** Emilia
**Movement:** III — The record
**Target length:** 2,800–3,600 words
**Story time / continuity position:** Late afternoon during the first day of Chapter 23's forty-eight-hour seal; the damaged service court is screened and the west retaining-wall perimeter is stable enough for exterior work, but the independent release review is incomplete and the Academy containment crew has not been admitted to the lower approach
**Location:** Roost upper maintenance shed and the screened west retaining wall above the sealed return
**Visible want by word 500:** Replace the council's automatic containment collar with a contact door that can offer the buried structure a path without measuring Arion, interpreting the response, or closing on its own
**Opposing want:** The three-Academy safety order requires a threshold collar that will drop containment pins if movement exceeds a calibrated load. Its engineers are protecting the court and everyone above it. Akamoto refuses any mechanism that can close across a possibly occupied interface, while Arion refuses every sensor or proxy attached to his body or tool
**Dramatic question:** Can Emilia make contact possible without turning uncertainty into an automatic act of custody?
**Irreversible turn:** After credentialed operator Tavin cycles the collar and it successfully stops an unoccupied weighted block, Emilia sacrifices the last usable teaching strip from her closed Forge credential in a second test. She removes the entire sensing and automatic-closing layer and signs a sealed local maker record for a door that reports only its own leaf state. Under separately stated limits and controls, the manual bridge is opened. The buried wall withdraws inward before the containment crew is ready
**Persistent cost:** The opening voids the official containment test, makes Emilia personally accountable under local rather than Forge authority, and exposes a concealed joint Academy closure seal. The inner door has no visible hold and begins returning while everyone remains outside. Movement IV must begin as an immediate witnessed-descent decision, not another deliberation
**Exit image:** Beyond Emilia's brass frame, a stone door stands open inward; three Academy authority marks occupy its inner edge, and no person stands on the other side

## Door and evidence mechanics

- **Council containment collar:** A temporary certified frame built for the west seam. A load vane and response fork compare movement against a prescribed band. Crossing the band releases two containment pins and a return bar without waiting for an operator. It does not read thoughts or identity, but it converts movement into automatic closure and therefore can trap whatever occupies the contact gap.
- **Credentialed operation and two honest tests:** Tavin has recovered from the Chapter 22 extraction and retains Forge operating authority. He alone cycles the certified collar. First, an unoccupied weighted block shoves outward through the test gap. The collar catches it before it crosses the chalk safety line. Emilia records the pass without qualification. Then she supplies and positions her own inert teaching strip as a flexible occupied-boundary surrogate before withdrawing behind the line. Tavin cycles the collar again. It passes: the load vane trips and both pins close within tolerance, cutting the strip and holding its halves on opposite sides. The certification definition counts this as containment success. Emilia cannot honestly call it safe for a possibly occupied interface.
- **No false technical proof:** A cut inert strip does not prove the buried response is alive or that a person would be harmed. It proves only that the automatic mechanism can close across an occupied boundary. The council's opposing case remains defensible: the same speed may stop an uncontrolled breach from reaching occupied courts.
- **Manual contact door:** Emilia retains the exterior oak-and-brass support frame, a human-operated shutter, its direct hinge indicator, and an unmarked flexible brass tongue. In the dramatized removals, the pin springs recoil into Akamoto's gloved hand, Mera labels the response fork *removed, not failed*, and Arion refuses the threshold scale when Emilia considers retaining it. The remaining automatic parts accumulate without recital.
- **Self-state only:** The indicator is physically tied to the shutter hinge. It can show that its own leaf is *shut*, *moving*, or *clear*. It cannot claim the route is safe, the buried wall is open, Arion is stable, or anyone should enter. Its state is called aloud and read back under the Chapter 23 public route rule.
- **Manual offer and control chain:** Akamoto alone opens or closes the shutter from a direct lever. Only after Mera calls *clear* and receives Akamoto's readback may Emilia lower the unmarked tongue across the hand-wide gap until it rests without a fastener or calibrated pressure against the sealed inner face. Any human stop call or outward return of the buried stone requires Emilia to withdraw the tongue, Mera to call and receive confirmation of withdrawal, and only then Akamoto to close the shutter. No signal is injected, no measurement is stored, and no automatic action remains.
- **Manual does not mean unprotected:** The court behind the operators is cleared to a fixed chalk boundary. Exterior anchors are verified independently of the buried surface. Shields protect the two manual stations from ordinary chip and recoil paths. No person occupies the space swept by shutter, tongue, or return stone.
- **Arion's boundary:** Arion permits his presence at the named infirmary-side observation mark, behind the no-entry chalk line, and nothing attached to his body, clothing, splint, or tool. He does not brace the frame, carry load, provide a sample, touch the tongue, or repeat Malachar's method. His right arm remains splinted, his two right fingers remain contracted, and his ribs remain bound. He may say stop at any time, and the manual offer ends.
- **Mera's boundary:** Mera observes visible material state and makes a local time record. She does not read a memory, open either red-threaded vessel, infer Arion's interior condition, or serve as his private witness. Nemi retains that role from Chapter 23.
- **Bounded jurisdictions:** The three-Academy order controls certification and admission to the lower approach. Akamoto may authorize only a non-entry exterior structural trial inside the Roost's screened perimeter. He cannot authorize descent, certify Emilia through the Forge, or authenticate the working name *Hollow Root*.
- **Akamoto's authority:** Akamoto names the operators, no-entry boundary, stop calls, and absence of automatic closure. The general self-state rule from Chapter 23 remains public; the names and location of this trial do not.
- **Neris's role:** Neris carries and accurately states the three-Academy safety condition. She is not a villain, technician, or rubber stamp. She records that removing the containment layer ends the official test and that the opening cannot be represented as Academy-authorized evidence. She remains to witness what physically happens.
- **Sealed supplement:** Mera opens no existing folio. She prepares a separate supplement cross-referenced to Chapter 23's black report. Akamoto passes its black thread through his existing named Roost hold under the same original forty-eight-hour expiry, outside-review requirement, release conditions, and no-silent-renewal rule. Mera adds only her personal local-act witness mark. Operator names, precise west geometry, the opening, and the three-mark plate enter the supplement; the opening does not restart the clock.
- **The response:** During the first manual offer, the brass tongue stays still. After the call *clear* is read back and before anyone touches Arion or the inner face, the old stone behind the frame withdraws inward. The device reports only its own already-clear shutter; it does not measure or name the force opening the buried wall.
- **The revealed seal:** The inner edge bears one old joint closure plate with three separate, legible Academy authority marks under the same mineral accretion as the buried surface. Its age and purpose are not fully authenticated in this chapter. Its physical position behind six-year uncut exterior work establishes prior institutional contact strongly enough that the Academies can no longer describe the structure as merely external to them.
- **Closing window:** The inner stone stops open without a visible latch, wedge, or holding mechanism, then reclaims the first fixed exterior interval after the plate is recorded. Mera records visible outward wall movement, not a door-state reading or predicted duration. The tongue is already withdrawn and confirmed. Akamoto begins the agreed manual-shutter closure; Chapter 25 must establish descent terms and reopen the exterior shutter before anyone enters.
