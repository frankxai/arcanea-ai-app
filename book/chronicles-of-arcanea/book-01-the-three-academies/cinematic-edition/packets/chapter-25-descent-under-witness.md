# Chapter packet — 25. Descent under witness

## Story contract

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** IV — The refusal
**Target length:** 2,800–3,500 words
**Story time / continuity position:** Continuous with Chapter 24; Akamoto's exterior shutter is moving closed after the buried inner wall began returning; the Chapter 23 forty-eight-hour hold and Chapter 24 supplement remain on their original clock
**Location:** Screened west retaining-wall threshold, then the visible first landing and a short level vestibule inside the buried structure
**Visible want by word 500:** Secure a descent in which Mera and Emilia enter as people with their own stop authority, not as instruments attached to his risk, while his medical and custody limits remain real
**Opposing want:** The council order controls lower admission and requires one unambiguous rule for stopping a suspended descent; Akamoto can authorize only exterior action and wants fewer bodies below; Anet's current restriction bars Arion from climbing, load work, and unsupervised movement; the physical window is closing
**Dramatic question:** Can three damaged people enter under terms that do not quietly make one responsible for the choices or safety of the others?
**Irreversible turn:** Neris obtains one named, non-transfer evidence-preservation admission; Anet grants stair-sledge ingress under narrow medical limits; the council accepts separate personal stops under one mechanical rule: any named stop freezes the winch; its caller must clear that stop before Arion may request resumed lowering, and Anet must recheck him while Neris's admission remains active. A stop by Neris ends the admission and cannot be cleared locally. The team then repeats Chapter 24's manual offer. No person or order controls the buried wall; it happens to withdraw far enough for entry
**Persistent cost:** Mera leaves all sensitive sources and prior records above; Emilia leaves her receipt and severed strip; Arion enters without hammer, Gate authorization, or the disclosed existence of his unentered method. Akamoto and Anet remain outside. After the sledge and lowering lines are withdrawn, the buried inner wall returns closed and Akamoto closes the exterior shutter, withdrawing adult rescue and institutional sight. The order can authorize help only if the trio later presents itself at the threshold; it cannot retrieve them or compel the wall to open
**Exit image:** The last upper light closes over Akamoto's gloved hand and Anet's white sleeve; inside, the water in three unequal stone basins returns lamp flame and mineral edge but omits Arion's lowered hand

## Admission, medical, and custody mechanics

- **Immediate closure:** Chapter 24's manual chain completes first. Akamoto closes the exterior shutter after Mera calls *moving* and he reads it back. The shutter crosses the hand-wide exterior gap and reaches its own stop without meeting the buried wall. The wall's continuing return remains audible beyond it; the shutter neither arrests nor controls that movement.
- **Council authority:** Neris requests one named evidence-preservation descent for Arion, Mera, and Emilia. The issued order:
  - authorizes one ingress if the currently witnessed interface opens again and one later return if the trio can present itself at that threshold;
  - carries no transfer, placement, custody, source-access, or automatic-consent effect;
  - grants no remote control over people or the manual frame;
  - expires if no inward withdrawal follows the repeated manual offer before the witnessed window ends, or after the named return attempt;
  - leaves Akamoto as exterior local custodian, Neris as institutional order witness, and Anet as medical stop authority.
- **Akamoto's limit:** He may reopen/close his exterior shutter and operate the Roost descent rig once the council order exists. He cannot authorize lower admission, authenticate the structure, waive medical limits, or order any member of the trio to enter.
- **Repeated manual offer:** After the order is read, Akamoto reopens the exterior shutter with the same state call/readback used in Chapter 24. Emilia offers the same unmarked flexible tongue under the same limits: contact only, no fastening, scale, automation, or inference. If the buried wall does not withdraw, the admission expires. If it withdraws, Emilia removes the tongue and Mera confirms it clear before anyone crosses.
- **Anet's clearance:** Arion is cleared only for ingress on a measured, low-backed rigid stair-sledge and assisted movement on a level surface. The sledge fits the shoulder-width aperture, runs on lowering lines independently anchored in the exterior court, and transfers no load into the buried wall or Emilia's frame. Load travels through seat and left thigh, not right ribs/arm. Conditions: no climbing, no right-arm load, no Foundation brace, no unentered method, continuous self-report of breath and right-hand sensation, immediate stop on changed sensation or route grade, and no unsupervised separation from both named companions.
- **No medical fiction:** Anet cannot make this safe. She states that the clearance permits a bounded risk; it does not improve the injury. Arion may refuse without adverse classification. If he understates symptoms, later harm remains his breach rather than evidence that clearance was sound.
- **Mera's custody:** Mera places her red-threaded rain vial and derived cup frame in one locked exterior-cradle compartment. Her separate document sleeve—containing the boundary-only refusal copy and closed meal-story coordinates—enters a second compartment. Mera retains one release key; Nemi accepts the second as non-access custodian. Neither can open alone. Nemi receives no disclosure, transfer, or interpretation authority.
- **Existing records and claims:** Lio's candidate amendment remains in the candidate registry. Emilia's maker record and Mera's separate personal limit note remain in the safety tray. The two Chapter 22 living-claim vessels remain separately sealed with Nemi and Iven. The Chapter 23 report and Chapter 24 supplement remain sealed on their original clock. The lost rig and its derivatives remain inaccessible below. None is retrieved, merged into Mera's new cradle authority, copied, or treated as descent authorization.
- **Emilia's custody:** Her read-only receipt and the two halves of the inert teaching strip remain above in the safety tray under her maker mark. The original instrument/model and Chapter 14 exit witness remain inaccessible/sealed. She carries no powered or certified instrument.
- **Arion's custody:** His repaired hammer remains inside the sealed Chapter 16 chamber under its existing custody state. He carries no tool, sample, or source record.
- **Permitted descent kit:** Three hand lamps; unmarked chalk; one coiled manual route cord with simple tension bells; blank pages selected by Mera; the low-backed stair-sledge and lowering lines. Emilia carries the cord down first and places its weighted bell-board on the level landing, entirely inside the threshold, without fastening anything to the unknown structure. No cord crosses the buried wall when it closes. The cord carries no data, instruction, automatic pull, or exterior rescue function.

## Separate stop authorities

- **Arion:** may stop his own descent or the team's forward movement for bodily change, load, or a demand that he elicit a response. He will not carry another person's consent, memory, instrument judgment, or rescue obligation. He sincerely accepts the prohibition while withholding the method's existence, so Anet cannot evaluate what he has not disclosed.
- **Mera:** may stop for source pressure, involuntary memory contact, unclear custody, or a request to open/identify content. She will not carry Arion's interior account, decide Emilia's risk, or turn observation into ownership. She carries blank paper only.
- **Emilia:** may stop for changed route state, interface ambiguity, lost manual exit, or any attempt to treat her mechanical observation as a human decision. She will not infer anyone's consent from a system state or operate a remote person-control surface.
- **Akamoto:** controls the exterior shutter and descent rig only. Any named stop freezes the winch. The caller must explicitly clear their own stop before Arion may request resumed lowering, and Anet must recheck him while Neris's admission remains active. A stop by Neris ends the admission and cannot be cleared locally. Akamoto does not gain authority over the person who called the stop.
- **Anet:** may stop Arion's ingress for breath, hand sensation, sledge load, or route grade. Her authority is medical, not investigative or custodial.
- **Neris:** may stop the council admission if its exact use conditions are breached. She cannot override personal or medical stops or convert the descent into custody.
