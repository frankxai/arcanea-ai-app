## Report layers and sealing mechanics

- **Public incident notice — thin white sheet:** may state that a mechanical service-court failure occurred, five people were recovered, no deaths occurred, and access is closed pending safety review. It does not disclose survivor identities, exact route geometry, living-source custody, Arion's condition, unverified buried-response evidence, lost derivative labels, the preliminary failure chain, or the restricted report's timing.
- **Permanent public Roost procedure — brass-edged rule strip:** Akamoto places the Chapter 15 correction into standing field rules under his own name:
  - every divided-access route must show route state to all active teams;
  - two-way route confirmation must preserve distinguishable route and condition terms;
  - any aid reports its own mounted state only and carries no decision authority;
  - failed, ambiguous, or environmentally changed state stops entry until locally revalidated;
  - a board marked *sealed* is not proof that a physical route has ceased to exist or move.
- Akamoto's exact responsibility statement is: “I barred the aid. I left one voice to carry two route names. The wrong-route entry gave Lio the second compression. That procedure was mine.” He does not claim sole cause, ask Lio to respond, or seek absolution. He removes the old black route strip and signs the replacement under his own name.
- Lio is present because the rule changes his candidate route record and future trial conditions. From his chair, he aligns the replacement strip over the old board and says: “Write *moving*, not *open*. I called a route, not a guarantee.” Nothing further is asked of him about Akamoto.
- **Restricted rescue report — black-thread folio:** contains the preliminary ordinary failure chain, fixed court cross-section, five-person exit accounting, named competent acts, lost rig and derivatives, Lio's route call, Emilia's local route-state operation, Mera's bounded custody verification, Arion's stated structural exposure, and the council assessment *humane and strategically damaging*.
- The preliminary mechanical chain remains provisional: cistern pressure parted a corroded gate pin, water entered the old seam, and the decayed brace sheared under ordinary load. No field/model action appears in the sequence; buried-response identity, source, life, intent, and motive remain unauthenticated.
- **Medical redactions — translucent sheet:** Anet keeps the sheet under her own control, then aligns and seals it over the restricted folio so Arion's permitted sentence remains visible while all other medical fields become unreadable blocks.
- **Seal scope:** survivor identities and current living-source custody; exact west-return/vent/drain geometry; Arion's medical detail and unentered method; unauthenticated response recurrence; labels of lost restricted derivatives.
- **Seal authority and limit:** Akamoto uses named Roost local safety authority for forty-eight hours. Release review requires the service-court perimeter secured, each living-source custodian consulted on identity disclosure, medical redactions set by Anet, and an independent non-council route-security reader appointed. The council may request extension but cannot silently renew Akamoto's order.
- Mera's first abstract already bears her personal mark and states *five recovered through the west return* and *two protected vessels preserved*. She removes those details only after seeing Arion's blank witness line and recognizing she wrote her own discovery credit before asking boundaries.
- Akamoto does not lecture her about inference. He lays the public notice and closure board on the table, points to Arion's generic posted movement restriction, and asks what Lio's publicly visible splint adds. Mera combines those facts with the infirmary threshold and reconstructs the west-return location closely enough to stop publication herself; the notice never names the structural exposure or incident time.
- Mera retains and owns her observed upper-court account; it is not confiscated. Publishing it during the forty-eight-hour hold would be an attributable breach she elects not to commit.
- Mera signs in **personal capacity as local act witness**, not as recognized Athenaeum witness. Her signature verifies that Akamoto named the error, procedure, sealed scope, reason, interval, and review conditions. It does not endorse his judgment or restore her standing.
- Mera's dissent is attached as a separate visible page: she argues that the public notice should include the provisional ordinary failure chain once the perimeter is secured and that the seal must expire rather than drift into inquiry custody.
- Mera's rain-memory vial and Chapter 20 derived cup remain unopened, self-carried, and outside the rescue report. Their red threads never become part of Akamoto's black report seal.

## Prose constraints

- Close third, past tense, entirely inside Mera's senses, knowledge, and inference.
- Open on Mera's public abstract taking rainwater through the paper or another concrete failure of the document—not an inquiry summary or ethical maxim.
- Give each layer one physical form and use its formal name only once in prose: thin white notice, brass-edged rule strip, black-thread folio, translucent medical sheet. Keep the red-thread vessels off the table. No character recites a layer taxonomy.
- Do not reopen the rescue action. Use five handprints, damaged route board, lost rig line, splints, wet papers, and named report entries as aftermath objects.
- Mera's first public draft must overreach in a concrete, sympathetic way. She corrects because people name boundaries, not because she receives a moral lecture.
- Akamoto's Chapter 15 responsibility statement must match the existing record: he barred an unvalidated interface, retained flags with a single verbal relay, and that procedure contributed to wrong-route entry and second compression. He does not erase hook failure, environmental load, or other causes.
- Do not let Lio forgive, condemn, or summarize Akamoto for the reader. His route competence and chosen limits are enough.
- Lock Arion's boundary exchange to four blunt lines: “What may I write?” “Structural exposure. Method not entered.” “Private witness?” “Nemi.” No apology or interpretation follows.
- Mera does not open or access the rain vial, derived cup, rescued vessels, lost derivatives, Arion, or any private medical record.
- Mera may sign as local act witness under Akamoto's named authority; the prose must state this does not restore Athenaeum recognition.
- Akamoto's seal is fixed at forty-eight hours and cannot renew silently. This is not indefinite benevolent secrecy.
- The public route procedure may be released immediately because it exposes no identities or vulnerable geometry.
- Keep containment risk specific and inferential; no generic “they will come for us” speech.
- Do not make traceability a moral victory. The public still lacks the evidence, and Mera's signature materially legitimizes the delay.
- Keep the final seal exchange shorter than the threading action. Mera requires “forty-eight hours, named reviewer, no silent renewal”; Akamoto writes each condition. She signs only after black thread passes through his authority line and attaches dissent as a separate page. End on separate thread systems, not concluding ethics dialogue.
