# Chapter packet — 10. A beautiful weight

## Story contract

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** II — The measure
**Target length:** 3,200–3,600 words
**Story time / continuity position:** Several days after the completed conditional transfer to the Roost; Arion separately accepts one Foundation exercise after reviewing its Slate Row application
**Location:** Luminary Academy civic refectory and Foundation exchange hall, reached by witnessed escort from the Roost
**Visible want by word 500:** Learn one ordinary load-sharing practice he can use in the public repair house he wants to build
**Opposing want:** The instructor needs a mixed-skill group to complete a safe communal support exercise and quietly protects novices by assigning extra strain to students classified as durable
**Dramatic question:** Will Arion preserve a beautiful working structure when its hidden fairness depends on who can endure being used?
**Irreversible turn:** He exposes the concealed load assignment and interrupts the lesson instead of taking the excess into himself
**Persistent cost:** The hall remains safe, but Arion is removed from the exchange; a visibly overburdened student thanks him in private and refuses to support his complaint publicly
**Exit image:** A perfect radial ceiling above a chalk floor map where one student's line is dark with everyone else's weight

## Reader experience

**Emotional movement:** guarded arrival → craft delight and belonging → bodily recognition of hidden cost → principled disruption without social victory
**Pleasure lane:** repair stories, invisible-seam competition, civic food, professional vanity, sketching the repair house among people who understand the dream
**Academy/world texture:** Luminary civic crews make public responsibility social, skilled, and aspirational; the institution's best practice is genuinely useful
**Magic in material form:** Foundation students form a live support circle that routes roof load through breath, stance, stone contact, and assigned structural tolerances
**Material consequence:** The exercise hall holds; the unequal routing becomes physically visible; Arion loses access to a technique his district needs
**One image the reader should remember a week later:** Twelve students under an elegant ceiling while one pair of boots slowly sinks into warm stone

The chapter must work as fiction without an accompanying image or lore note.

## Prose constraints

- The Academy lesson must change status, trust, and the repair-house dream; it cannot read as a curriculum demonstration.
- Show why people love the Luminary Academy before exposing its failure.
- Belis must be correct that abrupt equalization could injure novices; her disputed choice is concealment and classification, not cartoon cruelty.
- Arion must not solve the conflict by taking Nara's burden, collapsing the hall, or delivering a speech about fairness.
- Give the refectory scene appetite, vanity, and humor unrelated to custody terminology.
- Avoid using “beautiful” as generic awe; attach beauty to a working load path and its human price.
