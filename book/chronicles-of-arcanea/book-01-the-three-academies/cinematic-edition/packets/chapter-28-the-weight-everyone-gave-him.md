**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** IV — The refusal
**Target length:** 2,700–3,500 words

**Irreversible turn:** Beside three small chalk marks, Arion tells Emilia where each contact occurred, what he did with breath and body, what he felt afterward, and what he concealed. The first mark includes the warm-stone voice's Malachar Lumenbright self-identification and Arion's false burn report; the next two cover the later bodily uses and the returned branch. He then names Malachar's present offer. Rather than accept private relief, Arion refuses to give Malachar the stop, asks Emilia to watch the floor joint while he moves his arm, and lets her decide anew whether she will help him arrange a longer hold away from visible stone

Emilia learns that he knowingly made her observation part of an undisclosed bodily method and does not forgive him. The returned movement holds without observed worsening for now but is not removed; his hand and rib injury remain worse. Mera stays physically separated and turns toward the uninspected continuation of the retained-return route

## Malachar pressure and evidence boundary

- **Perceptual channel:** Warmth travels through cold operator-bay stone and arrives in Arion as the remembered sensation of sun remaining on a wall after the street enters shade. Italic dialogue is heard by Arion alone, as in Chapter 19. Emilia sees only his attention change and receives his report.
- **Attractive truth:** Malachar accurately recognizes that Arion is most often asked to choose after a structure has already begun to fall and that other people's refusals can arrive too late to spare him pain. He does not flatter Arion's power or promise victory.
- **Embodied offer:** Warmth lies alongside the painful branch so Arion can feel the possibility of one continuous path, but the movement does not ease before he accepts. Limit the entire exchange to: *They ask for your choice after the weight has reached bone.* / *Give me the choosing. I will set one path and keep it until the movement ends.* / “And if it returns?” / *It will return by the path I chose.* No additional thesis dialogue or lore.
- **False promise:** Malachar offers relief without the risk of another person's refusal. The cost is not hidden: Arion would no longer own the stop, the destination, or the next use of his body as a path.
- **Tragic posture:** Malachar's offer comes from intolerance of witnessed suffering and his fatal belief that compassion grants authority to remove dangerous choices. He is calm, exact, and emotionally credible; never gleeful, taunting, omniscient, or generically evil.
- **Evidence limit:** The locked canon establishes Malachar, but the chapter's public evidence is only Arion's report that a voice identifying itself as Malachar spoke through warmth. No interface line, water response, Academy plate, or lower-structure movement authenticates the speaker or locates him outside sealed Shadowfen.
- **Refusal:** Arion does not win by enduring pain theatrically. He refuses because the offered method gives its stop to Malachar. He speaks the refusal aloud so Emilia can witness it, accepts that she may report him or stop helping, and then asks rather than assumes her participation.
