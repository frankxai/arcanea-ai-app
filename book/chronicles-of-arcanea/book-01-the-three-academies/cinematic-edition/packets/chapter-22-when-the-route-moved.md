# Chapter packet — 22. When the route moved

## Story contract

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** III — The record
**Target length:** 3,400–4,300 words
**Story time / continuity position:** The morning after Chapter 21; Arion's right forearm still carries the Chapter 19 strain, Mera is no longer his chosen personal witness, Emilia has no Forge credential or sponsor, and Lio remains splinted and non-weight-bearing on the Roost wind terrace
**Location:** The Roost service court, west maintenance return, wind terrace above it, and the lower inquiry walk cut off when the buried structure opens
**Visible want by word 500:** Reach the five-person inquiry team trapped below the service court before rising water and settling stone close the usable crawl clearance
**Opposing want:** The official route is failing; a passive structural rig supplies the only continuous load map; the council team wants it preserved long enough to guide rescue; Mera refuses to abandon living consent records to rupture; Emilia refuses to send anyone through an exit whose state is hidden; Arion's body is already carrying a strain he has concealed
**Dramatic question:** Can the group rescue every person without turning one student's body, one witness's source, or one instrument into the unquestioned price of certainty?
**External trigger:** During a council-authorized no-field visual and mechanical survey, accumulated cistern pressure parts the north rain gate's corroded holding pin and drives water into a six-year-old drain seam. The decayed brace beneath the service court shears under ordinary water load. No Chapter 21 edit, model action, field initiation, measurement pulse, or protagonist choice causes the breach
**Irreversible turn:** Lio identifies the moving west maintenance return; Nemi and Iven identify their own living-source obligations while Mera verifies custody and recommends priority, and Akamoto records institutional copies abandoned; Emilia exposes every available exit state with local physical indicators; Arion then uses Malachar's bodily extension without disclosing it, taking the route's trapped movement into himself long enough to open the west return
**Persistent cost:** All five people escape and the two living-claim vessels remain with their named custodians, but the passive rig falls into the opening. The route's branching movement remains inside Arion from right hand through forearm and ribs after the stone closes. The council records the outcome as humane and strategically damaging; the lost rig removes the cleanest structural map while Arion becomes easier for the buried pressure to find
**Exit image:** Five wet handprints dry on the ledge. Arion tries to add a sixth and cannot open his right hand; below, the rig's white line slips out of sight

## Rescue geometry and authority

- The survey is passive and bounded: sight mirrors, plumb lines, chalk seam marks, and one mechanical rig that records displacement. It does not initiate Foundation, compare Arion, open a Gate, query the Root, or use Emilia's custody-model revision.
- **Fixed cross-section before failure:** The upper service court sits above the lower inquiry walk. The east stair descends along one side. The west maintenance return rises behind the opposite retaining wall. The north drain enters a central filled opening. The wind terrace overlooks the full court. Establish this once before the collapse and do not add unexplained rooms or routes during action.
- **External failure chain:** overnight rain fills the north cistern; accumulated pressure parts a corroded holding pin in the stuck relief gate during the scheduled mechanical survey; the old drain seam accepts the water; a corroded brace under ordinary load shears; the service-court floor settles; a buried responsive structure opens along an existing filled joint. The timing is dangerous, not proof of life, identity, source, or intent.
- In the live collapse, show only three causal events: the rain gate releases, water punches through the old seam, and the brace shears. Reserve the corroded pin, six-year interval, and full reconstruction for the temporary preservation record in the brief aftermath.
- Five people are isolated on the lower inquiry walk: Nemi as Roost local custodian, Tavin as Forge equipment operator, Iven as Athenaeum catalogue observer, Physician Anet as Luminary structural medic, and Surveyor Pell for the council. All retain competence and a locked exit order.
- The lower team still receives air through the west vent. Rising water and shrinking crawl clearance are the immediate threats. The fallen east landing leaves a triangular voice-and-vent slit above the lower walk; once dust and water erase direct sight and narrow that slit, a fixed brass service speaking tube in the west wall remains the lower team's communication channel. Lio uses a separate terrace tube to speak to the upper court.
- The passive rig is not mere property. It continues to show load transfer after sight is lost and could identify why the structure opened. The council surveyor's effort to preserve it is defensible because rescuers need a map and future students need the cause recorded.
- The east stair is the declared evacuation route but loses two supports. The west maintenance return is shown sealed on the current board after the Chapter 15 divided-access suspension and Chapter 16 lower-field closure. Lio recognizes from the terrace flags and the old course route that the water load has moved the return rather than mechanically destroyed it.
- Lio remains on the wind terrace with his leg splinted and bears no weight. He uses the route board, sight-mirror flags, and a speaking tube. He does not enter the collapse, remove the splint, or become miraculously recovered.
- Akamoto holds overall local rescue authority from the upper court. He names stop conditions and authorizes Emilia as emergency local route-state operator. He does not authorize measurement of Arion or the Root.
- Emilia has no Forge credential. Under Akamoto's emergency Roost appointment she may operate only the local mechanical route panel and attach three physical indicators: *open*, *unknown*, and *closed*. She does not access the custody model, original instrument, read-write Forge systems, or a live participant state.
- Mera's rain-memory vial and Chapter 20 derived cup remain self-carried in a closed two-vessel frame on the upper court. They are not among the trapped evidence.
- The lower team has six inquiry vessels and a case of institutional duplicates. Nemi and Iven identify their own living-source obligations and explicitly choose to carry one current living-consent vessel each. Mera verifies visible labels, custody, and their spoken confirmation—no memory opening—and recommends priority. Akamoto records four institutional derivatives and the duplicate case abandoned under emergency seal. Mera does not decide which personal records matter on anyone's behalf.
- The group must not carry the rig. Emilia keeps its state line visible only until the last usable route readings are copied manually. The council surveyor then chooses to release it rather than make a person stay attached.
- Competent acts and exit order are locked: Iven exits first with one self-identified living-claim vessel; Tavin exits second after hand-copying and disconnecting the last rig readings; Anet exits third after stabilizing Pell's shoulder and setting the crawl order; Nemi exits fourth with the second self-identified vessel after calling lower-side states and counting every passage; Pell releases the rig and exits fifth.

## Malachar extension and bodily cost

- Arion receives no new instruction and no live conversation from Malachar in this chapter. He applies the Chapter 19 method at a larger scale: identify the blocked return, locate the last bodily path, take trapped movement on the exhale, and release it when a freer structural path accepts it.
- After the collapse, Akamoto authorizes one bounded external Foundation brace for rescue: visible contact, no load transfer into a body, and a named stop on hand numbness or route-state loss. His later bodily stop terminates that permission. Malachar's extension occurs afterward as unauthorized, non-Foundation action.
- The bodily extension is not standard Foundation practice, a new Gate technique, or an Academy-approved method. Arion conceals its source and uses it after Akamoto ends the authorized brace and orders a bodily stop.
- The west return is physically jammed by opposed stone loads. Ordinary levering and the bounded Foundation brace reach a safe incomplete stop but leave too little clearance for the trapped team.
- Arion's existing right-hand/forearm strain echoes the return angle. He braces the outside of the right forearm against the moving seam and sets his stance through the right ribs and left heel. The route's last movement enters nerve, joint, bone, and intercostal tissue rather than disappearing.
- Ordinary bracing already holds a narrow gap. The movement lets the stone travel one additional handspan into a temporary free pocket, producing a shoulder-width crawl slot; Arion does not lift the whole structure or overpower the buried response. Emilia's visible state flags and Nemi's below-side confirmation determine when people may move.
- The concealed load transfer complicates the rescue once: while Iven is inside the crawl, Emilia's panel changes from *open* to *unknown*. She calls stop. Iven holds position, and Nemi physically checks the pocket from below before Emilia may restore *open* and movement resumes.
- Arion holds the transferred movement continuously through all five exits. After the fifth person and two living-claim vessels clear, the free pocket collapses before he can return the final branch to stone. The rig and abandoned derivatives fall below. Arion releases contact, but a branching pressure remains from his two contracted fingers through the forearm and along three right ribs. Ordinary Foundation grounding cannot find a free end.
- After closure, Arion feels the buried response recur along the retained movement and infers that it can find the same path again, setting up the later Chapter 27 beacon effect without authenticating life, identity, intent, source, or Hollow Root connection. No one learns Malachar's role in this chapter.
