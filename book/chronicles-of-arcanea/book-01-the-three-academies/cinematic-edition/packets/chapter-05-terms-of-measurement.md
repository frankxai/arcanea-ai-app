# Chapter packet — 05. Terms of measurement

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** I — The fracture
**Target length:** 3,600–4,000 words
**Access:** Paid
**Visible want:** Set enforceable limits on a routine Foundation intake, finish it, and return to Slate Row.
**Opposing want:** Akamoto, the three Academies, seven House observers, and local authorities each need a different certainty before Arion can leave containment.
**Dramatic question:** Can Arion negotiate consent when refusal itself is being treated as evidence of danger?
**Irreversible turn:** He accepts a bounded Foundation-only test after securing repair-ledger delivery, tool access, named witnesses, and a closed memory channel; the observers expand the protocol when the hall answers from more than one direction.
**Persistent cost:** Arion continues after recognizing the changed test because stopping now will be interpreted as proof that he cannot be released.
**Exit image:** The measurement ring closes around the visible seam in his hammer.
