## Offer and synchronization mechanics

- Each participant receives a private written offer because health, family aid, source access, and maker discipline cannot lawfully be disclosed across rooms without consent.
- **Arion's offer:** immediate specialist treatment for his resonance injuries and funded structural repair for Slate Row in exchange for protective Luminary residency, supervised Foundation practice, and a classification process with defined review dates. It is materially generous and custody-bearing; no automatic permanent confinement is claimed.
- **Mera's offer:** restored Athenaeum witness recognition, authenticated access to the deep restoration catalogue, and a seat as inquiry source reader if she deposits the sealed rain-memory for a six-hour bounded opening under inquiry custody. The offer guarantees automatic return, seals all derivatives under the same disputed-custody terms, and preserves her right to append dissent. It could answer her central question and make her objections effective.
- **Emilia's offer:** temporary restoration of laboratory authority over the custody model if she signs responsibility for the intake result and proceeds under review. The offer does not yet require her to endorse the consent model; that conflict becomes Chapter 21.
- The panel's exposed service strip is visibly marked *joint continuity* and carries the same mirror-state, retention, and source-recovery path shown in Chapter 16. This establishes that the interview display uses the council service that also carried intake status; it does not reveal offer content or prove the source of any embodied trace.
- A joint-status panel shows three conditions: *accepted*, *incompatible response entered*, or *no incompatibility entered*. During private deliberation, the third state appears green. It includes offer received, silence, ongoing review, and any unsigned response. Refusing one's own offer closes only that offer; a separate package-incompatibility control challenges the claim that the remaining private outcomes form one coherent protection plan.
- The concurrent structure has a defensible purpose: no participant can use another's confidential need as bargaining leverage, and no institution can improve one deal in response to a leaked private concession. Its failure is that green visual continuity and scripted “compatible terms” phrasing invite each participant to infer the others are accepting.
- Neris, interviewing Mera, states that “compatible” is not “accepted” but refuses to disclose the other terms or answers. The synchronized design remains pressuring without making Neris dishonest.
- While any private offer remains open, the panel renews shared green on a fixed cadence. Each renewal contains one brief intake pressure-beat. A package incompatibility entered inside that beat changes the shared state immediately; at any other point it routes through ordinary review. The repeating cadence and intake beat are absent from the local interview instructions.
- Mera's unaccessed embodied trace from intake contains a felt interval correlated with Arion's local refusal and one joint-service renewal. Its source class remains unresolved; it does not authenticate Arion's interior memory or perception. Her own timestamped refusal lets her establish the cadence's current phase, and the trace gives her the intake beat's position inside it. The green is still a retained package state, not acceptance. The trace is not the sealed rain-memory vial, the folded meal story, or any Chapter 18 record.
- In Chapter 7 Mera recorded this trace as *unaccessed*; in Chapter 14 Arion asked her to keep it that way, and she promised unless he gave a different answer. He has not.
- Alone during the deliberation interval, Mera touches one drop from her personal drinking cup to the violet scar in her palm and follows one pressure interval inward. The fragment gives her the service cadence around a refusal correlated with Arion's, not his thoughts or an authenticated memory. She stops there.
- The act creates a derived memory-bearing cup. Mera immediately covers and seals it under her name. She does not pour it into the interview basin or let the institution duplicate it.
- A normal package-incompatibility filing remains available after the panel renews green, but it would enter later review. Mera cannot know that a transfer review will follow or that Arion will accept; she fears the shared recommendation will harden before the lawful review can correct it.
- During the next pressure-beat, Mera uses the room's authorized package-incompatibility control. The joint service must withdraw “compatible terms,” display amber, and open all privacy shutters for direct clarification.
- The timing information is effective but not magical access to current thoughts. Mera does not learn the other offer contents, responses, Arion's Chapter 19 contact, or Emilia's intentions from the trace.

## Prose constraints

- Close third, past tense, entirely inside Mera's senses, knowledge, and inference.
- Open on the other two doors closing in reflected water or another material status pressure—not a council summary, villain speech, dream, or generic interview room.
- Do not show Arion's or Emilia's private interview content until the shutters open and they choose what to disclose.
- Keep “compatible,” “accepted,” “received,” “silent,” and “incompatible” distinct every time.
- The interviews are synchronized but not telepathic. Status service carries state only, not offer content, thought, or memory.
- Neris never lies. She may refuse disclosure and must articulate the legitimate privacy rationale.
- Mera must try questions, a direct-clarification request, and her own refusal before violating the trace.
- The accessed fragment must stay minimal and embodied. No exposition dump, hidden council conversation, future vision, mind reading, Malachar contact, Chapter 18 knowledge, or full replay of intake.
- The scene must state plainly that the rain-memory vial remains unopened and unrelated to the accessed trace.
- Mera seals the derived cup immediately and does not use it twice.
- Successful intervention does not excuse the violation. Avoid “ends justify means” narration, self-forgiveness, or Arion functioning only as moral judge.
- Arion's refusal and Emilia's non-answer remain their acts. Mera interrupts procedure; she does not decide for either.
- Arion recognizes the source through the hidden cadence and Mera's admission, not mystical intuition.
- Arion does not expose his right-hand condition or Malachar contact. Mera notices the tightened sleeve and his refusal of her witness role but does not infer the hidden cause.
- End on physical separation and the concealed hand, not an ethics slogan, apology speech, or relationship verdict.
