# Chapter packet — 13. Body before miracle

## Story contract

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** II — The measure
**Target length:** 3,400–3,800 words
**Story time / continuity position:** The morning after Chapter 12; first Roost field lesson Arion separately accepts under his daily medical terms
**Location:** Akamoto Roost lower service court and marked practice wall
**Visible want by word 500:** Demonstrate enough control to earn unsupervised movement beyond the Roost perimeter and regain a path toward Slate Row
**Opposing want:** Akamoto requires ordinary load work and stops Arion whenever he compensates for another student's mistake; he will not reward spectacular control built from self-erasure
**Dramatic question:** Can Arion allow a safe structure to fail instead of making his body the hidden support?
**Irreversible turn:** He reads the practice wall's last free movement and lets it fall inside the marked zone
**Persistent cost:** The collapse sound triggers the Slate Row event; one student calls him dangerous, his movement clearance does not advance, and the failed wall makes his method socially visible
**Exit image:** The repair-house sketch spread across fallen practice blocks, with Lio drawing a door that is designed to break outward

**Emotional movement:** frustrated determination → boredom and humiliation → attraction to a precise physical law → triggered panic under safe collapse → tentative craft friendship
**Pleasure lane:** outdoor work, competitive hauling, terrible Roost breakfast, practical teasing, sketch collaboration, the satisfaction of a failure that behaves as designed
**Academy/world texture:** The Roost teaches dangerous practitioners through body position, ordinary tools, visible hazard zones, and repeatable stops before any spectacular practice
**Magic in material form:** Foundation reading begins with grain, prior movement, friction, and the last direction a structure travelled freely; no force projection is required
**Material consequence:** A practice wall falls safely, becomes reusable material, and exposes which participants were relying on Arion's compensations
**One image the reader should remember a week later:** Arion standing still as a wall falls away from him along a chalked path he did not alter

The chapter must work as fiction without an accompanying image or lore note.

## Prose constraints

- Do not make ordinary labor a fake prelude before “real” magic. The work is the lesson and changes status.
- Arion's triggered response must be embodied through sound, dust, breath, and mistaken load paths; avoid diagnostic or therapy vocabulary.
- Lio must not become instant loyal comic relief. He wants a rescue license, disagrees with Akamoto, and refuses to defend Arion from a fear he cannot disprove.
- Akamoto must stop Arion consistently even when compensation helps the group.
- The wall falls only inside a clearly established safe zone; no near-miss manufactured for spectacle.
- End on shared craft, not emotional declaration or team formation.
