# Chapter packet — 03. The instrument that passed

**POV:** Emilia
**Movement:** I — The fracture
**Target length:** 3,200–3,600 words
**Visible want:** Pass a supervised safety review for the prism instrument she repaired after a prior exit failure.
**Opposing want:** Her Forge sponsor needs a clean, comparable instrument for the Three-Academy intake and regards user-controlled interruption as corruptible data.
**Dramatic question:** What does Emilia do when the instrument is safe by every written rule and wrong by a rule no one recorded?
**Irreversible turn:** She discovers involuntary anomaly capture, adds a physical manual release, and signs the demonstration agreement anyway.
**Persistent cost:** She becomes accountable for the upcoming measurement while the mirrored data feed remains beyond her local control.
**Exit image:** The brass release tab breaking the instrument's perfect symmetry beside Arion's tool signature.
