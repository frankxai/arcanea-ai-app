## Comparison mechanics

- The authorized Chapter 16 refusal copy contains a drawn boundary of the hammer mark, source-labeled as an observed tool state. Arion's permission is boundary-only and permits an attributed public comparison while excluding all other tool and chamber data.
- Mera deliberately does not use the covert meal-story coordinates to locate the public derivative set, and they do not enter the new note. Her public service-class search produces the thirty-one catalogues.
- The three derivative catalogues use different materials and copying practices: Athenaeum blue glass bands, Luminary white paper maintenance abstracts, and Forge copper-foil parts indexes.
- Accidental damage expresses differently across those materials. Each derivative was altered after entering separate institutional custody through its local material practice—neutral wax over an Athenaeum intake scratch, a stitched blank above cut original Luminary binding loops, and planished cells that soften a Forge receipt punch. The removals map to the same four shared-source positions, carry local execution marks with one matching joint-order suffix, and lack a transfer seal or named restoration handoff.
- Iven corrects one unit conversion, preserving a genuine offset in the Forge copy. After correction, the boundaries still align at the shared-source interval rather than at the page edge.
- Mera may claim separate deliberate removals following a shared coordinate instruction and therefore cross-institutional coordination. She may not claim the removed content, responsible people, motive, or identity of the Chapter 16 buried response.
