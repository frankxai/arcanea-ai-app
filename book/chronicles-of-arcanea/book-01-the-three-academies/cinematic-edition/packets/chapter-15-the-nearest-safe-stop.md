# Chapter packet — 15. The nearest safe stop

## Story contract

**POV:** Arion
**Movement:** II — The measure
**Target length:** 3,800–4,400 words
**Story time / continuity position:** Morning after Chapter 14; Lio's rescue-license trial remains scheduled on the upper course after he accepts the revised access plan
**Location:** Akamoto Roost upper rescue course, divided approach, and field infirmary
**Visible want by word 500:** Complete the rescue exercise under Akamoto's safe-stop rules and prove he can protect a team without becoming its hidden support
**Opposing want:** The course requires a route decision under incomplete shared state; when Lio is genuinely trapped, continuing may free him but destabilize the platform supporting three others
**Dramatic question:** What does responsible refusal mean when stopping protects the group and prolongs one person's pain?
**Irreversible turn:** Arion stops at the nearest safe point and calls a second team instead of crossing the unstable brace to free Lio himself
**Persistent cost:** Without a shared route-state signal, the second team takes the closed approach, reloads Lio's trapped leg, and delays extraction long enough to worsen the injury
**Exit image:** Akamoto's amended procedure written beside Lio's empty boot while Arion's stopped-position chalk remains unaltered on the course

## Rescue-course mechanics

- The upper course crosses a roofed timber frame with west and east approaches converging on a central casualty bay.
- A deliberately released brace simulates post-impact settling; a guide hook with a concealed grain fracture fails unexpectedly and pins Lio's boot for real.
- Three participants stand on the return platform whose load transfers through the same central brace.
- Arion can reach Lio from the west throat, but advancing or reinforcing secretly would convert his body into the platform's hidden support.
- He stops at the marked stable joint and requests the second team through the west relief approach.
- Dust and a broken sight flag force a verbal relay. The runner sends a full command, but noise corrupts what reaches the second team; their explicitly wrong named-route readback returns with both route terms masked, the relay falsely confirms it, and the second team enters east.
- East entry reloads the failed brace before Akamoto stops them. Lio's leg remains pinned longer and receives a second compression.
- External shores from the maintenance rack finally distribute the platform load. Arion then participates in extraction without exceeding his accepted terms.
- The sealed exit witness could have shown whether a mounted route segment remained continuous; it could not have diagnosed Lio, ranked lives, or ordered Arion to continue.

## Prose constraints

- Do not manufacture peril by making staff careless. The route is inspected and approved; the concealed grain fracture becomes visible only after failure.
- Lio must not become a lesson prop. He accepts the revision, stays technically active while trapped, and later attributes his injury precisely.
- Arion's stop is correct under the known load path. Do not retroactively make heroic continuation safe.
- Akamoto's decision is defensible and wrong: distrust of unvalidated interfaces protects students from false authority, but he failed to provide an equivalent shared-state method.
- The witness would inform route continuity only. Do not imply it reads bodies, predicts collapse, or chooses whose safety matters.
- Injury should be painful and consequential without gore or melodramatic permanent-disability claims.
- End with changed procedure, not absolution, inspirational speech, or instant reconciliation.
