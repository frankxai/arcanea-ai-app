## Prose constraints

- Close third, past tense, with no camera drift outside the POV's knowledge.
- Start inside material pressure, not lore summary, weather prelude, dream, or generic awakening.
- Prefer observed behavior and specific objects to named emotion.
- Use Arcanean terms only when action supplies meaning.
- Avoid prophecy cadence, therapy vocabulary in narration, repeated rhetorical triads, fragment piles, generic awe, and ornamental synonyms for light.
- No paragraph exists only to explain the system.
- Preserve quiet intervals after major turns; do not explain the reader's feeling back to them.
- Dialogue must contain an immediate want and a withholding, evasion, bargain, or test.

## Review gates

### Developmental

- [ ] Want, opposition, turn, and persistent cost are unmistakable.
- [ ] The chapter changes the conditions of the next chapter.
- [ ] No scene is a lore tour, lesson demo, commute, or hearing without irreversible action.
- [ ] At least one sympathetic position conflicts with another sympathetic position.
- [ ] The chapter earns its place in the movement architecture.

### Character and continuity

- [ ] POV is identifiable without the name line.
- [ ] Body behavior, private history, and ordinary desire prevent argument-machine characterization.
- [ ] Relationship movement is not a repeat of an earlier beat.
- [ ] Object, injury, knowledge, status, timeline, and magic costs reconcile with adjacent chapters.
- [ ] Locked canon is preserved; staging claims remain labeled.

### Line and humanizer

- [ ] Openings, paragraph rhythms, syntax, and images vary naturally.
- [ ] Abstract ethics appear through choices, objects, and consequences.
- [ ] No systematic model mannerisms, fake quotations, vague attributions, meta claims, or inflated significance.
- [ ] Proper nouns and mythic language do not obscure comprehension.
- [ ] The final image is specific to the POV rather than a trailer tagline.

### Release and provenance

- [ ] Any factual or source-derived claim is traceable.
- [ ] No confidential prompt, chain-of-thought, private path, secret, or unlicensed asset appears in the public ledger.
- [ ] Plate identity, rights, prompt, model, generation date, edits, and crop approvals are recorded when art exists.
- [ ] Independent reviewer verdict is PASS or documented WARN with an accepted owner.
