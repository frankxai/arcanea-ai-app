# Book 1 title gate

`The Three Academies` remains the migration label and repository path. It is not yet the recommended cover title: it names the institution count but not the emotional promise, central choice, or distinctive mechanism.

## Shortlist

| Candidate | Story specificity | Fantasy signal | Emotional / thematic charge | Discoverability | Main risk | Verdict |

| *The Three Academies* | 3 | 4 | 2 | 1 | Generic and institution-first | Migration label only |

## Recommendation

Carry two title lanes through the free-opening reader test:

1. **The Last Free Path** — stronger as a premium novel title. It names the safe Foundation principle, Malachar's corrupted extension, and the trio's final method without explaining the whole book.
2. **The Gate No One Owns** — stronger as immediate high-concept fantasy positioning. It answers the governing question and connects directly to Arcanea's Gate cosmology.

Do not choose by internal preference alone. Test both on the same cover system and book description after the manuscript opening passes line review and the character identity sheets exist. The chosen title must work at thumbnail size, aloud in conversation, in a series lockup, and without a subtitle doing rescue work.

## Search record

Exact-phrase web searches on 2026-08-29 surfaced no exact fiction-title match for *The Last Free Path*, *The Gate No One Owns*, or *The Unowned Gate*. This is a preliminary discoverability check, not legal clearance.

The same search surfaced:

- current fantasy uses of “Hollow Root” as a place or faction;
- an existing essay titled *What No One Owns But You*;
- widespread generic use of “three academies” in school/fantasy contexts.

Before cover approval and paid metadata:

- search ISBN/book catalogues and major retailers in target territories;
- search series-title and entertainment marks through official trademark databases;
- check domains and social handles only after the title wins the creative test;
- obtain human/legal review for the combined title, series lockup, tagline, and visual trade dress.

Absence from a search result is never recorded as rights clearance.

## Positioning sentence under either title

Three young creators collide inside an Academy system built to protect everyone from their gifts. When that system measures Arion without meaningful consent and a buried living structure begins returning evidence the Academies erased, they must decide who may hold a miracle, who may remember it, and whether safety can exist without possession.

## Cover-copy guardrail

Do not sell “AI-written fantasy,” a tool count, a prompt archive, or generic Academy wonder. Sell the finished story: a builder who cannot stop carrying, a witness learning when not to know, a maker whose perfect instrument commits a wrong act, and a mentor who understands that protection can become ownership. The Creator's Ledger is a paid-edition benefit one level below the story promise.
