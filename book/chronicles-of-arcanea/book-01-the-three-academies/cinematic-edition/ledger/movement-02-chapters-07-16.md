## Story decisions owned by the project

- Academy teaching must create irreversible choices, not lore demonstrations.
- A correct safety result may still be produced by a wrong act.
- Advisory tools may report their own state without measuring a person or taking decision authority.
- Responsible refusal may still harm someone; rules do not grant innocence.
- Akamoto's defensible procedural choice worsens Lio's injury, and he changes the procedure without asking Lio to absolve him.
- Mera and Emilia become friends through ordinary repair pleasure and bounded disagreement independent of Arion.
- Arion and Mera establish a direct participant-witness relationship in which source boundaries are respected.
- The midpoint response follows a refusal-status transmission through an existing continuity service, not Arion initiating a Gate practice.
- Root relevance remains a bounded recurrence and staging inference, not authenticated motive or chosen-one proof.
