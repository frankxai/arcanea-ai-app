- Academy teaching must create irreversible choices, not lore demonstrations.

- The midpoint response follows a refusal-status transmission through an existing continuity service, not Arion initiating a Gate practice.
- Root relevance remains a bounded recurrence and staging inference, not authenticated motive or chosen-one proof.
