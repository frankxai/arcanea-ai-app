## Story decisions owned by the project

- Absence may establish coordinated institutional action without proving who ordered it or why.
- A record remains ethical only when observation, inference, access, consent, custody, and publication are kept materially distinct.
- Malachar offers useful craft and a coercive worldview; learning from him creates bodily, relational, and institutional cost.
- The three Academies make synchronized but defensible offers rather than revealing themselves as simple villains.
- Mera's fear drives a real consent violation whose useful result does not excuse the act.
- Emilia may improve an institutional model and still refuse the authority that would absorb her work.
- Rescue agency belongs to trapped people, injured students, local operators, witnesses, and physical labor—not only to the protagonist carrying unusual power.
- Akamoto's public accountability changes procedure without requesting absolution from Lio.
- Contextual silence may be bounded and attributable while still costing the public evidence and implicating the people who witness it.
- A certified containment system may prevent a real public harm and also close across an occupied boundary; the conflict is in the success condition, not incompetent engineering.
- The manual door reports only itself. It enables a bounded offer but does not solve consent, prove personhood, authenticate the Root, or authorize descent.
- A joint Academy plate proves prior institutional contact while leaving purpose, date, motive, maker, and legal meaning for later evidence.

## Rejected directions

- Perfect visual matches, authenticated Root motive, chosen-one proof, prophecy explanation, and automatic canon promotion.
- Mera as an infallible witness, secret access without consequence, instant forgiveness, or restored Athenaeum standing.
- Malachar as a benevolent tutor, omniscient expositor, or source of cost-free superior magic.
- The Academy council as a single malicious conspiracy or its containment engineers as incompetent.
- A rescue caused by magic, a passive trapped group, or a protagonist-only save.
- Lio's injury used to absolve Akamoto, fail Lio's trial, or turn Lio into a warning object.
- Indefinite sealing, anonymous authority, public route leakage, and medical detail visible to Mera.
- Automatic containment presented as foolish rather than useful and dangerous under different boundary assumptions.
- Emilia operating certified equipment with a closed credential, reusing sealed tools, or measuring Arion by proxy.
- A manual door that decides safety, authenticates life, remains permanently open, or authorizes descent.
- Word-count padding after chapter reviewers found each causal unit complete.
