# Opening chapters 01–04

This record covers the first controlled rewrite pass for the free opening. It is a production receipt, not yet the buyer-facing ledger entry.

## Human story decisions

- Open with a neighborhood rescue already under material pressure, not childhood exposition, prophecy, dream, or an Academy tour.
- Make Arion's first magic act save people and damage homes, so usefulness and harm remain inseparable from page one.
- Give Akamoto the role of hazardous-custody responder and field teacher. He makes Arion stop; he does not recruit through spectacle.
- Introduce Mera through a repeatable evidence dispute in which the Athenaeum collector is careful, lawful, and substantively right about privacy risk.
- Introduce Emilia through a device that passes every written test while failing to give a person a usable exit. She recognizes the larger consent problem and still signs to preserve limited control.
- End Chapter 3 with Arion's assignment and repaired-tool signature entering Emilia's work, making convergence logistical.
- Keep Chapter 4 free so Arion, Mera, and Emilia share a room, disagree about one test, and create human chemistry before the paywall.
- Keep all four chapters below the threshold of cosmological explanation. Magic appears through material behavior, professional practice, and consequence.

## Owned task-prompt summary

Draft a premium adult/crossover academy-fantasy opening in close third person, rotating Arion, Mera, and Emilia. Each chapter must establish a concrete want, a defensible opposition, an irreversible turn, a persistent material or relational cost, a distinct sensory language, and one ordinary-life trace. Preserve locked canon; treat working continuity as staging. Use the legacy manuscript only as source material. Avoid prophecy, chosen-one coronation, generic wonder tours, lore exposition, effortless ethics, consequence-free magic, model mannerisms, and any title/terminology likely to create unnecessary IP association.

## Editorial interventions already made

- Compressed the developmental outline so the intake breach lands in Chapter 6 instead of after a long arrival sequence.
- Added the public repair-house dream so Arion wants more than safety and absolution.
- Made Mera's collector correct the timeline and limit claims, preventing her from becoming an effortless ethics authority.
- Added a live-body test to Emilia's chapter so the visibility failure is observed through her own hand rather than discussed abstractly.
- Expanded the initial three-chapter pass from 7,509 to approximately 8,800 draft words with family-work history, storm-source discovery, and embodied device testing.
- Accepted the independent humanizer finding that three origins alone did not fulfill the relationship promise; drafted Chapter 4 and moved the paid boundary to the first shared scene. The revised free opening is approximately 11,000 draft words.
- Removed shared polished-reversal cadence, made Akamoto corrigibly wrong, embodied Mera's prior disclosure wound, and exposed Emilia's private ownership motive.

## Rejected directions

- The legacy names Kael/Mira and ensemble “three roads” structure.
- A generic recruiter promising power or greatness.
- A sorting stone, prophecy, all-House selection, or triumphal “Confluence” reveal.
- An explicit casualty count for Arion's origin event.
- An early Crystalpeak wonder tour or classroom demonstration chapter.
- A corrupt collector who exists only to make Mera correct.
- A malicious Emilia. Her choice is informed, compromised, and accountable.
- A magical weapon as Arion's identity object. His object remains an ordinary repaired mason's hammer.
