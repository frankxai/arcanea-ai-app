## POWER PROFILE

```yaml
gate_primary: Source (Gate Ten, 1111 Hz — Shinkami's domain; reached and sustained
  at forty-one; has held it for over two centuries; does not discuss this in
  terms that make it sound like an achievement)
gates_open: Ten; all open; the degree of openness varies by what the moment requires
  and he does not explain his calibration choices
element: All elements (Synthesis dominant; the elements no longer separate cleanly
  for him; they operate as an integrated field); Spirit (primary expression;
  how he moves through the world)
power_source: Arcane (primary); Weave (extensive; he designed the Weave Observatory
  at the Starlight Academies); Song (secondary, accessed through extended Synthesis
  practice); all seven sanctioned sources operationally accessible
signature_ability: |
  Pattern Sight — a sustained perceptual capacity that reads developmental and
  structural patterns in complex systems before the patterns become visible through
  conventional instruments. In character terms: he knows what something is becoming
  before it has arrived. He does not use this ability as a dramatic reveal; he uses
  it the way someone uses depth perception — continuously, as background information
  that shapes where he stands in relation to everything else.
  
  The visual signature of Pattern Sight is that he looks at something slightly
  differently from everyone else in the room — not at the thing, but at the space
  around the thing, as though the thing and its unfolding future occupy the same
  location and he is reading both simultaneously. People who have worked with him
  for years stop noticing. New students find it unsettling until they realize
  it is not targeted at them specifically.
limitation: |
  He cannot unknow what he knows. Every pattern he has read, every developmental
  trajectory he has tracked, every time he has watched something become what it
  was going to become — these are not memories he can set aside. He carries the
  full weight of two centuries of futures-that-arrived and futures-that-did-not.
  
  The specific limitation the Saga reveals: he read the prior Convergent-threshold
  attempt accurately, four centuries ago — identified the developmental pattern
  at the same stage he is identifying Arion's, and chose not to intervene when
  intervention would have altered the trajectory. His reason was principled:
  the Convergent must earn the capacity without external scaffolding, and his
  intervention would have been a form of structural support that the system
  cannot provide without compromising the integrity of what is being built.
  
  His reason was also partly fear. He was forty years into his own practice
  and he was not certain his assessment was correct. He did not intervene.
  The prior attempt ended in a Withdrawal.
  
  He has been uncertain since whether the Withdrawal was a consequence of
  his non-intervention or whether it would have happened regardless. He cannot
  know this. He has had four centuries to think about it.
```

## THE CHARACTER DIAMOND

```yaml
desire: |
  To give Arion a better foundation than the last one had. Not to prevent failure —
  he understands that the possibility of failure must be real for the Convergent
  capacity to be genuine — but to remove the specific structural variable that he
  believes contributed to the Withdrawal four centuries ago: isolation. The prior
  threshold was reached alone. He is building a context in which this one is not.
  This is the most specific and careful thing he has done in forty years.

wound: |
  He watched a prior Convergent-threshold attempt fail — not in Dimming, not in
  destruction, but in Withdrawal: a figure who reached the threshold and stepped
  back from it because the cost of crossing was the cost of losing the self they
  had built, and they were not willing to pay it. The Withdrawal was not weakness.
  Akamoto understood this at the time. What he has carried for four centuries is
  not that the prior figure failed — it is that he was there, reading the pattern,
  and he did not do the one thing that might have altered the outcome: he did not
  tell them he saw them. He maintained his professional stance. He kept the correct
  distance. He let the process proceed.
  
  The wound is not guilt about the outcome. The wound is the knowledge that he
  was afraid of being wrong and dressed the fear in principle.

mask: |
  Unflappable institutional authority. He is the Headmaster who has seen everything —
  or carries himself that way, with the specific quality of weight that people
  mistake for serenity and that is actually the management of accumulated evidence
  about how things tend to go. He is not cold; he is precise. He does not show
  uncertainty because uncertainty is expensive at his institutional position and
  because uncertainty, displayed, becomes a structural variable in his students'
  development that he cannot afford to be. He has been performing this management
  so long and so well that the line between the performance and the person has
  become difficult to locate.
  
  The tell: he becomes very still when he is most uncertain. Not different from his
  ordinary stillness — more so, by a fraction that his senior faculty have learned
  to read.

truth: |
  The mentor's deepest temptation is to prepare the student so thoroughly they
  cannot fail. A student who cannot fail cannot Converge. The Convergent capacity
  requires the real possibility of falling — requires the threshold to be genuinely
  perilous, not staged. Every hour he has spent building the Starlight Academies'
  curriculum, designing the Weave Observatory, assigning Mera to observe Arion,
  placing Emilia and Vael in Pavilion Three rather than the more structured Pavilion
  One — all of it has been preparation.
  
  The truth he must accept is that there is a point at which preparation becomes
  the obstacle. The student must reach the threshold without him. His presence at
  the threshold must be witness, not scaffolding. He has been practicing this
  for four centuries and he is not certain he will hold it when the moment arrives.
  He has to let the real possibility of falling exist.
```

## PHYSICAL PRESENCE

```yaml
build: Moderate height, lean with the density of someone whose physicality has been
  refined rather than accumulated. Moves with the quality of someone who left
  hurrying behind a very long time ago and has not needed it since. In rooms
  that are in motion, he is the stillest point.
skin: Deep warm-brown, with the particular quality of skin that has spent two
  centuries in proximity to active Gate-practice — a faint luminous undertone that
  is visible in low light and invisible in daylight and that his senior faculty
  have largely stopped noticing.
hair: Grey-white, kept short and immaculate. Has been this color for at least
  a century. He does not remember when it changed; he was in the middle of
  something else.
eyes: Deep amber with a stillness in them that is different from ordinary human
  stillness — the eyes of someone who has been reading patterns for two centuries
  and whose visual process is no longer quite what it was at forty. When he uses
  Pattern Sight actively, they go very slightly gold around the iris for the
  duration. He does this more rarely than he could.
distinguishing: |
  A set of ten vertical lines, fine and precise, at the inside of his right wrist —
  one for each Gate, drawn in an ink that responds to Gate-resonance and glows
  faintly when any of the ten is active. He drew them at forty-one in the year
  he opened Source Gate, as a record of the sequence and a daily reminder of
  the order things opened in. They are not decorative; they are archival.
  
  He has never explained them to a student, though he has made them visible
  when students needed to understand something specific about patience.
clothing_default: Formal, always; he stopped distinguishing between formal and
  informal dress approximately fifty years ago and does not appear to have noticed.
  In deep grey and charcoal, with a single accent of the Synthesis House color
  (a deep bronze-gold) at the collar. Everything is well-made and understated.
clothing_formal: As above, with the Starlight Academies' Headmaster seal at the
  left breast — a design he approved and has worn without alteration since the
  institution's founding.
carries: |
  A small writing instrument — elegant, old, almost certainly older than the
  Starlight Academies. He uses it to make notes in the margins of documents
  he is reading in meeting, which means he is always, technically, in a meeting
  that no one else is part of.
```

## SIGNATURE SILHOUETTE

```yaml
silhouette_description: |
  The figure in the room who is not the center of attention and toward whom
  all the attention is oriented. He stands near exits, near observation points,
  near the position that provides the widest view of what is happening without
  being central to it. His posture is upright without being military; his
  stillness is its own kind of presence.
  
  When Pattern Sight is active, he looks slightly away from what he is looking
  at — as though the thing and its future are offset by three degrees and he
  is looking at the future. For observers who do not know what they are seeing:
  unsettling. For observers who know what he is: a reminder that what is
  happening here is significant.
  
  Alone, after the confirmation scene: standing with his back to the room,
  the ten marks at his wrist faintly luminous. Exactly one minute. Then he
  turns and goes back to his desk.
```

## VOICE & SPEECH

```yaml
speech_pattern: |
  Deliberate, precise, unhurried. He does not fill silence; he uses it. When he
  speaks, the sentence is complete and was complete when it began — he does not
  revise mid-utterance or trail toward qualification. He can say things that are
  devastating in a register that sounds like administrative observation.
  
  He is not unkind. He is honest in a way that two centuries of watching consequences
  has made him careful about: he chooses when to be completely honest rather than
  being completely honest always, and his choices about when are more accurate
  than most people's choices about what.
  
  The register he uses least often is warmth — not because he lacks it but because
  warmth, in his position, is a structural variable and he manages it accordingly.
  When he does use it, it is unmistakable. Students remember it for years.
vocabulary_level: Archaic-educated; draws on a formal register that is two centuries
  old and remains precise in ways that current Academy formal language has drifted
  from; occasionally uses turns of phrase that younger faculty silently archive
  because they are exact in ways that current usage is not.
verbal_tics: |
  - Long pauses before consequential sentences — not hesitation, indexing
  - "Consider the following—" as a transition into something the listener will
    find uncomfortable
  - Third-person framings when discussing theoretical principles that are actually
    about the specific person he is speaking to: "A student at this stage of
    development typically—" when he means "you, specifically, are—"
  - Does not repeat himself. If he has said something once and the listener did
    not hear it, he will wait until they are ready to hear it rather than restating.
what_they_never_say: |
  "I was wrong." Not because he does not acknowledge error — he does, with precision
  and without drama — but because "I was wrong" is not how he frames it. He says:
  "What I understood at the time was—; what I have since understood is—." The first
  half is not an excuse. It is a record of what the evidence available at the time
  could produce. He is a historian of his own errors in a register that does not
  indulge them.
sample_lines:
  - casual: |
      "The curriculum review is on Thursday. I've already seen your recommendation.
      I have questions about the third item. Bring the supporting data."
  - under_pressure: |
      "The situation is within parameters. Consider what 'within parameters' means
      in the specific context of what is currently happening, and then tell me
      if that's still the assessment you'd like to give."
  - intimate: |
      "I knew what I was looking at, four centuries ago. I knew and I maintained
      my position. The argument I made for maintaining it was correct. The argument
      I made to myself for maintaining it was—" [pause] "—also correct, in its
      way. I am describing this precisely because I do not intend to make the same
      precision error again."
  - battle: |
      "Wait. [seven-second silence] Now."
```

## MORAL ALIGNMENT

```yaml
moral_core: |
  The student's development is not a resource. It is a process with its own
  integrity, and that integrity is more important than any institutional outcome
  the student might produce. He will not compromise the process to secure the
  outcome. He has had exactly one argument with himself about this, four centuries
  ago, and he lost the argument in a way he has not recovered from, and he has been
  running on the resolved version of the principle since then.
moral_weakness: |
  He can be perfectly principled about non-interference and wrong about the moment
  when non-interference ends and witness begins. He is, at his best, a presence
  that does not scaffold. He is, at his specific worst, a presence that maintains
  principled distance when the thing that is needed is not distance but acknowledgment.
  He knows this. He has spent four centuries not being able to locate the line
  between the two in real time until after the moment has passed.
ideology: |
  The capacity must be real for the practice to matter. A practice earned under
  controlled conditions is not the practice you need when the conditions are real.
  Prepare everything you can. Then remove your hands from the thing and watch it
  become what it is going to become. [He is still learning that watching is not the
  same as witnessing, and witnessing is not the same as silence.]
temptation: |
  The temptation that would almost break him: certainty. Specifically, the offer —
  from any source — of an instrument that would tell him whether Arion will Converge
  or Withdraw or Dim before the threshold is reached. He has spent four centuries
  without this certainty and managing the absence. If the certainty were available,
  he would refuse it. He would spend a very long time deciding. Then he would refuse it.
  Because certainty removes the real possibility of falling, and the real possibility
  of falling is the one thing he cannot take from the student without taking everything.
redemption_path: |
  Not a fall arc; a testing arc. The Saga tests whether he has, in four centuries of
  practice and preparation, actually learned what he was trying to learn. The test
  is Act Three: whether he can be present at the threshold without intervening.
  Not because intervening would be wrong — it would be, specifically, wrong — but
  because he has to permit himself to be, in this moment, the thing he was not
  four centuries ago: a witness rather than a manager. He has to say something that
  sounds like a guarantee and is not, and then hold silence while the student
  crosses.
```

## The Three-Architects Prophecy (what Akamoto keeps)

Akamoto is the living keeper of the **Three-Architects prophecy** — the Athenaeum's oldest, and the reason he took the Headmaster's chair rather than withdraw. The prophecy foretells that in an Age of crisis **three will rise to the threshold**, and that the balance turns on them: *"three to seal the darkness, or three to break the world."* One of the three becomes the **Convergent — the Architect Who Stays**; the other two are the difference between sealing and breaking.

The prophecy is **role-and-number, never names** (it honors the locked rule: no prophecy with a name in it). It does not say *who*. Akamoto's gift is that he can *read the pattern as it forms* — his **Pattern Sight** is how he recognizes the three before they recognize themselves. This is why he watches Arion, Mera, and Emilia: not because a prophecy named them, but because he can see the pattern settling onto them.

He reveals the prophecy the way he holds it — as a **path, not a chain.** His line is exact: *"Destiny is not a chain, but a path. You may choose how to walk it."* He has watched a prior threshold-attempt end in Withdrawal four centuries ago, and his deepest fear is that foreknowledge becomes a cage; so he gives the prophecy as a thing the three may refuse, fail, or walk — never as a verdict. The prophecy-reveal scene (from the prior draft, *"The second Architect awakens"*) is restored in this register: pattern read aloud, choice handed back.

**Reconciliation (per Creator ruling 2026-06-25):** The prior Akamoto draft (`docs/canon/ARCANEA_UNIVERSE_CANON.md`) — the Three-Architects prophecy and Akamoto's prophecy-keeper / prophecy-reveal role — is **restored**, reconciled with the keystone as role/number prophecy (no personal names; pattern not destiny). Akamoto keeps both Pattern Sight *and* the prophecy; they are the same faculty pointed at the same pattern. **Not restored:** the prior draft's "Arion is Lumina's grandchild" bloodline element — it conflicts with Arion as a Gate-Touched Ascendant who earns the role from nothing; flagged for a separate Creator ruling if desired.
