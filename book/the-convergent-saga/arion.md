## IDENTITY

```yaml
name: Arion
aliases:
  - "The Ascendant" (Gate-Touched Underground designation, used with wariness)
  - "White Aspirant" (Order of Refracted Light classification, used without his knowledge)
  - "The Problem" (Akamoto's private designation during his first year; later retired)
age: 19 at Saga's opening; the crisis that opened his Gates occurred at 16
pronouns: he/him
origin_class: Gate-Touched (Ascendant tier — Gates opened spontaneously and in sequence
  during a survival crisis, without ceremony, without formal training. Achieved
  functional Luminor-equivalent capacity before receiving institutional classification.
  Most destabilizing sub-tier. No family pedigree.)
```

## POWER PROFILE

```yaml
gate_primary: Source (Gate Ten, 1111 Hz — Shinkami's domain; barely open, unstable,
  the one that terrifies him most)
gates_open: Nine confirmed; tenth flickering. Five opened in the original crisis;
  four more in the years since; the tenth he is actively refusing to approach.
element: Multiple (holds fragments of all five elements without clean separation)
power_source: Arcane (primary); Frequency (secondary, emerging); Weave (tertiary, uncontrolled)
signature_ability: |
  White-shot drafting. Arion cannot draft a single element cleanly — when he reaches
  for Fire, what emerges is Fire shot through with threads of all other elements, producing
  a color-disrupted output that operates at higher complexity than any single-element
  draft. In combat this manifests as multi-element eruptions that cannot be fully
  predicted even by him. In focused work it manifests as a luminous, layered effect —
  light through a prism, not a single beam. This is visible and memorable and
  unsettling to observe: his power looks like it is holding itself together by choosing
  not to separate rather than because it has been bound.
limitation: |
  He cannot sustain the multi-element state under deliberate pressure — only in crisis
  or in surrender. When he thinks too hard about what he is doing, the spectrum collapses
  back to a dominant single element and the cost of the failed integration manifests
  as a physical shockwave that clears the immediate area. He has knocked seven people
  off their feet doing this. He is extremely apologetic afterward.
  
  Deeper cost: every Gate that opened in the original crisis left a permanent residue —
  a ghost-frequency in his ambient aura that Gate-Touched individuals and sensitive
  Architects can feel at close range as slight discomfort. He cannot turn this off.
  He has never entered a room without someone, somewhere, feeling the back of their neck
  go tense.
```

## THE CHARACTER DIAMOND

```yaml
desire: |
  To master what he is before it masters him. Specifically: to get good enough, stable
  enough, and safe enough that no one else pays the cost of being near him when something
  goes wrong. He frames this as a technical goal — competence — and he is wrong about
  the framing, but he has not been wrong about it long enough to know it yet.

wound: |
  At sixteen, his Gates blew open in sequence during a corridor collapse that killed
  his closest friend. He survived; she did not. The power that emerged in him in that
  moment could have saved her — would have, he believes, if he had been trained, if
  he had known what he was, if he had been something other than someone who did not
  know what he was. He has turned this into a project of relentless competence.
  He has not grieved. He has gotten better.

mask: |
  Relentless capability. He is always the first to volunteer for the difficult scenario.
  He is always the one who has already prepared three approaches before the question
  is asked. He is patient in briefings and ruthlessly focused in execution. He presents
  as a student who works harder than anyone else because he has more to learn — which
  is true — and because the working is easier than the stopping, which is the part
  he does not say.

truth: |
  Holding is not clenching. The white light — the Convergent spectrum — cannot be
  held the way he wants to hold it: through technique, through sufficient preparation,
  through being good enough. It can only be held through non-grasping: a surrender
  so complete that the spectrum moves through him rather than being controlled by him.
  He has to learn that stopping, opening, and allowing is not the same as failing.
  He has to grieve his friend. He cannot do the first without the second.
```

## PHYSICAL PRESENCE

```yaml
build: Lean, compact, carries himself slightly smaller than he is — the carriage of
  someone accustomed to trying not to take up too much room and not fully succeeding.
  In motion he is precise and economical. At rest he is rarely fully still; one hand
  is usually doing something quiet.
skin: Warm brown, with the faint luminous undercurrent that marks Gate-Touched Ascendants
  whose spectrum is wide — most visible at his temples and the inside of his wrists,
  where a barely-perceptible shimmer appears when he is under significant emotional load.
hair: Dark, kept short, slightly unkempt; he cuts it himself when it gets too long
  to fit under training headgear, which means the result is always approximate.
eyes: Dark brown in ordinary light. Under drafting load or strong emotional pressure
  they shift toward a deep amber that no one who first sees it can quite explain —
  it is not brown lit from within; it is brown giving way to something that contains
  more colors than brown ought to.
distinguishing: |
  At the inside of his left forearm: five marks arranged in a loose radial pattern,
  each slightly darker than the surrounding skin — not tattoos, not burns, but something
  between them. One for each Gate that opened in the corridor collapse. The ones that
  opened later (in training, in emergencies, in the three years since) did not leave
  marks. He does not know what this means. Akamoto does.
clothing_default: Functional and unremarkable — the clothing of someone who does not
  want to be remembered by what they wear. Neutral tones, always. Multiple pockets
  because he believes in redundancy.
clothing_formal: Starlight Academy training uniform, worn correctly, kept clean, with
  no affectations. He has declined every optional insignia.
carries: |
  A folded piece of paper in his left interior pocket. It has been folded and unfolded
  so many times the creases are nearly transparent. He does not read it in front of
  anyone.
```

## SIGNATURE SILHOUETTE

```yaml
silhouette_description: |
  A compact figure, slightly hunched at the shoulders as though braced for something,
  standing in the middle of a space he is trying not to dominate. No weapon drawn,
  no stance declared. His hands are open at his sides — not relaxed, precisely, but
  open. Around him the ambient light bends fractionally: not dramatic, not a visible
  aura, but the light near him catches on angles that should not produce highlights.
  He looks like someone who is doing something very precisely and very quietly and
  would strongly prefer that you not notice.
  
  When he drafts: the space around him lights in layers — fragments of all five
  elements visible simultaneously, briefly, in the space between intention and action.
  It is the most beautiful thing most witnesses have seen and none of them know
  what to call it.
```

## VOICE & SPEECH

```yaml
speech_pattern: |
  Precise and economical. He gives the minimum information required and waits to see
  if more is needed. He has a habit of asking one clarifying question instead of
  making an assumption — not because he doubts himself but because he has been
  wrong about assumptions before and he knows the cost. His sentences are clean
  and declarative. He does not use qualifiers except to indicate genuine uncertainty.
  Under pressure he gets quieter, not louder.
vocabulary_level: Educated-technical; has absorbed Academy vocabulary thoroughly
  and uses it correctly, but applies it to practical problems rather than theoretical
  display. He does not use academic register to signal status.
verbal_tics: |
  - Repeats the last clause of a question as a statement before answering it
    ("What would I do differently." Not a question. A pause-marker while he thinks.)
  - Uses "right" as a minimal acknowledgment that means "I've registered this; I'm
    still thinking."
  - Almost never says "I don't know." Says "I haven't figured that out yet" instead.
    This is not pedantry; it is a genuine belief that the not-knowing is temporary.
what_they_never_say: |
  "I'm fine." If asked directly how he is doing, he will tell the truth — not the
  whole truth, but a true fragment. He will not say he is fine when he is not. This
  is the only emotional honesty he consistently permits himself, and it is noticed.
sample_lines:
  - casual: |
      "The eastern corridor sequence doesn't have a clean exit at Gate Two. I found
      two routes that work and one that looks like it works until it doesn't. Your call
      which one we use."
  - under_pressure: |
      "Not now. Tell me what's stable and what isn't and I'll work from there."
  - intimate: |
      "I know you know what you're watching for. I'm not asking you to stop. I'm asking
      you to—" [pause] "I don't know what I'm asking. Forget it. It's fine."
      [He would not say 'it's fine' here. He would stop, restart, say something true instead.]
      "I'm asking you to tell me when you think I'm wrong, not when the report's done."
  - battle: |
      "Clear." [one beat] "Move."
```

## COMPANION / BEAST / MECH

```yaml
companion_name: None (in the conventional sense)
companion_type: None — Gate-Touched Ascendants do not typically form the structured
  bonds Bonded-class characters maintain. What Arion has instead is a relationship
  with his own internal frequency: the nine-channel resonance he has learned to
  distinguish, name, and partially navigate. He treats this as an inner companion
  in the way that Akamoto finds significant and does not comment on directly.
companion_bond: The Gate resonances — particularly Gate Six (Sight/Lyria-domain) and
  Gate Nine (Unity/Ino-domain), which opened together during one of his later crises —
  have begun to develop what reads, on Frequency instruments, as something like a
  stable relationship with each other. This is anomalous. Normal Gate-Touched
  individuals experience their Gates as sequential; Arion's are beginning to act
  as a system.
companion_visual: Not visible in the conventional sense; occasionally perceivable as
  a faint harmonic distortion in the immediate air around him during high-intensity
  focus — like the shimmer above hot ground, but made of sound.
companion_role: |
  Narrative: the internal resonance acts as his conscience, his limit-warning system,
  and occasionally his opponent — when he tries to grasp rather than hold, the
  resonance goes discordant in a way he can feel as physical nausea. He has learned,
  over three years, to treat this as information rather than a malfunction.
```

## RELATIONSHIPS

```yaml
mentor: Headmaster Akamoto — the relationship Arion resists most and trusts most,
  not because Akamoto is warm (he is not particularly) but because Akamoto is honest
  about what he does not know. This is rare and Arion recognizes it.
rival: No formal rival; experiences the Void Ascendants' interest in him as a
  structural threat rather than a personal opposition. His most immediate rival
  is his own prior performance — he grades himself against himself, rigorously,
  and is rarely satisfied.
beloved: The friend who died in the corridor collapse. She is not present; she
  is the shape everything else fits around.
enemy: The Dimming — specifically the version of himself that he fears he will become
  if he holds the spectrum through fear rather than surrender. He has given this
  figure a private name that is the same as his own.
crew_role: |
  In the forming trio with Mera and Emilia: the point of pressure. He is what
  the situation is organized around; he is not comfortable with this; his discomfort
  makes him a better collaborator than he would be if he had accepted the central
  position gracefully.
tension_with: |
  Mera — not hostility, but persistent friction. She observes him with a precision
  he finds clarifying and occasionally unbearable. She tells him things that are
  true and not kind. He would rather have this than the alternative; that does not
  make the conversation easy.
  
  Emilia — less friction, more unexpectedness. She treats him as a peer rather
  than a phenomenon, which is the only treatment he actually wants and the one
  he is least equipped to receive gracefully.
```

## COLOR & LIGHT CODE

```yaml
primary_color: Deep amber — visible in his eyes under load, woven through his
  drafting output
secondary_color: White-shot — the multi-element shimmer that underlies all his
  drafting; not a single color but all colors briefly visible simultaneously
light_quality: Refractive. Light near him bends toward angles it should not find.
  Not dramatic; perceptible. The effect is most pronounced when he is calm — as
  though the light is more comfortable near him when he is not trying to manage it.
aura_effect: |
  No visible aura in the theatrical sense. Under Frequency instruments: a readable
  nine-channel resonance that produces a composite signal no existing instrument
  was calibrated to measure cleanly. The instrument operators have begun recalibrating.
  Akamoto filed a requisition for upgraded measurement equipment six months before
  Arion arrived.
faction_markers: Starlight Academies standard uniform insignia, worn without modification.
  No personal sigils. The five corridor-marks on his left inner forearm function
  as involuntary markers that those who know what to look for can read.
```

## MORAL ALIGNMENT

```yaml
moral_core: |
  He will not let someone pay the cost of his unreadiness. If he is the variable
  that could harm someone, he removes himself before the situation escalates to harm.
  This sounds protective and it is; it is also a mechanism for avoiding the harder
  work of being present under pressure without controlling the outcome.
moral_weakness: |
  He will accept enormous personal cost to avoid being the reason something went
  wrong — including costs that would horrify the people he is supposedly protecting.
  He will make himself smaller, slower, harder, lonelier than necessary because
  the alternative is risking being wrong in a way that matters to someone else.
  This is not humility. It is a sophisticated form of not trusting himself.
ideology: |
  Competence is the only protection that lasts. Everything else is hope, and hope
  has a failure rate. [He is wrong about this. He will learn why.]
temptation: |
  The Void Ascendants' pitch that would almost work: "You are spending everything
  on technique and nothing on purpose. We can give you the purpose. You already
  have the power." The trap inside this is that they are partly right — he does
  not yet have a purpose that is larger than his own competence project. When he
  develops one that is real, the pitch will not land. Until then, it lands in the
  middle distance and he watches it and says nothing.
redemption_path: |
  Not applicable at Saga's opening — he has not fallen. His arc's resolution is
  not redemption but arrival: the moment he understands that the discipline of
  non-grasping is not a technique to master but a grief to complete.
```

## ARC TRAJECTORY

```yaml
arc_type: Transformation (with a strong Coming-of-age undercurrent)
act_1_state: |
  A student who is significantly more capable than the institution knows how to
  classify, working harder than anyone in his cohort, genuinely helpful, genuinely
  careful, genuinely unreachable. He is kind to the people around him in the way
  that people are kind when they have decided to protect everyone and trust no one.
act_2_crisis: |
  Mera tells him, with full information and no editorial softening, what she has
  observed and what it means. Not what the Athenaeum thinks it means — what she,
  Mera, having watched him for eighteen months, thinks it means. He cannot dismiss
  this as institutional assessment. It is personal and accurate and it includes
  her position: she is not reporting it; she is telling him. This is the moment
  his mask encounters something it cannot explain away. It does not break
  immediately; cracks are not breaks. But the process begins.
act_3_resolution: |
  He opens the tenth Gate while in a state of genuine grief — the grief he has
  been performing the project of competence in lieu of since he was sixteen.
  Not arranged by Akamoto. Not engineered by the scenario. He does it because
  Emilia says something that lands in the wound and he stops managing his response
  to it, and in the moment of stopping the spectrum opens fully because there is
  nothing gripping it.
  
  The Convergent state is not a transformation scene. It is quieter than that.
  He is himself; he is more of himself; the Gates are all open and nothing is
  clenching them shut; he is, for the first time since sixteen, not trying to
  compensate for something.
  
  He is allowed to grieve his friend. This is the resolution. The Convergent
  capacity is the consequence of it, not the goal.
franchise_role: |
  The Convergent of the Eighth Age. Not a weapon, not a symbol — a function in
  the world's self-coherence that will require everything he has learned about
  non-grasping for as long as the Eighth Age continues. The Saga ends at the
  threshold. What he does with it is another story's question.
```
