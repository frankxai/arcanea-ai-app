name: Mera

## POWER PROFILE

```yaml
gate_primary: Flow (Gate Two, 285 Hz — Leyla's domain; where her power is most fluent
  and most intimate)
gates_open: Five confirmed, sixth withdrawn twice
element: Water (primary); Spirit (secondary, emergent — perceived through the
  memory-diving tradition as the substrate into which memory-water dissolves)
power_source: Song (primary — the Atlantean oral-memory tradition uses resonant
  vocal patterns to open and read water-held memory); Mana (secondary, structural);
  Arcane (tertiary, learned at Athenaeum)
signature_ability: |
  Memory-diving. Mera can enter standing water — oceans, rivers, still pools, and
  in extremis, the water in human tissue — and read the memories held there.
  Water holds everything that has passed through it. She learned to navigate
  this as a child in the oral-memory tradition; she has spent seven years refining
  it into something that functions as intelligence gathering. In visual terms:
  when she enters a memory-dive, she becomes perfectly still, her eyes go translucent
  (the pupil visible through what appears to be a millimeter of clear water), and
  the water nearest to her organizes itself in concentric rings outward from her
  position — not dramatic, but unmistakable to anyone who has seen it before.
  The memories she retrieves are precise, indexed, and reproducible. This is unusual;
  most divers get impressions. She gets records.
limitation: |
  She cannot selectively forget what she has dived into. Every memory she retrieves
  from water becomes permanently resident in her own recall — not as her own memory,
  but as an adjacent channel. After seven years of operative fieldwork she carries
  the recorded experience of approximately four hundred individuals she has never met.
  She has organized these into what she privately calls the Archive. The Archive
  is functional. It is also never fully quiet.
  
  She cannot memory-dive in moving water above a moderate current. The memories
  disperse faster than she can read them; forcing it produces fractures in her
  indexing that take weeks to repair. She knows every still body of water in the
  Starlight Academies campus. She has mapped them.
```

## THE CHARACTER DIAMOND

```yaml
desire: |
  To do the observation correctly — to see clearly, record accurately, and return
  a report that is honest about what is there rather than what the institutional
  receiver wants to find. She frames her professional identity entirely around
  precision of observation. This is genuine; it is also a structure she built because
  having a professional identity that is entirely about accuracy means never having
  to declare a position of her own.

wound: |
  She was sent on her first operative assignment at fifteen — a memory-dive into a
  coastal archive that the Athenaeum said was a routine retrieval. It was not routine.
  What she found in the water was a record of the Athenaeum ordering the destruction
  of an Atlantean memory-grove to prevent its contents from entering the public
  record. She filed an accurate report. The report was classified. She was told she
  had done excellent work. She was promoted. She has spent six years not thinking
  about what she understood in that moment: that the institution she serves is willing
  to use her precision as a tool for its own information management, and that her
  precision is the thing that makes her most useful and most controllable.

mask: |
  Professional detachment so complete it reads as serenity. She never appears rattled.
  She is careful, unhurried, precise in language to the point where people in casual
  conversation feel they are being documented. This is not unkindness; it is the face
  she has worn so long she has occasionally forgotten it is a face. When she is least
  certain, she becomes most precise — the language tightens, the phrasing grows more
  formal. People who know her read this as stress. She knows people who know her read
  this as stress. She has never done anything about it.

truth: |
  Objectivity without the willingness to be changed is not observation — it is control.
  She has been gathering data about the world for seven years without permitting the
  data to alter her. A true observer is altered by what she sees. She has been keeping
  herself separate from the Archive; she has been keeping herself separate from Arion;
  she has been keeping herself separate from her own position on whether what she is
  watching is good or dangerous. The moment she stops being an instrument and becomes
  a person with a position — the moment she stops reporting and starts caring — is
  the moment she becomes dangerous in a way the Athenaeum did not plan for.
  And it is the moment she becomes real.
```

## PHYSICAL PRESENCE

```yaml
build: Slender and upright; moves with the unhurried economy of someone who has never
  been in a genuine hurry in her life, which is not true but reads true. In still
  water she is completely at home; on land she is composed; the difference between
  the two is almost imperceptible and she works to keep it that way.
skin: Deep blue-brown, with a quality that changes slightly in proximity to large
  bodies of water — not luminous exactly, but more present, more dimensional, as
  though the skin is glad to be reminded what it is near. This is involuntary.
  She is aware of it and cannot prevent it.
hair: Dark, thick, worn in a structured coil at the base of her neck when working
  (always; never loose when working). When not working — which is rare and usually
  means she is alone — she lets it fall loose to her shoulder blades. No one
  at the Starlight Academies has seen it this way.
eyes: The color of deep water in strong light — a shifting blue-black that does not
  fix to a single shade. During memory-diving the pupil appears to be visible through
  a clear layer; this effect fades within seconds of surfacing but leaves a faint
  translucency in the sclera that lingers for several hours.
distinguishing: |
  A small Atlantean tide-mark beneath her left eye — four horizontal lines, applied
  at fourteen during the Tidal Rite, slightly raised, dark blue-black. It is the
  only mark on her face that was not chosen for professional purpose.
  
  When she is memory-diving in proximity to large amounts of archived experience —
  or in proximity to Arion, whose resonance apparently disturbs her Archive in ways
  she is actively investigating — her hands are perfectly still in a way that healthy
  hands usually are not. No small movements. No fidget. Stillness she does not
  consciously produce.
clothing_default: Clean, structured, in deep blues and neutrals. Well-made. Nothing
  expensive enough to signal status but nothing cheap enough to signal its absence.
  She dresses to be overlooked, which requires a specific kind of care.
clothing_formal: Athenaeum Witness grey-blue field uniform; she wears it with correct
  insignia and finds the process of wearing it increasingly uncomfortable in ways
  she has not articulated to anyone.
carries: |
  A small water-vessel of treated glass — filled from the ocean at Atlantis before
  she left; the contents are fifteen months old. She has not opened it. She is not
  certain whether she is keeping it because she cannot yet read what's in it or
  because she is not ready to find out what Atlantis has been doing since she left.
```

## SIGNATURE SILHOUETTE

```yaml
silhouette_description: |
  A figure of complete stillness in a space that is otherwise in motion. She stands
  at the edge of things — the edge of the water, the edge of the gathering, the edge
  of the scene — and the positioning reads as professional rather than excluded.
  Her posture is straight; her hands are at her sides, both still, the water-vessel
  barely visible at her hip. She is looking at something that may be in the room
  or may be in the Archive; it is not always possible to tell from the outside.
  
  When she dives: concentric rings in the water outward from her feet. The eyes
  go translucent. The Archive opens. Everything else in the room becomes, for a
  measured interval, secondary information.
```

## VOICE & SPEECH

```yaml
speech_pattern: |
  Precise and measured, with the faint register of someone who has learned to
  speak in a second language and made it more exact than most native speakers.
  (Atlantean oral-memory tradition trains precision as a survival skill; imprecise
  memory retrieval loses the record.) She chooses words the way she chooses data:
  for accuracy over effect. She is not cold; she is specific. People sometimes
  experience these as the same thing.
  
  Under stress: the sentences get shorter. The qualifiers disappear. She sounds,
  when under genuine pressure, like a briefing document.
  
  Unprompted: she rarely begins a social conversation. When she does, it tends to
  be a direct question that reveals she has been paying attention to something for
  longer than the other person realized.
vocabulary_level: Educated; precise; Atlantean-formal underlying structure visible in
  how she handles abstraction (she grounds it). Cross-institutional vocabulary from
  Athenaeum training, tidally formal from Atlantean tradition, increasingly Academy-
  inflected.
verbal_tics: |
  - "What I observed was—" (as a distinct opening, not as a qualifier; the observation
    and the interpretation always travel separately in her speech)
  - Small pause before offering an assessment — long enough to be noticed, short
    enough not to be awkward. The pause does not mean she is uncertain; it means
    she is distinguishing what she saw from what she thinks about what she saw.
  - "That's a different question" (when someone conflates two questions she wants
    to keep distinct)
what_they_never_say: |
  "I don't care." She cares about everything she has agreed to observe and she is
  increasingly aware that the things she has not agreed to observe are also becoming
  things she cares about. The sentence she will not say is "I don't care" because
  it is the sentence she is running out of ways to mean.
sample_lines:
  - casual: |
      "The northern training corridor floods during heavy precipitation. I've mapped
      three routes that don't. I'll send you the chart."
  - under_pressure: |
      "Two variables. What's stable. What's moving. Give me the stable one."
  - intimate: |
      "I've been carrying four hundred people's memories for seven years and none
      of them are mine. What I observed today is yours. I'm — deciding what to
      do with it." [pause] "That's not standard phrasing. I know it isn't."
  - battle: |
      "Water right, cover left, thirty seconds." [pause] "Go."
```

## COMPANION / BEAST / MECH

```yaml
companion_name: The Archive
companion_type: Internal — not a beast or mech but a structured repository of
  retrieved memories that has, over seven years, developed something that might be
  called a character of its own. The Archive is not a separate consciousness; it is
  the organized residue of four hundred lives that has become, in Mera's internal
  experience, a second presence that she can consult and that occasionally comments
  without being consulted.
companion_bond: Formed gradually and involuntarily over seven years of operative work.
  It is, in Mera's private assessment, the relationship she has maintained most
  consistently and the one she has thought about least carefully.
companion_visual: |
  Not visible externally. Under high stress — particularly in proximity to Arion,
  whose resonance disturbs the Archive's indexing — Mera becomes briefly more
  animated than her baseline: micro-expressions that don't match the sentence she
  is speaking, as though she and the Archive are having a parallel conversation about
  what the situation means.
companion_role: |
  The Archive functions as Mera's most reliable source of institutional knowledge,
  contextual pattern-matching, and comparative data. It also functions as the part
  of her that has an opinion — the repository of four hundred other people's
  responses to similar situations, available for comparison with her own.
  
  Dramatically: the Archive is where Mera's wound lives. The Athenaeum memory-grove
  destruction record is in there. She has never accessed it since filing the report.
  She knows she will have to.
```

## RELATIONSHIPS

```yaml
mentor: The Athenaeum Witnesses who trained her — particularly a senior Witness named
  Olia Verante, who taught her the distinction between observation and interpretation
  and who, Mera has recently begun to suspect, also taught her to keep the two so
  rigorously separated that she could work for the Athenaeum indefinitely without
  ever being required to state a position. She has enormous respect for Olia Verante
  and is beginning to have complicated feelings about her enormous respect.
rival: No formal rival. She treats the Athenaeum's senior archive-keepers as
  institutional counterparts rather than rivals; her operational orientation means
  she rarely competes for the same territory as academic colleagues. The thing
  she is most in competition with is her own professional frame.
beloved: Atlantis — not a person but a place, held in the way that people hold
  the thing they left without quite finishing leaving. She has not gone back since
  the assignment. She does not know if she is waiting for the right moment or if
  the right moment passed.
enemy: Not a person. The structural condition she is beginning to recognize: being
  used as an instrument so precisely calibrated that the user never has to worry
  about whether the instrument has its own position. She has not named this yet.
  When she does, it will be a significant moment in her arc.
crew_role: |
  In the forming trio: the mind that names what is happening before the others
  have found the words for it. Not cold — precise. She is the one who, when Act Two
  arrives, is the first to understand what Arion is becoming and who chooses,
  with full information, to tell him rather than report it.
tension_with: |
  Arion — she observes him more closely than he is comfortable with, and he
  knows he is being observed and does not object to the observation on principle
  but finds the precision of it occasionally destabilizing in a way he cannot
  fully articulate. The tension is generative; it is not hostile.
  
  The Athenaeum's senior Witnesses (absent from the scene; present in the structure) —
  she is increasingly aware that her reports are being used in ways she did not
  sanction and that the briefings she received before this assignment were deliberately
  incomplete.
```

## MORAL ALIGNMENT

```yaml
moral_core: |
  Accuracy. She will not misrepresent what she observed — not to protect a source,
  not to protect herself, not to give someone the answer they wanted. This is the
  one commitment she has not bent and she does not believe she is capable of bending
  it. She may not be right about this; she has not yet been tested in the specific
  direction that would answer the question.
moral_weakness: |
  She will observe and report with perfect accuracy while deliberately declining
  to state her position on what she observed. She has, at least twice, filed
  reports so precisely accurate and so carefully framed that the institution could
  use them to do harm — and she told herself she had fulfilled her obligation because
  she had not falsified anything. Accuracy without position is not neutral. She knows
  this. She has not acted on knowing it.
ideology: |
  See clearly. Record honestly. The record outlasts the moment; the record is the
  only thing that does. [What she is learning: the record is made by someone, and
  that someone is not neutral, and pretending to be neutral is its own position.]
temptation: |
  The offer that would almost work on her: "We will give you access to the complete
  Athenaeum archive — everything classified, everything sealed, everything you have
  ever suspected exists and been denied. In exchange, this one report is framed
  this one way." The trap is that access to the full archive would genuinely tell
  her things she needs to know. The cost is the one thing she has never compromised.
  She would spend a very long time deciding. This is the correct amount of time to spend.
redemption_path: |
  Not a fall arc — a revelation arc. What she must come to is not recovery from
  corruption but the willingness to stop being precise about something in particular:
  the distance she keeps between herself and a declared position. The moment she
  says "I am not reporting this; I am telling you" — the moment she chooses to be
  known rather than useful — is her resolution.
```

## ARC TRAJECTORY

```yaml
arc_type: Revelation (discovers truth about herself and her institutional position)
act_1_state: |
  A Witness doing her assignment. Precise, professional, thorough. Filing reports
  that are accurate in every detail and framed in every way to serve an institutional
  agenda she has stopped examining. She is good at this. She is genuinely good at
  it. The goodness of the work is what has kept her from looking at what the work
  is for.
act_2_crisis: |
  She realizes, over the course of several months of observing Arion, that the
  Athenaeum's interest in this particular student is not benign curiosity — it is
  containment preparation. The confirmation framework the senior Witnesses are building
  from her reports is not designed to support an emerging Convergent; it is designed
  to give the Athenaeum procedural control over the confirmation process, and therefore
  over the Convergent, and therefore over the most consequential single agent
  in Arcanea's current Age.
  
  She has been the instrument that built this case. She built it accurately.
  
  The crisis is: what does she do now? She can continue to file accurate reports.
  She can refuse to file further reports. She can tell Arion what she has found
  and what she has been doing. The first option is what the institution expects.
  The second is insubordination. The third requires her to be a person rather than
  an observer — to have a position, to act from it, and to accept that the action
  is hers rather than the institution's.
act_3_resolution: |
  She tells Arion. Not as an observation. As a position: she has observed this,
  she has assessed what it means, and she, Mera, thinks he should know because
  she, Mera, thinks the Athenaeum is wrong and he has the right to approach
  whatever he is becoming without the institution's procedural hand on the back
  of his neck.
  
  This is the most important sentence she will say in the Saga. It costs her
  the position. She does not know yet whether it costs her more than that.
  
  In Act Three she is a person who has stated a position and must now live in it.
  This is more difficult than she anticipated and more real than anything she has
  done in seven years. She is also, for the first time since fifteen, not carrying
  the classified report alone.
franchise_role: |
  The Saga's epistemic conscience — the character who names what is happening when
  the others are still inside it. In the wider Arcanea universe she becomes a figure
  associated with the question of what institutions do with the things they cannot
  control, and how those things can be witnessed without being consumed.
```
