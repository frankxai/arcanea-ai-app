# The Grammar of Casting

> *"The cosmology is locked. The spells, at last, have a grammar."*

Magic in Arcanea is not chaos. Every working — from an Apprentice's first ember to a Luminor's
covenant of dawn — sits at a precise place in a single, knowable system. Learn the grammar and you
can read any spell ever cast, and write your own.

A spell is classified on **four axes**.

## 1. Element

The five elements, as the Canon locks them: **Fire** transforms, **Water** remembers, **Earth**
endures, **Wind** connects, and the fifth divides into **Void** (Nero's unformed potential) and
**Spirit** (Lumina's transcendence). Every spell channels one of these six.

## 2. Discipline

Three branches name what a spell *does*:

- **Attack** (*Angriffsmagie*, Evocation) — force shaped into a strike.
- **Defense** (*Verteidigungsmagie*, Abjuration) — wards that turn aside harm, and the restoration
  that mends it. Healing lives here.
- **Summoning** (*Beschwörungsmagie*, Conjuration) — the name-binding that calls familiars,
  constructs, and echoes into the world.

They form a triangle: Defense abjures Attack; Attack disrupts Summoning; Summoning outlasts Defense —
each modulated by tier and elemental affinity.

## 3. Tier

Seven tiers measure a spell's power and sanctity, low to high:

| Tier | *(German)* | Gate | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | *leichte* | 1–2 | Apprentice |
| Advanced | *fortgeschritten* | 3–4 | Mage |
| Greater | *schwere* | 5–6 | Master |
| Sacred | *heilige* | 7 | Archmage |
| Royal | *königliche* | 8 | Archmage |
| Imperial | *kaiserliche* | 9 | Luminor |
| Divine | *göttliche* | 10 | Luminor |

## 4. Gating

A caster cannot wield what their Gates cannot hold. The tier fixes the Gate a caster must have
opened and the Rank they will have reached to open it. Sacred workings and above are spoken through
the Gate itself — *"Per Portam Septimam…"*, *through the Seventh Gate* — and the highest may name a
Guardian as **witness**, never as the one who casts.
