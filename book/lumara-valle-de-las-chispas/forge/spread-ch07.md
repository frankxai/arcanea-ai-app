# Spread Ch 7 — The Owl and the Long Silence

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-chispas-spread-ch07.png`
**Book beat:** Lila sits at the base of an enormous old tree and learns to
share a long silence with the owl on the branch above.

## What this image must do

This is the **quietest image** in the entire book. It must teach the
viewer what silence-as-love looks like. The image is mostly emptiness.
Two beings holding still inside it. That is the whole thing.

If the viewer feels they want to be quiet just looking at this, the
image has worked.

## Composition

A vast nocturnal old-growth forest. Massive dark tree trunks recede into
deep plum. The image is almost monochrome — the **narrowest value range
of any image in the book.**

On a thick low branch in the middle distance, at the upper-right
rule-of-thirds intersection: **a large old owl** — lechuza, pale luna
plumage in soft cream and grey, two enormous amber eyes calmly fixed
forward.

Below the owl, on a raised tree root at the forest floor, at the
lower-left rule-of-thirds intersection: **Lila** sitting cross-legged,
Conejito in her lap, three-quarter view, her small face turned up
toward the owl in mirror stillness.

The diagonal between the two of them is the entire composition. Owl
above-right, child below-left, attention between them.

A few small florchispas grow shyly between the roots, their glow
muted, almost hidden in the dark.

## Negative space

**Most of the frame is dark forest.** The vast space between owl and
child, the receding trunks, the upper-left and lower-right corners —
all of these are paper with only the slightest brush-wash to suggest
shadow. The image is mostly **breath.**

Negative space is the subject of this image.

## Colour choreography

- **Cream-pale grey** for the owl's plumage — the brightest mass in
  the image, but kept soft.
- **Amber gold** for the owl's two large eyes — the second-brightest
  point, intentionally warm.
- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown, muted by the deep ambient
  shadow.
- A few faint **florchispa-gold** dots between the roots, the
  smallest light source.
- Grey-violet paper for everything else, with the merest hint of
  cooler wash on the tree trunks.

## Light

Almost no light source. A whisper of moon-wash from upper-left barely
touches the owl's left side and casts the longest, softest shadow down
the trunk. The owl's amber eyes seem to hold their own faint light
(observed reflection, not magical glow). Florchispas at the roots are
the only confirmed light. The image is **as dark as the book allows.**

## Lila's & Owl's faces

Lila: turned upward in three-quarter view, calm, mouth closed, eyes
on the owl. Patient. Settled. She has just remembered her abuela.

Owl: head and body facing forward, two enormous amber eyes calmly on
Lila. Not blinking. Not moving. The animal stillness of a creature
that knows how to wait.

The two faces hold the same expression in two different bodies.

## Make-or-break detail

This image is about **value range.** The whole picture must sit in a
very narrow band of dark-grey-violet to slightly-less-dark-grey-violet,
with only:
1. The owl's pale plumage as the brightest mass
2. The owl's two amber eyes as the warmest points
3. Lila's cream nightgown as a third soft anchor
4. A few florchispa-gold pinpricks at the roots

If the image is rendered with too much contrast or too many light
sources, the silence collapses. The picture must feel **almost
underexposed.** That is its truth.

The owl's eyes are the entire chapter's lesson, made into two amber
dots. Get those right and the rest is just frame.
