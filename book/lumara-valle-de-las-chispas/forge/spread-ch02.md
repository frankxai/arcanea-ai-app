# Spread Ch 2 — The Crystal River

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape
**Output:** `lumara-valle-de-las-chispas-spread-ch02.png`
**Book beat:** Lila steps barefoot into the river, and the pez de luz
draws a circle of light around her ankle.

## What this image must do

Show the **decision to enter another's world.** The image is about
crossing — from shore to water, from observer to participant. The
emotional weight is in the soles of two bare feet visible through
perfectly clear water.

## Composition

Low water-level angle. The horizon line sits very low in the frame, at
the lower third. The river fills the centre and lower portion; the deep
plum sky fills the upper two-thirds.

Lila stands ankle-deep, slightly left of centre, in three-quarter view.
Her cream linen nightgown hem brushes the surface of the water. Her two
bare feet are clearly visible through the perfectly transparent water
as pale shapes resting on small pale-rose pebbles. The water shows them
without distortion — it is more transparent than glass.

Just under the surface, threading a slow figure-eight around her left
ankle: a **small pez de luz**, the size of her little finger, made
entirely of soft cool cream-white-blue light. Its tail leaves a faint
glow-trail in the water that fades within a hand's width.

Conejito sits patiently on the dry pebble shore at the right-third
intersection of the frame, watching. He is not in the water.

A pale-rose limestone boulder crests the river at upper-left.

## Negative space

The deep plum sky above takes the upper two-thirds — almost empty paper,
with a scatter of tiny pencil-point stars. The right edge of the frame
is left breathing for caption type.

## Colour choreography

- **Cream linen** on Lila's nightgown.
- **Pale cool cream-blue** glow on the pez de luz around her ankle —
  the brightest point in the image, but kept small and soft.
- Pale-rose limestone in subtle warm pink wash (still within the cream
  register, just slightly warmer).
- Grey-violet paper for sky, water-tinted ground, distant shore.
- A few faint florchispa-gold dots on the far shore in the middle
  distance.

## Light

Waxing moon overhead and slightly upper-right. A faint cream wash on
Lila's hair and shoulders. The pez de luz is its own small source —
the brightest single point in the image, but small enough not to
dominate. Florchispas faint in the distance.

## Lila's face

Three-quarter view, calm, looking down at her ankle where the fish
circles. Mouth closed, expression careful and attentive — the look of
a child trying very hard not to startle something small.

## Make-or-break detail

The water must be **invisible.** The viewer should see the pebbles and
Lila's feet so clearly that they only realise water is there when they
notice the surface line cutting at her ankle. The pez de luz's
fade-trail is the only visible mark in the water — and even it should
last only a hand's-width before vanishing. The transparency is the
miracle, not the fish.
