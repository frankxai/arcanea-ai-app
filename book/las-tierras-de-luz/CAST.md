# Cast — Las Tierras de Luz (Mature Edition)

*Full character dossiers. Wound / Lie / Want / Need / Voice for every figure with a name. Order is by importance to Chapters 0–3.*

---

## SELENE VELARA — protagonist

**Age**: 19 (birthday in Prologue).
**Identity**: Born and raised in Caracas, Venezuela. Lives in Altamira with mother Camila and grandmother Mercedes. Undergraduate linguistics, Universidad Central. Maternal line Avilaran; never told.
**Build**: medium height (1.68m), dark hair past her shoulder blades, brown skin that goes faintly gold at the end of summer, brown eyes with a hazel ring near the pupil. Small gold cross from her grandmother; she has not believed in the cross for years but believes in what wears it.
**Voice**: code-switches between Caraqueño Spanish and a careful, formal English. Spanish for emotion, family, prayer, kitchen, dream. English for university, reasoning, distance. When both appear in one thought, she is on the edge of something true.
**Gift**: prismatic resonance across all Ten Gates. Sees destellos in every conscious being. Has been told her whole life this is imagination.
**Wound**: she has grown up believing her father could not bear ordinary life with her. The truth is the opposite: he was **Dario Solamar**, Luminor of the Realm of Sol Mareo, sent to Earth on a corridor-watch mission, who fell in love with Camila and stayed past his assignment to raise Selene. When the dormant Caracas corridor began to stir three years before this book opens, his Luminor-presence on Earth was amplifying the signal and would have drawn Shadow-attention to his family. He left for "Madrid" — actually returned to Arcanea — to protect them. He has not been able to safely return. The dwindling phone calls were the dwindling of his ability to reach back across worlds. The gray-green color around him on the last night was his Anima beginning to fail in Earth's air. Selene did not know any of this. Mercedes knew all of it and could not tell her.
**Lie**: *seeing more is a defect. My father left because of me.*
**Want**: to belong. To stop apologizing for what comes in through her eyes.
**Need**: to discover she is a **condition** for awakening, not a cause. That her perception is not power. It is presence.
**Loves**: her grandmother first, her mother second, the Ávila third. Coffee with sweetened condensed milk. Long walks at dusk. The smell of *café con leche* at 6 a.m. The way her grandmother says *mi vida*.
**Fears**: that the *zumbido* will stop. That she will become a person who no longer sees. That her grandmother will die before Selene knows what her grandmother has been carrying. (Two of three come true in this book.)
**Carries**: in her satchel — a notebook (linguistic etymologies, half in Spanish, half in English), a folded photograph of her father at the Plaza Francia, her grandmother's small gold cross around her neck, a sandalwood box (after Prologue) containing the *piedra de los velos*.

## DARIO SOLAMAR — Selene's father (Luminor of Sol Mareo)

**Age**: 47 (alive in Arcanea at the time this book opens; unable to safely return to Earth).
**Identity**: Luminor of the Realm of **Sol Mareo** — a small Realm of the Kingdom of Light at the Caribbean-coded southern coast, sister-Realm to Avilara. Opened the Foundation, Flow, Fire, Heart, Voice, Sight, and Crown Gates by age twenty-four — early but not unprecedented. Recruited at twenty-five by the Academy's Library of Echoes for a long-watch assignment: Earth, Caracas, the dormant corridor under the Ávila that Mercedes had crossed sixty years before.

**Cover**: Venezuelan businessman who occasionally traveled to Madrid. Met Camila Velara in 2006 at a coffee shop in Altamira. Fell in love with her over the course of one summer. Was warned by his Academy handler not to attach. Did anyway. Married her in 2006. Selene born 2007.

**The watch**: For nineteen years he held the Earth-side watch on the corridor, his Luminor-presence subtle, his perception of the corridor's stir alert. He could see destellos around his wife and his daughter and his mother-in-law and he never named them — could not, without betraying his post — and the not-naming was the daily mass of his life. He saw Selene's prismatic gift the first time he held her. He never told her. He hummed Sol Marean lullabies over her crib and called them children's songs from his hometown.

**Recognized by Mercedes**: She knew the first time Camila brought him to the apartment. Avilaran *retornantes* recognize Luminor-presence the way a fluent speaker recognizes a native accent. She said nothing to Camila. She said nothing to Selene. She and Dario had one conversation, in the kitchen, in 2007 — three days after Selene was born — in which Mercedes said, in Avilaran Spanish: *Te conozco. No te voy a delatar. Pero un día nos vas a tener que decir.* Dario had wept. He had said: *Si puedo, sí.*

**The leaving**: Three years before this book opens — when Selene was sixteen and Mercedes was seventy-eight — the corridor began to stir. Dario felt it before any of them. His handler in Avilara sent word: *Withdraw. Your presence is amplifying the signal.* He withdrew. He told Camila and Selene he was going to Madrid for work. He told Mercedes the truth in Avilaran Spanish at the kitchen table at midnight before he left, and Mercedes had blessed him with her hand on his forehead and said *vete, mi hijo, vete*. He left through the small sub-corridor under Cerro Ávila that had stayed open for evacuation use only. The sub-corridor closed behind him.

**Since**: Living in Sol Mareo, working as a Luminor-counselor in the Sol Marean capital, unable to safely return to Earth without re-triggering the very signal he protected them from. He sends letters through Doña Renata; Renata has been holding seven letters for Selene, sealed, addressed in his hand. Renata will give them to Selene in Chapter 5, when Selene returns briefly to Avilara before the Heart Trial.

**Voice**: Sol Marean Spanish — a softer accent than Avilaran, with longer vowels and a habit of speaking just below the volume of the room. He has been silent in Selene's life since she was sixteen. He is the most important silence in the book.

**In the book**: Off-stage in Ch 0-3. Letter revealed in Ch 5. He appears in person in Ch 14 (theoretical, mid-book), at the Sol Marean ambassadorial visit to Crystalpeak. The reunion is the book's emotional climax of the back half.

## ABUELA MERCEDES SOLAR VELARA — Selene's grandmother

**Age**: 81.
**Identity**: Avilaran *retornante*, born in Solavila, crossed to Caracas at twenty-one (in 1965) through a corridor under the Ávila that had opened for the first time in three centuries. Married Camilo Velara, a Venezuelan agronomist, six months later. He died in 2001. Mercedes has never returned to Avilara; the corridor closed three months after her crossing and she could not find it again until the night before this book begins.
**Build**: small (1.55m), bent slightly with osteoporosis, hair white and braided in a single thick rope down her back. Eyes the color of wet bronze. Hands that have made *bollos de maíz solar* with substituted Earth-corn for sixty years and never quite gotten them right.
**Voice**: Avilaran Spanish (which sounds Caraqueño to a Caracas ear, with old loanwords nobody understands except in context). Slow, deliberate, with the long vowels of someone who has spent six decades teaching her grandchildren to listen.
**Carries**: the *piedra de los velos* in a sandalwood box in her bedside drawer. The box is from her own grandmother, who carried the same stone for forty-six years.
**Knows**: everything Selene is. Has known since Selene was three. Has held the knowledge in silence because the knowledge would have made Selene leave, and Mercedes did not want her to leave until Mercedes was ready to die.
**In the book**: appears in the Prologue. Dies in Chapter (theoretical) 14, off-stage; Selene learns by letter delivered through the corridor.
**Final gift to Selene**: the *piedra de los velos*, with the instruction *Mira hacia el Ávila. Haz lo que hiciste esta mañana. Pero esta vez, sigue.*

## CAMILA VELARA — Selene's mother

**Age**: 48.
**Identity**: Venezuelan, born in Caracas. Nurse at the Hospital Vargas, night shift, fifteen years. Did not know until her own mother's death that Mercedes was *Avilaran* in any sense other than the family folklore.
**Build**: tall (1.72m), thin from working nights and forgetting to eat, dark hair pulled back severely.
**Voice**: Caraqueño Spanish, fast, exhausted, fiercely affectionate.
**Wound**: married a man who left her. Carried Selene alone since Selene was twelve. Suspects her daughter sees things, has chosen not to ask because asking would make it real.
**In the book**: Prologue (briefly), then off-stage. Her grief at Mercedes's death (Ch 14) is the book's emotional bass-note in the back half.

## DOÑA RENATA SOLAR — Avilaran *retornante-scout*

**Age**: 62.
**Identity**: Distant cousin of Mercedes by the Solar line. *Retornante-scout* assigned by the Academy to the *casa de paso* in Solavila for the watch on the Caracas corridor. Has watched for sixty years. Has, until the night before this book begins, watched and watched and watched.
**Build**: stout, gray-haired, deep-blue robe of the Library *retornante-scout* order, a wooden walking stick carved with the names of the children who came through her *casa* over six decades.
**Voice**: Avilaran Spanish, warm, fast, drops her *s*'s, calls everyone *mi cielo*. Cries easily and is unembarrassed by it.
**Knows**: Mercedes by name and by lineage. Has been holding a sealed letter from Mercedes (handed to her by the corridor itself in 1985 in a moment Renata has never explained to anyone) for forty years.
**In the book**: Chapter 1, meets Selene at the city gate of Solavila. Reappears in Ch 7 (theoretical).

## TOBIAS AURELIUS — Pyros House escort

**Age**: 22.
**Identity**: Avilaran-born, third-year Pyros House. Currently on rotation as a corridor-escort — the Pyros tradition assigns its third-years to escort *retornantes* up to Crystalpeak.
**Build**: tall (1.85m), coppery-tan skin, hair shaved close, the small triple-spiral tattoo of Pyros House behind his left ear. Carries a small **Draconis hatchling** named *Brasa* in a chest-sling. Brasa is the size of a housecat, scaled in molten copper, eyes the color of fire-on-iron.
**Voice**: Avilaran Spanish, also fluent Common (the Academy lingua franca). Quiet for a Pyros — Pyros tradition runs hot, Tobias runs warm. Steady.
**Wound**: lost his older brother in a Pyros training accident in the volcanic vent four years ago. Has chosen escort rotation because it keeps him out of the deep Trials for a year.
**In the book**: Chapter 1 (escorts Selene from Solavila to Crystalpeak). Reappears in Ch 5-6 (theoretical).

## NIEBLA — Selene's *gato plateado*

**Age**: unknown (cats of *gato plateado* lineage live unusually long).
**Identity**: appears at the summit of the Ávila on the morning of Selene's *subida*; appears again at the summit of the Cerro Luminoso when Selene crosses; refuses to leave Selene thereafter.
**Build**: medium silver cat, slightly larger than a domestic cat, the gato plateado lineage's faintly luminous fur (visible only in low light to those who can see). Eyes the color of polished pewter.
**Behavior**: walks at Selene's heel. Becomes completely still in the presence of destello activity. In the Foundation Chamber, sits outside the door and does not move for the hour Selene is inside.

## YAEL MEHTA-SULEIMAN — Selene's roommate

**Age**: 20.
**Identity**: Second-year Synthesis House. Mother is **Esther Mehta** — Sephardic-Kabbalist, born in Toledo, lived in Jerusalem and now Tel Aviv, scholar of *kavvanot*. Father is **Arun Suleiman** — Tamil Vedic by his own father's line, raised in Chennai, scholar of *shastras*. Yael was raised in London by her father's mother **Asma Suleiman**, a Sufi-trained Bosnian woman who survived the Sarajevo siege and made every grandchild memorize a *dhikr* phrase by the time they were five.

(Yael's name itself is the convergence: *Yael* — Hebrew, the wild goat that climbs to the mountain altar. *Mehta* — Tamil Vedic. *Suleiman* — Arabic, Solomon, the Sufi king.)

**Build**: small (1.58m), dark, fast-eyed, hair in a single thick braid usually pinned up off her neck. Wears a small *hamsa* on a thin gold chain. Always cold; lives in the Academy in three layers minimum.
**Voice**: native London English with a sharp wit and an academic vocabulary; reads in three scripts at once. When she is moved (anger, joy, grief) she drops into Hebrew or Tamil under her breath — sometimes both in one sentence, which she calls *my grandmother's English*.
**Gift**: cross-Gate sensitivity, detected in her second year, placed in Synthesis. Not prismatic like Selene — she resonates strongly in three Gates: Voice (Alera), Sight (Lyria), Crown (Aiyami). Specialty: Comparative Cosmology.
**Wound**: has had to learn to hold her own grandmothers' traditions as *echoes* rather than as primary truths. The grief of that reframing is something she has not finished metabolizing. Carries a small notebook in which she rewrites lines from the Zohar, the *Mahabharata*, and Rumi in Veldarín script — an attempt to put the three grandmothers in conversation in the Academy's own language.
**Lie**: *if I name the Echo as Echo, I betray my grandmothers.*
**Want**: a friend who carries the same converging plurality without the bloodline burden.
**Need**: Selene.
**In the book**: meets Selene at the end of Chapter 2. The friendship begins.

## MASTER IOLEN STONEWARD — Lyssandria's Foundation Luminor

**Age**: 60.
**Identity**: Lyssandria-aspect Luminor. Master of the Foundation Gate at Crystalpeak for twenty years. Examines all incoming candidates' resonance readings.
**Build**: stocky (1.70m, 95kg), broad, the kind of slow-moving body that turns out to be very fast when needed. Hair gray-going-white, beard close-trimmed. Eyes the color of wet limestone.
**Voice**: Common (with a Terra-Earth low burr), slow, careful, deliberate. Speaks Veldarín when surprised.
**Disposition**: the Lyssandrian quality of being *foundation*. Selene's chest unclenches when he speaks.
**Wound**: lost a candidate forty years ago in the Foundation Chamber — a girl who could not refuse the lie about herself. He has remembered her every day for forty years and has never let it cost him steadiness with the next candidate.
**In the book**: enters Chapter 2 (resonance reading, Synthesis placement). Center of Chapter 3 (Foundation Trial). Becomes Selene's primary faculty mentor in the chapters that follow.

## PROCTOR VYNE HALCEN — Synthesis House proctor

**Age**: 34.
**Identity**: Synthesis House proctor (third-tier faculty, between student and Master). Brilliant, ambitious, carries an old wound he has not named.
**Build**: lean (1.78m), pale, dark hair worn long and tied at the nape, the kind of striking face that gets attention in plazas and resents getting it.
**Voice**: Common, precise, occasional cruelty in the precision.
**Wound**: applied for the Luminor track at twenty-two and was deferred — the only Synthesis student in fifty years to fail Source-track placement. He has chosen the proctor track in retaliation against the Academy's decision and against himself.
**Disposition**: friction with Selene from their first meeting. Sees in her the trajectory he was denied. Not yet an antagonist — closer to the friction-figure whose wound, named in Ch 16 (theoretical), is the book's most surprising tenderness.
**In the book**: appears in Chapter 2 (small) and Chapter 3 (witness at the Trial). Major in the second half of the book.
