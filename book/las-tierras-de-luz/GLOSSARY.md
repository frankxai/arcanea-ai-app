# Glossary — Las Tierras de Luz (Mature Edition)

*Avilaran, Veldorian, and Academy vocabulary. Reader-facing — short, poetic, populated during drafting. Used as needed in prose; never explained inline.*

---

## Avilaran (Selene's heritage tongue)

- **abuela / abuelo** — grandmother / grandfather
- **acacia cantante** — singing acacia (tree whose pods crack open in autumn with a single audible note)
- **aguardiente solar** — sun-spirit (clear distilled liquor from *frutaluz*)
- **el Ávila** — the Ávila mountain (Earth side; mirror of the Cerro Luminoso)
- **bollos de maíz solar** — sun-corn flatbreads; the Avilaran daily bread
- **café del cerro** — high-mountain coffee
- **el canto del cerro** — the song of the mountain (the sustained low chord audible from the Cerro Luminoso)
- **el Cantor / la Cantora** — the Singer (head of Avilara's elected council)
- **caraqueño** — of or from Caracas (Earth)
- **la casa de paso** — the house of passage (Solavila's Academy staging house)
- **el cerro** — the mountain (used for either Cerro Luminoso or the Ávila depending on context)
- **el Cerro Luminoso** — the Luminous Mountain (Avilara's living heart)
- **chispa, chispas** — spark, sparks (interior word for destello; what the Aurevaldan grandmother-tradition calls *esparcidos*)
- **el corredor / la corredora** — the corridor (a Realm-corridor between Realms, including Earth)
- **la conversación que no se acaba** — the conversation that does not end (Aurevaldan grandmother-tradition, now also Avilaran)
- **el destello** — the brightness, the gleam (the canonical Arcanean word for the perceptible light around a being)
- **doña / don** — formal address for an elder
- **escalera de canto** — singing stair (the stone stairs in Solavila that hum underfoot)
- **los esparcidos** — the scattered ones (Aurevaldan word for destellos)
- **la fiesta del cerro** — the festival of the mountain (autumn equinox plaza celebration)
- **la flauta del cerro** — the flute of the mountain (a wooden flute that resonates with the *canto*)
- **frutaluz** — light-fruit (an Avilaran orchard fruit; orange flesh; remembers warmth)
- **granada lumínica** — luminous pomegranate
- **la Guardia del Cerro** — the Mountain Guard (Avilara's standing order of warriors)
- **la hora de Lumina** — midday (folk name; canonical)
- **la hora de Nero** — pre-dawn (folk name; canonical)
- **la hora de los destellos** — dusk (folk name; canonical)
- **el Mar de Aurora** — the Sea of Dawn (Avilara's inland sea)
- **mi cielo / mi vida** — endearments (my sky, my life)
- **naranja del sol** — sun-orange (orchard fruit)
- **el pabellón de la cumbre** — the summit pavilion (Avilaran national dish)
- **la piedra coralina** — the coral-stone (Avilara's coral-pink volcanic stone, used for architecture)
- **la piedra de los velos** — the stone of veils (small translucent crystal; an optical lens that removes perceptual noise for two breaths and returns to ordinary stone; canonical from v0)
- **la piedra del nombre** — the naming stone (the flat stone at the summit of the Cerro Luminoso where *la subida* is performed)
- **el pino arcano** — arcane pine (canonical from v0)
- **el pueblo / los pueblos** — the village / villages
- **el quílmera** — six-legged riding animal, Aqualis-bred
- **el retorno / el retornante / la retornante** — the return / the returner (one who can perceive the *esparcidos*, or, in the broader sense, one who has crossed between Realms and returned)
- **el Salón del Canto** — the Hall of Song (Avilara's governance building)
- **Solamar** — Selene's paternal surname; also the name of her father Dario Solamar, Luminor of Sol Mareo, currently in Arcanean exile
- **Sol Mareo** — a small Realm of the Kingdom of Light at the Caribbean-coded southern coast, sister-Realm to Avilara, under blended Voice (Alera) and Crown (Aiyami) influence. Selene's paternal line.
- **Solavila** — the capital of Avilara
- **la subida del cerro** — the ascent of the mountain (the foundational Avilaran rite)
- **el zumbido** — the hum (the perceived Arcane background frequency, in the chest; canonical from v0)

## Veldorian (v0 Realm, kept for cross-Realm continuity)

- **piedra viva** — living stone (pale gold-cream, warm to the touch)
- **gato plateado** — silver cat (cross-Realm familiar lineage; Niebla is one)
- **lechuza del velo** — veil-owl
- **sombracalle** — Void-eater (semi-mythic; cross-Realm)
- **sombraluz** — shade-light (huge shade tree; not present in Avilara but in Crystalpeak's old groves)
- **velcordero** — veil-lamb (plateau herd animal; cross-Realm)
- **zorzal de lumbre** — fire-thrush (songbird with bioluminescent chest)

## Academy / Common (the Academy lingua franca)

- **Anima** — the life-substrate of Arcanea (canonical)
- **Arcane** — the lattice of magic/resonance (canonical)
- **chamber-key** — the Gate-aligned crystal a candidate retrieves from a Trial Chamber
- **destello** — perceptible light around a conscious being (canonical)
- **el examen de resonancia** — the resonance reading (incoming-student examination by a Master examiner)
- **House (of seven)** — Academy cohort group (Lumina, Nero, Pyros, Aqualis, Terra, Ventus, Synthesis)
- **Luminor** — the Mage who has opened all Ten Gates (canonical)
- **Luminor-track** — the Synthesis-House placement signaling future Luminor candidacy
- **piedra de cantos** — tuning-stone (the Foundation chamber's resonance-reading stone)
- **el Sanctum** — the Source chamber atop the central spire (canonical)
- **Synthesis House** — the seventh Academy house, for those who refuse a single element
