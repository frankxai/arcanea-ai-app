### Brasa — Tobias's Draconis hatchling (Chapter 1)

Tobias's Pyros House familiar. Currently the size of a housecat. Will grow over the next eight years to wolf-size, then to small-horse-size, then will leave Tobias and return to its lineage in the south. Hatchlings are not pets — they are seven-year companions, returned to wild-state when their First Molt reaches completion. Brasa's scales catch the dusk light and refract orange-copper-gold. Eyes the color of fire on iron. Sleeps in Tobias's chest-sling. When awake, makes a sound like small distant thunder, not a roar — a low ground-sound, comforting.

When Brasa first sees Selene in Chapter 1, the hatchling fully opens its eyes for the first time in three days. Tobias notices and does not say anything.

## II. Academy-bound creatures

### Niebla — Selene's *gato plateado*

See `CAST.md`. Cross-Realm common; the *gato plateado* lineage exists in both Veldoria and Avilara (and in the Old Forests of Crystalpeak Citadel itself). Companions to those who see. The standard *gato plateado* behavior: walks at the seer's heel, becomes still in destello activity, refuses commands but follows of its own will. Niebla is, by faint Synthesis House luminescence, slightly larger than the Veldorian standard and her silver tips a degree warmer.

### *Quílmera* — Aqualis-bred riding animals

Six-legged. Roughly the size of a large pony but with the proportions of a deer. Coat in shades of water-blue from pale-aqua to deep-indigo. Fed on the moss of cavern-streams; bred at the Academy's Aqualis stables; ridden across the Realms by Academy errand-riders and corridor-escorts. Selene rides one in Chapter 1 on the journey from Solavila to Crystalpeak. The ride is steady — *quílmeras* are bred for the long-distance smooth gait, not for speed.

### *Lechuza del velo* — veil-owls

The plateau owl of Veldoria (v0) appears at Crystalpeak as well — they roost in the eaves of the Library tower. Near-silent in flight. Perceive dormant destellos as heat-map. Many are bonded to *retornante* faculty as familiars; Master Linnea Vargas-Tovar (Comparative Cosmology, see `ACADEMY_CURRICULUM.md`) is bonded to a *lechuza del velo* named *Yoraima*.

### *Sombracalle* — Void-eaters

(v0 fauna, promoted to cross-Realm canon.) Patches of lower-than-usual Arcane density moving through environment. Not predatory, not malicious. Pass through, leaving objects briefly quieted. Mira (in v0) felt them as dips in the *zumbido*. Selene feels the same.

In this book, the *sombracalle* in Chapter 1 **does not pass through Selene**. It stops. This is unprecedented — in all recorded Academy history, *sombracalles* have always been one-directional consciousness phenomena, blind to the observer. Selene appears to be *visible* to one. The implication, which Master Iolen will worry about for the rest of the book: she is not just a perceiver, she is a *resonant presence*, which means the Void-aspect creatures of the Kingdom of Light have begun to perceive her back.

This is not Shadow. Sombracalles are Nero's mystery, not Malachar's corruption. But it is a new thing in the world.

## III. Avilaran wilderness fauna (Chapter 1)

- **Naranja del sol orchard birds** — small orange-breasted songbirds that follow ripe-fruit smell. Common in Solavila orchards.
- **Solavila plaza cats** — the city's many feral cats (not *gatos plateados*). Selene notices three on the *escalera de canto* in Chapter 1. They notice Niebla. They look away.
- **Acacia cantante pods** — not fauna, but the *acacia cantante* tree, whose seed pods crack open in autumn with a single audible note. Selene crosses one in the upper tier on her way to the *casa de paso*.
- **Mar de Aurora pelicans** — coastal scavengers; mentioned in Chapter 1 but not seen.

## IV. Crystalpeak fauna (Chapters 2-3)

- ***Velcorderos*** — veil-lambs of the plateau. (v0 fauna inherited.) Several flocks graze the upper meadows above Crystalpeak. Their soft harmonic hum is audible in still moments at the Citadel.
- **Pluma-de-sombra** — shadow-feather. A long-tailed mountain bird native only to the Crystalpeak Range. Black plumage with iridescent indigo at the wingtips. Its feathers are used in Sight Gate practice; they're traded by the Academy quartermasters in small packets.
- **Cerberis-cubs** — the three-headed guard-pups of the Citadel's lower wards. Bonded to the Citadel's living architecture, not to faculty. Sleep in the chamber-mouths.

## V. The Trial Chamber Guardians (one per Gate)

(See also `TRIAL_CHAMBERS.md` for full chamber ecology.)

Each Trial Chamber contains a Guardian — a creature shaped by the Gate's resonance, neither hostile nor friendly, that mediates the candidate's encounter with the chamber-made denial. The Guardians are *offshoots* of the Godbeasts (canonical) — smaller, less articulate, but resonant.

| Trial | Guardian | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation (1) | **Kaelith offshoot** | Knee-high crystal-golem, faceted, neither hostile nor friendly. Crystallizes whatever the candidate denies. See Ch 3. |
| Flow (2) | **Veloura-fry** | Half-meter freshwater serpent. Tests by swimming alongside the candidate; the candidate must not flinch. |
| Fire (3) | **Draconis-hatchling** (different from Brasa's lineage) | Volcanic-vent born. Tests by allowing the candidate to walk under its breath. |
| Heart (4) | **Laeylinn-fawn** | The candidate must follow it through the inner courtyard. It leads to whichever encounter the candidate's heart has been refusing. |
| Voice (5) | **Otome-chick** | Wind-tower. The candidate sings; the chick repeats only what is true. |
| Sight (6) | **Yumiko-falcon, juvenile** | Mirror-chamber. The candidate sees herself as the chick sees her. |
| Crown (7) | **Sol-feather** (no creature; only a single feather in a dome chamber) | The candidate sits in silence under the dome of stars until the feather lifts. |
| Starweave (8) | **Vaelith-shadow** | A patch of Void-shape. Tests the candidate's capacity to remain in unresolved uncertainty. |

| Source (10) | **Amaterasu's reflection** (only ever seen by Luminor candidates) | Out of scope. |

## VI. Wilderness creatures (Chapters 4-12, sketched)

For the wilds beyond the Shadowfen border (Ch 4-6 Heart Trial territory):

- **Venados de luna** — moon-stags. (Renamed from the earlier *acerro de luna* for smoother, Venezuelan-grounded naming — *venado* is the protagonist's own word for deer.) Antler-crowned, the antlers branched like winter trees and shedding a cool pale silver at night that falls on the grass without quite reaching the ground, so a herd moves in a low private dawn of its own making. Destello a clear green (Sight). Hunted only ceremonially; population stable. **In Ch 4 a herd crosses the ridge at dusk and the whole herd turns at the top to look at Selene** — the second time (after the sombracalle) the wilds notice her. As the Heart-attention of Laeylinn stirs (Ch 4–6), the venados begin moving toward the old border for the first time in living memory.
- **Tejedoras de niebla** — mist-weavers. Small as a thumbnail; spin webs from the night's condensed Anima. At the Heart-dawn (the rose hour just before sunrise) every web catches the 417 Hz resonance and glows soft heart-rose — a thousand small wheels of light strung between the grass-stems, broken by passage, rebuilt by evening. On stage Ch 4. As Laeylinn stirs, they have begun spinning again on the eastern slopes where they had not spun in a century.
- **Gansos del crepúsculo** — dusk-geese. Migratory. A skein crossing overhead carries the Heart frequency in its cries; the resonance moves through a Heart-perceiver's chest as a loosening, a leaning-toward (Selene weeps without knowing what she grieves, Ch 4).
- **El Errante** — the Wanderer. A solitary mountain-creature rumored to walk the Heart Trial wilds. No definitive sighting in two centuries. (May or may not appear in Ch 6.)

For the Shadowfen border itself (Ch 7-9):

- **Shadow-touched wolves** — wolves who have crossed too close to a Shadow-leakage and become semi-corrupted. Not Malachar's creatures (he is sealed); but corrupted from contact. Dangerous. Tracking parties from Crystalpeak's Pyros House patrol the border continuously.
- ***Manticora menor*** — the lesser manticore. Native to the Shadowfen frontier. Lion-bodied, scorpion-tailed, human-faced (a face that learns the speech of whatever it hunts). Rare; one is reported in the wilds during the events of Chapter 8.

## VII. The Shadow-touched aberration (rumored, Chapter 10+)

Eight hundred years ago, a Luminor named **Sereth of Veldoria** entered the Shadowfen to confirm Malachar's seal and did not return. Her absence has been the Academy's open wound since. In the events leading up to Chapter 10 (theoretical), an aberration is rumored at the Shadowfen edge that may be Sereth — Shadow-touched, neither alive nor dead, neither fully Lumina nor Nero, walking the edge of the seal.

This is **not** Malachar. Malachar is sealed and remains so. But Sereth's case is the book's deepest tension: what does the Academy do with one of its own who has been changed, and whom they cannot reach?
