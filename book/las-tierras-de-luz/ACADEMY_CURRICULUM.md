## The curricular spine — the Ten Gates

The Academy teaches the Ten Gates directly. Each Gate is approached through four channels — **frequency, breath, element, image** — and tested through the corresponding **Trial Chamber** beneath the Citadel. A candidate may not advance to a Gate's Trial until she has passed instruction in all four channels for that Gate.

## The Outer-World Echoes — comparative cosmology

The mystic traditions of Earth appear in-world in the **Library of Echoes**, an archive on the fifth floor of Crystalpeak's library tower. **The traditions are named openly in the Library catalog, in faculty and student speech, and in the prose of this book** — Kabbalah is called Kabbalah, Vedanta is Vedanta, Sufism is Sufism, Hesychasm is Hesychasm, Hermeticism is Hermeticism. The in-world names (Ladder of Names, Wheel of Light, Stillness Behind, Turning of the World) exist as parallel scholarly terms — used in formal Library catalog entries — but they do not replace or veil the historical names. The Academy is older and more direct, but it does not erase what came before. The Library is staffed by *retornantes* — Academy faculty who have lived on Earth (or other Outer-Worlds) and returned with cataloged knowledge.

The framing is: the Academy teaches the **direct** truth. Every conscious world has, through long attention, perceived fragments of this truth and built traditions around the fragments. These traditions are not wrong — they are *echoes*. The Academy honors them by name, archives them carefully, and teaches them in a single seminar called **Comparative Cosmology** (taken in the second year, after Foundation Trial and before Voice Trial). The seminar uses the historical Earth names of the traditions throughout. Students who come into the Academy carrying one of the traditions (like Yael Mehta-Suleiman, carrying three) are not asked to translate their grandmothers' words into Academy vocabulary; they are asked to bring the grandmothers' words *with* them into a wider conversation. This is the work.

### How the seminar is taught

Comparative Cosmology is taught by **Master Linnea Vargas-Tovar**, a *retornante* who lived twenty years on Earth (Madrid, the early 2000s, as a graduate student in religious studies at the Complutense) and returned to the Academy with what is widely considered the definitive cross-Echo cataloguing. The seminar has twelve students at a time and runs as a closed circle — chairs in a ring, no notes during session.

Each session begins with a question: *what does this Echo perceive that we have not yet named?* Students bring fragments from the Library — a paragraph of Zohar in translation, a Vedic *sloka*, a Sufi *dhikr* phrase, a passage from Pseudo-Macarius — and read them aloud. The seminar listens. Then the seminar attempts to map the fragment onto the Gate-language they have been learning. Mastery is not in the mapping but in the *listening* — the recognition that the Echo says something the Academy's direct language has not yet learned to say.

Yael Mehta-Suleiman is the seminar's most advanced student in her cohort because her three bloodlines give her access to three Echoes from the inside. She is also its most heartbreaking — she has had to learn to hold her own grandmothers' traditions as *echoes* rather than as primary truths, and the grief of that reframing is something she has not finished metabolizing.

Selene takes Comparative Cosmology in Chapter (theoretical) 9-10, after her Voice Trial. In this book she encounters it only in Yael's nightly study habits — the books open in three scripts.

## Examination structure

- **Year One**: Foundation (Lyssandria), Flow (Leyla), Fire (Draconia), Heart (Maylinn). Four Trial Chambers in the first year. Most students fail one or two and retry.
- **Year Two**: Voice (Alera), Sight (Lyria). Comparative Cosmology seminar.
- **Year Three**: Crown (Aiyami), Shift (Elara). Begin field rotations.
- **Year Four**: Unity (Ino). Begin Luminor-track if Synthesis-placed.
- **Year Five (Synthesis only)**: Source (Shinkami). The Luminor threshold.

Selene's accelerated path: Foundation in Chapter 3 (Week 1 of Year One). Heart Trial in the wilds (Chapters 4-6, out of scope this session). The cohort is alarmed because no first-year has taken Foundation and Heart back-to-back in two centuries.

## The Sanctum — Gate 10, Source

The top of Crystalpeak Citadel's central spire. A single circular chamber, no roof, open to the sky. Stars visible at all hours via a permanent Sight-Gate effect. Only Luminors enter. The Source Trial here is the apprenticeship's end and the Mage's beginning. Out of scope for this book.

## The Library of Echoes — practical detail

Located on the fifth floor of Crystalpeak's library tower. Three reading rooms (Veldarín-script, Common, and Outer-Script). A small private cell where Yael often studies. A locked archive accessible only to *retornantes* and the Synthesis-track Luminors-in-formation. The Library's catalog uses a five-character code: tradition (K / V / B / H / A) plus three-digit accession. Mercedes's *piedra de los velos* — when Selene presents it in Chapter (theoretical) 7 — is catalogued under LE-A-024.
