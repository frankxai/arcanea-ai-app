# Avilara — Realm of the Kingdom of Light

*A new Realm canon. Spanish-coded, coastal-cosmopolitan, sister to Veldoria. Caracas-inspired but not Caracas — what Caracas is made of, if you peel back the volcanic rock and listen for the singing underneath.*

---

## Canon placement

Avilara is a **Realm of the Kingdom of Light**, sovereign and longstanding, populated in the centuries after the Sealing of the Dark Lord. It is **not** a Realm of the Second Settling (like Veldoria) — Avilara's stones carry wound-memory from the First War, when the Realm stood at the southern fire-front and burned and was rebuilt three times. Its Anima moves fast and warm. Its Arcane responds to heat and to song. Avilarans have learned to live with their inheritance: a faster speech, a louder market, a deeper laugh, a quicker grief. They believe — collectively and without doctrine — that the world is a fire that wants to be sung to.

Under blended **Third Gate (Fire/Solar — Draconia, 396 Hz)** and **Second Gate (Flow/Water — Leyla, 285 Hz)** influence. The two influences cross at the capital, **Solavila**, where the great singing mountain (the *Cerro Luminoso*) meets the inland sea (the *Mar de Aurora*). Avilara's signature: the marriage of fire and water in a single Realm.

**Aurevalde** — the v0 origin Realm of Bela — is Avilara's spiritual ancestor. The grandmother-tradition (*la conversación que no se acaba*) traveled from Aurevalde to Avilara some six centuries before the events of this book, carried by a single *retornante* lineage that settled in the Solavila tiers. By Selene's time, *la conversación* is part of Avilaran cultural substrate — not the dominant tradition, but a known one, with practitioners in every generation.

## Geography

### The Cerro Luminoso

The great singing mountain at the heart of Avilara. 4,200 meters at its summit. Visible from every part of the central Realm. Volcanic in deep geology but not in temperament — the volcano has been still for thirty thousand years, but the Arcane in its core continues to sing.

The *canto del cerro* is the sustained low chord that rises out of the mountain's core, audible only to the attentive. Avilarans say a child first hears the *canto* when she is ready to be alone in the world — typically between thirteen and seventeen. Selene first hears it on the summit of the Ávila in Caracas, which is the Earth-mirror of the Cerro Luminoso. (The Ávila is what the Cerro is, on Earth, with the volume turned down.)

The summit holds a single weathered stone called the **Piedra del Nombre** (the Naming Stone) — flat-topped, the size of a small altar. Avilarans climb the Cerro alone once in their life, in dawn light, and speak their own name into the wind. The mountain speaks the name back. This is *la subida del cerro*.

### Solavila — the capital

Built in seven tiers up the southwestern flank of the Cerro Luminoso, facing the Mar de Aurora. Architecture in *piedra coralina* — a coral-pink volcanic stone quarried from the Cerro's lower slopes, warm to the touch, slightly translucent at the edges. Roofs in dark blue tile from the sea-cliff kilns. Plazas at every tier-break, connected by stone stairs called *escaleras de canto* (singing stairs — they hum faintly underfoot).

**Tier 1**: the harbor and the markets. Loud, polyglot, salt-spray.
**Tier 2**: the artisan quarters. Glass-blowers, dye-vats, copper-smiths.
**Tier 3**: the residential heart. Café culture, plaza music, family compounds with internal courtyards.
**Tier 4**: the civic tier. The *Salón del Canto* (governance hall), the *Biblioteca de los Velos* (Avilara's small Library of Veils, an Earthly-archive Academy outpost).
**Tier 5**: the university and the academies. The Avilaran branch of the Arcanea Academy (a *casa de paso* — house of passage — for Avilaran-born students before they travel to Crystalpeak).
**Tier 6**: the gardens. Sustained-bloom orchards of *naranja del sol*, *granada lumínica*, the *acacia cantante* whose pods release a single note when they crack open in autumn.
**Tier 7**: the sky-gardens and the high temple. Closest to the Cerro's summit. Where the *retornantes* live.

Population of Solavila: about 200,000. Population of Avilara: about three million across the central plains, the coastal cities, the southern fishing villages, and the highland *pueblos del cerro*.

### The Mar de Aurora

An inland sea, Caribbean-coded — warm, bioluminescent at certain phases of the moon, fed by underground hydrology from three Realms. Coastal towns built up the cliffs in tiers like Solavila but smaller. Fishing fleets out of **Caleta Roja** and **Punta Solana**. The sea carries Leyla's flow-resonance; old captains can navigate by the *zumbido* of the water alone.

### The southern plains and the highlands

The breadbasket Realm. Wheat, *maíz solar* (sun-corn — the grain that becomes the famous *bollos*), olives, lemons, the strange Avilaran fruits that travel poorly (*frutaluz*, *granada lumínica*, *naranja del cerro*). Highland *pueblos* in the cooler reaches where *quílmera* (the six-legged riding animals) are bred and where the older Aurevaldan grandmother-tradition is most alive in daily life.

### The northern frontier

Borders the **Wastes of Tarn** (canonical Arcanean wildlands). Patrolled by the *Guardia del Cerro* — Avilara's small standing order of warriors trained at the Academy and returned home. The frontier is not contested — the Wastes are simply empty — but it is watched, because the Wastes change shape every century.

## Culture

### Language — Avilaran Spanish

A Romance-family language of Arcanea, descended from the same founding-settler tongue as Veldarín but diverged earlier and faster. **Avilaran sounds like Caraqueño Spanish** to a Caracas ear: the dropped *s*, the rapid syllables, the rising intonation on questions, the *vos* in intimate speech. But it carries Aurevaldan loanwords (*esparcidos*, *retornante*, *piedra de los velos*, *conversación que no se acaba*) and a handful of Avilaran-original words for things that do not exist on Earth.

A Caraqueño Earth-speaker understands ~85% of Avilaran without study. Selene understands it instantly when she arrives. Her Caracas accent passes for a southern-plains accent. By Chapter 2 she is being mistaken for a *highland girl from Punta Solana*, which is a quiet relief.

### Food

- **Bollos de maíz solar** — round flatbreads from *maíz solar*, the Avilaran sun-corn. Eaten with everything. Mercedes's recipe (passed down through six generations of *retornantes*) is the same as the Solavila plaza recipe; Selene tastes it for the first time properly in Chapter 1 and weeps without knowing why.
- **Pabellón de la cumbre** — Avilaran national dish. Slow-braised meat, black beans cooked with *acacia cantante* pods, white rice with *naranja del sol* zest, fried plantain. The exact mirror of Venezuela's *pabellón criollo*, with Arcanean ingredients.
- **Café del cerro** — the high-mountain coffee that grows only on the Cerro Luminoso's lower slopes. Roasted dark. Drunk with sweetened condensed *velcordero* milk.
- **Aguardiente solar** — clear spirit distilled from *frutaluz*. Drunk in tiny cups at the end of a meal.

### Music and movement

Avilaran music is plaza-music. Guitars, *cuatro* (the four-string), small hand-drums, the *flauta del cerro* (a wooden flute that resonates with the *canto del cerro* and is therefore in tune by being made). Songs are extempore as often as composed. Avilarans dance in the plazas at dusk on the night of the *fiesta del cerro* (autumn equinox) and at any wedding or *retornante*-return.

Avilarans are touchers — handshakes that linger, embraces, a hand on the shoulder while speaking. Northerners (Veldorians, for instance) find this disconcerting; Avilarans find Veldorian reserve sad.

### Religion and ritual

Avilara has no temple-faith. What it has is *prácticas* — practices. The most universal practice is *la subida del cerro* (the ascent), performed once in a lifetime between thirteen and seventeen, alone, in dawn light. A girl or boy who has done the *subida* is considered an adult. (Selene does her *subida* at nineteen, late by Avilaran reckoning but not unheard of — it is also acceptable to do the *subida* at the first crisis of true seeing.)

Other practices: the *fiesta del cerro* (autumn equinox plaza celebration); the *retornante-vigil* (a year-long watch kept by a family when a child crosses to Earth or to another Realm); the *piedra-pasaje* (the placing of a small *piedra coralina* on the threshold of a new home or marriage); the *canto del retorno* (the song sung when a *retornante* comes home).

The Aurevaldan grandmother-tradition (*la conversación que no se acaba*) is part of the substrate but not the dominant practice. Selene's grandmother carried it as her core; most Avilarans hold it more lightly.

### Politics and structure

Avilara is a council-democracy. Seven Houses of governance (unrelated to the Academy's Seven Houses, confusing but historical) elect a *Cantora* (or *Cantor* — singer) every five years. The current Cantora is **Liana Mendíbal-Quintero**, in her second term, popular, pragmatic. The *Cantora* does not appear in this book; she may appear in later books.

The Academy presence in Solavila is the *casa de paso* on the fifth tier — a small staging house where Avilaran-born admits to Crystalpeak spend a week before journeying north. Doña Renata Solar is the *casa*'s *retornante-scout*.

## Avilarans Selene meets in Chapter 1

- **Doña Renata Solar** — sixty-two, *retornante-scout* at the *casa de paso*. Distant cousin of Mercedes. Has watched the Caracas corridor for sixty years on assignment from the Academy. Will appear briefly in Ch 1 and again in Ch 7 (if/when written).
- **Tobias Aurelius** — twenty-two, third-year Pyros House Academy student, currently on rotation as a corridor-escort. Avilaran-born. Carries a Draconis hatchling (his familiar) in a chest-sling.
- **The proprietress of the café** — unnamed, late forties, serves the *bollos* and watches Selene eat them with the practiced attention of someone who has served many *retornantes* their first home meal.

## Avilara vs Veldoria — at a glance

| Quality | Avilara | Veldoria (v0) |
|---|---|---|
| Climate | Warm, coastal-tropical, volcanic-fed | Temperate, valley, slow river |
| Speed | Fast, kinetic, loud | Slow, contemplative, hushed |
| Architecture | *Piedra coralina* + dark-blue tile | *Piedra viva* (pale cream-gold) |
| Music | Plazas, guitars, extempore | Bakery-hum, three notes, *Lucía*'s evening guitar |
| Gate influence | Third (Fire/Draconia) + Second (Flow/Leyla) | Fifth (Voice/Alera) |
| Wound-memory | First War southern fire-front | None — Realm of the Second Settling |
| Sister-tradition | Aurevaldan substrate inherited (six centuries) | Aurevaldan substrate fresh (60 years, via Bela) |
| Relation to Crystalpeak | Has *casa de paso* (Tier 5) | Has none — graduates rare |

Avilara is the Realm Selene's grandmother is *from*. Veldoria is the Realm of the quiet valley novella. They are both Spanish-coded Realms of the Kingdom of Light, sister-Realms, no narrative overlap.
