# ARCHIVED — Tier-2 Edition

This directory contains the original *Las Tierras de Luz* — the quiet-valley novella starring eleven-year-old Mira in the Realm of Veldoria. It is preserved here as a Tier-2 *Legend of the Kingdom of Light*: a short literary-magical-realism work that lives in the shadow of, but no longer above, the canonical *Las Tierras de Luz* epic now located at `book/las-tierras-de-luz/`.

The canonical Terras de Luz is the Academy of Light epic following Selene Velara across Caracas, Avilara, and Crystalpeak Citadel. The two books share a Kingdom but no plot, no characters, and no temporal connection. Veldoria (this novella's Realm) and Avilara (the new book's Spanish-coded Realm) are sister-Realms; that is the only bridge, and it is geographic.

Everything in this directory is preserved as-is for archival reference. The Aurevaldan oral cosmology (`FOLK_COSMOLOGY_AUREVALDE.md`), the bestiary, the color taxonomy, and the folk origin myth are all promoted in the new book at the level of cosmology — but the characters and chapters of this draft remain its own quiet thing, unrevised.

If you came here looking for the Brent-Weeks-grade Academy story, you want `book/las-tierras-de-luz/`.

If you came here looking for an eleven-year-old in a valley discovering she sees something no one else can, this is still that book.
