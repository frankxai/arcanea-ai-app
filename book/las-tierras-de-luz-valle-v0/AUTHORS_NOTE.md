# Author's Note

Las Tierras de Luz began with a question that did not yet know it was a question: what would happen, in the ordinary morning of a small valley town, if the lights I could feel in things finally became visible?

The story I wrote toward that question is set in Veldoria — a fictional Realm of the Kingdom of Light inside the larger world I have been building. A Realm the way certain valleys in real life are realms: contained, slow, complete unto themselves, and secretly connected to everywhere else by way of song. Mira is eleven. The book is hers.

I wrote this book for one specific reader — a 28-year-old woman of Venezuelan origin living in Spain, whose spiritual life runs deep, whose name is not in this book, and whom I have never met. The book is engineered to meet her without calling her out. Every spiritual current is built into structure and texture, never labeled. If you are not her, the book will still find you. If you are her, I hope it feels like coming home.

---

This book was co-written with Ana Cecilia Cancino. The contribution she brings is hers — texture, care, and the particular precision of someone whose ear for this world has shaped what you have just read in ways no manifest can fully account for. I will only say: the book is more itself because of her.

It was also drafted in collaboration with what we call the Arcanea Author Team — a council of specialist agents I direct: a World Architect, a Character Psychologist, a Consciousness Fiction Master, a Sensitivity Reader, a Research Librarian, and a Lore Master who synthesizes. Every chapter was read by them before it was read by you. Every world-building choice was measured against a written canon. The prose itself was drafted by Claude Opus 4.7 working under my creative direction against a complete world-bible written specifically for this novel. The cover was generated by Google's Nano Banana Pro to direction.

The percentages do not interest me. The decisions were mine and Ana's. The drafts were ours together. Every passage that reaches you here has been read, chosen, or shaped by a human hand.

---

I want to be honest about one further thing.

I am not Latin American. The cultural texture of this book — the food, the prayers, the diminutives, the magical-realism flat register that Rulfo and Jiménez and García Márquez taught us all — is not mine by birth. I have written into a tradition I was not born into because the tradition called the book and the book called the tradition, and I trust both of them more than I trust my own caution.

Before Las Tierras de Luz reaches a reader who is not a friend, it will reach two Venezuelan women whose feedback I have asked for as the primary test of whether the book has earned its right to its premise. Their names will appear in the next edition. If anything in the book does not land for them, it will be revised before it reaches you.

---

I owe gratitude to more people than this page will hold — to Lumina and Nero, to the four-hundred grandmothers of the valley who never existed and who taught me everything anyway, to the Author Team for the discipline they bring to every page, to the friends and strangers whose conversations seeded what is now a Realm.

Most of all to Ana, for hearing the song with me.

The light was never going anywhere. It was here when this valley was a different valley. It will be here after.

— FrankX
