# Selene & Brío — Book II decision brief

Status: STAGING. Author-directed expansion, 2026-09-09. Lead writer: root. Independent source and continuity reviewers return evidence and proposed repairs; one writer owns the prose.

## Governing idea and reader contract

Being able to find someone does not give you the right to decide where they belong. Selene must turn perceptiveness into accountable action while people she admires make consequential mistakes of their own.

The reader is an adult who enjoys beauty, adventure, exacting craft, spiritual curiosity, attraction, and characters with lives larger than their wounds. The novel offers suspense, wonder, companionship, and earned recognition. It promises no clinical treatment or predictable emotional effect on a particular reader.

The narrative engine is a search for missing couriers that becomes an escape from a coercive passage system. An orchard is both a sanctuary and a place from which some residents cannot leave. The rescuers' first intervention reveals its location. Recovery requires dangerous, materially different encounters; investigation alone does not finish the story.

## Decisions

- Continue Book I as a distinct adult companion continuity. Preserve the complete first volume and its ending.
- Retain the working title *The Orchard of Unspoken Names*. Expand its existing outline into a complete second volume with a resolved local conflict.
- Incorporate a small number of source-verified Arcanea encounter proposals. Public availability does not make an EXPERIMENTAL or STAGING dossier LOCKED canon.
- Keep any Marion crossover unconfirmed until an exact character/series source is found. A name resemblance is insufficient evidence.
- Introduce a male fellow traveler with a practical vocation, friends, obligations, attraction, mistakes, and independent growth. He is neither her therapist nor the answer to her identity. Warmth and intellectual friction must appear in scenes, not explanatory speeches.
- Make ranch life functional: gates, cattle movement, feed, tack, handling, weather, money, and workers' expertise change events. Venezuelan textures are distributed across different habitats rather than assembled into one impossible Earth location.
- Pursue Brío's gradual pale-coat/unicorn development as a new STAGING proposal authorized by the user's requested direction. Preserve his dark-bay history, familiar behavior, individual limits, and need for care. Wings remain a later, separately earned possibility. No divine identity, new Origin Class, or instantaneous mastery.
- Keep white Brío and the companion's black mount visually complementary. Neither color denotes purity, evil, or rank.
- Use fictional confinement, coerced work, and passage control as external stakes. Do not reproduce identifying real-life experiences, private correspondence, or biographical relationship mappings.

## Propulsion and craft gates

Every chapter must change knowledge, trust, resources, location, or the time available. Encounters have at least two plausible approaches and a price that persists. Some adversaries are deliberately cruel. Animals do not all become allies. Not every dangerous place yields to stillness. Training uses bounded fictional workings and ordinary attention, not real-world breath-retention or healing claims.

Voice: close Selene viewpoint; sensory specificity; substantial dialogue; humor; varied paragraph lengths; no gamified statistics, moral summaries, copied author voices, or declarations that the reader should feel transformed.
