## Causality and timing fences

Book II begins four months after Book I's closing errand. Field travel occupies weeks; metamorphosis matures over subsequent months. No overnight whitening, full horn or wings. Walking corridors carry travelers; aquifer-corridors do not. The lens still clarifies already-seen living light for two breaths, then requires a day's warmth. Perception is never a truth detector. Repeated wet reflections replay observed actions, not inaccessible memory. A borrowed identity is a limited access instrument, not a change of personhood.
