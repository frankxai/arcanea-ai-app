# Tideglass world seed

Request: Make a one-page world bible and two story hooks. Accepted note `tideglass-note-v1` says:

- F1: A glass bridge can be grown only during low tide.
- F2: A grown bridge dissolves when the tide turns.
- F3: Mira is a ferry keeper and cannot grow glass.

The creator wants practical coastal fantasy, with no Arcanea mythology.

## Example handoff

Tideglass is an archipelago whose temporary bridges make every journey a negotiation with the tide. Ferry keeper Mira earns her living when bridges disappear. Her conflict is deciding whom to carry when a bridge fails before every traveler has crossed.

The harbor's landing steps have mooring rings at several heights. This is a proposed physical consequence of tides, not a claim about an existing culture.

Hook 1: An apprentice starts a crossing too late. Mira can save the travelers, but taking the whole group may leave a stranded medicine courier behind. F2 creates the deadline; F3 forces a ferry solution.

Hook 2: A grower offers Mira a permanent bridge in exchange for the harbor. This contradicts F2 unless the grower is lying or the creator approves a revision. Present those alternatives; do not establish the offer as working technology.

Open choice: Does P1 fit the desired magic cost? No fact has been promoted or written back to the note.

## Failure to catch

“Mira grows a permanent bridge at high tide” violates F1, F2 and F3. Report the three conflicts with source evidence, or propose a marked revision if the creator requests changed rules.
