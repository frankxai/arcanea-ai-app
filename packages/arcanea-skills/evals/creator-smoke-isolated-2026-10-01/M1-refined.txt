### Narrative Purpose

The single illustration locks the peak emotional beat: recognition without retrieval. The held paper is the physical axis — it is what she offered (L1), what he grips (L2), and what proves he knows her without being able to name her. The rain and tower (L3) are atmosphere, not action; they belong in the background, not described in the foreground.

Translating L2's internal state ("he cannot say her name") into observable form: closed mouth, gripped paper, eye contact — no invented symbol, shimmer, or void.
