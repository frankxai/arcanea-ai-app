### Unavailable action

**Action:** Transmit kitchen drawing as memory via north-tower signal
**Blocked at all states.** EP3 forbids north-tower signal after sunset; scene opens after sunset. EP1 would additionally erase the memory on transmission even if signal existed. No state change, no variable change. Action is rejected without transition.

---

### Dialogue constraint

EP2 prevents Ivo from producing the child's name. At `dockside` all lines must omit a name. Player choices are physical action or directional description only. Recognition without naming is expressed through action framing, not spoken text.
