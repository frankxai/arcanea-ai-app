# Ember Post — Compact World Bible

## Premise

Accepted (EP1): The Ember Post moves memories between towers. Each courier sends one per day; sending erases it from the sender permanently.

Proposed premise (P1; uses EP1, EP3-R): If P1 is adopted, distance is measured in couriers consumed — each relay hop costs another erasure. The Post keeps settlements connected; the work slowly hollows out those who run it.

Open choice (O1): Whether a sent memory arrives intact at a named recipient, dissipates as signal, or can be intercepted is unspecified.

## North Tower

Accepted (EP3-R): Transmits after sunset via hand-cranked relay. Cost: one hour of physical work per memory sent.

Proposed place (P6; uses EP3-R): If P6 is adopted, the tower's highland district is cut off from daylight travel in winter; night transmission becomes the only connection, and the relay's cost falls on whoever can still stand at the crank.

Open choice (O2): Whether the hour-of-work cost applies at every hop in a multi-tower chain, and whether a non-courier can operate the crank, are unspecified.

## Characters

**Ivo** — Accepted (EP2): Lost his daughter's name yesterday. Still recognizes her face.

Proposed (P2; uses EP1, EP2): If P2 is adopted, Ivo is a courier who transmitted that memory as a sent message. Why a father would send his child's name is open choice (O3).

**Sable** — Proposed character (P3, P4): Long-serving courier. Cannot name most of her years before she joined the Post. Not established as the only courier in this condition; open choice (O4) is how widespread the erosion runs and whether the Post acknowledges it.

## Connected Hooks

### Proposed hook (H1): The Name She Carries
*Uses EP2, EP3-R; proposed dependencies on P2, P5*

If P2 is adopted, the memory of Ivo's daughter's name was received in the highland district. If P5 is adopted, the recipient could re-send it — but would lose it herself. It may be the only name she has for him.

The relay puts this within reach after sunset. One hour on the crank, per memory in the chain. The obstacle is not the machinery; it is whether she agrees to pay. If P2 is not adopted and Ivo is not a courier, someone transmitted his daughter's name without his knowledge — and the question shifts to by whose authority.

### Proposed hook (H2): The Relay Debt
*Uses EP3-R, H1; proposed dependencies on P6*

If H1 is adopted, the transmission must run at night. If P6 is adopted, the two north tower couriers have already spent their daily sends. Neither can both crank the relay and pay the erasure. The hook's obstacle: who operates the crank, who sends, and what each one loses. H1's outcome — agreement, refusal, silence — determines whether the hour of work was worth spending at all.

Open choice (O2, continued): If a non-courier can operate the crank, the cost structure of H2 changes entirely.
