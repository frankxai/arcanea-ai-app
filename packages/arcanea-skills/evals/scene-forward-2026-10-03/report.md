## Source issues and limits

The preserved input's Hook A says Vale can identify high-yield trees from their
glyphs. No supplied knowledge source supports that interpretation alongside CO3.
A proposed repair is to let him compare copied shapes while leaving readiness
interpretation to an explicitly proposed knowledgeable character. The scene makes
Neri's limited recognition such a proposal; it does not grant it as a source fact.
The input also swaps O5/O6 character-role and glyph-reading questions between
sections. Reconcile those labels before adopting the bible. Neither finding
overwrites or reclassifies the original evaluation output.

The lead read both complete scenes for time, geography, money, knowledge and
source status. Mechanical checks count prose, bind input/output bytes, verify
complete source/question sections and confirm the unchanged zero-ready catalog.
Those checks cannot establish emotional engagement or voice. Neri's prior
relationship with Vale, her involvement in his sale and the future usefulness of
the tiles remain author choices. A different provider/reader must judge a fresh
request independently before this meets the forward-evaluation release gate.
