## 2. DIALOGUE RULES BY FACTION

### 1. Starlight Corps
- **Cadence**: Crisp, economical, respectful, high-stakes military brevity.
- **Key Terms**: *Vector, Sentinel, Oath-line, Luminescence, Vanguard, Sector.*
- **Banned**: Whining, defeatism, slang without purpose, sloppy grammar.
- *Sample Line*: *"Hold the perimeter at 741. When the wave hits, trust your Shael plate. The dawn does not ask the night for permission."*

### 2. The Seven Academy Houses
- **Cadence**: Articulate, scholastic, Latinate phrasing, intellectual arrogance or refined diplomacy.
- **Key Terms**: *Resonance theorem, Harmonic ratio, The Archives, Eldrian syntax, Merit.*
- *Sample Line*: *"Your technique possesses brute combustion, Apprentice, but utterly lacks harmonic counterpoint. You are setting the room on fire when you should be conducting a symphony."*

### 3. The Gate-Touched Underground
- **Cadence**: Direct, rhythmic, street-smart, defensive, authentic emotional weight.
- **Key Terms**: *The Surge, Registry dogs, The Karst, Sparked, Hollowed, Gray line.*
- *Sample Line*: *"The Academy talks about magic like it’s a math equation. Out here in the Karst, magic is the only reason you woke up with a pulse."*

### 4. The Void Ascendants
- **Cadence**: Eerily calm, mournful, intimately philosophical, sorrowful pity for the listener.
- **Key Terms**: *The False Dawn, Dissonance, Malachar’s Peace, The Unraveling, The Cage.*
- *Sample Line*: *"You fight so hard to preserve a cage that was locked before your great-grandparents were born. Put down the blade, child. The silence is not your enemy."*

## 3. THE 5 LAWS OF MASTERFUL DIALOGUE (ANTI-SLOP)

1. **Subtext Over Exposition**: Characters never explain worldbuilding they both already know (no "As you know, Bob...").
2. **Pressure Generates Speech**: Characters speak because they want something urgently, or to prevent something catastrophic.
3. **Unique Sentence Length**: A Draconia-type speaks in short, punchy bursts (3–8 words). An Aiyami-type speaks in balanced, architectonic clauses (15–25 words).
4. **Physical Interruption**: High-tension dialogue is always interwoven with physical action, micro-expressions, and acoustic environmental shifts.
5. **No 21st Century Anachronisms**: Avoid modern corporate buzzwords, internet sarcasm, or casual pop-culture idioms.
