## 1. THE PHILOSOPHY OF THE ARCANEA GENOME

In lesser fantasy systems, characters are defined merely by stats, class archetypes (e.g. "Level 5 Mage"), or cosmetic descriptions.

In Arcanea, every character is designed as a **living emotional and physical pressure system**. A character’s magical powers are never accidental; they are direct physical manifestations of their deepest psychological wounds, moral convictions, and cosmic origin.

```
                         THE CHARACTER DIAMOND
                                   ▲
                                   │
                                DESIRE
                         (The Conscious Goal)
                                   │
      MASK ────────────────────────┼──────────────────────── WOUND
 (The Social Persona)              │                  (The Formative Loss)
                                   │
                                 TRUTH
                         (The Arc Resolution)
                                   ▼
```

## 3. HOW THE FIELDS GENERATE DRAMATIC MOMENTUM

1. **The Wound Fuels the Mask**: A Gate-Touched whose family was incinerated by uncontrolled fire power will wear a mask of frigid, emotionless discipline.
2. **The Limitation Creates Suspense**: If a character’s 741 Hz Crown laser blinds them for three minutes after firing, every combat encounter requires tactical planning.
3. **The Truth Closes the Character Arc**: When the character drops the mask, confronts the wound, and accepts the truth, their 10th Gate finally opens.
