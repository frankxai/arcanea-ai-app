## 4. PROPULSION & NAVIGATION SYSTEMS

### The Three Drive Types
1. **The Sol-Warp Drive**: Utilizes Sol Quartz lasers to compress spacetime ahead of the ship, achieving sub-light traversal across planetary systems.
2. **The Aquifer Slipstream Drive**: Enables ships to submerge into subterranean and inter-dimensional water channels, traveling between distant realms in hours instead of months.
3. **The Phase-Null Drive**: Channels Nero Void frequencies to skip entirely outside normal physics, bypassing physical barriers at extreme risk of temporal distortion.

## 5. STARSHIP COMBAT & SHIP-BOARDING RULES

- **Frequency Shield Inversion**: A ship’s shields operate on a specific Gate frequency. To penetrate, the attacking ship must tune its weapon batteries to the harmonic inverse (e.g. countering a 417 Hz Heart shield with a 396 Hz Fire frequency overload).
- **The Beast-Bay Launch**: Carriers can release bonded airborne Godbeast whelps (such as Solar Drakes or Crystal Falcons) to dogfight enemy fighter drones.
