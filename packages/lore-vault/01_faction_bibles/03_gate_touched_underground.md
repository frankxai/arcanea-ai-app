## 1. WHO ARE THE GATE-TOUCHED?

While Academy nobles spend decades studying ancient texts to open a single Gate under controlled laboratory conditions, **The Gate-Touched** are beings whose Gates blasted open spontaneously.

A Gate-Touched awakening is almost always triggered by:
1. **Luminor Fragment Meteor Falls** (Vael Rain or Ember Falls)
2. **Severe Physical or Psychological Trauma** (near-death adrenaline rupturing energy meridians)
3. **Genetic Anomaly / Latent Resonance Bloom**

Because their power is untuned, institutional governments view them as dangerous liabilities. The Academy maintains the **Mandatory Mutant Registry**, seeking to contain, tag, or chemically suppress anyone exhibiting spontaneous Gate resonance.

In response, the **Gate-Touched Underground** was forged — a covert network of safehouses, subterranean waystations, and rogue scholars dedicated to protecting and training these wild adepts.

## 2. THE MUTANT TAXONOMY & PHYSIOLOGY

| Classification | Trigger Mechanism | Manifestation & Symptoms | Danger Level | Societal Perception |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sparked** | Single Vael Crystal exposure | One Gate forced open; elemental leaks during anger or fear | Low–Medium | Feared by commoners |
| **Resonant** | Vael Rain atmospheric surge | Multiple Gates flickering; uncontrollable frequency shifts | High | Hunted by Registry agents |
| **Harmonic** | Conscious integration & training | Gate resonance stabilized; biomorphic crystal fusion | Low (Controlled) | Accepted only in secret |
| **Fractured** | Nero Shard radiation | Dissonance tears energy meridians; body phases randomly | Extreme (To self) | Quarantined / Pitied |
| **Ascendant** | The Gray Threshold (Vael + Nero) | Reaches Luminor-level power without formal rites | God-Tier | Outlawed by the High Council |

### The Gray Threshold (Vel'Thaan's Phenomenon)
The rarest and most formidable state occurs when a mutant is simultaneously exposed to both **Vael Light** and **Nero Void**. Instead of canceling each other out, the dual forces form a perpetual energetic vortex in the heart meridian. A Gray Ascendant can manipulate both radiant photons and null-space gravity simultaneously.

## 3. NETWORK HUBS & SAFEHOUSES

1. **The Sub-Basin Sanctuary (Veldoria Subterrane)**: An underground cavern system beneath the Silver Rapids where 2,000 Gate-Touched live in a hidden commune powered by raw geothermal crystal vents.
2. **The Rust Spire (Ironhold Outskirts)**: A decommissioned starship scrap yard converted into a triage clinic and frequency-dampening training facility.
3. **The Mirror Corridor (Between Realms)**: A drifting subterranean pathway that can only be entered by those whose Sight Gate resonates between 630 Hz and 650 Hz.

## 4. KEY CHARACTERS IN THE UNDERGROUND

### Mara "The Shattered Queen"
- **Origin**: Former Academy honors student whose Crown and Fire Gates ruptured during a laboratory accident.
- **Power**: Emits radiant solar plasma from her cracked skin, which she channels through heavy obsidian bracers.
- **Role**: Supreme Commander of the Southern Underground Safehouses.
- **Core Wound**: Believes her family abandoned her to preserve their noble reputation.

### Ren the Hollow
- **Origin**: Fractured mutant survivor of a Nero Strike.
- **Power**: Can turn entirely intangible, moving through solid bulkheads and absorbing incoming energy attacks.
- **Role**: Master Infiltrator and Chief Courier.

## 5. PLOT HOOKS & ADVENTURE MODULES

1. **The Infiltration of Spire 9**: The player characters must break into the Academy Registry Archives to wipe the biometric records of 50 newly awakened children.
2. **The Gray Surge**: A young orphan in a border settlement has awakened with dual Vael-Nero resonance, accidentally causing an entire town to phase in and out of reality.
3. **The Traitor in the Tunnels**: Safehouses across the Oceanic Sector are being raided with surgical precision; the Underground must root out a high-ranking spy before the Central Sanctuary is compromised.
