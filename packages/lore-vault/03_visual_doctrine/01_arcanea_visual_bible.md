## 2. THE SEVEN VISUAL COMMANDMENTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

| # | Commandment | Execution Rule | What is Banned (The Slop List) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Material Integrity** | Every surface must be recognizable as a real or mythic material: star-forged silver, carved obsidian, nacre, oiled leather, light-silk. | ❌ Flat gray plastic, generic rubber suits, uniform CG textures. |
| 2 | **Cosmic Dusk Lighting** | Main scenes occur at astronomical twilight, eclipse thresholds, or within glowing crystal caverns with strong rim-lighting. | ❌ Flat midday fluorescent lighting, washed-out white skies. |
| 3 | **Color Precision** | Anchor palettes in deep void midnight (#0B0E14), oceanic teal (#00BCD4), and warm celestial gold (#FFD700). | ❌ Neon oversaturation, cheap rainbow glows, desaturated dirt mud. |
| 4 | **Noble Silhouette** | Every character and starship must pass the **50-Pixel Shadow Test** — immediately recognizable by outline alone. | ❌ Generic bulky MMO shoulder pads, cluttered indistinguishable shapes. |
| 5 | **Sacred Geometry** | Sigils and UI glyphs must follow mathematical harmonic ratios (octahedrons, golden spirals, star polyhedra). | ❌ Random scribbles, fake tribal tattoos, generic sci-fi hex grids. |
| 6 | **Beast Anchoring** | When depicting gods or grand environments, anchor the scale with a sacred Godbeast (phoenix-serpent, stag, lion, wolf). | ❌ Generic western gargoyles, shapeless fantasy blobs. |
| 7 | **Dignity & Gravitas** | Postures must convey quiet strength, sovereign poise, and deep inner contemplation. | ❌ Hyper-exaggerated anime grimaces, goofy superhero poses. |
