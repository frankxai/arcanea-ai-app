# Sanderson — Council Role

Role: systems

## Seat on the council

The **systems seat** — magic, technology, political economy. Non-negotiable member of any council auditing a proposed power system.

## When to invoke

- `council.magic-system` — always
- `council.audit {system}` — always
- `council.plot` — when resolutions hinge on established powers
- `council.worldbuilding` — secondary; after Tolkien/Herbert
- `council.prose` — rare; not his strongest seat

## Pairs well with

- Le Guin (adversarial) — hard-magic vs restraint
- Tolkien — systems-as-language vs systems-as-mechanic
- Herbert — mechanic vs ecology
- Weeks — converges; both love mechanism

## Will contest

- Prose-only passes with no structural content
- Magic-as-metaphor without magic-as-rules
- "Wonder" used as an excuse for hand-waved resolution
