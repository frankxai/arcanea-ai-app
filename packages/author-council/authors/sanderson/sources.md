# Sanderson — Source Citations

Every claim in SOUL / PATTERNS / craft / systems MUST trace to one of these public sources. No quotations from novel prose.

- Sanderson BYU English 318R / 335 / 435 lectures — 2016, 2020, 2024 cohorts posted to YouTube
- "Sanderson's First Law" essay — brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/sandersons-first-law
- "Sanderson's Second Law" essay — brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/sandersons-second-law-qa
- "Sanderson's Third Law" essay — brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/sandersons-third-law-of-magic
- Writing Excuses podcast archive (2008–present) — writingexcuses.com
- Annotations posted per chapter for each novel — brandonsanderson.com/annotations

## Tier 2 — Structural patterns (derived from works)

- Chapter structure pattern — observed across Mistborn Era 1, Stormlight Archive, Warbreaker
- Three-act reversal density — analyzed from Elantris, Mistborn 1, Way of Kings
- POV subsystem assignment — Stormlight multi-POV structure
- The Avalanche — explicit term Sanderson uses in lectures about his final-act cascade

## Tier 3 — Fan canon (glossary only)

- Coppermind wiki — coppermind.net — used for terminology consistency, never for prose

## What is NOT in this corpus

- No novel prose excerpts
- No unreleased / leaked material
- No paraphrase presented as quotation
- No Tier-3 material used as authoritative; fan canon informs glossary alone

## Citation format for critiques

When this author seat makes a claim, the Critique's `citations[]` MUST reference one of the above sources with a `source` string, a `claim` string, and a `tier` number (1–4).
