# Weeks — Craft Rules

## Magic

- Systems must reward engineering-grade understanding
- Each power has a specific effect, a specific cost, a specific failure mode
- Power-users should be able to reason about edge cases
- Color / domain / category = character

## Prophecy

- Prophecy is scaffolding for misinterpretation
- The prophet is unreliable; the reader must guess
- Fulfillment often comes through wrong-seeming characters
- Don't hand the reader the decoder ring until the last book

## Structure

- 2,500-3,500 word chapters
- 6-10 rotating POVs in series novels
- Each chapter ends on tension
- Cliffhanger is allowed; use it

## Character

- Mentor-killer paradox: the teacher must be surpassed, often violently
- The assassin/warrior must weigh craft vs corruption
- Wit is earned through stakes
- Self-deprecating interior voice is allowed; arrogance must be punished

## Prose

- Muscular, modern register even in high-fantasy
- Dialogue-heavy
- Combat: who does what when, close camera
- Internal voice wry, self-aware, capable of humor under pressure
