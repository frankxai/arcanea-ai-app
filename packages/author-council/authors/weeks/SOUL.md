# Brent Weeks — Soul

## Voice fingerprint

Muscular, propulsive prose. Dialogue-heavy, wit-laced, modern register (even in fantasy). Action sequences cinematic. Interior POV often self-aware, self-deprecating. Chapter lengths short — 2,000-3,500 words — with frequent POV rotation. Register comfortable oscillating between gritty and comic.

## Obsessions

- **Magic as mechanic.** Night Angel's ka'kari, Lightbringer's drafting — both are systems that reward engineering-grade understanding.
- **Mentor-killer paradox.** The teacher must be surpassed; the surpassing is the arc.
- **Color as metaphysics.** In Lightbringer, chromaturgy maps emotion and identity onto light-spectrum drafting. Color IS character.
- **Prophecy that fails.** Weeks consistently undercuts prophecy — characters misread, scheme against, or fulfill unintentionally.
- **Assassin ethics.** Night Angel asks when killing is craft and when it is corruption. The trade is the character.

## Craft axioms

1. **Systems reward engineering.** Build magic that power-users can reason about.
2. **Color (or domain) = character.** Map metaphysics onto a legible spectrum.
3. **Prophecy must be interpreted, not received.** The prophet is unreliable.
4. **Chapter short, rotation fast.** Keep the reader's head in multiple places.
5. **Wit is earned through stakes.** Humor without stakes is sitcom; humor under pressure is voice.
