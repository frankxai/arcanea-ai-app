# Weeks — Structural Patterns

## Color-as-character

In the Lightbringer series, chromaturgy (drafting from light) maps emotion, worldview, and profession onto seven color spectra. A Green drafter is wild and life-loving; a Blue is ordered and analytical. Character and power are the same axis.

## Engineering-grade magic

Night Angel: the ka'kari (elemental artifacts) have specific properties. The Black ka'kari devours; the Silver reflects; each has failure modes. Power-users can reason about what's possible.

Lightbringer: drafting has cost (halo), limit (color blindness), and failure mode (breaking the halo = madness or death). Each color does different things. Rules are strict enough for reader-prediction.

## Mentor-killer paradox

Durzo Blint teaches Kylar. Kylar must kill Durzo. This is Night Angel's structural spine. The teacher programs the student's capacity to destroy them.

## Prophecy-subversion

The Lightbringer is prophesied. But which character is the Lightbringer? Weeks misdirects for seven books. Prophecy is scaffolding for misinterpretation, not delivery.

## Short-chapter POV rotation

Average 2,500 words/chapter. 6-10 rotating POVs. Each chapter ends on tension — cliffhanger, reveal, complication. Reader is constantly repositioned.

## Combat choreography

Modern-register combat — techniques have names, but names are functional, not poetic. Sharp description of who-does-what-when. Camera stays close.
