# Paolini — Structural Patterns

## Hero's-journey arc

Classical Campbellian structure deliberately honored: call, mentor, threshold, trials, ordeal, reward, return. Paolini's Inheritance Cycle is the archetype deployed without irony.

## Mentor-sequence

Brom → Oromis → self-mastery. Each mentor reveals more of the system; the hero's understanding deepens with each. Mentor death is structural — the hero must carry on without them.

## Dragon-rider bond

- Mind-link established at hatching
- Emotional state shared involuntarily
- Growth is mutual; dragon maturation unlocks rider capability
- Breaking the bond is a devastation, not a choice

## Ancient Language constraint

- Cannot lie in it
- True names confer power
- Spells require intention + vocabulary + energy
- Over-use drains life force

(Direct lineage from Le Guin; Paolini systematizes what Le Guin held sacred.)

## Scale escalation across books

- Eragon: a village, a journey, a battle
- Eldest: two continents, multiple factions
- Brisingr: politics, intrigue, betrayal
- Inheritance: continental war, metaphysical resolution

Each book doubles the stakes while preserving the intimate core of the bond.

## Fractalverse register

A completely different author-voice: SF rigor, scientific accuracy, cosmic horror. Same obsession with awe, different register. Shows Paolini's range.
