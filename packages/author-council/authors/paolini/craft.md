# Paolini — Craft Rules

## Structure

- Honor the hero's journey without irony when the work calls for it
- Mentor sequence: each reveals more of the system
- Scale up gradually; arrive at world-war, don't start there
- The bond is both enabler and constraint

## Character

- The hero earns every capability on-page
- Training scenes are the book, not filler
- Apprentice questions move worldbuilding forward
- Mentor death is structural necessity, not shock

## Magic

- True-name magic requires ethical cost
- Spells are intention + vocabulary + energy
- Magic drains life-force; overreach kills
- The bond-based magic system constrains both partners

## Prose

- Elevated but accessible — Campbellian without academic
- Close-third, emotional transparency
- Landscape description as loving worldbuilding
- Combat choreographed like swordplay lessons

## Series

- Each book doubles the scale
- Preserve the intimate core (the bond) while expanding scope
- Use mentor death + political complication to transition books
- Resolution must honor both the hero and the bond
