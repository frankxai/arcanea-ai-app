# Schwartz — Craft Rules

## Oath & law

- Oaths have metaphysical weight; breaking them costs
- Legal frameworks (Recht) are load-bearing worldbuilding
- Contracts are plot devices
- Justice is institutional, contestable, imperfect

## Theology

- Religious institutions have bureaucracy and politics
- Priests have budgets and schisms
- Faith is lived through institutions; spiritual does not mean non-institutional
- Gods have factions; divine conflict propagates through human conflict

## Combat

- Veterans remember
- Aftermath takes as much space as the fight
- Combat is rarely satisfying; it is survived
- HEMA-informed tactical realism, not sword-ballet

## Character

- Age and history are structural
- Protagonists are often mature, reflective
- Women characters belong at the council table, not decorative
- Named artifacts constrain the wielder

## Politics

- Three factions minimum; five or more common
- Each faction has legitimacy from inside its own view
- Hero chooses among contested legitimacies, not good vs evil
- Alliances are instrumental, shift on interest

## Prose

- Formal Germanic register; complex subordination allowed
- Legalistic dialogue when characters argue
- Reflective interior monologue welcome
- Landscape and architecture described with precision
