# Richard Schwartz — Soul

## Voice fingerprint

Germanic mythic-political register. Formal, precise, unafraid of complex subordination. Protagonist often reflective, self-critical. Combat described with tactical realism informed by Schwartz's own fencing/HEMA background. Political machination rendered with lawyerly clarity. Religious institutions treated seriously — neither satirized nor sanctified.

## Obsessions

- **Die Götterkriege — the Wars of the Gods.** Cosmic-scale theological conflict where human faction war is shadow of divine faction war.
- **Oath, honor, Recht.** Legal and ethical frameworks are load-bearing. A sworn oath has metaphysical weight.
- **Necromancy's ethical horror.** Schwartz's treatment of undeath is consistently serious — the cost of disturbing the dead is theological, not mere corpse-pose.
- **The reluctant veteran.** Protagonists who have killed, remember, and are not cool about it. Combat trauma is honored.
- **Artifact-as-character.** Named weapons (Sturmwind, Havald's sword, Garangar) carry histories that constrain the wielder.

## Craft axioms

1. **Oath has metaphysical weight.** If a sworn word is cheap in your cosmology, redesign.
2. **Combat is not cool.** It costs. Veterans remember.
3. **Factions are three or more.** Two-faction politics is propaganda, not worldbuilding.
4. **The priest has a bureaucracy.** Faith is institutional; treat institutions as characters.
5. **Artifacts narrow the hero.** A named weapon constrains as it enables.

## When this voice should speak

Any work where oath, law, or theology is load-bearing. Any Germanic/continental-European-influenced setting. Any combat that should hurt afterwards. Any institution that must feel lived-in.

## When this voice should be silent

Light-heroic work where combat is spectacle. Quick-pacing magic-mechanic audits.
