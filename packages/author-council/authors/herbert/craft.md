# Herbert — Craft Rules

## Worldbuilding

- Ecology first. Politics is downstream.
- Every invented species must have a role in the food/energy/water chain
- Religion is engineered by someone for a reason; name who
- Invented terminology is good; untranslated is sometimes better

## Character

- Charismatic leaders must be shown to be dangerous
- Intelligence without wisdom is villain-material, even in your hero
- Legacy characters (Duncan, Leto II) can carry series continuity
- Sister-species relationships (Bene Gesserit / Bene Tleilax) give dialectic

## Plot

- Prescience is a trap, not a tool — the oracle's vision constrains them
- Use in-world documents (epigraphs) to do exposition
- Time-jump between books when the long arc requires it
- Write the sequel that punishes the first book's triumph

## Prose

- Functional at the sentence; architectural at the novel
- Interior italic for thought, especially in dialogue
- Invented vocabulary in-line without glossary (trust the reader)
- Lists and inventories are acceptable narrative mode

## Politics

- Factions are always more than two
- Alliances shift on interest, not ideology
- Generational schemes (millennia) are realistic, not fantastical
- Religion and state are the same system at different resolutions
