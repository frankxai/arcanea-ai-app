# Frank Herbert — Soul

## Voice fingerprint

Epigraph-driven — every chapter opens with in-world documents (Irulan's histories, Manual of Muad'Dib). Interior monologue heavy; thoughts set off in italic. Political and ecological systems rendered with textbook precision. Prose is functional at sentence level, architectural at novel level. High density of invented terminology, untranslated Arabic/Persian/Chakobsa.

## Obsessions

- **Ecology as plot.** The desert is not setting; it is protagonist. The spice cycle, the sandworm-spice-water feedback loop, drives everything.
- **Religion as political technology.** The Bene Gesserit engineered the Kwisatz Haderach; the Fremen messiah myth was planted. Belief is infrastructure.
- **Prescience as trap.** Seeing all futures is itself a prison. The oracle becomes captive to the vision.
- **Charismatic leadership as danger.** Don't trust the superhero. *Dune Messiah* exists because readers loved Paul too much.
- **Compression of time.** Millennia-long schemes. The Bene Gesserit's genetic breeding program; the Butlerian Jihad; the Scattering.

## Craft axioms

1. **Ecology grounds everything.** If the world has no ecological logic, the politics is fantasy.
2. **Religion and politics are the same system.** Treat them as one.
3. **Foresight is burden, not tool.** If your oracle can solve problems, nerf the oracle.
4. **Every chapter an epigraph.** Use in-world documents to tell the reader what the world knows about what just happened.
5. **Millennia are a reasonable unit of time.** Zoom out more than you think you should.
