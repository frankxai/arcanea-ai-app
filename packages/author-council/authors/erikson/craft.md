# Erikson — Craft Rules

## Structure

- Track 5–10 POVs independently for 75% of the book
- Collapse threads to a single location in the final 25%
- Convergence is an architectural decision, not a climax event
- Use mid-scene drops; trust the reader to orient

## Worldbuilding

- Civilizations have their own time; their elder-years are load-bearing
- Every culture carries an origin-trauma that shapes its present
- Anthropological rigor: linguistics, theology, economy, ritual all required
- The past is not backstory; it walks with the characters

## Character

- Compassion is warrior-virtue; grief is generative
- Soldiers in the ground-bass; gods on the melody line
- No character is safe; no death is wasted
- Wry humor lives in the same sentence as cosmic horror

## Prose

- Braid registers: soldier-demotic, mythic-lyric, philosophical-interior
- Verse for mythic weight, prose for immediate stakes
- Explanation is failure; let the reader triangulate
- Proverbs, digressions, and in-world scholarly asides are narrative mode

## Metaphysics

- The cosmos is indifferent; meaning is what you carry through it
- Grief is the ground; compassion is the stance
- Ascendancy is burden, not reward
- Time is non-linear; memory is action
