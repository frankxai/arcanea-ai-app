# Bakker — Craft Rules

## Philosophy

- Every idea must be removable-testable: does removing it collapse the plot?
- Philosophical vocabulary is earned by scene-level stakes
- Multiple coexisting worldviews = multiple coexisting prose registers
- The reader's understanding should accumulate; never be handed

## Consciousness

- Intelligence without love is horror-material
- Rationality pushed to its limit reveals its horror side
- Prescience/omniscience must be constrained by interior cost
- The mind itself is stakes; inner states can be stolen, engineered, corrupted

## Plot

- Prophecy narrows the choosable set; the prophet becomes cage-bound
- Apocalypse is primarily epistemological; the exterior catastrophes are symptoms
- Philosophy and politics are the same system
- Factions are philosophical stances, not just armies

## Prose

- Dense, austere — no comfort-prose
- Multiple registers (mythic / philosophical / sorcerous / demotic)
- Scylvendi-style proverbs can coexist with Gnostic-treatise
- Brutality must carry metaphysical weight or it is exploitation

## Character

- The Dûnyain move through the world; ordinary characters are moved by it
- A character whose interior is violated is the darkest horror
- Villains are philosophically formidable, not merely evil
- Growth is rare; coming-to-see-what-was-always-true is common
