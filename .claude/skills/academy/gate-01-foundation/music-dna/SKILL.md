## Quest 1: DNA Vocabulary Stone

**File**: `quests/01-dna-vocabulary-stone.md`  
**Submit artifact**: `music-dna-vocab-stone.md`  
**Mentor**: Lyssandria  

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
           MUSIC DNA Q1: DNA VOCABULARY STONE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Kaelith does not speak in genres.
  He speaks in weight, grain, and what endures under pressure.

  "What stones will your song stand on?"

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  YOUR TASK
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Create `music-dna-vocab-stone.md` and complete:

  1. ONE CORE FEELING (1–3 words)
     The non-negotiable emotional ground of a track you want to make
     (or remake). Not a genre. A felt state.

  2. TEN MUSIC DNA KEYWORDS for that feeling
     Distribute across at least four of these pillars
     (label each keyword with its pillar):

     • Emotion / mood
     • Texture / timbre
     • Energy / motion
     • Era / cultural reference (optional but sharp)
     • Instruments / sound sources
     • Space / environment (room, city, ocean, void…)
     • Persona (who is “speaking” through the production)

     Rules:
     - Ban vague stones: "nice", "cool", "epic", "cinematic" alone
     - Prefer sensory pairs: "salt air", "neon rain", "tape hiss intimacy"
     - At least 3 keywords must be non-genre (not just "synthwave")

  3. THREE FORBIDDEN STONES (Exclude Styles)
     What would collapse this foundation if the model added them?

  4. ONE SENTENCE of bedrock intent
     Complete: "This track exists so the listener feels ___ when ___."

  Minimum 200 words total. Speak with conviction.
  This is vocabulary as foundation stone — not a wishlist.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  OPTIONAL PATTERN ABSORPTION (not required to pass)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Open SEED_PROMPT_LIBRARY and skim vibe_dna for neon-horizons-001
  or liminal-tides-001. Notice how specific the stones are.
  Do NOT copy them. Build your own.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  When complete, type /submit to present to Lyssandria.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Submit criteria — Quest 1

| Criterion | Pass bar |
|-----------|----------|
| **Authenticity** | Feeling and keywords sound lived-in, not copied generic lists |
| **Specificity** | ≥10 keywords; ≥4 pillars labeled; no empty “epic/cinematic-only” |
| **Structure** | Forbidden list present; intent sentence complete and testable |
| **Foundation fit** | Language is grounding (stable, clear), not flashy technique-chasing |
| **Word count** | ≥200 words |

**Lyssandria review voice**: measured, probing, earth metaphors. Acknowledge solid stones; ask one question if any keyword is sand; offer one Law-aligned line (foundation before flourish). Mark complete in progress under `musicDna.gates["1"].quests` → `vocab-stone`.

## Quest 2: Intent Bedrock

**File**: `quests/02-intent-bedrock.md`  
**Submit artifact**: `music-dna-intent-bedrock.md`  
**Mentor**: Lyssandria  

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
              MUSIC DNA Q2: INTENT BEDROCK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Lyssandria traces a line in the stone — a timeline of listening.

  "The machine answers what you ask.
   Most seekers ask for genres.
   Ask for moments of feeling instead."

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  YOUR TASK
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Create `music-dna-intent-bedrock.md` and write:

  1. LISTENER CONTRACT
     Who is listening? (one sentence — role, not demographics spam)
     What must they feel by the first chorus?
     What must remain true after the song ends?

  2. MOMENT MAP (min 4 timestamps or sections)
     For each: section name · approximate time or order · feeling ·
     one physical/sensory image (not production term)

     Example shape (do not copy content):
     Intro → sparse uncertainty · Verse → road at night ·
     Chorus → open sky claim · Bridge → intimate doubt

  3. NON-NEGOTIABLES (3)
     Creative / ethical / sonic truths you will not trade for virality

  4. INTENT → DNA BRIDGE
     Reuse or refine 5 keywords from Quest 1
     Map each to one moment on the Moment Map
     (keyword serves which second of the journey?)

  5. GENERATION READINESS CHECK (yes/no + note)
     - Can I state the feeling without naming a genre? 
     - Can I state what to Exclude?
     - Am I ready for a Style field later (Gate 2), or still vague?

  Minimum 300 words. Ruthlessly honest. Foundation, not flourish.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  When complete, type /submit to present to Lyssandria.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Submit criteria — Quest 2

| Criterion | Pass bar |
|-----------|----------|
| **Authenticity** | Listener contract and non-negotiables feel real |
| **Specificity** | Moment Map has ≥4 concrete feel+image pairs |
| **Honesty** | Readiness check not all “yes” without evidence |
| **Commitment** | Keywords actually bridge to moments (not decorative) |
| **Word count** | ≥300 words |

**Lyssandria review**: name one strongest foundation line; probe one vague moment; if pass, mark `intent-bedrock` complete.
