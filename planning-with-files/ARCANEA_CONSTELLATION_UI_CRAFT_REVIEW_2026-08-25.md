# Arcanea Living Constellation — UI craft review

## Verdict

**HOLD for public release; PASS for preview preparation.** The source-level experience clears the clarity, evidence, authority, server-rendering, keyboard, reduced-motion, responsive-rule, and copy-integrity gates. It has not received actual desktop/mobile browser inspection, contrast measurement, Core Web Vitals evidence, or human brand approval. The active machine posture forbids opening that verification lane.

## Product contract

- **Audience:** AI-native worldbuilders, creators, designers, and agent builders who need to understand Arcanea's identities and reproduce its governed visual method.
- **Primary outcome:** inspect a real identity or prompt contract and understand exactly what is evidence, proposal, generated output, approval, and human-held authority.
- **Dominant idea:** a living constellation is an evidence system, not a decorative cast gallery.
- **Primary action:** explore the 16 agent dossiers.
- **Secondary action:** inspect the governed 100-study campaign.
- **Exit ramp:** inspect the existing visual encyclopedia and its historical proposal evidence.
- **Visual proof available:** 100 prompt contracts, 66 entity dossiers/records, current campaign counts, structurally verified prior-wave evidence, and explicit empty-media states. New v1.10.0 campaign imagery is not yet available.
- **Motion policy:** essential content is server-rendered and complete without motion; reduced-motion rules are present. No decorative client animation was added.

## Changes made in this review

1. Reduced the first viewport from five competing section buttons to one primary action, one secondary campaign action, and one visual-evidence exit ramp.
2. Replaced the ambiguous `Ready` metric with `Contract-ready`; execution readiness still remains false until a fresh preflight and narrow grant exist.
3. Replaced `Campaign ACV-100`, which could be mistaken for job `ACV-100`, with `Campaign · evidence to image`.
4. Narrowed `Every identity ships with an explanation` to the accurate `Every agent blueprint ships with an explanation`.
5. Reframed the gallery handoff as historical proposal evidence, exposed its separation from the current campaign, and added the prior collection's rights, publication, and 30-path repair boundary to public campaign metadata.
6. Added explicit accessible names and pressed state to the gallery's icon-only mobile view controls.

## Known risks

- The light editorial field-note body deliberately differs from Arcanea's default cosmic-dark product chrome. It is an original evidence-reading proposal, not an approved brand-system change.
- The first complete media activation cannot be judged until approved images enter the exact public URLs; empty states are intentionally honest but not a substitute for final art direction.
- The page is long and dense by design. A real mobile walkthrough must confirm that the section sequence remains teachable rather than exhausting.
- No browser screenshot, Lighthouse receipt, Vercel preview, commit, push, or deployment exists for this branch.
- The prior Resonant Kinforms packet is structurally verified but not release-ready: all 130 records remain rights-pending and publication-held, and 30 Wave-01 dossier paths require packet regeneration.
