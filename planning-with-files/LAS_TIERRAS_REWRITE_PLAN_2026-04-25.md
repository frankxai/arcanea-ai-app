## 0. Executive synthesis

Three chapters are in (~12K words). They are good. Register scores 7-8 across the council. The book has a strong spiritual spine, a sound voice register, and disciplined folk-vocabulary execution. The world bible is more complete than 90% of literary novels ever achieve.

**The book has one signature prose flaw, surfaced independently by all five reviewers:**

> *The prose lands the miracle in the correct flat tone — and then appends one sentence explaining that a miracle just landed.*

Fix that one habit across the manuscript and the book moves from 7-8 to 9. That is the single highest-leverage edit. Everything else in this plan supports it.

The second-most-load-bearing finding is structural: **chapters 1-3 cover Beat 1 (Distinction) and the threshold of Beat 2 (Loneliness of the Seer). The book needs ~9 more chapters to land Beats 2-5.** Total target: 12 chapters, ~50-55K words. Tight literary novel, not epic.

Two Venezuelan women readers — ideally with diaspora experience and the spiritual formation described — must read the completed manuscript before publication. This is non-negotiable in this plan.

## 2. Dialogue convention — LOCKED

**Convention: Spanish raya (—) for all dialogue throughout the book. Glossary of Veldarín at the back. NO bracketed translations. NO inline English glosses.**

**Reasoning** (from Sensitivity Reader and Deep Fiction Master, converged):
- Spanish-language literary tradition uses raya. García Márquez, Rulfo, Cisneros, McCarthy in *The Crossing*, Junot Díaz refusing translation as a political stance.
- The target reader (28-year-old Venezuelan woman in Spain) is a native Spanish speaker. Quote marks would signal "this book is explaining itself to an Anglophone outside gaze." Raya signals "this text is not explaining itself to someone who isn't from here."
- Brackets would patronize the bilingual reader and yank the monolingual reader out of the prose to be fed a vocabulary item — the opposite of immersion.
- Context-revealing translation (the surrounding sentence makes meaning land through action or response) is the craft move. The glossary handles proper nouns and unique world-words (destello, luzfruta, piedra viva, zumbido, hora de Nero, raya).

**One-pass cleanup required:** at least two existing passages already auto-translate the Spanish (Chapter 3's "como maíz tostado, pero más lento. Con algo de sol adentro" parenthetically translated; Chapter 2's notebook entry followed by "I don't know what it means. But it is true."). Strip these. The Spanish stands.

This convention is being added to BIBLE.md in this same session.

## 3. The signature prose flaw — fix list (chapters 1-3)

These are the cuts that move the book from 7-8 to 9. They were independently flagged by Deep Fiction Master, Consciousness Fiction Master, Character Psychologist, and partially by Sensitivity Reader.

### Chapter 1

**CUT 1.1** — the closing omniscient summary block
> CURRENT: *"She did not know yet that the lights had been waiting. She did not know that the whole country she lived in was a sleeping thing, held carefully in the palm of itself, complete, content, unawakened."*
> ACTION: Cut. The dramatic irony is structural. The reader is already inside the unknowing. The line "held carefully in the palm of itself" is extraordinary — save it for a moment Mira herself discovers, where it will earn it.

**CUT 1.2** — the doubled fragility metaphor
> CURRENT: *"She walked with the slow careful care of a child carrying something fragile. She walked with the weight of a power she had not asked for and the quiet of a witness who had just understood that the world had more than one kind of weather."*
> ACTION: Keep first sentence. Cut second. The metaphor of fragile carrying contains all of it.

**ELEVATE 1.3** — Mother's not-seeing
> CURRENT: *"Her mother had seen something — the brightness of her eyes, perhaps the shift in the air of the kitchen, the way a room gets warmer when someone you love walks into it."*
> TRIM TO: *"Her mother had seen something — the brightness of her eyes, perhaps the shift in the air of the kitchen. It was not enough. It was so close it was worse than not enough."*
> WHY: The "way a room gets warmer" parenthetical softens the rupture. The wound should not be cushioned for the reader.

**OPENING DECISION** — the first sentence test
The current opening is "She woke before the bells did. That was the first strange thing." Deep Fiction Master flagged "That was the first strange thing" as a narrator's sentence categorizing the experience for the reader. Recommendation:
> NEW OPEN: *"She woke before the bells did. Over her heart, a warmth was waiting for her to open her eyes."*
> Reasoning: removes the narrator's annotation, places the body immediately at the center, lets the reader feel the second strange thing without being told there is a list.
> **Status: PROPOSED. Frank's call.** The current opening also works — this is a stylistic decision, not a craft fix.

### Chapter 2

**CUT 2.1** — over-italicized landing of the discovery
> CURRENT: *"Not I saw it wake. Not I watched it happen. I can wake them. It was the three notes. It was her voice. It was something in her voice meeting something in his destello, and the meeting was the waking."*
> TRIM TO: *"Not I saw it wake. Not I watched it happen. I can wake them. It was the three notes. Her voice. Something in her voice meeting something in his destello."*
> WHY: "The meeting was the waking" names what the reader has just witnessed. One sentence too many of holding the reader's hand after the insight has already arrived.

**CUT 2.2** — the busy-mother editorializing
> CURRENT: *"Remedios, who had a shop to run and a small brother to feed and the long narrow thing called being a woman in charge of everything to do, had kissed the top of Mira's head..."*
> ACTION: Cut "the long narrow thing called being a woman in charge of everything to do." Replace with a gesture: *Remedios capped her pen and looked at Mira with full attention, then kissed her head and returned to the ledger.*
> WHY: Character Psychologist: "Remedios is *capable*, not taxed. She is not failing to see because she is too busy — she is failing to see because she is constitutionally oriented to the surface layer of things, lovingly and competently. The busyness framing misrepresents the wound."

**CUT 2.3** — pre-attributed notebook phrase
> CURRENT: *"She would think, later, trying to name it for the notebook"* introducing "como maíz tostado, pero más lento. Con algo de sol adentro."
> ACTION: Either give Mira the phrase NOW in the present moment of smelling, or let her write it in the notebook later without the preview. The pre-attribution announces the poetry and removes it from present tense.

**COMPRESSION TARGET:** Chapter 2 can lose 600-800 words without losing texture. Specifically: the second-pass commentary on notebook entries (the entries themselves do the work; the paragraph after explaining what she thought while writing them is mostly redundant) and the three-sentence analysis after Tomás goes back to the paper boat (the notebook entry *No se puede empujar* IS the sentence; everything before it is scaffolding).

### Chapter 3

**CUT 3.1** — narrating the loneliness before showing it
> CURRENT: Chapter opens *"By the second week, Mira had woken eight destellos"* immediately followed by *"That was the shape the aloneness was beginning to take."*
> ACTION: Open on a scene — Mira doing something — and let "eight" be established by behavior rather than narration. The aloneness should not be named before it is felt.

**CUT 3.2** — over-counted "three fingers"
"Three fingers above her right shoulder" appears 4× in close succession in Ch 3 (lines ~126, 127, 137, 272). By the fourth iteration the measurement is mechanical. ACTION: keep at most twice. Once Bela's gaze moves to that distance, the reader holds it. The dream version should drop the phrase entirely.

**CUT 3.3** — the certainty undermine
> CURRENT: *"It could have been a weather remark. It could have meant... It could have meant any of these. Mira heard, in her own chest, the word it actually meant."*
> ACTION: Cut "Mira heard, in her own chest, the word it actually meant." End on "It could have meant any of these." The hovering ambiguity IS the moment. Naming "the word it actually meant" collapses what the entire scene built toward.

**CUT 3.4** — owl repetition
> CURRENT: *"It was still watching when Mira opened her courtyard door. It was still watching when she closed it behind her."*
> ACTION: Once. *"It was still watching."* Twice removes the owl from the uncanny into the symbolic.

**ELEVATE 3.5** — Bela's recognition needs an unconscious physical gesture
The BIBLE establishes Bela's Aurevaldan gesture: *touching her own chest before reaching toward someone — Aurevaldan for I bring only what I have.*
Character Psychologist's specific recommendation: in the recognition scene, after Bela's gaze finds Chispa, **Bela's hand should briefly, unconsciously move to her chest.** She would not do it deliberately. She would not even know she did it. She would simply, involuntarily, revert to the gesture of her origin culture in the one moment that sixty years of Veldorian acclimation could not suppress. Mira would not know what the gesture means. The reader will, after the dream sequence. **This is the line that gives the recognition scene the weight of "encountering something I thought was myth."**

**ELEVATE 3.6** — the dream sequence's final image
> CURRENT: *"In the dream, the two lights — the opalescent and the prismatic — touched. In the dream, both began, very slowly, to sing."*
> ELEVATION: *"In the dream, the two lights touched. What began first — she could not have said. It was not sound. It was the way the valley's hum had sounded for one full breath, that first morning, when everything was at the same frequency. Like that, but longer. Like that, but answered."*

**TRIM 3.7** — dream genealogy compression
The dream lays out the full migration of the three notes from Aurevalde → Bela's neighbor → Don Emilio → Mira's father → floración azul → Mira. Beautiful but exhaustive. END THE DREAM EARLIER: at the image of the young woman carrying the song across the corridor. Withhold the full accounting. Let the reader feel the vastness of the travel; the auditing can come later in the book or never.

**Length target:** ~50-55K words across 12 chapters. Average ~4-4.5K per chapter, with Ch 9 (Wrong Move) and Ch 11 (First Contact) running slightly longer.

## 5. Hero's journey mapping

The book follows Campbell's monomyth in the **literary-magical-realism mode** — outward action minimal, inward journey total. Mira does not slay a dragon. She learns the difference between attention and agenda.

| Hero's Journey beat | Las Tierras chapter | Spiritual beat |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary World | Ch 1 (opening) | Pre-Beat 1 |
| Call to Adventure | Ch 1 (Chispa's appearance) | Beat 1: Distinction |
| Refusal of the Call (briefly) | Ch 1 (mother's not-seeing) | — |
| Meeting the Mentor | Ch 3 (Bela's first appearance) | Beat 2 threshold |
| Crossing the Threshold | Ch 2 (Don Emilio waking — first deliberate use) | Beat 2 opening |
| Tests, Allies, Enemies | Chs 4-6 (testing the gift, Tomás, Marisol, the absent father, Remedios's pauses) | Beat 2 deepening |
| Approach to the Inmost Cave | Ch 7-8 (coast, Vidal preparation) | Beat 3 preparation |
| Ordeal | Ch 9 — Treinta y Dos (the Wrong Move) | Beat 3 |
| Reward (seizing the sword) | — | (Inverted) Ch 10 — the gift as silence; reward is the empty interior |

| Return with the Elixir | Ch 12 (Sufficiency — the ordinary plaza, fully alight) | Beat 5 |

The standard hero's journey mostly applies, with **one literary inversion**: the "Reward" stage in Las Tierras is the burning-off of the ego of the gift, not the seizing of new power. This is the McCarthy/Le Guin lineage — the hero who must *unlearn* to mature.

## 6. The Wrong Move — locked design

**Locked: Candidate A (Vidal market scene).** Recommended by Consciousness Fiction Master with three-candidate analysis. Reasoning compressed:

- Mira is not reckless, not cruel, not even wrong in intention. She applies what she knows to something that requires what she does not yet know.
- The oscuro is not a problem to be solved with Voice Gate frequency. It is a different order of being that asks for silence and patient witness rather than activation.
- The error is *ontological, not moral.* Sight Gate hubris in its purest form — vision replaced by technique.
- Vidal's humanity is preserved (he is not punished, simply not met where he actually is).
- The damage is real but limited and survivable, leaving room for the dark interior chapter without external crisis.

**The two rejected candidates** (saved here for record):
- **B. Forcing Tomás:** emotional power but pulls the story toward guilt-and-redemption rather than hubris-and-correction. Risks making Tomás a victim and Mira a child who hurt her brother.
- **C. A grieving stranger at market:** ethically clear but dilutes relational stakes (stranger). Shifts scale toward trauma rather than texture-violation.

## 8. Arcanea world placement & Academy connection

### The Academy question — LOCKED: Option B (threaded hint)

**Single passing reference in Ch 7 (coast), folk-level, off-foreground.** A valley woman casually says her nephew was taken to study at one of the Great Houses in the east, and she doesn't understand why they want the quiet ones. That is sufficient.

**Reasoning** (World Architect):
- This book is a Realm-level Legend, not a Saga spine novel.
- The Academy would give Mira language, rank, role — the book's spiritual beat structure explicitly defers that knowledge beyond the final page.
- Foregrounding it (Option C) would change the ending from "the light does not need her urgency" to "and now a door is opening for her" — a different story, a prelude story.
- Complete silence (Option A) loses the canon reader's pleasure and the target reader's gift of knowing there are people elsewhere who study what she does instinctively.

**No Academy representative sees Mira in this book. Her arc closes inside the valley.** Whether she goes to an Academy later is canonically open. The book promises nothing.

### Aurevalde — LOCKED: pure absence

Bela's vowels, the gesture, the bollos de maíz solar, the dream in Ch 3. No physical crossing. The dream has already done the genealogy work. *Let Aurevalde remain a country that exists in the back of a song.*

### Other Realms

One folk-level mention in Ch 7 (coast): *dicen que este mar nace de cinco países distintos, bajo las piedras.* The reader hears "five Realms." The valley resident hears "five distant sources." No second Realm gets named.

### Saga arc placement

Standalone Realm-Legend, set ~four centuries after the Sealing of the Dark Lord, well before any nine-film Saga events. Functions as the entry-point book — the one a reader opens first, and that makes them trust the world before they encounter its larger architecture. Mira may or may not appear in the Saga later; the book promises nothing.

### Folk-vocabulary discipline — LOCKED

Author-only (NEVER appear in prose): Arcane, Anima, Song, Mana, Lumina, Nero, Yggdrasil, Ten Gates, Luminor, Malachar, World Song, 432 Hz, 528 Hz.

Folk-level vocabulary used in prose: destellos, chispas, el zumbido, la hora de Nero, la hora de Lumina, la hora de los destellos, el retorno de la luz, oscuro (only as the surface-language word a child finds for what she sees, not as the canon term).

One Ch 3 watch item: the dream sequence uses "Realms" (capitalized) and "corridor between Realms." This is the only register-shift in 3 chapters and works because of the dream context, but it should not be repeated outside dreams. **Future chapters: keep "Realms" inside dreams or Bela's voice only.**
