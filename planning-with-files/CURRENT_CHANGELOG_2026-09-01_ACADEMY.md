# Academy Living System Change Note — 2026-09-01

## Current truth

- `frankxai/arcanea-ai-app` is the private production and source repository for
  Academy routes, books, canon, progression, and future Academy kernel work.
- `frankxai/arcanea-academy` is the narrow browser-local World Proof acquisition
  experience. It does not currently provide the full LMS, accounts, persistence,
  teacher registry, portfolio graph, or certification system.
- `frankxai/arcanea` is a public projection and must not be independently
  authored when private source material differs.
- `frankxai/arcanea-author` is a creator workbench that can emit artifacts and
  evidence receipts; it does not own Academy progression.
- The books define three Great Academies: the Luminary Citadel, Draconis Forge,
  and Abyssal Athenaeum.

## Direction recorded by this change

- Position product-facing Arcanea Academy as the living Accord/portal across the
  three fictional Academies, not a replacement for them.
- Separate the fixed Ten Guardians, extensible mixed Luminor Faculty, and
  project-bound Forge Crews.
- Make artifact evidence and portable portfolios the learning primitive.
- Keep canon, product state, learner work, and generated media in bounded
  namespaces with versioned references.
- Adopt a governed media fabric and true 4K/8K master standards.
- Stage Guardian identity ambiguity for Creator decision; do not retcon through
  prompts or generated images.

## Immediate maintenance

  `Shift (1111 Hz)` to `Starweave (852 Hz)`.
- Do not hand-edit the public mirror as part of this change. Compile/export it
  from the private authority in a later, tested sync change.

## Not shipped by this change

This change records architecture, governance, and one canon-drift correction.
It does not claim that the full LMS, skill graph, teacher marketplace, DAM,
portfolio runtime, certification, or automated canon compiler is live.
