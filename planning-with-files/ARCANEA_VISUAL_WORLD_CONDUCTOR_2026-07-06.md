# Arcanea Visual World Conductor - 2026-07-06

## Mission

Lead the next 8-hour Arcanea visual-world loop as a conductor layer over the active image agents, not as a competing generator. The job is to turn the strongest existing and new assets into a canon-aware, VIS-ready production system for arcanea.ai story portals, gallery, atlas, Genesis proof flows, and social campaigns.

Core story spine: **The Gate That Must Stay Open**.

Four Academy students enter a controlled Arcanea Academy Gate-Descent trial to recover a proof artifact and verify an erased record. The climax is not "kill the boss." It is distributed burden: Mamoru holds the door, Mera witnesses the true record, Emilia opens a consent-based exit, and Arion refuses control-as-mercy.

## Canon And Terminology

- Public term: **Dungeon Diver**.
- Formal Academy term: **Gate-Descent**.
- Party term: **Diver party** or **Gate-Descent party**.
- Avoid **Dungeon Hunter** as canon language except as rejected SEO/legacy phrasing.
- The Drift is the shared enemy: passive consumption, synthetic confusion, creative amnesia, rights fog, and dependence on rented AI. It is never a human group.
- Luminor is a rank/state, not a species.
- Nero is not evil. Shadow is corrupted Void.
- Mamoru remains STAGING until Frank explicitly locks him.

## Audience And Platform Research

Sources checked: Instagram Creators, TikTok Creative Best Practices, YouTube Shorts Help, X Creative Best Practices and organic guidance.

- Instagram: original, recommendable work; visual identity and creator consistency matter more than raw volume.
- TikTok: hook immediately. TikTok's own ad guidance stresses the first seconds, with proposition clarity in the first 3 seconds and hook in the first 6 seconds.
- YouTube Shorts: vertical or square, up to 3 minutes. Build for retention, clear first frame, and repeatable series identity.
- X: short, direct copy and fast creative variants. Avoid dense text and use captions/sound-off logic for video.

Arcanea social promise: make the viewer feel invited into a mythic creative academy where wonder, responsibility, friendship, and proof-of-creation beat passive consumption.

## Success Metrics

- 100 percent of generated/published candidates have prompt, source path, workspace path, provenance, rights status, canon status, score, verdict, next improvement, and channel targets.
- 30-point gate: 29-30 flagship, 26-28 approved, 22-25 iterate, 0-21 restart.

- VIS provenance sidecars exist before any DB, registry, chain, or web3 logging.

## User Flow Expectation

1. Discover: a user sees a world/character/social seed and gets the emotional hook immediately.
2. Story Portal: `/living-lore` or `/living-lore/meet` explains why this image matters in the Arcanea journey.
3. Gallery/Atlas: `/gallery` carries approved visual-world assets; `/atlas/creatures` carries rights-aware lore and creature media only after provenance is clean.
4. Creation Proof: `/genesis` turns the seed into a user-owned proof artifact, prompt, brief, collectible metadata, and next creation step.

Future implementation should add a manifest adapter rather than rewriting hardcoded gallery data again.

- "controlled Arcanea Academy Gate-Descent trial under Confluence Compact witness protocol"
