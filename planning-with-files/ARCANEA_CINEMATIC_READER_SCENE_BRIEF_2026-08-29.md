# Arcanea cinematic reader scene brief

**Status:** Working art-direction brief
**Surface:** Book 1 edition landing and reader act thresholds
**Visual rail:** Photoreal cinematic myth-tech for story; AI-lab premium for interface chrome
**Asset generation:** Blocked until machine/media preflight opens

## Creative thesis

The Academy is beautiful because it has learned to make power look safe. The images should let the viewer feel the hairline fracture beneath that beauty: every instrument is also a measurement device, every threshold is also a custody line, and every act of magic leaves evidence.

## Audience response

The ideal reader should feel, in order:

1. This is an adult literary fantasy, not a game landing page.
2. These three people have incompatible ways of caring.
3. The Academy is a place I want to enter and do not fully trust.
4. The magic has material weight, cost, and aftercare.
5. The paid edition contains a coherent visual world, not a gallery of unrelated generations.

## Visual grammar

- Photoreal cinematic myth-tech.
- High-fashion restraint and museum-grade material detail.
- Living stone, starlight metal, memory glass, celestial gold, cool cosmic blue, restrained teal.
- Volumetric atmosphere used to clarify space, never to hide anatomy or architecture.
- Layered depth with one dominant subject relationship per frame.
- Human-scale evidence: repaired tool, damp cuff, cracked tile, written refusal, bandaged hand.
- Arcanea mark and interface remain outside story images.

Forbidden:

- generic fantasy academy castle;
- anime-bright rendering on the canon/book rail;
- spandex, superhero pose, grimdark mud, cosplay armor, game splash-screen composition;
- glowing eyes as shorthand for power;
- purple-pink AI gradients;
- floating glyph soup, decorative portals, or lore grids;
- text generated inside the image;
- romantic-triangle staging;
- all three characters staring heroically at camera;
- images that make coercive surveillance look unambiguously aspirational.

## Character lock requirements

No cover or multi-character scene is generated until these are recorded in sidecars and approved:

### Arion

- Grounded physical center; strength reads as load-bearing, not dominance.
- Gesture vocabulary: tests weight, repairs edges, notices structural strain.
- Owned object: a visibly repaired ordinary tool.
- Wardrobe: tactile mineral/earth materials, practical wear, one precise Academy addition.
- Emotional tell: holds his breath before accepting help.

### Mera

- Formal, precise, observant distance; empathy is not softness on demand.
- Gesture vocabulary: protects vessels, measures consent, listens before contact.
- Owned object: sealed water-memory vial.
- Wardrobe: tide-cut tailoring, memory glass detail, deep water palette without mermaid coding.
- Emotional tell: adjusts a boundary before revealing emotion.

### Emilia

- Elegant maker energy; systems intelligence without “tech genius” clutter.
- Gesture vocabulary: calibrates, annotates, leaves visible exit paths.
- Owned object: repaired prism instrument/gauntlet.
- Wardrobe: Synthesis tailoring, crystal-metal precision, restrained prismatic refraction.
- Emotional tell: fixes the instrument when the human question is harder.

### Headmaster Akamoto

- Protective severity, field-worn authority, no universal-sage cliché.
- Bond/dragon-rider implication may appear through harness, wind abrasion, or empty perch; no named dragon or confirmed bond before canon approval.
- Never depict him as a floating ancient Luminor or all-Academy ruler.

### Malachar

- Beauty and grief remain legible; Shadow is corrupted Void, not black-costume evil.
- He should look like someone who still remembers why mercy mattered.
- First appearance is an impossible reflection or remembered presence, not a battle poster.

## Cover exploration lanes

Generate concepts from the same story thesis, not unrelated styles.

### Lane A — The measured miracle

Arion is small within a beautiful Academy measurement chamber; Mera and Emilia occupy opposing edges as witness and maker. A living root crosses the instrument floor and breaks its perfect geometry. No action pose.

### Lane B — Three acts of refusal

Three hands around a cracked memory/prism vessel: one supporting weight, one protecting the seal, one opening an escape mechanism. The Academy appears through material reflection. Strong thumbnail silhouette; no faces required.

### Lane C — The Hollow Root

A monumental living root holds an impossible Academy foundation above a dark archive void. Three human silhouettes descend for different reasons. Premium environmental fantasy, title-safe upper field.

Preferred first exploration: Lane B for originality and thumbnail strength, then Lane C for cinematic scale. Lane A becomes viable only after face consistency is locked.

## First-viewport composition

Mobile:

- Cover occupies roughly the upper 46–54% without pushing the first action below comfortable reach.
- Title is real HTML, never generated into art.
- Character faces are not required; silhouette/material concept should remain readable at 375 px.

Desktop:

- Asymmetric split: editorial copy and action on one side, cover/key art with controlled depth on the other.
- No full-screen background behind small white text.
- Art may extend into the next section to create continuity, but the CTA remains visually stable.

## Flagship scroll set-piece

One desktop/tablet sequence may show three paths converging:

- Arion's path carries physical fractures and repair marks.
- Mera's path carries recorded absences and sealed memories.
- Emilia's path carries calibrated prism lines and explicit exit mechanisms.
- The paths meet at an Academy threshold that first reads as sanctuary, then reveals measurement/custody geometry.

The sequence must communicate the same idea as a static layered composition under reduced motion. It uses transforms/opacity only, respects performance budget, and never scroll-locks the prose reader.

## Reader art placement

- Cover at edition start.
- One plate at each act threshold.
- A maximum of one mid-act plate when it changes understanding of place or consequence.
- Detail crops may appear in margins on desktop but collapse below prose on mobile.
- No art between a question and its answer, during emotionally intimate dialogue, or where it spoils the next sentence.

## Prompt packet contract

Every generation prompt records:

- use case and aspect ratio;
- canon status and source files;
- character identity anchors;
- setting, action, relationship, composition, lighting, material, palette, and emotional subtext;
- exclusion list;
- text-safe region when relevant;
- final generation tool/model/date;
- output path, crop variants, inspection verdict, and rights/provenance.

Prompts describe what is visible. Abstract themes must be converted into staging, objects, light, distance, and gesture.

## Inspection gate

Each candidate is inspected at full size and target crop for:

- face/identity consistency;
- hands, limbs, anatomy, gaze, and object contact;
- costume/material continuity;
- narrative truth and consent framing;
- lighting hierarchy and thumbnail read;
- mobile crop and title-safe space;
- accidental text/watermark/UI;
- generic-franchise or borrowed-style drift;
- provenance/rights sidecar completeness.

Only approved outputs move into `apps/web/public/books/**`. Raw outputs remain outside source until selected.

## Release verdict rule

The visual system ships only when the cover, four identity anchors, and core act plates look like one film made by one production designer. Twelve coherent images beat one hundred inconsistent ones.
