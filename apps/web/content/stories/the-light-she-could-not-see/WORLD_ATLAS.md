# The Light She Could Not See · story-local world atlas

Status: **fictional proposals for this independent serial**, 26 September 2026. This document does not amend `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md` or the locked _Las Tierras de Luz_ novel. Its protagonist is Iria Soreda, twenty-nine; the novel's Selene Velara remains a separate character. Earth landscapes, foodways, and equestrian culture can inform texture without naming an Earth city or country inside this serial.

| Mar Arcano | Inland sea with five contributing surface water systems, a coast and boat crews with authority over its markers. | Its aquifer corridors transmit water and resonance, not human travellers. Vela partners need spawning channels and open water. |

The locations connect by maintained roads, the mountain passage, barges, and occasional authorized flight. Geography has travel time. Each route has caretakers and people who can refuse an unsafe crossing.

## Sight, Gates, and artifacts

**Destello** is the visible sign of a Gate's awakening or resonance, consistent with the Veldorian usage in Arcanea lore. It can flicker in a person, an animal, or a living network responding to a Gate. A colour is not a feeling, virtue, diagnosis, permission, or guarantee of power. Absence of visible light says nothing about worth. Iria's Sight practice helps her notice some resonance and overreach; it cannot reveal motive or predict outcomes. Foundation workings cost muscle and recovery. Luminor remains a rank earned through Gate attainment. Nero is fertile unknown, not a source of evil.

**Mercedes's lens** clarifies resonance for two breaths and needs a day against a living chest to recharge. It does not show a route, mind, or destiny. Its five bands are a crafted optical artifact, not a new Gate. **Handglasses** hold images and city relay messages; upper-valley geological shifts interrupt relay service. **Oren's brass comb** listens to stress across stone seams; its worn teeth and a missing note reveal an unwatered bearing. **Oriván witness tiles** record water draws, repairs, consent, and operators. They are archival infrastructure, not prophecy. **Cael's copper bands** listen to ground movement and can be repurposed to draw the old cistern reserve. Their model omits stored-water loss, with consequences that can be audited.

## Living systems and built cultures

- **Basin crowns** hold rain on vast membrane leaves in the eastern cleft. A descending cascade wets the hanging forest below. When the Root Loom diverts stored water, leaves fold and the forest loses its evacuation height during storms. Their scale changes route safety, not merely the view.
- **Shellbacks** are six-legged bark-shelled herbivores that cross basin leaves to drink at their roots. Their weight tips water toward lower basins. Their migration follows seasonal pressure and canopy growth; they are wildlife, never default mounts or moral guides.
- **Silver shoals** sense pressure through the Bell Below's smoked glass. Their movement warns of a gate load but cannot replace a gauge or an operator's judgment.
- **Orchid roots, jaguars, chigüires, sea velas, and dragons** occupy different habitats and make demands on transport. Familiar animal kinship grounds the strange ecology. A local creature's light does not make it a Godbeast. Draconis remains the locked Fire Gate Godbeast; Aru is a living roost dragon.
- **Oriván** is a proposed ancient water-engineering culture of the eastern cleft. Its pressure vault used three independent hands and a durable civic ledger so no single operator could mortgage the valley's reserve. Later Avilaran crews repaired and extended it; living keepers and families inherit its maintenance, with both skill and neglected obligations. The architecture has inverted basalt ribs, suspended counterweights, water glass, and legible galleries. It is a built answer to hydrology, not a mysterious precursor that exists only to give the protagonist a quest.
- **Miralumbre's modern civic design** pairs water-collecting roofs, freight lifts, city relays, and ordinary office work. Modernity persists across the Realm; rural places are not a timeless primitive counterpoint.

## Dramatic promise and limits

The rider circle predates Cael. Iva measures and commands the upper water, Tami maps the high route, Luz moves households and animals, Iria works beside keepers, and Cael makes his flawed instrument legible during the inquiry. Oren can pull the release because she knows the vault; Varek can confess and signal the convoy without being instantly absolved. The “boss” is a pressure engine under bad incentives. Stopping it loses a road and exposes a financing trail. A later human opponent may be identified only by evidence, not by an aura or a conveniently matching seal.

Visual direction: human scale against a large, plausible system; rain-stained ceramic, worn brass, carved basalt, load-bearing roots, and practical clothing. The first twenty-one chapters retain their existing illustrations. The new Bell Below plate depicts the vault, basin canopy, and three workers. No borrowed Pandora species, spiritual network, floating landform, or character silhouette enters this setting. Anima is a canon term for connection; the world does not speak a single personalized verdict to Iria.
