| Mar Arcano | The distinct five-source inland sea of the coastal practicum, separate from Avilara's Mar de Aurora. | Its deep aquifer corridors carry water and resonance between Realms, never people. Boat crews govern markers; velas need open water and spawning channels. |
| Eastern cleft | Oriván pressure vault beneath an old road, hanging basin crowns and settlements below. | Stored water holds up the road; a draw that supports transport starves the forest reserve. Releases need three operators and warning to people above and below. |

The narrative's horse road, flight and sea passage are separate modes with weather, animal welfare, local knowledge and permission. Horsewomen know muddy banks that a pilot cannot inspect; dragons choose flights; sea crews can refuse a channel. No animal or rider is an all-purpose shortcut.

## Magic and instruments

**Destello** is a visible Anima signature in the separate novel's Avilaran usage. Do not narrow it to a signal that appears only when a Gate opens, or use color as a moral diagnosis. Iria's particular aptitude is to notice some **structural strain** in Foundation/Flow work; what she sees is partial and can be an echo. She does not read minds, see destinies, or inherit Selene's rare full-chord sight. Foundation work taxes her body and needs training. Nero is the fertile unknown, while corrupted Shadow is a separate danger. Draconis is the locked Fire Gate Godbeast; Aru is a living roost dragon.

**Faultglass:** a two-slot survey glass that compares angles through a stone working for two breaths, then its plates equalize. Running water resets them. It clarifies a local seam, never the operator's motive or an entire river's future. It is Iria's professional tool, not an inherited two-breath veil stone. **Handglass:** ordinary map/image and city-relay device; upper passes interrupt service. **Elvia's witness tile:** signed archival evidence of the third northern draw. **Oren's comb:** brass teeth listen for pressure differences in a bearing; one missing note matters only when confirmed by gauges and keepers. **Oriván witness tiles:** cumulative draw, repair and operator records. **Copper listening bands:** Cael's published survey design has been repurposed to feed a reserve draw; the shallow cut on recovered bands is an investigative lead, not proof that he installed every instance. Each tool has a maker, a maintenance history, a limit and someone with standing to contest its reading.

## Living systems and built cultures

- **Bell-cups** hold rain above upper farms. The chorus shifts with changing water timing; hearing a note is a clue to measure, not a voice offering instructions.
- **Basin crowns** store rain on broad leaves in the eastern cleft. Shellbacks cross them to drink and tip water toward lower basins. A reduced reserve folds the leaves and removes flood refuge below.
- **Orchid roots** register changing local flow; giant flowers can shelter a rider. A jaguar and cub protect their own route. Neither species exists to judge Iria's choices.
- **High roost** dragons are vulnerable animals with territorial partners and flight limits. A rescue creates no lifelong entitlement to ride.
- **Mar Arcano's velas** migrate through spawning channels with boat crews and riders who understand the current; silver shoals register local pressure. No creature is a proxy for a Godbeast.
- **Oriván** was an ancient water-engineering culture. Its inverted basalt ribs, counterweights and three-operator ledger made a monopoly draw difficult. Later Avilaran repair crews inherited the system, including damaged records and duties. Age does not make the builders infallible or erase the present keepers' authority.
- **Miralumbre and Solavila** are working modern cities with disagreements about shared water. Their architecture records climate and labor: rain roofs and lifts in one; sea-facing coral stone, blue tile, civic plazas and a recently built reserve quarter in the other.
