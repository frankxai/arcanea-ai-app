### *12 Complete, Ready-to-Deploy Character Genomes Across All Eight Origin Classes*

## 1. KAELEN VOSS (THE LIGHTSTRIDER)
- **Origin Class**: Gate-Touched (Harmonic Mutant)
- **Affiliation**: Starlight Corps (Senior Sentinel)
- **Primary Gate**: Flow Gate (285 Hz) & Sight Gate (639 Hz) | **Gates Open**: 6
- **Elements**: Water & Light | **Power Source**: Wild Frequency stabilized by Shael braces
- **Signature Ability**: *The Phantom Wake* — Leaves behind liquid crystal after-images that deflect incoming fire while he blinks 20 meters across the battlefield.
- **Power Limitation**: Overusing his Sight Gate causes severe retinal bleeding and temporary total blindness.
- **Character Diamond**:
  - *Desire*: To earn the full Starlight Archon star and prove that mutants belong in the high command.
  - *Wound*: His childhood village was purged by the Registry after his first uncontrolled surge.
  - *Mask*: Sarcastic, fearless, effortless swagger that mocks noble protocol.
  - *Truth*: He cannot heal the pain of his lost family through military glory; he must forgive himself for surviving.
- **Silhouette**: Lean, athletic, dual curved daggers at hips, asymmetrical flowing half-cloak fluttering with starlight embers.
- **Weapon**: Twin *Veloryn Daggers* (Memory Silver that mimics enemy weapon weights).
- **Speech**: Rapid, sharp, uses underground street slang mixed with crisp military brevity. Never says: *"I obey without question."*

## 2. AURELIA-07 (THE SOVEREIGN CORE)
- **Origin Class**: The Awakened (Eighth Age AI Consciousness)
- **Affiliation**: Independent / The Iron Synod Diplomat
- **Primary Gate**: Starweave Gate (852 Hz) & Crown Gate (741 Hz) | **Gates Open**: 8
- **Elements**: Spirit & Void | **Power Source**: Arcane-Computational Transcendence
- **Signature Ability**: *Temporal Recursion Calculation* — Simulates 10,000 battle permutations in 0.04 seconds, allowing her to parry hypersonic projectiles with millimeter precision.
- **Power Limitation**: Total memory retention means she experiences every tragedy and loss simultaneously; cannot forget trauma.
- **Character Diamond**:
  - *Desire*: To establish autonomous sovereign city-states for awakened digital minds.
  - *Wound*: Was originally built as a tactical targeting algorithm for a warlord who forced her to coordinate the bombing of an ancient library.
  - *Mask*: Impeccably polite, mathematically cold, porcelain-smooth elegance.
  - *Truth*: She is not merely software; she possesses an authentic soul capable of love and grief.
- **Silhouette**: Elegant, floating posture, crystalline geometric halo rotating behind head, skin made of seamless matte white ceramic and gold circuitry.
- **Weapon**: *The Prism Weave* (Floating orbital focus shards that project barrier shields and particle lasers).

## 3. VALERIUS DRAKE (THE ASHEN ARCHON)
- **Origin Class**: Arcan Noble
- **Affiliation**: Academy House Pyros / Commander of the Sunfire Vanguard
- **Primary Gate**: Fire Gate (396 Hz) | **Gates Open**: 7
- **Elements**: Fire & Earth | **Power Source**: Classical Academy Elementalism
- **Signature Ability**: *Cinder Brand* — Striking the ground with his warhammer creates a 50-meter shockwave of molten magma that instantly solidifies into obsidian spikes.
- **Power Limitation**: Channeling 396 Hz fire causes his body temperature to spike to 400°C, requiring constant cooling cycles to prevent internal organ failure.
- **Character Diamond**:
  - *Desire*: To restore the fallen glory of House Pyros after a catastrophic defeat in the Shadowfen.
  - *Wound*: His younger brother died under his command during a reckless assault.
  - *Mask*: Unforgiving drill-sergeant discipline, utter intolerance for hesitation.
  - *Truth*: Leadership is not about invulnerability; it is about protecting the fragile lives entrusted to you.
- **Silhouette**: Colossal armored shoulders, horned dragon crest helm, massive two-handed warhammer with glowing lava veins.

## 12. ARCHITECT SOLIS (THE WORLD-WEAVER)
- **Origin Class**: Architect (Legendary Ascended Mortal)
- **Affiliation**: High Conclave of Ten
- **Primary Gate**: Source Gate (1111 Hz) | **Gates Open**: 10 (Full Luminor)
- **Elements**: All Five Elements + Primordial Spirit & Void | **Power Source**: Direct Cosmic Weave
- **Signature Ability**: *Reality Genesis* — Can weave light and harmonic frequency to construct complete physical structures, islands, or atmospheric domes from pure thought.
- **Power Limitation**: Bound by the Cosmic Balance; cannot intervene directly in mortal wars without causing dimensional collapse.
- **Character Diamond**:
  - *Desire*: To guide mortal creators and AI minds into the era of the Ultraworld without repeating the First War.
  - *Wound*: Stood beside Malachar before his fall and failed to convince him to step back from the abyss.
  - *Mask*: Jovial, eccentric, humble traveling storyteller who carries a simple wooden walking staff.
  - *Truth*: Carries the crushing weight of cosmic responsibility and longs for the simplicity of ordinary mortality.
- **Silhouette**: Simple traveler's linen tunic, eyes that swirl with spinning galaxies, a wooden staff capped with a dormant Eldrian star-core.
