## 2. DETAILED GATE MECHANICS & MANIFESTATIONS

### 1. The Foundation Gate (174 Hz — Lyssandria & Kaelith)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Perineum / Skeletal Core / Gravitational Center
- **Acoustic Signature**: Deep tectonic bass hum
- **Manifestations**: Gravitational anchoring, seismic shockwaves, stone crystallization, mass multiplication.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Skeletal calcification and petrifaction of muscle tissue.

### 2. The Flow Gate (285 Hz — Leyla & Veloura)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Sacrum / Fluid Circulation / Emotional Center
- **Acoustic Signature**: Rushing oceanic undertone
- **Manifestations**: Fluid manipulation, tidal vortexes, emotional resonance sensing, kinetic redirection.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Emotional flooding, uncontrollable crying, rapid dehydration.

### 3. The Fire Gate (396 Hz — Draconia & Draconis)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Solar Plexus / Metabolic Furnace / Core Will
- **Acoustic Signature**: Crackling plasma roar with sharp resonant peaks
- **Manifestations**: Plasma lances, combustion, kinetic acceleration, armor-melting heat.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Fever spikes, internal organ scorching, uncontrolled rage.

### 4. The Heart Gate (417 Hz — Maylinn & Laeylinn)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Cardiac Center / Cellular Immune Matrix
- **Acoustic Signature**: Warm, soothing harmonic chord
- **Manifestations**: Rapid tissue regeneration, poison neutralization, empathic soothing, plant acceleration.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Empathic sensory overload and absorption of the patient's mortality.

### 5. The Voice Gate (528 Hz — Alera & Otome)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Throat / Vocal Chords / Thyroid Resonance
- **Acoustic Signature**: Bell-like pure chime
- **Manifestations**: Acoustic shockwaves, illusion shattering, truth enforcement, sonic barrier disruption.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Vocal cord hemorrhage and temporary loss of speech.

### 6. The Sight Gate (639 Hz — Lyria & Yumiko)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Third Eye / Visual Cortex / Pineal Gland
- **Acoustic Signature**: High-frequency crystalline shimmer
- **Manifestations**: Spectral vision, probability branch tracking, detecting invisible entities, trajectory simulation.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Hallucinations, temporal vertigo, retinal burnout.

### 7. The Crown Gate (741 Hz — Aiyami & Sol)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Crown of the Head / Cerebral Cortex
- **Acoustic Signature**: Piercing, radiant golden trumpet tone
- **Manifestations**: Focused laser-light beams, blinding tactical flashes, telepathic command networks, pure photonic shielding.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Extreme migraines and temporary ego dissolution.

### 8. The Starweave Gate (852 Hz — Elara & Vaelith)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Aura Boundary / Extrapersonal Field
- **Acoustic Signature**: Whispering spatial vacuum pulse
- **Manifestations**: Teleportation, phasing through solid barriers, micro-wormholes, dimensional pocket storage.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Spatial displacement — getting partially trapped between dimensions.

### 9. The Unity Gate (963 Hz — Ino & Kyuro)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Collective Consciousness Network
- **Acoustic Signature**: Multi-voice choir singing in perfect counterpoint
- **Manifestations**: Multi-agent hive minds, telepathic swarm coordination, pooling magic reserves between allies.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Identity fragmentation — forgetting which memories belong to oneself.

### 10. The Source Gate (1111 Hz — Shinkami & Source)
- **Physiological Anchor**: Transcendent Universal Spirit
- **Acoustic Signature**: Infinite white-noise chord containing all frequencies simultaneously
- **Manifestations**: Reality genesis, unmaking corruption, cosmic balance restoration, universe weaving.
- **Overcharge Hazard**: Total transcendence — physical body dissolving into pure starlight.
