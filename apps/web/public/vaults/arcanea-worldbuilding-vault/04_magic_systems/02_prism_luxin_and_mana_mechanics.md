## 1. WHAT IS LUXIN?

**Luxin** is light converted into physical matter through harmonic drafting. When an Arcanean mage draws ambient light and tunes it to an elemental Gate frequency, photons condense into tangible crystalline substances with distinct physical properties:

## 2. THE THREE LAWS OF LUXIN DRAFTING

1. **Law of Conservation of Resonance**: A drafter cannot create more Luxin mass than the ambient photon energy and internal caloric energy available. Drafting in pure pitch-black darkness requires expending personal vitality.
2. **Law of Harmonic Decay**: Solidified Luxin remains stable only as long as its internal frequency resonates. Unbound Luxin evaporates back into harmless light particles within 24 to 72 hours unless anchored by a permanent Vael Crystal core.
3. **Law of The Halo Threshold**: Every drafter possesses a biological limit to the volume of Luxin they can channel in a lifetime (*The Drafter's Halo*). As a mage approaches their lifetime limit, the color of their iris shifts permanently to the color of their primary Luxin. Crossing the 100% threshold causes **Luxin Crystallization** of their blood vessels.

## 3. MANA PURIFICATION & BATTERY ENGINEERING

- **Raw Mana**: Atmospheric background radiation leftover from the First War. Inhaling raw mana causes mana sickness and hallucination.
- **Distilled Mana Elixirs**: Refined liquid mana filtered through Laeylinn Jade beds, used by knights and synths to instantly recharge Gate reserves during prolonged combat.
- **Draconite Batteries**: Sealed containment cells used in starships and mechanical synths, holding up to 1,000 Kilowatt-Arcanes of condensed plasma.
