# FACTION DESIGN STANDARDS
### *Exact Color Palettes, Material Physics, Silhouettes & Light Behaviors*

> **Classification**: Master Visual Doctrine (Volume 2)  
> **Application**: 3D Asset Creation, Concept Art, UI Design, Merchandising, Wardrobe

---

## 1. STARLIGHT CORPS
- **Primary Color**: Starlight Silver (`#C0C0D0` / `rgb(192, 192, 208)`)
- **Secondary Color**: Deep Cosmic Blue (`#1A1A3E` / `rgb(26, 26, 62)`)
- **Accent Color**: Celestial White-Gold (`#FFD700` / `#FFFACD`)
- **Materials**: Star-forged Shael battleplate, crystalline rank stars, constellation light-silk.
- **Light Behavior**: Ambient, gentle, constant starlight luminescence. Armor plates emit faint silver glow in shadow.
- **Silhouette**: Regal military posture, high collar, sweeping constellation half-cloak, slender polearm or sidearm.

---

## 2. HOUSE LUMINA (ACADEMY)
- **Primary Color**: Pure Alabaster (`#F8F9FA` / `rgb(248, 249, 250)`)
- **Secondary Color**: Solar Amber (`#FFA000` / `rgb(255, 160, 0)`)
- **Accent Color**: Radiance Gold (`#FFD700`)
- **Materials**: Braided solar-silk robes, gold-inlaid circlets, white marble focus orbs.
- **Light Behavior**: Soft internal solar radiance. Light appears to emanate from within the fabric itself.
- **Silhouette**: Upright scholarly elegance, symmetrical flowing robes, tall gold-crested staff.

---

## 3. HOUSE NERO (ACADEMY)
- **Primary Color**: Abyssal Midnight (`#0B0E14` / `rgb(11, 14, 20)`)
- **Secondary Color**: Deep Void Purple (`#4A148C` / `rgb(74, 20, 140)`)
- **Accent Color**: Silver Starlight Pinpricks (`#E0E0E0`)
- **Materials**: Void-silk (absorbs 98% of ambient light), polished obsidian clasps, thin silver chains.
- **Light Behavior**: Absorptive. Ambient light dims slightly around the wearer; micro-constellations shimmer in fabric folds.
- **Silhouette**: Hooded, angular, flowing asymmetric lines, trailing shadow wisps.

---

## 4. THE GATE-TOUCHED UNDERGROUND
- **Primary Color**: Raw Slate Gray (`#37474F` / `rgb(55, 71, 79)`)
- **Secondary Color**: Distressed Leather Ochre (`#8D6E63` / `rgb(141, 110, 99)`)
- **Accent Color**: Wild Cyan Pulse (`#00E5FF` / `rgb(0, 229, 255)`) & Raw Ember (`#FF5722`)
- **Materials**: Distressed leather combat dusters, repurposed Shael scrap plates, copper energy wires, heavy bandages.
- **Light Behavior**: Erratic, flickering, pulse-reactive. Crackles of elemental energy during high emotion.
- **Silhouette**: Asymmetrical, armored combat boots, wrapped forearms, scavenged focus gauntlets, ready for instant evasion.

---

## 5. THE VOID ASCENDANTS
- **Primary Color**: Corrupted Pitch Black (`#05070A` / `rgb(5, 7, 10)`)
- **Secondary Color**: Bruised Ethereal Violet (`#311B92` / `rgb(49, 27, 146)`)
- **Accent Color**: Toxic Dissonance Crimson (`#B71C1C` / `rgb(183, 28, 28)`)
- **Materials**: Kurusei Iron (living, shifting metal with jagged thorn motifs), Hollow Glass blades, tattered burial shrouds.
- **Light Behavior**: Negative light. Pulls surrounding illumination into dark gravity sinks; casts inverted shadows.
- **Silhouette**: Towering, jagged, thorned crowns, tattered floating cloaks that dissolve into black mist.
